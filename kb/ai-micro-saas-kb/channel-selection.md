# Choose a channel by buyer access and the buying task

These are research-informed proposals. Relative effort and timing are planning judgments, not measured averages. Read the linked channel pages for evidence and counterexamples.

| Starting situation | First experiment | Why it is informative | Principal cost | Decision signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reachable professional niche | [Direct discovery and pilot](/playbooks/b2b-discovery.md) | Tests urgency and buyer authority | Founder time and onboarding | Paid pilot completes a real workflow |
| Buyer already searches for the job | [Narrow search test](/channels/paid-search.md) | Connects explicit demand to an offer | Clicks and landing-page work | Paid completion within margin budget |
| Useful searchable subtask | [One free utility](/channels/free-tools.md) | Lets a visitor experience value | Build, maintenance, compute | Users progress into paid workflow |
| Visual consumer transformation | [Small creator portfolio](/channels/creators.md) | Tests buyer response to a concrete result | Fees and creative coordination | Qualified paid cohort, after refunds |
| Existing relevant following | [Repeated demos](/channels/founder-publishing.md) | Uses access already available | Ongoing editorial effort | Activated buyers per demonstration |
| Credible audience-owning partner | [Bounded distribution pilot](/channels/partnerships.md) | Tests real access, not an agreement alone | Partner work and revenue share | Placement produces retained customers |
| Useful product for technical early adopters | [Launch event](/channels/launch-platforms.md) | Concentrates feedback and discovery | Preparation and support | Cohort still uses the product later |
| Product converts and has margin | [Affiliate pilot](/channels/affiliates.md) | Tests delegated selling incentives | Commissions and management | Incremental profitable orders |

## Sequence matters

Before activation works, purchase learning in small amounts. After activation works, compare cohorts from different sources. After retention and margin become clearer, increase the budget in the most credible channel. Building five channels simultaneously makes it difficult to learn which promise and audience are working.

A practical initial limit is **one primary acquisition test and one follow-up mechanism**. This is a proposed constraint to keep learning interpretable, not an optimal number proven by the cases. Keep customer support and reliability work outside the marketing experiment: they remain part of delivering the service.

## Where the evidence is insufficient

This bundle cannot rank cold email, app marketplaces, paid social, Discord, AI-answer-engine optimization, or partner integrations by return. Some are plausible routes, but the inspected cases lack comparable outcome data. Do not assign a winner merely because a channel appears in a successful founder’s list.
