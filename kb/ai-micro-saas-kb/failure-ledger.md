## Biases to keep visible

Public founders can benefit from reputation, consulting, courses, affiliates, or fundraising. A tool vendor benefits when a builder’s story makes its platform look effective. Those incentives do not make a claim false; they explain why the sample is not representative.

A business that attracts little attention is less likely to appear in search results, newsletters, or interviews. This research therefore cannot estimate the success rate of AI micro-SaaS. Anonymous forum revenue stories were not promoted into named success cases merely to increase coverage.

When revisiting a case, look for delayed costs: refunds, customer support, changing model costs, platform dependencies, and churn. Initial cash can be real while the operating business remains fragile. The [measurement guide](/playbooks/measurement.md) gives a way to record these separately.
