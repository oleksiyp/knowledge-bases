# Frame My Home: shipped product, distribution still unresolved

## Evidence snapshot

Berg’s other Bolt-built product, Frame My Home, converts home or pet inputs into framed art, with outsourced physical fulfillment. The same profile says it is generating revenue without quantifying it, while quoting distribution as work still to solve.[^bolt-berg]

## Distribution interpretation

This is commerce adjacent to micro-SaaS, not evidence of subscriptions. A visually appealing output suggests a demonstration opportunity, but it does not establish an effective paid-social channel. Proposed tests should count delivered, non-refunded orders after fulfillment costs. A fast checkout implementation cannot settle those economics. See [demo design](/playbooks/attention-to-activation.md) and [failures and limits](/failure-ledger.md).

## What would strengthen the evidence

Channel-level paid cohorts, acquisition costs, repeat use, and a dated billing reconciliation would be needed to establish repeatable customer acquisition. This page reports the cited snapshot; it does not establish current revenue or current team size.
