# Jenni AI: creator audience fit beats raw views

## Evidence snapshot

In April 2024, David Park said influencer marketing added $100,000 MRR to Jenni, an academic writing assistant. He described finding creators through existing users, testing smaller accounts, and tracking conversions. He contrasted a 30-million-view video with weak conversions against a much smaller video that sold well. He also reported roughly 16% monthly churn.[^jenni]

## Distribution interpretation

The lesson is to evaluate the viewer’s task and the paid cohort, not to purchase maximum reach. These remain founder-attributed results without campaign data. This is an AI-powered, funded-business comparator, not evidence that AI built a solo SaaS. See [creators](/channels/creators.md) and [measurement](/playbooks/measurement.md).

## What would strengthen the evidence

Channel-level paid cohorts, acquisition costs, repeat use, and a dated billing reconciliation would be needed to establish repeatable customer acquisition. This page reports the cited snapshot; it does not establish current revenue or current team size.
