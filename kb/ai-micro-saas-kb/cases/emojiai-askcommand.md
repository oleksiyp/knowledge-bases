# EmojiAI and AskCommand: the same founder did not always win

## Evidence snapshot

In June 2023, Tony Dinh described five or six AI experiments. Three reached a finished, priced, actively promoted state: EmojiAI, AskCommand, and TypingMind. He said only TypingMind generated significant revenue and described the other two as failures kept online for fun.[^typing-early]

## Distribution interpretation

This comparison partially holds the founder constant, but it is not a controlled experiment: timing, effort, audience size, and buyer needs could differ. It still challenges the inference that publicly shipping an AI product to an established audience is sufficient. Investigate willingness to pay and repeated use before blaming the algorithm. See the [failure ledger](/failure-ledger.md).

## What would strengthen the evidence

Channel-level paid cohorts, acquisition costs, repeat use, and a dated billing reconciliation would be needed to establish repeatable customer acquisition. This page reports the cited snapshot; it does not establish current revenue or current team size.
