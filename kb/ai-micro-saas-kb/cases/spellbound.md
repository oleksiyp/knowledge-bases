# Spellbound: LinkedIn visibility opens a partner conversation

## Evidence snapshot

Simon Berg, formerly Ceros CEO, built Spellbound with Bolt after his children struggled with spelling lists. It turns lists into practice activities and includes subscriptions. A LinkedIn announcement prompted a UK parent-network leader to approach him about distribution. The December 2025 vendor profile describes exploration, not a completed rollout or attributable customer count.[^bolt-berg]

## Distribution interpretation

A relevant audience owner can be a more valuable response than many consumer likes. However, an experienced executive’s network is not equivalent to starting without relationships. Treat this as a partnership lead. A pilot needs a named audience, a placement commitment, and paid-conversion measurement before it becomes a proven channel. See [partnerships](/channels/partnerships.md).

## What would strengthen the evidence

Channel-level paid cohorts, acquisition costs, repeat use, and a dated billing reconciliation would be needed to establish repeatable customer acquisition. This page reports the cited snapshot; it does not establish current revenue or current team size.
