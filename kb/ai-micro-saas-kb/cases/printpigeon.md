# PrintPigeon: search demand for a mundane task

## Evidence snapshot

Yannis, an experienced digital marketer, described building a letter-mailing service with Lovable in three days. He tested demand with £100 of Google Ads, found ads alone unsustainable, and moved to localized origin-to-destination search pages. He reported traffic from expats and seasonal Santa-letter demand. The interview gives no revenue, acquisition cost, or conversion rate. Its publisher is Lovable, which Yannis subsequently joined.[^printpigeon]

## Distribution interpretation

The customer promise is completing an inconvenient physical task; AI is the production method. This is an adjacent transactional service, not demonstrated recurring SaaS. The useful experiment is a narrow query tied to a payable action. Geographic page generation only helps if each page answers the actual delivery question. See [search](/channels/search.md) and [paid tests](/channels/paid-search.md).

## What would strengthen the evidence

Channel-level paid cohorts, acquisition costs, repeat use, and a dated billing reconciliation would be needed to establish repeatable customer acquisition. This page reports the cited snapshot; it does not establish current revenue or current team size.
