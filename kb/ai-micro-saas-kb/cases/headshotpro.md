# HeadshotPro: make distribution economically attractive to reviewers

## Evidence snapshot

Rewardful’s undated HeadshotPro case study reports more than $50,000 in monthly affiliate-attributed revenue and more than 15% of monthly company revenue. These are neither affiliate payouts nor demonstrated MRR. Danny Postma says commissions encourage people to write about the product. The signup page observed October 2026 offers 30% commissions, a 60-day cookie, and access to a review-package application.[^headshot-affiliates][^headshot-terms]

## Distribution interpretation

This gives reviewers both material to demonstrate and a reason to maintain recommendations. The vendor’s performance claim lacks an incremental-sales control, publication date, and refund reconciliation. A cookie can credit an order that would otherwise have arrived directly. Use the case to understand the mechanism, not to forecast returns. See [affiliates](/channels/affiliates.md).

## What would strengthen the evidence

Channel-level paid cohorts, acquisition costs, repeat use, and a dated billing reconciliation would be needed to establish repeatable customer acquisition. This page reports the cited snapshot; it does not establish current revenue or current team size.
