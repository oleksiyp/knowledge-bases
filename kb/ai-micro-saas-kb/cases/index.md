# Cases

* [PrintPigeon: search demand for a mundane task](/cases/printpigeon.md) — An AI-built letter service used paid search to test demand, then localized pages.
* [BuzzerBee: small revenue, concrete recurring pain](/cases/buzzerbee.md) — A modest AI-built business with disclosed operating costs but missing acquisition attribution.
* [Spellbound: LinkedIn visibility opens a partner conversation](/cases/spellbound.md) — An AI-built spelling app attracted an audience owner, with no closed-partnership result disclosed.
* [Frame My Home: shipped product, distribution still unresolved](/cases/frame-my-home.md) — AI-generated framed art illustrates the gap between building and customer acquisition.
* [Lumoo: domain expertise before broad attention](/cases/lumoo.md) — An AI-built fashion workflow business with reported B2B traction and an undisclosed acquisition mix.
* [Crown Services: asking the development ecosystem for visibility](/cases/crown-services.md) — A named AI-built ERP project with a public support request but no quantified traction.
* [TypingMind: an existing audience and repeated product demonstrations](/cases/typingmind.md) — A license-led AI product used founder updates and a coordinated launch.
* [Chatbase: novelty opens the door, positioning changes afterward](/cases/chatbase.md) — A tiny initial audience did not prevent reach, but later B2B growth used a broader motion.
* [SiteGPT: a launch sequence followed by utility content](/cases/sitegpt.md) — Founder-reported early traction across X, Hacker News, and Product Hunt.
* [HeadshotPro: make distribution economically attractive to reviewers](/cases/headshotpro.md) — Affiliate economics are documented; incremental lift remains unknown.
* [Jenni AI: creator audience fit beats raw views](/cases/jenni.md) — A founder interview connects influencer experiments to revenue while acknowledging churn.
* [PDF.ai: acquisition, a descriptive brand, and an initial lifetime offer](/cases/pdf-ai.md) — A founder account distinguishes monetization validation from proposed future marketing.
* [EmojiAI and AskCommand: the same founder did not always win](/cases/emojiai-askcommand.md) — A paired negative case limits what can be inferred from a successful founder’s audience.
