# TypingMind: an existing audience and repeated product demonstrations

## Evidence snapshot

Tony Dinh says he launched an improved ChatGPT interface on Twitter in March 2023, then tweeted new features as sales arrived. His existing Twitter and newsletter audiences supported a Product Hunt launch. In November’s interview he explicitly distinguishes mostly one-time purchase income from recurring revenue. The June interview separately reports $3,500 MRR for custom deployments.[^typing-launch][^typing-early]

## Distribution interpretation

A concrete improvement makes each release a new reason to demonstrate the product. The owned audience was an input to the launch, so copying the final launch day omits an important prerequisite. AI-powered functionality is established; AI-written implementation is not. Compare [founder publishing](/channels/founder-publishing.md) with [launch sites](/channels/launch-platforms.md), and keep license receipts separate from subscription growth.

## What would strengthen the evidence

Channel-level paid cohorts, acquisition costs, repeat use, and a dated billing reconciliation would be needed to establish repeatable customer acquisition. This page reports the cited snapshot; it does not establish current revenue or current team size.
