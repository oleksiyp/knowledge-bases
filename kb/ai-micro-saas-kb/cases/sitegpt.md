# SiteGPT: a launch sequence followed by utility content

## Evidence snapshot

Bhanu Teja’s May 9, 2023 AMA reports $10,000 MRR within a month for website-trained chatbots. He describes a Twitter launch, Hacker News visibility the next day, then Product Hunt’s top daily position. He later mentions word of mouth in the discussion. The product’s tools directory, observed October 2026, lists free utilities such as sitemap extraction and document-to-Markdown conversion; that page provides no acquisition results.[^sitegpt][^sitegpt-tools]

## Distribution interpretation

Separate the historical launch account from the later observable content surface. The existence of many free tools does not prove they caused revenue, nor that the launch still works similarly today. A useful funnel would connect a utility user’s job to the paid chatbot workflow. See [launch platforms](/channels/launch-platforms.md) and [free tools](/channels/free-tools.md).

## What would strengthen the evidence

Channel-level paid cohorts, acquisition costs, repeat use, and a dated billing reconciliation would be needed to establish repeatable customer acquisition.
