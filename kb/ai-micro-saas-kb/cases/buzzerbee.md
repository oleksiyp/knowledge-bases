# BuzzerBee: small revenue, concrete recurring pain

## Evidence snapshot

In an August 2025 interview, Darren described BuzzerBee, which automates apartment buzzer access for expected visitors and deliveries. He prototyped with Bolt and completed the app using Cursor. The account reports three weeks to a working product, 30 active customers, $150 monthly revenue, and $50 monthly maintenance costs. It does not identify how those customers were acquired.[^buzzerbee]

## Distribution interpretation

This is more useful as evidence of a narrow paid problem than as a marketing success story. A podcast feature is an observable publicity event; it cannot explain customers already present at the interview. A possible next experiment would target compatible buildings and resident communities, but that is a research proposal, not Darren’s documented strategy. Access-control trust and compatibility may matter more than broad reach. See [niche communities](/channels/communities.md).

## What would strengthen the evidence

Channel-level paid cohorts, acquisition costs, repeat use, and a dated billing reconciliation would be needed to establish repeatable customer acquisition. This page reports the cited snapshot; it does not establish current revenue or current team size.
