# Affiliates: pay for a measurable commercial action

## Evidence

[HeadshotPro](/cases/headshotpro.md) documents a commission program and vendor-reported affiliate-attributed sales.[^headshot-affiliates][^headshot-terms] Google requires paid links to be appropriately qualified; purchasing ranking credit is not a sound objective.[^google-spam]

## Mechanism and buyer fit — analysis

Affiliates need an offer their audience understands and believes will convert. Useful supporting materials include realistic outputs, limitations, pricing, and clear attribution terms. Reviewers choose where to publish: articles, videos, newsletters, or comparison pages.

## A testable experiment — proposal

Start with a few partners who already serve the buyer. Track referred purchases through the refund window and inspect customer quality. Set a commission budget from contribution margin rather than matching a competitor. Provide disclosure guidance and let reviewers report honest experience.

## Limits

Affiliate-attributed revenue may include existing demand. Coupon leakage, self-referrals, refunds, and overlapping creator campaigns complicate credit. Do not count tracked revenue as affiliate payouts, MRR, or net profit.

Use the common [measurement definitions](/playbooks/measurement.md); these proposals are not industry benchmarks.
