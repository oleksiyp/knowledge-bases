# Search: capture an existing task, not generic AI curiosity

## Evidence

[PrintPigeon](/cases/printpigeon.md) reports localized task pages; [PDF.ai](/cases/pdf-ai.md) credits relevant organic queries.[^printpigeon][^pdfai] These accounts do not supply controlled attribution. Google’s policies identify doorway pages and scaled content produced primarily to manipulate ranking as abuse.[^google-spam]

## Mechanism and buyer fit — analysis

A query such as a specific file conversion or delivery route can reveal a near-term job. Generic AI news traffic may be much less qualified. Useful pages answer the task, expose limitations, and offer the next step. A descriptive domain can explain the offer; it does not by itself establish search superiority.

## A testable experiment — proposal

Choose ten closely related buyer questions from conversations. Build three substantive pages first, each with examples and a task-specific call to action. Track impressions, qualified visits, task completion, and paid starts by landing page. Expand only after seeing actual query relevance. Judge indexing and learning separately from immediate payback.

## Limits

The historical accounts span different competitive periods. Ranking, conversion, and unit economics can deteriorate independently. Do not extrapolate a successful geographic example into thousands of near-duplicate pages.

Use the common [measurement definitions](/playbooks/measurement.md); these proposals are not industry benchmarks.
