# Directories and AI newsletters: discovery with weak attribution

## Evidence

[Chatbase](/cases/chatbase.md) includes AI directories among its early sources of discovery, alongside several other channels.[^chatbase] The account does not quantify directory-specific paid users or distinguish every free listing from paid placement.

## Mechanism and buyer fit — analysis

Directories may reach people actively comparing tools, but the category can be much broader than the buyer’s task. Newsletter mentions are similarly heterogeneous: an editorial recommendation, paid sponsorship, and founder-owned email list have different incentives and costs.

## A testable experiment — proposal

Select a few relevant publications or listings by audience fit, not the number of backlinks offered. Use accurate screenshots and a distinct link. Record whether the placement is paid, editorial, affiliate, or owned. Judge incremental qualified traffic and paid activation before purchasing more exposure.

## Limits

This research does not establish a best AI directory, expected return from paid listings, or a reliable backlink strategy. Bulk submission can create busywork without learning which buyer responds.

Use the common [measurement definitions](/playbooks/measurement.md); these proposals are not industry benchmarks.
