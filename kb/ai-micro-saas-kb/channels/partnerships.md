# Partnerships and B2B outreach: find people who already reach the buyer

## Evidence

[Spellbound](/cases/spellbound.md) offers a partner-interest example, while [Lumoo](/cases/lumoo.md) offers a domain-expertise example with an undisclosed acquisition mix.[^bolt-berg][^lumoo] Neither establishes a repeatable cold-email funnel.

## Mechanism and buyer fit — analysis

A publisher can supply attention, an agency can supply implementation, and a workflow vendor can supply access at the moment of need. These are different agreements. For a small builder, a narrow paid pilot may be more informative than prolonged discussion of a broad strategic alliance.

## A testable experiment — proposal

Identify ten organizations already serving the buyer. Send a specific demonstration of a relevant task to appropriate contacts. Offer a bounded pilot with a named owner, one audience placement or workflow integration, dates, and a paid-conversion definition. Track conversations, pilots launched, and retained customers separately.

## Limits

An introduction is not an agreement; an agreement is not a rollout. One partner can become a concentration risk. Integration, support, revenue sharing, and exclusivity can outweigh the apparent benefit of free access to an audience.

Use the common [measurement definitions](/playbooks/measurement.md); these proposals are not industry benchmarks.
