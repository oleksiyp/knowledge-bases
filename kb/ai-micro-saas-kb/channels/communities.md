# Niche communities: learn where the problem is already discussed

## Evidence

[Chatbase](/cases/chatbase.md) names Reddit among several early surfaces without isolating its contribution.[^chatbase] Reddit’s guidance discourages repetitive promotion and emphasizes community-specific rules.[^reddit-spam] The [BuzzerBee](/cases/buzzerbee.md) evidence does not disclose acquisition; local community outreach is a proposed fit, not an observed result.

## Mechanism and buyer fit — analysis

The useful unit is a recurring problem conversation. A SaaS-builder forum can help with implementation but may be far from the target buyer. Credible participation requires context and a clear disclosure when discussing one’s own product.

## A testable experiment — proposal

Read a small set of relevant communities and record recurring tasks, current workarounds, and objections. Contribute a useful answer or demonstration where allowed. Invite willing participants into a short research conversation. Track qualified conversations and completed trials, with community rules checked before posting.

## Limits

This bundle does not demonstrate that mass Reddit posting works. Automated promotional replies can damage the very trust a community channel needs. No amount of post volume compensates for the wrong audience.

Use the common [measurement definitions](/playbooks/measurement.md); these proposals are not industry benchmarks.
