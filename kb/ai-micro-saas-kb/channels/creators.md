# Creators and short video: recruit audience fit

## Evidence

[Jenni](/cases/jenni.md) provides direct founder testimony for creator acquisition and a counterexample where enormous reach converted poorly. Park describes performance-linked incentives and repeated tests, not one guaranteed viral formula.[^jenni]

## Mechanism and buyer fit — analysis

Start from the people customers already watch. A creator who demonstrates the customer’s workflow reduces the explanation burden. A general entertainment clip may reach many people who have no use for the product. Organic creator sharing and paid sponsorship must be recorded separately.

## A testable experiment — proposal

Interview five users about trusted creators. Select a small set with visibly relevant audiences, agree on a real-use demonstration, and use distinct links or codes. Specify deliverables and content reuse separately. Review trial activation and refund-adjusted paid conversion before expanding. Allow for time from watching to purchase.

## Limits

A promo-code count is attributed sales, not necessarily incremental sales. Fees, editing, founder coordination, free accounts, and paid amplification all belong in costs. No reliable universal cost-per-view or creator-size threshold emerges from this sample.

Use the common [measurement definitions](/playbooks/measurement.md); these proposals are not industry benchmarks.
