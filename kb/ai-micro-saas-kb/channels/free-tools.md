# Free tools: a sample of the paid workflow

## Evidence

[SiteGPT](/cases/sitegpt.md) visibly offers sitemap, conversion, and AI chat utilities.[^sitegpt-tools] This verifies a tactic in use, not claims that free tools produce a particular share of its revenue.

## Mechanism and buyer fit — analysis

The strongest conceptual link is a free task whose output naturally becomes input to the paid workflow. For example, a hypothetical website audit could reveal gaps a paid support product helps address. A generic converter may attract people who will never need that product.

## A testable experiment — proposal

Create one useful tool near the paid job. Let visitors obtain the promised result. Offer a relevant next step and measure tool completion → paid-workflow start → successful setup → payment. Include compute cost and abuse in the experiment budget. Compare the tool cohort’s retention with other channels.

## Limits

Counting tools shipped is not an acquisition metric. A free tool can be valuable without being a good funnel. Avoid attributing domain-wide search growth to the tool without page-level evidence.

Use the common [measurement definitions](/playbooks/measurement.md); these proposals are not industry benchmarks.
