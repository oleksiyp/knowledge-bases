# Launch platforms: Product Hunt and Hacker News

## Evidence

[SiteGPT](/cases/sitegpt.md) reports sequential exposure on X, HN, and Product Hunt.[^sitegpt] Product Hunt’s current guide says makers can launch themselves and should invite visits and comments rather than directly request upvotes. Show HN asks for something people can try and discourages barriers to trying it.[^ph-guide][^hn-guide]

## Mechanism and buyer fit — analysis

A launch gives a clear occasion to show the product and answer questions. The audience is selected for interest in new technology. It may provide unusually good feedback yet represent a small part of the eventual customer market.

## A testable experiment — proposal

Prepare a usable demonstration, concise buyer-specific explanation, pricing, and support coverage. Record each platform separately. Inspect what visitors actually attempt, then follow the cohort through its natural repeat-use interval. Reuse substantive questions to improve onboarding and documentation.

## Limits

A front-page appearance is not controllable. Overlapping exposures make last-click claims unreliable. Launch discounts can produce customers with different retention from normal-price buyers. Do not buy votes or treat rankings as a substitute for a functioning product.

Use the common [measurement definitions](/playbooks/measurement.md); these proposals are not industry benchmarks.
