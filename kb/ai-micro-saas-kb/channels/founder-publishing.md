# Founder publishing: X, LinkedIn, and email

## Evidence

[TypingMind](/cases/typingmind.md) connected feature announcements to sales and brought an existing audience to launch.[^typing-launch] [Spellbound](/cases/spellbound.md) is a different outcome: a LinkedIn post led to partner interest, not a measured consumer funnel.[^bolt-berg]

## Mechanism and buyer fit — analysis

A useful post is a small product demonstration with a recognizable problem, visible result, and one action. Public revenue updates can attract other founders while missing buyers. Email offers a follow-up path when an interested reader is not ready to buy; subscribers still need a reason to use the product.

## A testable experiment — proposal

For one buyer segment, publish three different problem demonstrations over two weeks. Give each a distinct landing URL. Ask new paying users what persuaded them, while retaining first-touch source. Offer a relevant example or update subscription rather than an unrelated giveaway. Compare activated users and paid cohorts, not likes.

## Limits

Existing reputation, prior customers, and a timely category launch are confounders. One viral post does not establish a repeatable publishing system. There is insufficient evidence here to rank X against LinkedIn generally.

Use the common [measurement definitions](/playbooks/measurement.md); these proposals are not industry benchmarks.
