# Tool-vendor ecosystems: a second audience for the building story

## Evidence

[Crown Services](/cases/crown-services.md) explicitly seeks help in Cursor’s community.[^crown] Several other cases were discovered through vendor customer profiles. Their inclusion demonstrates publicity, not that the publicity acquired the customers described in the article.

## Mechanism and buyer fit — analysis

The building story is interesting to people choosing an AI development tool. The customer story is interesting to people needing the finished service. Sometimes those audiences overlap; often they do not. A platform feature can still help recruit collaborators or partners.

## A testable experiment — proposal

Prepare an honest demonstration with a clear user problem, build method, known limits, and link to the working product. Share through an appropriate showcase. Distinguish resulting developer questions, vendor opportunities, and actual buyer activations in the tracking sheet.

## Limits

Tool vendors select attractive success stories and benefit commercially from them. Builder attention is not equivalent to product demand. Publicly saying an app is production-ready is not an independent reliability assessment.

Use the common [measurement definitions](/playbooks/measurement.md); these proposals are not industry benchmarks.
