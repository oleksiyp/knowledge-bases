# Channels

* [Founder publishing: X, LinkedIn, and email](/channels/founder-publishing.md) — Show changes that matter to buyers; distinguish audience building from customer acquisition.
* [Creators and short video: recruit audience fit](/channels/creators.md) — TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube are surfaces; the actual unit is a creator–buyer match.
* [Search: capture an existing task, not generic AI curiosity](/channels/search.md) — Intent pages, workflow content, and descriptive positioning address different kinds of searches.
* [Free tools: a sample of the paid workflow](/channels/free-tools.md) — Utility content can provide both discovery and activation, but traffic may have no commercial overlap.
* [Launch platforms: Product Hunt and Hacker News](/channels/launch-platforms.md) — Concentrated discovery can create an initial cohort; a rank is not retention.
* [Niche communities: learn where the problem is already discussed](/channels/communities.md) — Reddit, forums, and specialized groups can expose buyer language and real constraints.
* [Affiliates: pay for a measurable commercial action](/channels/affiliates.md) — Reviewer incentives can extend distribution beyond the founder’s publishing capacity.
* [Partnerships and B2B outreach: find people who already reach the buyer](/channels/partnerships.md) — Audience access, credibility, and workflow integration are distinct partner contributions.
* [Tool-vendor ecosystems: a second audience for the building story](/channels/vendor-ecosystems.md) — Lovable, Bolt, and Cursor offer visible stages for builders, without proving end-customer demand.
* [Paid search: buy a small demand test before buying scale](/channels/paid-search.md) — A high-intent click can still be too expensive for the underlying service.
* [Directories and AI newsletters: discovery with weak attribution](/channels/directories.md) — A listing is a distribution surface, not proof of a paying audience.
