# Start here

* [Open Source Successes & Failures, Oct 2024 – Oct 2026: Executive Summary](executive-summary.md) - Synthesis of 435 OSS projects, 175 organizations and 247 events across 15 domains, in two research passes (pass 2 verified pass 1 against primary sources and added 4 domains). AI agents became OSS's biggest customer. AI labs, platforms and chip vendors bought the stack. Foundation-governed projects survived while single-vendor ones were closed, sunset or decayed. China took the open-weight lead.
