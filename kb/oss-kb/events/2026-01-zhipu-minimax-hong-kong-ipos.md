# What happened
Zhipu (Knowledge Atlas) listed on HKEX on 8 Jan 2026, raising ~HK$4.3B (~$558M) at HK$116.20 per share, a valuation of roughly $6.6–7.1B (sources differ)[^cnbc-zhipu-ipo]. MiniMax followed on 9 Jan, pricing at HK$165, raising HK$4.8B, and closing day one at HK$345 (+109%, ~US$13.7B)[^scmp-minimax]. Both were loss-making: Zhipu ~$27M revenue / ~$330M loss (H1 2025); MiniMax ~$53M revenue / ~$512M loss (9M 2025)[^row-ipo].

# Why it matters
Public markets priced open-weight model labs richly before any US frontier lab listed, creating a capital channel for Chinese open models and a template for others (Moonshot, DeepSeek)[^fortune-ipo].

# Outcome so far
By May 2026 market caps were ~$55.9B (Zhipu) and ~$33B (MiniMax)[^tc-moonshot]; Zhipu's market cap peaked near HK$1T in June 2026 before shedding nearly half (≈$62B in Aug per Wikipedia); its H1 2026 revenue rose ~400% to ¥953.9M with a ~¥2B loss[^cm-gn-zhipu][^wiki-zai]. MiniMax's H1 2026 revenue rose 283% to $116.6M with an adjusted net loss of $293.0M[^minimax-h1-26]. Moonshot (confidential HK filing, Sept 2026) and DeepSeek (Shanghai) began IPO preparations[^fortune-ipo].
