# What happened
Cloudflare announced it acquired Replicate, whose catalog holds 50,000+ open and fine-tuned models; APIs continue to work and the catalog is being brought into Workers AI, with Cog used for custom-model deployment[^cf-replicate]. Terms were not disclosed; Replicate had raised more than $23M[^sa-replicate].

# Why it matters
Mid-sized model-hosting platforms consolidated into larger clouds as GPU-rich inference providers (Fireworks, Together, Baseten) raised billions.

# Outcome so far
Replicate operates as part of Cloudflare; Cog remains open source (Apache-2.0).
