# What happened
Valve launched the Steam Machine on June 29, 2026. Prices start at $1,049 for the 512GB model without a controller and run to about $1,428 for 2TB with a Steam Controller. To limit resellers, Valve put everyone who pre-ordered by June 25 into a randomly ordered reservation queue.[^rtvr-steam][^gadgeteer-steam]

# Why it matters
It is the first mass-market living-room PC from Valve running SteamOS, which is built on Arch Linux with KDE Plasma as its desktop mode. It extends the Steam Deck's role as the main driver of Linux desktop growth. Linux had reached a record 5.33% of Steam users in March 2026, about a quarter of it SteamOS.[^steam-mar26]

# Outcome so far
Too early for sales data. The high price (well above consoles) limits it to enthusiasts, and Linux's Steam share was around 4% in mid-2026.
