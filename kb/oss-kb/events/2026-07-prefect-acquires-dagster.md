# What happened
Prefect announced it is acquiring Dagster Labs — the Dagster product, codebase, customer relationships and many team members. The combined company operates under the Prefect name from August 2026; Dagster founder Nick Schrock and CEO Pete Hunt become strategic advisers. Terms undisclosed[^prefect-acq][^osfy-prefect].

# Why it matters
The two best-known open-source Airflow alternatives (both founded 2018) combined; Prefect frames the result as Dagster (assets) + Prefect (execution) + FastMCP (AI agents)[^prefect-acq].

# Outcome so far
"Dagster and Dagster+ are here to stay"; weekly Dagster 1.13.x releases continued through Oct 2026[^dagster-prefect].
