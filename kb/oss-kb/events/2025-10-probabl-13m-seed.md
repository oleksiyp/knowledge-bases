# What happened
Probabl announced a €13M seed round co-led by Serena and Capital Fund Management (CFM), with Apertu Capital, Mozilla Ventures and French Tech Souveraineté (France 2030), bringing total seed funding to €18.5M; it then had 30+ staff and its first enterprise clients, and described itself as scikit-learn's exclusive operator[^probabl-seed][^inria-probabl]. Press framed it around scikit-learn's use at firms such as Spotify and JP Morgan[^sifted-probabl].

# Why it matters
It is the clearest attempt to build a venture-backed company around a mature, academically governed scientific-Python library — and a European sovereign-tech bet (state fund participation)[^inria-probabl].

# Outcome so far
Within three months Probabl replaced its CEO (François Méro in; Yann Lechelle to chairman; Gaël Varoquaux CSO) and on 2026-03-05 launched the skore platform; no revenue figures have been disclosed[^probabl-ceo][^skore-live].
