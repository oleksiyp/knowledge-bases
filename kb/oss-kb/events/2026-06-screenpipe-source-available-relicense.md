# What happened
Screenpipe replaced MIT with a source-available commercial licence: personal, non-commercial, educational and research use stay free with a 7-day evaluation at any scale, but commercial production use needs a paid licence regardless of company size. The company argued that "open source on its own is not a business model" and that companies ran it in production without contributing back[^sp-license].

# Why it matters
It marks the trend reaching seed-stage AI apps: relicensing *before* scale and alongside an accelerator (YC S26 from 2026-05-14)[^sp-yc], rather than late-stage like HashiCorp or Redis.

# Outcome so far
Releases accelerated (app-v2.7.84 on 2026-10-01) and stars kept rising (~22k)[^sp-gh]; no notable fork reported.
