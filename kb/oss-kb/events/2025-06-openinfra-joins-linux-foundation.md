# What happened
The intent to join the Linux Foundation was announced on Mar 12, 2025. OpenInfra would operate as a foundation within the LF, keeping its own budget, governance and member fees.[^nw-openinfra] On Jun 3, 2025 executive director Jonathan Bryce wrote that "the ink is dry, the members have voted", and governance, mission and staff were combined.[^openinfra-closed] (Corrected in pass 2: date "around June 2025 (approximate)" → announced 2025-03-12, completed 2025-06-03.)

# Why it matters
OpenStack went from an independent foundation in decline to an LF project at the moment Broadcom's VMware licensing changes (from November 2023) pushed enterprises to look for alternatives. OpenInfra set up a dedicated VMware-migration working group[^openinfra-blog].

# Outcome so far
OpenInfra reports new Platinum members (Rackspace rejoined in Oct 2024; Viettel in Dec 2025), Kata Containers 4.0 aimed at AI-agent sandboxing, and OpenDev's millionth Gerrit change (Aug 2026)[^openinfra-blog].
