# What happened
Adam Wathan announced Tailwind Labs is joining Shopify, an early large-scale Tailwind adopter; "Everything will always be MIT-licensed"; Tailwind Plus and ui.sh closed to new signups[^tailwind-shopify]. Press framed it as a lifeline after vibe coding eroded the business[^gn-tw-shop].

# Why it matters
Completes the arc from AI-driven layoffs (Jan 2026) to corporate absorption — a template for popular-but-unmonetizable OSS.

# Outcome so far
Too early; terms undisclosed.
