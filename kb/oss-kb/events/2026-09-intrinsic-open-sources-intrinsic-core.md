# What happened
Intrinsic released Intrinsic Core, a set of ROS-compatible industrial robotics capabilities, under Apache-2.0, together with an Open Machine Tending reference design.[^intrinsic-core][^unite-intrinsic] Intrinsic had been folded from Alphabet's Other Bets into Google seven months earlier.[^cnbc-intrinsic]

# Why it matters
Forbes framed it as Google's "Android of robotics" play: open the middleware, and win on Gemini models and cloud.[^forbes-intrinsic] It also eases the concern dating from Intrinsic's 2022 absorption of Open Robotics' commercial arm, since the code now flows back into the ROS ecosystem.
