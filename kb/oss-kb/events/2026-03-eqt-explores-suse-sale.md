# What happened
Reuters reported on 9 March 2026 that EQT, which took SUSE private again in 2023 at €2.72B, had hired Arma Partners to sound out mainly private-equity buyers for a sale that could value SUSE at up to ~$6B (€5.1B). The talks were described as early-stage. SUSE was said to generate ~$800M in revenue and >$250M in EBITDA[^gn-suse][^heise-suse][^lwn-suse].

# Why it matters
SUSE is Europe's main enterprise Linux/Kubernetes vendor; a sale (potentially to a non-EU buyer) would test its "sovereignty" positioning just as the EU launched its open source strategy.

# Outcome so far
No deal found as of 2026-10-03.
