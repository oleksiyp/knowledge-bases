# What happened
Free security updates for mainstream Windows 10 editions ended on 14 October 2025 (ESU available)[^ms]. Free-software communities (KDE, FSFE and others) ran the "End of 10" campaign to move still-capable PCs to Linux, launched publicly in late 2024/2025[^ccc][^endof10], with KDE Plasma 6.4 marketed as an easy landing spot[^zdnet-kde].

# Why it matters
Windows 11 hardware requirements left many PCs unsupported, coinciding with SteamOS/Proton maturity. Linux reached a record 5.33% of Steam users in March 2026 (SteamOS ~24.5% of Linux installs)[^steam], then fluctuated (4.52% April, 3.69% June, 4.01% July)[^gol-apr][^july]. New distros — CachyOS, Bazzite, PikaOS, AnduinOS, Omarchy — displaced older names in popularity rankings[^top10].

# Outcome so far
Linux desktop share rose but remains volatile; the clearest winners were gaming-oriented and opinionated distros, and KDE (record donations).
