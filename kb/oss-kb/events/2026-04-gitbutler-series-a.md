# What happened
GitButler announced a $17M Series A led by Andreessen Horowitz, with seed investors Fly Ventures and A Capital following on and a16z's Peter Levine joining the board. Alongside it, GitButler shipped a technical preview of its CLI. Founder Scott Chacon framed the problem as "we're teaching swarms of agents to use a tool built for sending patches over mailing lists."[^gb-series-a][^siliconangle-gb]

# Why it matters
Version control was a settled category for 15 years. In 2025–26 AI coding agents reopened it. Ex-GitHub CEO Thomas Dohmke's Entire raised a $60M seed in February 2026 to store agent prompts and traces alongside commits.[^rcp-entire] East River Source Control, which builds jj-native hosting, hired Jujutsu's creator as CTO in September 2026.[^ersc-martin] GitButler is the Fair Source (FSL) entrant in this race.

# Outcome so far
GitButler shipped native stacked PRs (0.22, July 2026). It co-hosted Git Merge 2026 with OpenAI. No revenue figures are disclosed.
