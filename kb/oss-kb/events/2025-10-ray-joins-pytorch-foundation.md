# What happened
At PyTorch Conference 2025 the foundation announced Ray as a hosted project; Ray then had 39k+ stars and 237M+ downloads[^ptf-ray]. Anyscale said downloads grew ~10x in a year and Ray drives "billions of dollars of annual compute consumption"[^anyscale-ray-ptf].

# Why it matters
Vendor-neutral governance for the main distributed-execution layer of AI; it also de-risked Ray ahead of Anyscale's later sale.

# Outcome so far
Nine months later Nscale agreed to acquire Anyscale (~$1.65B), explicitly leaving Ray community-governed and joining the foundation.
