# What happened
TechCrunch reported the spinout at ~$400M with Accel leading and Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan as an angel[^tc-radixark]. On 2026-05-05 RadixArk announced a $100M seed at $400M post-money, led by Accel and co-led by Spark Capital, founded by Ying Sheng and Banghua Zhu[^bw-radixark]. This was the same single round TechCrunch had reported in January, not a second raise.

# Why it matters
Within a week both leading open LLM engines (vLLM and SGLang) became venture-backed companies, both from Ion Stoica's Berkeley lab lineage.

# Outcome so far
SGLang out-committed vLLM in Q3 2026; RadixArk revenue undisclosed.
