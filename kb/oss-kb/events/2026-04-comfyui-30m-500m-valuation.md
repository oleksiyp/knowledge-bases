# What happened
Comfy Org announced a $30M round led by Craft Ventures with Pace Capital, Chemistry and TruArrow at a $500M valuation, bringing total funding to ~$47M; it claimed 4M users, 60k+ community nodes and 150k+ daily downloads, and pledged ComfyUI "will always stay open"[^comfy-30m][^gnw-comfy]. The prior $17M round closed 2025-09-16[^comfy-17m].

# Why it matters
The largest disclosed valuation for an open-source end-user AI app in the period; it validates the "GPL engine + paid cloud" model in generative media after A1111, Fooocus and Invoke's business faded.

# Outcome so far
Funds went to cloud and collaboration; Comfy API (workflow-as-endpoint) went GA on 2026-09-30[^comfy-api].
