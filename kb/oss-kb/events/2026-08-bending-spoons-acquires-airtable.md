# What happened
Bending Spoons, which listed publicly in July 2026 at an ~$18B valuation, agreed on 4 August 2026 to buy Airtable for $1.28B in cash (enterprise value; ~$2.25B including Airtable's net cash). Airtable reported ~$480M ARR growing 20%+ and 500,000+ customer organisations; it had been valued at over $11B in 2021[^tc][^sa]. The deal closed on 4 September 2026[^close].

# Why it matters
Bending Spoons is known for buying products (Evernote, WeTransfer, Vimeo, Eventbrite), cutting costs and raising prices[^tc]. Competitors immediately published migration guides[^softr]. For the OSS Airtable alternatives — NocoDB (now source-available), Baserow (MIT core), Grist (Apache-2.0) and Teable — it is both a demand opportunity and a valuation reality check: even a $480M-ARR market leader sold at ~2.7× ARR.

# Outcome so far
Too early to measure migration to OSS alternatives (no data found as of Oct 2026).
