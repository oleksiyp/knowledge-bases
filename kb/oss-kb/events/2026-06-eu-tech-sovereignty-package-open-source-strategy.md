# What happened
The package bundled the Open Source Strategy, the Cloud and AI Development Act and Chips Act 2.0. Open source measures: €2B over seven years; a proposed Open Source Maintenance Instrument (≥€350M estimated need); EU OSPO network; Digital Commons EDIC; open source EU Digital Identity Wallet; target of 30M users of open collaboration tools by 2030. It notes EU spending of €264B/yr on IT[^tpp-eu][^gn-eu].

# Why it matters
The largest public commitment to open source as industrial policy anywhere; creates procurement tailwinds for EU COSS vendors (SUSE, Nextcloud, Mistral) and maintenance funding for critical OSS.

# Outcome so far
Proposals in legislative process; critics note the sums are small relative to IT spend.

## Additional notes (end-user-apps)
- The Strategy was preceded by a call for evidence opened in January 2026[^hn-call-evidence] and published on 3 June 2026 as one of four instruments of the Tech Sovereignty package[^ec-oss-strategy][^oc-oss-strategy].
- For end-user software it codifies a trend already visible bottom-up: Schleswig-Holstein's exit from Exchange/Office, Denmark's digital ministry moving to LibreOffice, the ICC's switch to openDesk, and Nextcloud's 50%+ bookings growth. The "30M users of open collaboration tools by 2030" target directly benefits [Nextcloud](/projects/end-user-apps/nextcloud.md), [LibreOffice](/projects/end-user-apps/libreoffice.md), Euro-Office/[OnlyOffice](/projects/end-user-apps/onlyoffice.md) and [Matrix/Element](/projects/end-user-apps/matrix-element.md).
- See also: [ICC drops Microsoft for openDesk](/events/2025-10-icc-drops-microsoft-for-opendesk.md), [Euro-Office fork](/events/2026-06-euro-office-onlyoffice-fork.md), [end-user-apps domain review](/domains/end-user-apps.md).
