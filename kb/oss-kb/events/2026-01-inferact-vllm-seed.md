# What happened
Inferact (co-founders include Woosuk Kwon and Ion Stoica) raised $150M at $800M from Andreessen Horowitz and Lightspeed, with Databricks Ventures and the UC Berkeley Chancellor's Fund, to build managed/serverless vLLM while contributing upstream[^sa-inferact][^bbg-inferact].

# Why it matters
One of the largest seed rounds for an OSS infrastructure team; validates the "foundation-hosted project + venture-backed commercial company" model.

# Outcome so far
Product and revenue not yet public as of Oct 2026.
