# What happened
In the week of 2026-03-20 about eight Deno employees posted that they had left the company, and Wes Bos reported hearing these were layoffs.[^wesbos-x][^bushell] Critics tied the cuts to Deno Deploy and JSR underperforming, and pointed out there was no clear public statement from CEO Ryan Dahl.[^bushell]

# Why it matters
Of the three VC-backed alternative JS runtimes and toolchains, Bun and VoidZero ended in acquisitions while Deno is contracting. This suggests "a better Node" alone does not support a venture-scale business.

# Outcome so far
Deno Deploy Classic shut down on 2026-07-20. The new platform runs in 2 regions instead of 6 and does not support Deno Queues.[^byteiota] The runtime is still being released (2.9 in June 2026).
