# What happened
Khoj announced the end of Khoj Cloud on 2026-04-15, citing the long tail of integrations, weak defensibility as major AI labs shipped similar features, and early architectural choices that limited scale; the team moved to Open Paper and Pipali[^khoj-sunset].

# Why it matters
Illustrates the squeeze on consumer "personal AI" OSS startups once ChatGPT/Claude added memory, connectors and deep research.

# Outcome so far
Code remains open and self-hostable; last release 2.0.0-beta.28 (2026-03-26) and last commit 2026-08-02[^khoj-gh].
