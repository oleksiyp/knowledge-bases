GitLab cut ~350 roles (~14%), reduced its geographic footprint ~37% by exiting 22 countries, flattened management, and redirected spend to infrastructure for AI-driven traffic; shares fell ~7% after hours and were −17% YTD[^tc-gitlab-cuts][^gitlab-10q].

# Why it matters
The largest public open-core dev platform restructuring under AI pressure on seat-based models.
