# What happened
The OSI's 2025 board election was disputed. A candidate was disqualified over a deadline and timezone issue, and community members petitioned for publication of the full results, after which the OSI published a retrospective.[^reg-osi-2025][^osi-petition][^lwn-osi-retro] In Jan 2026 the board paused the spring 2026 elections and set up a working group to redesign board selection by Sept 2026. Interim ED Deb Bryant wrote that public elections had "not produced the results we hoped for".[^osi-2026-elections]

# Why it matters
The body that decides what counts as "open source", including whether SSPL, FSL and AGPL relicensings qualify, is itself struggling with legitimacy. That weakens its voice in licensing fights.

# Outcome so far
Working-group recommendations were due Sept 2026. A permanent executive director was said to be "expected soon"; no appointment was verified by Oct 2026.
