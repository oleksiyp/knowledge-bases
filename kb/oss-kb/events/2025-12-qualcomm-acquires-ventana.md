# What happened
Qualcomm acquired Ventana Micro Systems, founded 2018, whose Veyron V2 offered up to 32 RISC-V cores at 3.85 GHz. Terms were not disclosed.[^reg-ventana]

# Why it matters
A top-3 mobile chip vendor now has an in-house high-performance RISC-V team, a hedge against Arm licensing disputes.[^reg-ventana][^toms-ventana] But it also removes an independent RISC-V server vendor.

# Outcome so far
Qualcomm followed up with reported Tenstorrent talks (denied) and the Modular acquisition in June 2026.
