# What happened
The Drupal Association announced on 15 July 2026 that Tim Doyle was stepping down; board veteran Tiffany Farriss will serve 6–12 months as interim CEO while a permanent search is run. No reason was given for Doyle's exit[^ceo][^dt-ceo]. On 4 August it published its 2025 audit: a $451K deficit, a restated $923K deficit for 2024, reserves down 60% since 2022 to ~$960K (2.3 months of expenses), and Drupal.org infrastructure costs up 61% to $2.1M[^audit].

# Why it matters
Drupal's product relaunch (Drupal CMS 1.0/2.0) succeeded technically, but the nonprofit that runs its infrastructure is underfunded — a common failure mode for foundation-style OSS that depends on events and a few large sponsors.

# Outcome so far
Farriss proposed program-level cost accounting and a shift to enterprise funding[^dt-funding]; the DA promised a balanced 2027–28 budget[^audit].
