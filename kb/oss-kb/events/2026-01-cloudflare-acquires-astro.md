# What happened
On 2026-01-16 Cloudflare announced it had acquired The Astro Technology Company, the creators of the Astro framework. Terms were not disclosed. Cloudflare said Astro would remain open source and that it would continue the Astro Ecosystem Fund alongside Webflow, Netlify, Wix and Sentry. It cited Unilever, Visa and NBC News as users and noted that Webflow and Wix already run Astro on Cloudflare.[^cf-astro-pr][^gigazine-astro] The Astro 6 beta shipped the same week.[^cf-astro-pr]

# Why it matters
Astro is the third framework/tooling company absorbed by a hosting platform within about a year, after NuxtLabs to Vercel (Jul 2025), with VoidZero to Cloudflare following in Jun 2026. Astro's own attempt at a managed product, Astro Studio, had been shut down in 2024–25.[^astro-goodbye-studio] The deal shows that a popular MIT framework alone did not support a venture-scale business.

# Outcome so far
- Releases sped up: Astro 6.0 (2026-03-10) and Astro 7.0 with a Rust compiler (2026-06-22).[^astro-blog]
- Community discussion focused on the risk of vendor tilt toward Cloudflare Workers.[^hn-cf-astro]
