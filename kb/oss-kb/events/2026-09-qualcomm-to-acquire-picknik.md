# What happened
Qualcomm Technologies agreed to acquire PickNik (founded 2015). Terms were not disclosed. It pledged to keep MoveIt 1 and 2 open-source under existing licenses with community-driven roadmaps and support for third-party hardware.[^robotreport-picknik][^qcom-picknik] The exact announcement day is uncertain: coverage dates range from Sept 23 to 27, 2026.[^ew-picknik]

# Why it matters
It is a fresh test of Qualcomm's "buy the open community" pattern after Arduino. The world's most-used open manipulation planner now belongs to a chip vendor.

# Outcome so far
The deal was pending close as of 2026-10-03.
