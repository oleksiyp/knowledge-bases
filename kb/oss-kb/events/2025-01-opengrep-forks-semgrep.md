# What happened
Opengrep launched as "a fork of Semgrep CE" after language support, meta-variables and fingerprinting were moved behind commercial licenses. It has a dedicated full-time team funded by the consortium.[^opengrep] It was announced on Jan 23, 2025 by a consortium of 10+ security vendors (Aikido, Arnica, Amplify, Endor Labs, Jit, Kodem, Legit, Mobb, Orca and others), in response to Semgrep's Dec 13, 2024 licensing changes.[^prn-opengrep][^tns-opengrep] (Pass 2: launch date confirmed from the press release.)

# Why it matters
A relicensing-triggered fork in security tooling, similar to the OpenTofu and Valkey forks.

# Outcome so far
Actively developed. Semgrep continues as an open-core company.
