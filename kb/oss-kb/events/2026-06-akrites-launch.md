# What happened
Launched on 2026-06-25 as a shared SIRT with a standard CVD process. It aims to coordinate fixes with upstream maintainers, push patches to critical infrastructure, act as "maintainer of last resort" for unmaintained critical packages, and work with government defenders. Seed funding comes from Alpha-Omega. Founding members include AWS, Anthropic, Chainguard, Cisco, Citi, Endor Labs, Ericsson, Google, IBM, JPMorganChase, Microsoft/GitHub, NVIDIA, OpenAI, RapidFort, Red Hat, Rust Foundation, Sonatype, Vodafone and Zscaler.[^lf-akrites]

# Why it matters
An industry attempt to absorb the AI-discovery flood instead of passing it on to volunteers.

# Outcome so far
Too early to judge. Funding amounts were not disclosed.[^lf-akrites]
