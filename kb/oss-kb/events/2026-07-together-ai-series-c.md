# What happened
Together AI raised an $800M Series C at an $8.3B valuation on 1 July 2026. Aramco Ventures led, with Vista Equity Partners, General Catalyst, Emergence Capital, NVIDIA, March Capital, Pegatron and SentinelOne's S Ventures participating. The company reported more than $1.15B in annual bookings as of the prior quarter. Total capital raised is about $1.2B ($102.5M Series A in 2023, $305M Series B in 2025, $800M Series C)[^tc-together-c]. The previous round was a $305M Series B at $3.3B in February 2025[^together-b].

# Why it matters
Together pairs an open-model inference and training cloud with open research (FlashAttention, Mamba, the Together Kernel Collection)[^together-b]. The round shows that serving open-weight models has become a billion-dollar revenue business. Together's valuation still trails Fireworks AI's reported $17.5B.

# Outcome so far
No later round or acquisition was found as of 2026-10-03.
