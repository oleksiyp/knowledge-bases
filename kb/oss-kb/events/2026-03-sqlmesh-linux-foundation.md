# What happened
Fivetran transferred SQLMesh to the Linux Foundation; founding members are Benzinga, CloudKitchens, Harness, Infinite Lambda, Jump AI and Minerva[^fivetran-lf][^lf-sqlmesh][^tns-sqlmesh].

# Why it matters
Defuses concerns that Fivetran would let SQLMesh wither after merging with dbt Labs; follows a growing pattern of acquirers parking OSS at foundations.

# Outcome so far
Repo moved to the SQLMesh GitHub org; 0.235–0.236 releases through Sept 2026; still pre-1.0.
