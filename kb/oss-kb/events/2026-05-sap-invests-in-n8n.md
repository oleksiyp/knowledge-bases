# What happened
On 12 May 2026 n8n announced a strategic investment from SAP at a $5.2B valuation, more than double its Oct 2025 mark, with n8n to be embedded natively in SAP Joule Studio; n8n cited 1,400+ enterprise customers and 1.7M monthly active builders[^n8n-sap-blog]. Bloomberg put SAP's commitment at more than €60M[^bbg-n8n]; the ~1.3% stake figure appears only on Wikipedia[^n8n-wiki]. (Corrected in pass 2: date 2026-05-01 (month placeholder) → 2026-05-12.)

# Why it matters
Valuation more than doubled in ~7 months; an ERP giant chose a fair-code OSS tool as its agent-workflow layer.
