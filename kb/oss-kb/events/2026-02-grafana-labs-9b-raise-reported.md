# What happened
On Feb 13, 2026, SiliconANGLE, citing The Information, reported that Grafana Labs was finalizing a funding round at about $9B, up from a prior mark SiliconANGLE gave as $6.6B (the Aug 2024 round itself was at ~$6B[^bw-grafana-2024]), with Singapore's GIC expected to lead[^sa-grafana][^ti-grafana]. Reported metrics: $400M ARR as of September 2025 (vs $250M in Aug 2024), a $150M tender offer completed in 2025, and "no plans to go public in the near future"[^sa-grafana].

# Why it matters
It confirms that an AGPL open-core observability company can grow ~60% a year at a $400M scale and command a premium private valuation, while staying private.

# Outcome so far
Pass-2 check (2026-10-03): Grafana Labs has never announced the round's size or close. Its press page lists no funding release in 2026; the 2026-02-03 release confirmed $400M+ ARR for the fiscal year ended 31 Jan 2026[^cm-grafana-pr], and the 2026-08-26 release reported ARR above $600M and 10,000+ customers, naming GIC among existing investors but giving no new-round details[^grafana-600m]. Tracker sites list a $250M Series E (Feb or Mar 2026) at $9–9.5B — unconfirmed. Claims of an S-1 filing appear only on IPO-speculation sites; no filing was found on EDGAR, so they are treated as false. Note: the 2024 transaction was $270M (primary + secondary) at ~$6B[^bw-grafana-2024]; the $6.6B "prior" figure in the report likely reflects the 2025 tender.
