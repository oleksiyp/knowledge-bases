# What happened
Anaconda acquired Outerbounds, the company founded around Metaflow, the ML/AI workflow framework created at Netflix. Terms were not disclosed. Anaconda said "Metaflow remains open source. That will not change." Founder Ville Tuulos framed the deal as combining trusted packages with production orchestration[^anaconda-outerbounds][^outerbounds-blog][^tns-outerbounds].

# Why it matters
It moves Anaconda from package distribution into MLOps/orchestration. It was the first of three acquisitions in about four months: Outerbounds, then Kilo Code (July) and Enkrypt AI (August)[^anaconda-inc5000].

# Outcome so far
Metaflow remains open source as of Oct 2026. Anaconda continued acquiring and says its valuation now exceeds $2B[^anaconda-inc5000].
