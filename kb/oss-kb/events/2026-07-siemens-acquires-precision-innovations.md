# What happened
Siemens Digital Industries Software agreed to buy Precision Innovations. The company develops OpenROAD-based AI SoC planning and has provided the bulk of OpenROAD's engineering and support since 2019.[^eng-siemens-pi][^ew-siemens-pi]

# Why it matters
A Big-3 EDA vendor now owns the main commercial contributor to the leading open EDA flow. Analysts stress that OpenROAD itself (BSD, UCSD-led) is not being taken over.[^vlsi-kr]

# Outcome so far
Closing was expected in calendar Q3 2026. Siemens has made no public commitment on upstream OpenROAD funding (none found).[^eng-siemens-pi]
