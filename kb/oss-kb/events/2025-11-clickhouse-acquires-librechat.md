# What happened
ClickHouse announced the acquisition of LibreChat on 2025-11-04; founder Danny Avila and team joined, and ClickHouse pledged continued investment in LibreChat as an open-source project[^bw-lc][^osfy-lc]. Terms were not disclosed.

# Why it matters
First of two ClickHouse purchases of AI-app-layer OSS within ~10 weeks (Langfuse followed in Jan 2026), signalling that data-infrastructure vendors see open AI UIs and LLMOps tools as demand funnels.

# Outcome so far
LibreChat stayed MIT, moved to the LibreChat-AI GitHub org (~45k stars) and accelerated to weekly RCs and monthly stables through v0.8.8 (2026-10-01)[^lc-gh].
