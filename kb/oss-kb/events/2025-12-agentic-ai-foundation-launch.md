# What happened
On 2025-12-09 the Linux Foundation formed the Agentic AI Foundation, with Anthropic contributing MCP, OpenAI contributing AGENTS.md and Block contributing goose; platinum members include AWS, Anthropic, Block, Bloomberg, Cloudflare, Google, Microsoft and OpenAI[^lf-aaif-pr][^siliconangle-aaif]. MCP had ~97M monthly SDK downloads and ~10k active servers at the time[^mcp-aaif-blog].

# Why it matters
Rival labs placed agent interoperability standards under neutral governance early, pre-empting forks and standards wars.

# Outcome so far
A2A joined in Aug 2026 (see linked event); membership reportedly grew to 250+ by Aug 2026 (per secondary reporting, see the AAIF organization file).
