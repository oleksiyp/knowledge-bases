# What happened
Five streaming vendors — Aiven, Confluent (IBM), Redpanda, StreamNative and Ververica — announced the Streamhouse Working Group on 2026-09-15 to establish an open category/architecture for real-time data powering applications and AI agents[^conf-blog][^rp-press][^conf-press].

# Why it matters
Fierce competitors cooperating signals that streaming vendors need a shared narrative against warehouse/lakehouse platforms (Databricks, Snowflake) absorbing streaming workloads. "Streamhouse" was previously a Flink/Paimon/Fluss community term.

# Outcome so far
Too early; no specification or foundation home verified.
