# What happened
Modular announced BentoML would join to unify MAX/Mojo hardware optimisation with BentoML's deployment platform; "nothing breaks" and the OSS project continues under Apache-2.0[^modular-bento].

# Why it matters
Consolidation of independent model-serving startups into broader AI-compute platforms.

# Outcome so far
Last BentoML release v1.4.39 (2026-05-07); only 6 commits in Q3 2026[^bento-gh]. Modular itself was acquired by Qualcomm in July 2026.
