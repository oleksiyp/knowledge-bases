# What happened
Quivr decided on 2025-02-14 to focus solely on automating customer-support replies inside helpdesks, reaching ~$10k MRR within three months, and dropped maintenance of its MegaParse parser[^quivr-pivot].

# Why it matters
Shows the gap between GitHub-star traction for a horizontal "chat with your docs" app and a viable business.

# Outcome so far
The OSS repo's last commit is 2025-06-19; it now resolves to The-Vibe-Company/quivr[^quivr-gh].
