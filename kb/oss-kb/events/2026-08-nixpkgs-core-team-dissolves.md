# What happened
The two remaining members of the Nixpkgs Core Team stepped down and dissolved the team. They cited an unsustainable workload, a recruitment call that drew one applicant, and a Steering Committee that "lacks a native instinct for the delegation envisioned by the constitution". A Steering Committee member disputed the micromanagement characterisation.[^linuxiac-coreteam]

# Why it matters
It follows the Sept 2025 resignation of moderation-team members over SC interference.[^nix-mods-resign] The 2024 constitution has not produced stable delegated governance.

# Outcome so far
Core Team duties now have no direct owner. Adoption nevertheless grew: the Dutch government chose NixOS for its DAWO sovereign desktop weeks later.[^reg-dawo]
