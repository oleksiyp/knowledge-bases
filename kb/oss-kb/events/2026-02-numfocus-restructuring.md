# What happened
In a February 2026 post, executive director Rachel Kerestes (in post since 2025-08-01[^perrett-kerestes]) wrote that "to get NumFOCUS back on solid financial ground" the board had approved a balanced 2026 budget that "eliminates the practice of budgeting to goal revenue", which "meant making the hard decision to reduce the size of the NumFOCUS team". From 2026-02-01 staff were reorganized into community (events + project advocacy, under a Chief Community Officer), finance & operations (under a new CFOO) and advancement (development, fundraising, marketing)[^numfocus-new-structure]. The number of roles cut was not disclosed.

# Why it matters
NumFOCUS is the fiscal sponsor for much of scientific Python. Its public filings show expenses rising from $5.74M (2022) to $11.09M (2024) while revenue fell from $9.29M to $8.38M, halving net assets to $5.67M[^propublica-numfocus]. The cuts came amid 2025's federal grant terminations and the end of CZI's EOSS program, the two largest external grant sources for its projects.

# Outcome so far
Fiscal sponsorship, PyData events and GSoC participation continued in 2026; no project departures attributed to the restructuring were found.
