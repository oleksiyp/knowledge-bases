# What happened
Project Jupyter, a NumFOCUS fiscally sponsored project since its early days, became hosted by LF Charities on 2024-10-17[^lf-charities]. On 2024-11-19, at the LF Member Summit, the Linux Foundation announced the Jupyter Foundation, a directed fund with premier members AWS, Google, Meta and Bloomberg, general members Anaconda, Quansight and QuantStack, and associate member MD Anderson Cancer Center, governed by a board mixing member representatives and Jupyter's Executive Council[^lf-jf].

# Why it matters
It is the highest-profile departure of a flagship project from NumFOCUS, and shows scientific-Python infrastructure moving to the LF corporate-membership model to raise larger, recurring budgets.

# Outcome so far
By Oct 2026 premier membership had grown to include Apple, Snowflake and Uber, with Databricks, JetBrains, Posit and OpenTeams as general members[^jf-members]. The Foundation ran community-funding rounds in 2025 (seven grants) and 2026, and funded community managers[^jf-funding]. Governance roles among the Executive Council, Software Steering Council and Foundation board needed clarification, as Executive Council member Chris Holdgraf described[^holdgraf].
