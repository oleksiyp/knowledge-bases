# What happened
Azul announced a definitive agreement for a majority strategic investment by Thoma Bravo on 2025-11-18. Existing investors Vitruvian Partners and Lead Edge Capital reinvested and kept minority stakes; Ares Management provided debt financing; the price was not disclosed.[^tb-azul][^axios-azul] Weeks later (2025-12-10) Azul acquired Payara, the Jakarta EE application-server company.[^azul-payara]

# Why it matters
It is a private-equity bet that Oracle's per-employee Java SE pricing and audits will keep pushing enterprises to OpenJDK alternatives — Azul's own 2026 survey says 81% are migrating at least part of their Oracle Java estate.[^itpro-azul-2026] It shows OpenJDK's GPL+Classpath-exception licence enables a durable third-party support market around a single-vendor-led project.

# Outcome so far
Azul used the backing for M&A (Payara) and continued to market migration off Oracle Java through 2026.[^azul-payara][^itpro-azul-2026] No financial results are public.
