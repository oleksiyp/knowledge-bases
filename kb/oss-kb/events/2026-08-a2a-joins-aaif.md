# What happened
On 2026-08-17 AAIF announced A2A had joined its stack (MCP for tools, A2A for agent-to-agent, AGENTS.md for instructions, goose runtime, agentgateway), citing 150+ backing organizations and production use in HarmonyOS, WeChat, Azure AI Foundry and AWS Bedrock[^aaif-a2a][^axios-a2a].

# Why it matters
Anthropic-, OpenAI- and Google-originated agent standards now share one neutral foundation.

# Outcome so far
Too early to assess; A2A v1.0 shipped in March 2026.
