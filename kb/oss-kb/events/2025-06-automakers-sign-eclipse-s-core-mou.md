# What happened
Eleven German and European OEMs and suppliers signed a VDA memorandum of understanding committing to "code-first" pre-competitive open-source development of an SDV and autonomous-driving software platform through Eclipse S-CORE.[^vda-mou]

# Why it matters
It marks the European auto industry's shift from proprietary middleware (and failed solo efforts) to shared open source, framed as a response to Chinese software velocity.[^newatlas-score]

# Outcome so far
At CES 2026 the circle widened to 32 executives, Traton joined, and S-CORE shipped a 0.5 beta. 1.0 is targeted for end-2026.[^sdv-ces]
