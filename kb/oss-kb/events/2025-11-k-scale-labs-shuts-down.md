# What happened
Founder Benjamin Bolte wrote that he had "not been able to find a lead investor" and had laid off most of the team. Preorders for the K-Bot were refunded, and all IP was released (hardware CERN-OHL-S-2.0, software MIT). The exact date is approximate: early Nov 2025.[^kalil-kscale][^x-kalil]

# Why it matters
Open hardware alone cannot finance humanoid manufacturing against state-backed and VC-flooded Chinese competitors.[^robotreport-kscale]

# Outcome so far
The designs live on as open IP. Unitree, by contrast, won STAR Market IPO approval in July 2026.[^caixin-unitree]
