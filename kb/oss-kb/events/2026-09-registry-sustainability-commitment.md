# What happened
The commitment covers PyPI, Maven Central, crates.io, RubyGems, npm, NuGet, OpenVSX and Packagist. It supports funding enterprise-scale use while keeping registries free for individuals. Context: downloads grow 30–50% a year with flat funding, 1.8M malicious packages were detected in 2026, and teams are 2–3 people.[^openssf-registries]

# Why it matters
It makes paid tiers for heavy enterprise users normal, which was controversial when Maven Central and others first raised it.

# Outcome so far
A commitment in principle. No dollar amounts.[^openssf-registries]
