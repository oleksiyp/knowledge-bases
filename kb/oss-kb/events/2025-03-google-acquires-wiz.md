# What happened
Alphabet announced on 2025-03-18 that Google would buy Wiz for $32B in cash.[^goog-8k] After clearance by competition authorities including the US, the European Commission, Australia and Israel, the deal closed on 2026-03-11. Wiz joined Google Cloud but keeps its own brand. It is Google's largest acquisition ever.[^tc-wiz-close][^cd-wiz]

# Why it matters
It set the high-water mark for security valuations and sped up platform consolidation. Independent AppSec and supply-chain vendors now compete against hyperscaler bundles.

# Outcome so far
Closed. Followed by Palo Alto Networks' buying spree (CyberArk, Chronosphere, Koi and others).
