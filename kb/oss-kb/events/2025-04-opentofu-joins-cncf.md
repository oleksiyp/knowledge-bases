# What happened
OpenTofu, forked from Terraform after HashiCorp's 2023 BSL switch, was accepted into the CNCF at Sandbox maturity on Apr 23, 2025.[^tns-tofu-cncf]

# Why it matters
It gave the fork the same neutral home as Kubernetes, which reassured enterprises (Fidelity published its migration in Oct 2025) and the TACOS vendors that build on it.[^tofu-blog]

# Outcome so far
Releases 1.10 through 1.13 followed, and Terraform 1.15 adopted features OpenTofu shipped first.
