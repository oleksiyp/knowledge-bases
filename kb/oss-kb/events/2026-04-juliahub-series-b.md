# What happened
JuliaHub raised $65M led by Dorilton with General Catalyst, AE Ventures and Bob Muglia, and launched Dyad 3.0, billed as an agentic AI platform that designs complete systems from specifications. It cited Fortune-100 users in aerospace, government, automotive, HVAC and utilities[^juliahub-b][^engineering-com].

# Why it matters
It is the largest raise in the Julia ecosystem, but the money is for a proprietary vertical product built on the open language. That shows how niche-language stewards monetize.
