# What happened
Ghost 6.0 shipped on 4 August 2025 with "social web" features: publications become followable (@you@yourdomain.com) and can send/receive likes and replies across the fediverse, free on all Ghost 6 sites including self-hosted; it also added native analytics[^g6][^wedistribute]. The founder noted publishers had earned $100M on Ghost[^john].

# Why it matters
It is the largest deployment of ActivityPub by a CMS and makes open protocols a distribution channel for independent publishers competing with closed newsletter platforms.

# Outcome so far
Ghost kept compounding: $10M+ ARR in March 2026 with ~$130M publisher revenue[^arr].
