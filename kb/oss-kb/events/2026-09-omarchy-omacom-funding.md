# What happened
The Omacom Foundation, steward of Omarchy, said its backing rose from $8M to $10M (late Aug–early Sept 2026) and to ~$18.5M by 17 Sept, including multi-year pledges and AI-token credits from Meta Superintelligence Labs, OpenAI, Fireworks and Anthropic, plus corporate patrons DigitalOcean, OpenRouter and Four Technologies[^reg][^dealroom]. It hired kernel developer Krzysztof Wilczyński, infrastructure head Emir Beganović and Quickshell creator "outfoxxed", and announced Omarchy M for Apple Silicon[^reg]. Anthropic was delisted from patron status on 18 Sept[^reg].

# Why it matters
It is by far the largest funding pool for a new desktop Linux distro in the period, achieved in weeks via founder celebrity — contrasting with GNOME's ~$550K annual budget. It also arrived days after a published root-escalation flaw[^root] and amid accusations that DHH's politics make the project incompatible with free-software values[^reg].

# Outcome so far
Funding partly in non-cash credits; patron churn visible; security and governance capacity are the key watch items.
