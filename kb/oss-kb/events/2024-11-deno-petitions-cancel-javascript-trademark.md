# What happened
Deno filed a petition with the USPTO on Nov 22, 2024 (reported Nov 26) to cancel Oracle's "JavaScript" trademark, arguing that the mark is generic, has been abandoned, and was renewed fraudulently using a Node.js screenshot as evidence of use.[^sdtimes-petition][^reg-petition] Oracle declined to withdraw voluntarily (Jan 2025).[^infoworld-refuse] On June 18, 2025 the TTAB dismissed Deno's fraud claim, which was about Oracle using a Node.js screenshot as evidence of use. The genericness and abandonment claims moved on to discovery, with Oracle's answer due Aug 2025.[^deno-update4]

# Why it matters
It is the most prominent attempt to free a language name from a corporate trademark. It belongs to a wider pattern of trademark fights in open source (WordPress, NATS).

# Outcome so far
Still in progress. The campaign site cites 27k+ signatures and a $200k fundraiser for the discovery phase.[^jstm] The TTAB schedule published in Jan 2025 ran discovery to Sept 2025 and trial periods into 2026, with an optional oral-hearing request due July 8, 2026.[^techspot-schedule] Pass-2 searches (Oct 2026) found no later public update from Deno after June 2025 and no final TTAB decision; schedules in TTAB cases often slip, so the timing of a ruling is unknown.
