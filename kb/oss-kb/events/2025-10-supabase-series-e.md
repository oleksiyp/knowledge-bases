# What happened
Supabase closed a $100M Series E at a $5B valuation, led by Accel and Peak XV, with Figma Ventures and existing investors YC, Coatue, Felicis, Craft and Square Peg[^sb-series-e][^orrick-e]. It came after the $200M Series D at $2B (Apr 22 2025, led by Accel)[^fortune-series-d][^bdw-series-d][^tc-series-e]. Supabase again offered employees liquidity on 25% of vested stock and announced a $1M community round for customers and contributors[^sb-series-e].

# Why it matters
It confirmed that AI app builders (Lovable, Bolt, Claude Code and others) were turning an open-source Postgres platform into infrastructure valued at decacorn-trajectory prices.

# Outcome so far
Eight months later Supabase raised $500M at $10.5B ([/events/2026-06-supabase-series-f.md](/events/2026-06-supabase-series-f.md)). It then raised $150M and bought Turso ([/events/2026-10-supabase-acquires-turso.md](/events/2026-10-supabase-acquires-turso.md)).
