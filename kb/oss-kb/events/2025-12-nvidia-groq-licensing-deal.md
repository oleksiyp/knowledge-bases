# What happened
Groq described a non-exclusive licensing agreement; Jonathan Ross, Sunny Madra and other team members joined NVIDIA, Simon Edwards became Groq CEO and GroqCloud continues[^groq-nv]. CNBC first reported the price at about $20B in cash, Nvidia's largest deal ever (previous record: Mellanox, ~$7B in 2019).[^cnbc-groq][^tc-groq]

# Why it matters
Context for the inference market: NVIDIA used a license-and-hire structure (avoiding a formal acquisition) to absorb a leading inference-chip rival, underscoring how strategic inference had become. Groq later raised $650M from existing investors to pivot toward an inference-cloud business (May 2026, per Wikipedia; not independently confirmed in pass 2)[^wiki-groq].

# Outcome so far
Groq remains independent; it is context rather than an OSS event.
