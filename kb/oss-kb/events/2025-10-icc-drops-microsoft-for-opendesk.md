# What happened
After Microsoft blocked the ICC chief prosecutor's email in connection with US sanctions (reported May 2025)[^heise-block], the ICC confirmed on 30–31 Oct 2025 that it would move its internal work environment from Microsoft Office to openDesk, the open-source suite provided by Germany's Center for Digital Sovereignty (ZenDiS)[^euractiv][^reg][^ejil]. openDesk bundles Element/Matrix (chat), OX App Suite (mail), Collabora Online (office), Nextcloud (files), OpenProject, XWiki, Univention Nubus, Nordeck and CryptPad[^opendesk][^reg-matrix]. openDesk had earlier won a framework agreement with the Bundeswehr (April 2025)[^bundeswehr].

# Why it matters
It made "kill-switch risk" from US cloud vendors concrete for institutions, accelerating European procurement of open-source alternatives.

# Outcome so far
Cited widely as a precedent; part of the context for the EU's June 2026 Open Source Strategy.
