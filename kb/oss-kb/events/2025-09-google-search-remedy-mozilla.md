# What happened
Following his August 2024 liability finding, Judge Mehta issued remedies in early September 2025: Google may not enter exclusive contracts for Search, Chrome, Assistant or Gemini distribution, but may continue paying partners for default placement; Chrome and Android divestitures sought by the DOJ were rejected[^omg][^itsfoss]. Mozilla had testified that losing Google payments could "crush" Firefox[^courthouse].

# Why it matters
Google provided ~86% of Mozilla's M+ 2024 revenue[^hentzschel]. The ruling averted an immediate financial crisis for the only independent major browser engine.

# Outcome so far
Mozilla's revenue base is intact but now non-exclusive; Mozilla framed the outcome as a defense of the open web[^moz-blog] and has since pursued AI partnerships (e.g., Mistral, Sept 2026 — see the Firefox file) to diversify.
