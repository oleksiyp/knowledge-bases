# What happened
Unleash closed a $35M Series B led by One Peak with Spark Capital, Frontline and Firstminute participating, bringing total funding to $51.5M; it reported doubling ARR for three consecutive years, 140% NRR, 500+ paying customers and 40M+ downloads, and launched "Impact Metrics"[^tnw][^blog].

# Why it matters
One of the few sizeable 2026 rounds for a web-platform COSS company, won on the argument that AI-generated code needs runtime kill switches and progressive delivery ("FeatureOps")[^tnw].

# Outcome so far
Continued releases (v8.x by Sept 2026); category consolidation around it (OpenAI bought Statsig for ~$1.1B in Sept 2025)[^statsig].
