# What happened
Temporal raised a $550M Series E at a $12.55B valuation on Sep 14, 2026. Lightspeed led, with Wellington Management, Goldman Sachs Alternatives (Growth Equity) and Tiger Global as co-leads; T. Rowe Price joined, and a16z, Sequoia, Index, GIC, Sapphire and Amplify returned[^cm-temporal-e][^gw-temporal-e]. The company reported ARR above $250M (200%+ YoY), net dollar retention above 200% since February, 4,300+ paying customers, 43M+ open-source installs and 570 employees (Temporal's announcement gives ARR growth of >200%; the >$250M ARR figure comes from press coverage)[^cm-temporal-e][^gw-temporal-e]. Press puts total capital raised at ~$1.2B[^gw-temporal-e].

# Why it matters
It is the largest funding event in the cloud-native domain in the window. It shows that permissively licensed (MIT) infrastructure can be re-rated by a new workload, here AI agents that need durable, retryable execution. The valuation rose 2.5x in seven months, from the $300M Series D at $5B on Feb 17, 2026[^temporal-d].

# Outcome so far
The funds are earmarked for global expansion, R&D, go-to-market and infrastructure scaling[^temporal-e].
