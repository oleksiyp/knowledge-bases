# What happened
On 2025-07-16 a jumbotron at Coldplay's Gillette Stadium show captured CEO Andy Byron with CHRO Kristin Cabot; the clip went globally viral. The board opened an investigation and on 2025-07-19 accepted Byron's resignation; co-founder and CPO Pete DeJoy became interim CEO[^cnbc-byron][^tc-byron][^cnbc-dejoy].

# Why it matters
The most-publicized leadership scandal in data infrastructure; it occurred just after Astronomer's M Series D and tested whether a vendor crisis would spill into the Airflow project.

# Outcome so far
DeJoy is CEO as of 2026 and new executives were hired[^astro-cfo]. Apache Airflow releases continued on schedule — ASF governance insulated the project.
