# What happened
OpenTeams (founded by Travis Oliphant) acquired Quansight's AI consulting division including its core engineering team; Quansight CTO Dharhas Pothina became OpenTeams CTO[^openteams-acq]. Quansight converted from an LLC to a Public Benefit Corporation, with former Labs co-directors Ralf Gommers and Tania Allard as CEO (Technology) and CEO (Impact), Ashley Baal as COO and Oliphant on the board[^quansight-pbc].

# Why it matters
Quansight employs maintainers across NumPy, SciPy, pandas, Jupyter and conda-ecosystem projects. Separating the commercial consulting engine from the maintainer-employing Labs (now legally mission-bound) is a template for "sustainability company" structures — but removes the internal cross-subsidy[^quansight-pbc].

# Outcome so far
OpenTeams repositioned around its Nebari private-AI platform for government; in March 2026 Oliphant became its CEO and the company said it had initiated a $100M Series A at a $400M pre-money valuation (closing not confirmed)[^openteams-ceo]. Quansight PBC continues as a maintainer employer.
