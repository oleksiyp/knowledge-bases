# What happened
The report (Boysel, Lavergne, Trifiro) found COSS startups raised $26.4B in 2024 (avg ~$9B/yr across ~250 deals 2019–2024); COSS median IPO valuation $1.3B vs $171M for closed-source peers (7x) and median M&A $482M vs $34M (14x); seed→Series A progression nearly 2x; funding lifts contributors 27% and downloads 7x; US 65%/EU 25% of the market; 12% exit rate[^lf-coss-2025][^lf-coss-report].

# Why it matters
The most rigorous quantitative case that open source is a business advantage, published just as AI concentration made COSS funding look like a few mega-rounds.

# Outcome so far
Widely cited in 2025–26 COSS fundraising and EU policy discussion.
