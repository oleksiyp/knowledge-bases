# What happened
Anaconda raised over $150M in a Series C led by Insight Partners with Mubadala Capital, at a valuation of roughly $1.5B; it said it had passed $150M in annual recurring revenue and was operating profitably. Proceeds were earmarked for product development, acquisitions, international expansion and employee liquidity[^yahoo-series-c][^bw-series-c].

# Why it matters
It is the biggest validation of the "monetize the distribution channel" model in scientific Python. The revenue rests partly on enforcing paid licenses for the defaults channel at organizations with 200+ employees. That enforcement alienated academia but clearly converted enterprises.

# Outcome so far
The money funded a 2026 acquisition spree (Outerbounds, Kilo Code, Enkrypt AI) and a pivot to enterprise "AI-native development"; Anaconda says its valuation passed $2B after the Kilo deal[^anaconda-inc5000].
