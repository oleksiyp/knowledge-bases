# What happened
Drupal CMS 1.0 was released on 15 January 2025 as the first product of the Starshot initiative started in May 2024: a pre-configured distribution of Drupal core with "recipes" (≈30 pre-built features), SEO, media, analytics and AI-assisted content tools aimed at marketers rather than developers[^cms1][^imagex].

# Why it matters
It marked an established OSS CMS repositioning itself as a product to compete with WordPress and commercial DXPs (Adobe, Sitecore, Optimizely) without relicensing or forking[^cms1].

# Outcome so far
Drupal CMS 2.0 shipped on 28 Jan 2026 with the Canvas drag-and-drop builder, site templates and AI features[^cms2]. Adoption figures are not published; the Drupal Association meanwhile entered a deficit (see related event).
