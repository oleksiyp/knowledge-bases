# What happened
On 2025-10-27 the PSF board unanimously withdrew a $1.5M NSF proposal (Safety, Security, and Privacy of Open Source Ecosystems program), which had been recommended for funding and would have been its largest grant ever. The award terms would have required the PSF to affirm it does not operate any programs that "advance or promote DEI", across the whole organisation.[^psf-nsf] Within two weeks 295 new $99 supporting members joined (not re-verified in pass 2). On 2026-01-13 Anthropic announced a two-year, $1.5M partnership with the PSF focused on PyPI and ecosystem security.[^willison-anthropic-psf][^helpnet-anthropic-psf]

# Why it matters
US federal funding for OSS infrastructure now comes with political conditions. Private donors stepped in.

# Outcome so far
Funding gap closed by Anthropic's $1.5M two-year commitment and community donations.[^willison-anthropic-psf][^helpnet-anthropic-psf]

## Additional notes (devtools-languages)
- Details: the PSF applied in January 2025 to the NSF's Safety, Security, and Privacy of Open Source Ecosystems program. The award would have funded automated review of PyPI uploads. Its terms barred the PSF from operating *any* DEI programs organization-wide and allowed clawback of funds already spent. The board vote was unanimous. The PSF's budget is about $5M with 14 staff.[^reg-nsf][^willison-psf]
- Precedent: The Carpentries had withdrawn a $1.5M NSF proposal over the same terms in June 2025.[^reg-nsf]
- Anthropic's matching $1.5M is a two-year commitment covering PyPI security and core programs such as Developer-in-Residence.[^psf-anthropic]
