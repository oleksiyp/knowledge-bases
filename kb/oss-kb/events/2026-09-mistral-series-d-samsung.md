# What happened
Mistral raised €3B (~$3.5B) at more than €21B (~$24.4B) post-money, led by Samsung Electronics, with the EU's ~€5B Scaleup Europe Fund (managed by EQT) and PSG Equity co-leading; new investors Advent, BlackRock funds and Luxembourg joined NVIDIA, a16z, ASML, Bpifrance, DST, Index, Lightspeed and Salesforce Ventures[^dcd-series-d][^technode-series-d][^mistral-news][^tc-mistral-d]. CNBC put total equity raised at roughly €6B and reported CEO Arthur Mensch expects ARR above $1B in 2026[^cnbc-mistral-d]. Mistral's announcement headline: "make sovereign, open-weight AI the technology frontier"[^mistral-news].

# Why it matters
It is the strongest market validation of an open-weight business model outside China, and shows European public capital backing open models as sovereignty infrastructure.

# Outcome so far
Funds earmarked for research, compute and expansion[^dcd-series-d]; new partnerships (Cloudera, Mozilla) followed within days[^mistral-news].
