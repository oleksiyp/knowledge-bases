# What happened
Laravel announced a $57M Series A in partnership with Accel on 2024-09-05; the company, based in Little Rock, Arkansas, had ~35 employees and had never raised before[^laravel-accel][^sa-laravel]. The money funded Laravel Cloud, a managed PaaS that launched on 2025-02-24 with Laravel 12[^heise-cloud].

# Why it matters
A counter-example to the 2023–24 relicensing wave: a framework company monetizing via first-party hosting while keeping the framework MIT.

# Outcome so far
Otwell claimed in 2026 that Cloud had already tripled revenue for the year and was on pace for 5x (no figures disclosed)[^otwell-5x]. No follow-on round announced as of 2026-10-03.
