# What happened
Spain's Sociedad Española para la Transformación Tecnológica (SETT), a public body under the Ministry for Digital Transformation, invested €1.92M in Kaleidos, the Madrid company behind Penpot. The investment was part of a €6.9M early-stage round, made through SETT's Next Tech facility with EU recovery funds.[^moncloa][^sett][^muy-penpot] The money is for scaling Penpot's commercial team and paid product. It is Kaleidos' first disclosed raise since an $8M Series A in Sept 2022.[^muy-penpot][^cnbc-2022]

# Why it matters
It is a rare direct state equity investment in an open-source end-user application, justified as reducing European dependence on a single US design platform (Figma).[^moncloa] It gives Penpot new capital after its 2025 "Open Nitrate" self-sustainability plan.

# Outcome so far
Penpot kept a monthly release cadence (2.17 in July, 2.18 with an Enterprise tier in Sept 2026).
