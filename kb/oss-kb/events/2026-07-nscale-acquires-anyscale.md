# What happened
Nscale signed a definitive agreement to acquire Anyscale (~200 employees), which keeps its brand; close expected H2 2026; Nscale will join the PyTorch Foundation[^nscale-pr]. Bloomberg and TechCrunch put the price at ~$1.65B, vs Anyscale's 2022 Series C valuation of $1.38B[^bbg-nscale][^tc-nscale].

# Why it matters
Compute owners are buying the software layers above them; Ray's prior foundation donation insulated users from the ownership change.
