# What happened
The Document Foundation released LibreOffice 26.8 on Aug 26, 2026. On Sept 2 it reported 1,031,162 downloads from the official website in the first seven days, the highest first-week figure for any LibreOffice release. The count excludes most Linux users, who install from distro repositories, Flathub or Snap.[^omg-268] Downloads later passed 2M, and TDF linked the surge to its public position that LibreOffice ships no generative-AI features.[^heise-record][^cybernews-2m]

# Why it matters
It is a measurable sign that "no AI" can be a selling point for open-source desktop software, alongside European sovereignty migrations. It came only months after TDF expelled Collabora-affiliated members, which shows that user demand and developer-base health can diverge.

# Outcome so far
Demand is strong; whether Collabora's reduced involvement slows development in the 26.8/27.2 cycles is the open question.
