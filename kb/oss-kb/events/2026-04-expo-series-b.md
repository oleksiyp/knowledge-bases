# What happened
On 2026-04-16 Expo announced a $45M Series B led by Georgian, with Leadout Capital, A.Capital Ventures and Red Swan Ventures participating. Alongside the round it launched Expo Agent in public beta and named Seth Webster Chief Developer Evangelist.[^prn-expo-b] Expo reported about 4M weekly downloads, more than 3M developers, and customers including Phantom, Pizza Hut, MTA and PrizePicks.[^prn-expo-b]

# Why it matters
It is a large 2026 raise for a permissively licensed (MIT) developer framework whose company makes money from cloud services (EAS) rather than licenses.[^finsmes-expo] Co-founder Charlie Cheever's pitch was that "business critical apps are not making it to production" from agentic app development, which positions OSS frameworks as the production layer under AI app builders.[^prn-expo-b]

# Outcome so far
- Expo shipped SDK 56 (May 2026) and SDK 57 (June 2026) after the round. No valuation was disclosed.
