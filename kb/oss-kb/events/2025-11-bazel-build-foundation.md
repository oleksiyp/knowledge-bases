# What happened
In October 2025 the Bazel project said a new Linux Foundation entity was being formed to fund open-source Bazel rules.[^bazel-q3-2025] It was announced at BazelCon 2025 (Atlanta, November 2025; exact announcement day approximate) as the BUILD Foundation, organized by community figures including Helen Altshuler and Alex Eagle and funded by Canva, Spotify and Uber.[^thestack-bazel] By April 2026 it was described as a Linux Foundation Directed Fund "to accelerate the community roadmap for Bazel and related build technologies", recruiting founding members, with Google retaining governance of core Bazel.[^bazel-q1-2026]

# Why it matters
More people now use Bazel outside Google than inside it. The foundation gives those users a funded way to fix what Google does not prioritize and to act as "the voice of the customer" toward Google.[^thestack-bazel] It is a middle path between pure single-vendor control and a full foundation transfer.

# Outcome so far
Bazel 9 LTS shipped in January 2026. The foundation is enrolling members. Whether Google will ever cede core governance is open.[^bazel-q1-2026]
