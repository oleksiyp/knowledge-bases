# What happened
Desai, CEO since 10 Nov 2025 (~11 months), left to become Meta's chief enterprise platform officer, reporting to Mark Zuckerberg[^gn-mdb-stock]; Ittycheria (CEO 2014–2025) became interim CEO and the board launched a search; guidance was reaffirmed[^mdb-ceo]. Shares closed down ~18% after falling as much as ~26% intraday[^yahoo-mdb-18][^thestreet-mdb]. (Corrected in pass 2: "~18–24%" → ~18% close / ~26% intraday.)

# Why it matters
Came four weeks after MongoDB's best quarter in years (+30% revenue)[^mdb-q2fy27]; shows how AI-talent wars now reach public COSS leadership.

# Outcome so far
CEO search under way.
