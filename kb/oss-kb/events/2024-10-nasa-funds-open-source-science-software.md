# What happened
NASA announced $15.6M for 15 projects on 2024-10-24[^nasa-oss-2024]. Six "Foundation" awards went to Ames Stereo Pipeline, Astroquery, arXiv infrastructure, GMAT, Matplotlib and Cartopy (NumFOCUS) and the Astropy Project (NumFOCUS). Nine "Sustainment" awards included NumPy/SciPy/scikit-learn (Quansight), QGIS planetary updates (USGS), GDAL, SunPy and F Prime documentation[^nasa-oss-2024][^jpl-oss-2024]. NASA said proposals nearly doubled versus the previous cycle[^nasa-oss-2024].

# Why it matters
It was explicit federal recognition that general-purpose libraries are mission infrastructure — and it came months before the 2025 federal science budget fights, in which the administration proposed deep cuts to NASA science (see the linked event).

# Outcome so far
Multi-year awards continued; Congress's FY2026 appropriations (January 2026) cut NASA only 1.6%, rejecting the proposed deep cuts — see [/events/2025-04-us-federal-science-grant-terminations.md](/events/2025-04-us-federal-science-grant-terminations.md). No later equivalent NASA OSS award round was found for 2025–2026.
