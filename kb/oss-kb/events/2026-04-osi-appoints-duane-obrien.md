# What happened
On April 15, 2026 the OSI announced that Duane O'Brien had become its executive director, effective April 13. O'Brien most recently led Collaborative Engineering, including the open-source program office, at Capital One. Before that he built Indeed's OSPO, directed open-source programs at PayPal, and serves on the board of the Open Source Collective.[^prn-obrien][^osi-obrien]

# Why it matters
The OSI spent 2024–2026 under strain: the contested Open Source AI Definition and paused board elections in January 2026. A permanent executive director with an enterprise-OSPO and sustainability background is the first step toward the governance redesign the OSI promised.

# Outcome so far
Board-governance reforms were still pending as of Oct 2026.
