# What happened
On 27 Feb 2025 Mozilla published Firefox's first Terms of Use and an updated Privacy Notice. Language granting Mozilla "a nonexclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license" over information users input, plus deletion of the FAQ promise "we never sell your data", triggered accusations that Mozilla would sell data to advertisers or AI firms[^ghacks][^tc]. Mozilla explained that broad legal definitions of "sale" (e.g., CCPA) covered its ad-partner data sharing, and on ~3 Mar 2025 rewrote the terms to clarify it does not gain ownership of user data[^moz][^tc].

# Why it matters
Firefox's differentiation is trust; the episode signaled that advertising revenue diversification was colliding with its privacy brand.

# Outcome so far
The terms were rewritten, but the episode fed a narrative of decline (e.g., Register columnist "Firefox is dead to me", June 2025)[^reg-op] and amplified later skepticism about Mozilla's AI plans.
