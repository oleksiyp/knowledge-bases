# What happened
Hugging Face acquired Pollen Robotics for an undisclosed sum and offered its Apache-2.0 Reachy 2 humanoid for research (~$70k).[^tc-pollen]

# Why it matters
It gave the leading open-AI hub a hardware arm. A month later Hugging Face announced the ~$3k HopeJR and the ~$300 Reachy Mini.[^tc-hopejr]

# Outcome so far
Reachy Mini became a consumer-scale open robot: 3,000 shipped with Seeed before the 2025 holidays.[^seeed-reachy] See LeRobot for later developments, including NVIDIA's pending purchase of Hugging Face.
