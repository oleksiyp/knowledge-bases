# What happened
The Linux Foundation announced FAIR, with TSC co-chairs Carrie Dils, Mika Epstein and Ryan McCue and support from Fastly, Crowd Favorite, the CNCF and the OpenJS Foundation, as a federated, cryptographically secured source of WordPress plugins and themes.[^lf-fair] Mullenweg responded publicly within days.[^sej-mullenweg-fair]

# Why it matters
The community decentralised distribution instead of forking WordPress. That fits a dispute that was about control of infrastructure, not about the license.

# Outcome so far
The FAIR plugin has a steady release cadence (1.4.0 in May 2026, 1.4.1 in Aug 2026) but niche adoption.[^fair-gh]
