On 2025-10-09 n8n announced a $180M Series C led by Accel with Meritech, Redpoint, NVentures (NVIDIA) and others, at $2.5B, bringing total funding to ~$240M; it cited 6x user and 10x revenue growth in a year[^n8n-seriesc][^tfn-n8n].
