# What happened
Ukraine's SBU struck several Russian airbases with FPV drones launched from trucks. 404 Media reported that the drones used ArduPilot, which let them keep flying pre-programmed routes when the link dropped.[^404-spiderweb][^chatham-spiderweb][^csis-spiderweb]

# Why it matters
Open-source autopilots (GPL ArduPilot, BSD PX4) are now strategic military technology. That raises export-control, ethics and maintainer-liability questions.

# Outcome so far
Defense money flowed into the open-autopilot ecosystem. Weeks later Auterion (PX4) won a $50M Pentagon contract for 33,000 strike kits for Ukraine.[^auterion-pentagon]
