# What happened
VoidZero announced Vite+ on 2025-10-13. The plan was free use for individuals, OSS projects and small companies, with paid licenses for startups (flat annual fee) and enterprises (custom pricing).[^viteplus-announce] The Vite+ alpha shipped on 2026-03-13 under MIT instead. VoidZero said Vite+ "can only achieve our mission ... when it is truly free and open source" and pointed to community feedback and "the exhausting debate about which features should be free".[^viteplus-alpha][^heise]

# Why it matters
It shows how hard it is to charge for a developer CLI built on top of a beloved MIT project. The commercial license plan was abandoned before it ever launched.[^viteplus-announce]

# Outcome so far
Three months later VoidZero was acquired by Cloudflare. Vite+ reached 1.0 under MIT.
