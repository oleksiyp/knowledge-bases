# What happened
On 1 April 2026 TDF removed roughly 30 Collabora-affiliated people from its membership (eliminating their governance votes), froze development tenders and adopted bylaws requiring members in disputes with TDF to resign or face removal with a three-year reapplication ban[^lwn][^tdf]. TDF cited 2023–24 audits finding that board members from Collabora and CIB had voted on contracts and trademark licenses benefiting their employers, and German tax-authority requirements to preserve charitable (Gemeinnützigkeit) status[^lwn]. Collabora called it ejecting "its core developers" and said it would build "an entirely new, cut-down, differentiated Collabora Office" and reduce LibreOffice involvement[^collabora][^meeks][^lwn].

# Why it matters
Collabora's 47 staff produced ~43% of recent LibreOffice patches[^lwn]; alienating them risks hollowing out development just as European governments adopt LibreOffice at scale.

# Outcome so far
As of Oct 2026, LibreOffice continues releasing and reports record downloads, while Collabora pursues its own product line; the long-term contributor impact is not yet measurable.
