# What happened
IBM announced plans to acquire DataStax on 2025-02-25 and closed the deal in late May 2025; DataStax had acquired Langflow in April 2024[^reg-ibm][^dbta-ibm][^tns-ibm].

# Why it matters
Langflow became a big-tech-owned OSS project folded into watsonx — a common exit path for low-code LLM builders.

# Outcome so far
Langflow remains MIT and active (v1.12.4, Sep 2026; ~155k stars), contrasting with Flowise's sunset after its Workday acquisition.
