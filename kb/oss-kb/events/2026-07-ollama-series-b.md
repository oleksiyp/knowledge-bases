# What happened
Theory Ventures led, with Benchmark, 8VC and Y Combinator; total funding $88M after a $15M Benchmark-led Series A. Ollama reported ~8.9M MAU, 85% Fortune 500 presence, 176k GitHub stars and 14 employees; cloud plans range from free to $100/month; valuation undisclosed[^tc-ollama].

# Why it matters
The largest raise for a local-AI tool; shows cloud upsell from a free local runtime can work, despite OSS-community criticism.
