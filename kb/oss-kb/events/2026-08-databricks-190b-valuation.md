# What happened
Databricks raised $5B at $190B (up from ~$134B six months earlier), disclosing >$7B run-rate revenue and >80% YoY growth; TechCrunch reported it sought $1B but investors wanted to put in $15B[^gn-dbx][^cnbc-dbx-aug26].

# Why it matters
A single COSS company raising in one round what the whole COSS sector averaged per year in 2019–2024.

# Outcome so far
Databricks reported positive adjusted free cash flow over the trailing 12 months, Lakebase above a $100M run-rate and 1,000+ customers above $1M[^gn-dbx]. Some analysts questioned the valuation (a Morningstar critique was reported but not fetched in pass 2); no IPO filing has been made.

## Additional notes (data-engineering)
- The round was first reported at $188B in July 2026 and closed at $190B post-money, led by Coatue with Blackstone, MGX, T. Rowe Price-advised accounts and new investor Sixth Street Growth, six months after the $134B Series L.[^dbx-tc][^dbx-bbg][^dbx-wiki]
- For the open-source data stack it underwrites Apache Spark, Delta Lake, Unity Catalog OSS and — via the 2024 Tabular deal — a large share of Apache Iceberg engineering. Follow-on 2026 moves: Panther Labs and Electric acquisitions, Omnigent OSS agent framework.[^dbx-wiki]
