# Summary
Couchbase, a 2021-IPO NoSQL database vendor, agreed on 2025-06-20 to be acquired by **Haveli Investments** (led by ex-Vista president Brian Sheth) for **$24.50/share (~$1.5B)**; shareholders approved on 2025-09-09 and the deal closed **2025-09-24**, delisting the stock from Nasdaq[^couchbase-close][^sa-couchbase].
