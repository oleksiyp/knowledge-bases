# Summary
The Open Home Foundation took ownership of Home Assistant and related projects in April 2024[^hackster]. By April 2025 it had 39 full-time project staff moved from Nabu Casa and 56 people working full-time on Open Home projects; donations from Espressif and DuckDuckGo ($25K each) supplement Nabu Casa's Home Assistant Cloud subscriptions, the main funding source[^sooh]. In 2026 it released open TTS datasets with the Mozilla Data Collective (June)[^tts], an anonymized community survey dataset (Aug)[^survey], and celebrated an EU DMA decision forcing Google to open Android wake-word and other APIs (July)[^dma]. Verdict: thriving.

# Monetization model
Nabu Casa (for-profit) sells Home Assistant Cloud and hardware (Green, Voice PE, Connect dongles) and shares profits with the Foundation; corporate donations.

# Successes
- No VC; mission-locked ownership; large paid subscriber base (number not public).
# Failures / risks
- Concentration on one revenue source; vendor API lockouts.
