# Summary
NuxtLabs employed key Nuxt and Nitro maintainers (Daniel Roe, Pooya Parsa, Anthony Fu) and sold add-ons on top of the MIT framework.[^vercel-nuxtlabs] Vercel acquired it on 2025-07-08. Nuxt kept its MIT license and open governance, and the paid products (UI Pro, Studio, NuxtHub Admin) were made free and open source.[^vercel-nuxtlabs][^nuxtlabs-site][^nuxt-studio-oss] Daniel Roe framed the deal as a fix for financial sustainability.[^redmonk-roe]

# Monetization model
Before the acquisition: paid UI kit (Nuxt UI Pro), hosted CMS (Nuxt Studio) and NuxtHub. After: none. The team is funded by Vercel.[^nuxtlabs-site]

# Successes
- Kept the framework's core team funded for years, then secured a well-capitalized owner.[^redmonk-roe]

# Failures / risks
- The add-on model did not reach standalone sustainability.[^redmonk-roe]
