# Summary
RadixArk was founded by Ying Sheng (ex-xAI, ex-Databricks) and Banghua Zhu to grow SGLang[^bw-radixark][^tc-radixark]. TechCrunch reported the spinout at ~$400M valuation with Accel leading on 2026-01-21[^tc-radixark]; the formal launch on 2026-05-05 disclosed a $100M seed (Accel lead, Spark co-lead) with NVIDIA, AMD and MediaTek all investing, plus angels such as Lip-Bu Tan, Hock Tan, John Schulman, Soumith Chintala, Olivier Pomel and Thomas Wolf, at a $400M post-money valuation[^bw-radixark]. The January report and the May announcement describe the same single round, a $100M seed at $400M; there was no separate earlier round. It also develops Miles, an RL framework, and plans paid hosting while keeping SGLang free[^tfn-radixark].
