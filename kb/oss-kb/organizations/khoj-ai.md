# Summary
Khoj AI ran Khoj Cloud on top of its AGPL project. On 2026-04-15 it sunset the cloud, explaining that hand-built integrations were "a long tail of effort", defensibility eroded as major labs shipped similar features, and early choices (subscription, complex syncs, many clients) made scaling hard; founders moved to Open Paper and Pipali[^khoj-sunset]. Aggregators list under $0.5M raised[^khoj-cb].
