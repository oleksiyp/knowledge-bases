# Summary
n8n raised $1.5M seed (2020, Sequoia/firstminute), $12M Series A (2021, Felicis), EUR 55M Series B (Mar 2025, Highland Europe, ~EUR 250M valuation), $180M Series C (2025-10-09, Accel, $2.5B), and an SAP strategic investment in May 2026 at $5.2B (Bloomberg: commitment of more than €60M; the ~1.3% stake figure is from Wikipedia only), with n8n to be embedded in SAP's Joule Studio[^n8n-wiki][^n8n-seriesc][^n8n-seriesb][^cm-gn-n8n][^n8n-sap-blog]. n8n reported 1,400+ enterprise customers and 1.7M monthly active builders at that point[^n8n-sap-blog]. ARR was reported above $40M in 2025 with ~10x revenue growth YoY[^n8n-wiki][^n8n-seriesc]. Verdict: **thriving**.

# Monetization model
Source-available self-hosting (free for internal use) plus paid cloud and enterprise licenses; license forbids competing hosted resale[^n8n-wiki].

# Successes
- ~20x valuation increase in ~14 months; strategic distribution through SAP and Deutsche Telekom[^n8n-wiki].

# Failures / risks
- "Fair-code" is not OSI open source; category threat from coding agents replacing workflow builders.
