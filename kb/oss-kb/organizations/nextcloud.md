# Summary
Nextcloud GmbH (founded 2016 by Frank Karlitschek after forking ownCloud) is fully employee-owned, profitable and self-funded[^s2030]. Since 2025, US political shifts turned "sovereignty" into its main sales driver: >2M new professional users and >50% bookings growth in 2025, tripled inbound leads, ~300% partner growth[^momentum]. On 5 Nov 2025 it announced Sovereignty 2030 — €250M+ through 2030 and a 7× workforce expansion[^s2030]; it co-founded the EuroStack foundation (Oct 2025)[^eurostack] and co-led the Euro-Office fork with IONOS (GA June 2026)[^eo], at the cost of its OnlyOffice partnership[^xda-oo]. Verdict: thriving.
