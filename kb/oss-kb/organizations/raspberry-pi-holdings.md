# Summary
Raspberry Pi listed on the LSE on June 11, 2024 at 280p (a £541.6M market cap).[^lse-ipo] FY2025: revenue $323.2M (+25%), PBT $26.5M (+63%), 7.6M boards and 8.4M MCUs shipped.[^rpi-fy25] H1 2026: revenue $256.9M (+90%), PBT $19.6M (+216%) and a 2.6M-unit backlog, driven by memory stockpiled in 2025. The credit facility was raised to $140M.[^tnw-h1] Shares spiked after the results, then fell 9% on Sept 25 as Jefferies flagged a ~$15M non-recurring inventory gain.[^yahoo-fall9]
