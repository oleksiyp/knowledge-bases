# Summary
The Ghost Foundation is a non-profit that cannot be bought or sold; revenue from Ghost(Pro) managed hosting is reinvested in the open-source project[^wiki]. It crossed **$10M ARR in early March 2026**, with publishers' cumulative earnings on Ghost around $130M[^arr]. Ghost 6.0 (Aug 2025) added ActivityPub federation and native analytics[^g6]; a local-news program targets independent newsrooms[^news]. Verdict: thriving.

# Monetization model
Ghost(Pro) managed hosting subscriptions (the foundation reinvests revenue in the project)[^wiki].

- Eight-figure ARR without VC; open-protocol distribution[^arr][^g6].
