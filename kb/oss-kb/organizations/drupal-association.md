# Summary
The Drupal Association (DA) stewards Drupal.org infrastructure, DrupalCon and fundraising. On **15 July 2026** it announced that CEO Tim Doyle was stepping down (no reason given) and long-time board member **Tiffany Farriss** would serve 6–12 months as interim CEO[^ceo][^dt-ceo]. On **4 Aug 2026** it published its 2025 audit: a **$451K operating deficit** in 2025 plus a **restated $923K 2024 deficit** (~$1.15M over two years), reserves of ~$960K (2.3 months of opex, below its 3-month minimum for the first time since 2019) and Drupal.org infrastructure costs up from $1.3M (2022) to $2.1M (2025); it says it remains a going concern and will publish a balanced 2027–28 budget[^audit]. Farriss proposed cost accounting by program and a shift toward enterprise funding[^dt-funding]. Verdict: struggling.
