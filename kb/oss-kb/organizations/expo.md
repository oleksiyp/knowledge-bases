# Summary
Expo was founded in 2015 by Charlie Cheever and James Ide. It builds the MIT-licensed Expo framework and earns revenue from Expo Application Services (EAS): cloud builds, store submission and over-the-air updates.[^finsmes-expo] On 2026-04-16 it raised a $45M Series B led by Georgian and launched Expo Agent, reporting about 4M weekly downloads and more than 3M developers.[^prn-expo-b]

| 2026-04-16 | $45M Series B (Georgian; Leadout, A.Capital, Red Swan); Expo Agent public beta [^prn-expo-b] |

# Monetization model
Usage-based and subscription cloud services (EAS) on top of a permissively licensed framework. No license restrictions.[^finsmes-expo]

# Successes
- Became React Native's default toolchain and raised a large round without a license change.[^prn-expo-b]

# Failures / risks
- Depends on Meta's React Native. AI app builders may compete for the developer relationship.
