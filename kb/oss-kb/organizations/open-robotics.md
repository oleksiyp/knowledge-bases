# Summary
After Alphabet's Intrinsic absorbed Open Robotics' for-profit arm, the community questioned ROS's independence.[^tc-intrinsic-osrc] The nonprofit OSRF responded in March 2024 with the **Open Source Robotics Alliance**, a mixed membership and meritocratic model. Inaugural platinum members were Intrinsic, NVIDIA and Qualcomm; gold were Apex.AI and Zettascale.[^osra] Since then it has shipped ROS 2 Kilted Kaiju (May 2025) and the Lyrical Luth LTS (May 2026).[^or-kilted][^lyrical] Its 2026 ROSCon in Toronto hosted Intrinsic's open-sourcing of Intrinsic Core.[^intrinsic-core]
