# Summary
Mistral AI is the most successful commercial open-weight lab outside China. It combines Apache-2.0/Modified-MIT open weights with proprietary models, the Le Chat / Mistral Vibe assistant, enterprise deployments and its own compute (Mistral Compute). Valuation rose from €5.8B (Jun 2024) to €11.7B post-money (Sept 2025, €1.7B Series C, ASML lead with €1.3B for ~11%)[^wiki-mistral][^cm-gn-mistral25] to more than €21B post-money (8 Sept 2026, €3B Series D led by Samsung with EQT's Scaleup Europe Fund and PSG Equity as co-leads — which Mistral calls the largest equity round ever completed by a European tech company)[^mistral-series-d][^tc-mistral-d]. CNBC puts total equity raised at roughly €6B and quotes CEO Arthur Mensch expecting ARR to exceed $1B in 2026[^cnbc-mistral-d]; Mensch had told the Financial Times that ARR passed $400M in early 2026, up ~20x from ~$20M a year earlier[^decoder-mistral-arr].

# Monetization model
Open-weight models as distribution; revenue from API, enterprise/on-prem licenses and deployments, Vibe subscriptions ($14.99/month Pro)[^wiki-mistral], and compute/sovereign-cloud contracts.

# Successes
- Valuation nearly doubled in 12 months[^dcd-series-d]; 1,000+ employees by 2026[^wiki-mistral].
- Strategic investors spanning chips (ASML, NVIDIA, Samsung) and EU public money[^technode-series-d].

# Failures / risks
- Small global download share vs Chinese labs; license inconsistency (see project page).
- Capital intensity of owning compute.
