# Summary
SUSE sells enterprise Linux (SLES), Rancher Kubernetes management and edge products, and markets itself as Europe's sovereign open source infrastructure vendor. On 2026-03-09/10 Reuters reported that owner **EQT was exploring a sale valued at ~$6B**; EQT hired Arma Partners to sound out mainly private-equity buyers; SUSE was said to have ~$800M revenue and >$250M EBITDA[^gn-suse]. (The Register later questioned how an ownership change would square with the sovereignty pitch; not re-fetched in pass 2.) No transaction had been announced by 2026-10-03 (none found). The EU's June 2026 Open Source Strategy strengthens the policy tailwind for EU-headquartered vendors[^tpp-eu].
