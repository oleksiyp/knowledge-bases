# Summary
Proton moved to foundation control in June 2024 (Proton Foundation as controlling shareholder; its early outside funding was ~$0.5M crowdfunding in 2014 and $2M from CRV and FONGIT in 2015, and no VC holds shares today)[^wiki][^proton-foundation] and kept broadening its open-source-client suite: Docs (2024), Lumo privacy AI and Authenticator (July 2025), Meet (Sept 2025, public March 2026)[^wiki][^lumo]. It co-founded the EuroStack foundation (Oct 2025)[^eurostack], joined a suit against Apple (June 2025)[^apple], and in May 2025 threatened to leave Switzerland over a proposed surveillance law, shifting infrastructure investment (≈€100M) to Germany and Norway[^swiss][^wiki]. Controversies: CEO Andy Yen's January 2025 political posts[^wiki], suspension of journalist accounts at a cybersecurity agency's request (Sept 2025)[^intercept], and disclosure that Proton data helped the FBI identify a protester (March 2026)[^media404]. Verdict: growing, with trust management challenges.
