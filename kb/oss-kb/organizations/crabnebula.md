# Summary
CrabNebula was co-founded by Daniel Thompson-Yvetot (CEO) and Lucas Nogueira (CTO), who also co-founded Tauri. Thompson-Yvetot chairs the board of the Tauri Programme within The Commons Conservancy.[^crabnebula-about] On 2026-06-11 it announced that CrabNebula Cloud would be free from 2026-06-19, upgrading every account to the top plan including DevTools. It said it was moving community services into a foundation (not yet named), linking the change to the EU Cyber Resilience Act's lighter obligations for "open-source stewards".[^crabnebula-free] The business verdict is "struggling" because it is giving away its main paid product. This is our inference; the company did not report layoffs.
