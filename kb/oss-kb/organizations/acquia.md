# Summary
Acquia is the largest Drupal company and a key funder of Drupal's Starshot and AI work[^ai-init]. Owned by Vista Equity Partners since a ~$1B 2019 deal[^vista], it replaced CEO Steve Reny with former Khoros CEO **Chris Tranquill on 19 Aug 2025**[^tranquill][^dries-ceo]. Anonymous employee reviews describe a large Oct 2024 sales/presales layoff and another company-wide cut in July 2026 with roles moved offshore (unverified by press)[^glassdoor], consistent with Vista's stated plan to shrink portfolio headcount via AI[^investnews]. Verdict: struggling (stable revenue base, shrinking investment).
