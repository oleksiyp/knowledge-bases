# Summary
SiFive sells proprietary RISC-V core IP on the open ISA, the "open standard, closed implementation" model. After cutting ~20% of staff in Oct 2023,[^wiki-sifive] it re-positioned around datacenter and AI CPUs. On Apr 9, 2026 it announced an oversubscribed **$400M Series G at $3.65B**, led by Atreides with Apollo, NVIDIA, Point72 Turion and T. Rowe Price.[^sifive-g] CEO Patrick Little described it as the last private round before an IPO.[^tnw-sifive]
