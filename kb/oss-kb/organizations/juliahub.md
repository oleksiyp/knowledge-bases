# Summary
JuliaHub, led by CEO and Julia co-creator Viral Shah, has refocused from selling Julia cloud compute to Dyad — a modeling/simulation and "agentic engineering" platform built on Julia's SciML stack (launched June 2025, v2.0 Dec 2025 with a Synopsys partnership, v3.0 April 2026)[^dyad-launch][^dyad-v2][^juliahub-b]. On 2026-04-30 it raised a $65M Series B led by Dorilton with General Catalyst, AE Ventures and Bob Muglia, citing Fortune-100 customers in aerospace, automotive, HVAC and utilities[^juliahub-b][^hpcwire-b]. Aggregators put total funding near $107M, including earlier SAFEs converted into the B (unverified)[^sacra-juliahub]. Verdict: growing, but its value is a proprietary vertical product layered on the MIT-licensed language.
