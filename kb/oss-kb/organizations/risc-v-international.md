# Summary
RISC-V International is a membership-funded standards body. Under Calista Redmond membership grew from 236 to more than 4,600 (counting individuals) across 70 countries. She resigned in Dec 2024 and later joined NVIDIA. Andrea Gallo, previously VP Technology, succeeded her; the annual report says he took the CEO role in May 2025.[^rvi-redmond][^rvi-gallo][^rvi-annual-2025] Key outputs were the RVA23 profile (Oct 2024),[^rvi-rva23] ISO PAS-submitter status, 17 new members in 2025 including Infineon as Premier, and a server platform spec targeted for end-2026.[^rvi-annual-2025] Its political exposure is that 12 of 24 premier members are Chinese-based.[^csis-riscv]
