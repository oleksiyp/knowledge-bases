# Summary
Ai2 publishes fully open models (OLMo 2 → Olmo 3/3.1), instruction recipes (Tülu 3), multimodal Molmo, and domain models (OlmoEarth, AstaBrief)[^wiki-ai2][^ai2-blog]. It maintains 972 models and 1,291 datasets on Hugging Face[^hf-allenai]. CEO Ali Farhadi left on 10 Mar 2026 after ~2.5 years, with Peter Clark as interim leader[^wiki-ai2]. Its Oct 2026 Olmo-core 3 release targets fully open training of trillion-parameter MoEs[^ai2-blog].
