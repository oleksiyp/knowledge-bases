# Summary
Founded in 2024 by ex-DeepMind researchers Misha Laskin and Ioannis Antonoglou, Reflection AI emerged in Mar 2025 with $130M at ~$545M, then raised $2B at $8B in Oct 2025 (NVIDIA, Eric Schmidt, Citi, Sequoia) on a pitch to be the US open-weight frontier lab[^wiki-reflection]. In 2026 it signed a 250 MW data-center MoU with Shinsegae (Mar), became a DOE Genesis Mission model provider (May), signed a $150M/month compute deal with SpaceXAI through 2029 at a reported ~$25B valuation (Jun), and a $1B+ Nebius GB300 deal (Jul)[^wiki-reflection]. Its website still describes the mission ("build open models...") without listing released frontier weights[^reflection-site] — the gap between capital raised and open output is the key watch item.
