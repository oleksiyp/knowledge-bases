# Summary
comma.ai sells aftermarket devices running MIT-licensed openpilot. It launched the **comma four** ($999, made in San Diego, 300+ supported vehicles) on Nov 25, 2025.[^comma-four] It shipped openpilot 0.10 (Aug 2025) and 0.11 (Mar 2026), the latter trained entirely in a learned simulator.[^comma-010][^comma-011] Revenue and funding are undisclosed (third-party estimates unverified).
