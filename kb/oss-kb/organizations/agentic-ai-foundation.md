# Summary
AAIF was announced on 2025-12-09 as a Linux Foundation directed fund co-founded by Anthropic, OpenAI and Block, with founding projects MCP (Anthropic), AGENTS.md (OpenAI) and goose (Block); platinum members include AWS, Anthropic, Block, Bloomberg, Cloudflare, Google, Microsoft and OpenAI[^lf-aaif-pr][^mcp-aaif-blog]. Google's A2A joined on 2026-08-17, alongside agentgateway[^aaif-a2a]. Membership reportedly grew from under 40 to 250+ by Aug 2026, and the Gates Foundation joined as first philanthropic member in Sep 2026[^intuition-aaif][^aaif-gates]. Verdict: **thriving**.

# Successes
- Put the three largest labs' agent standards (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) under one neutral roof within 9 months.

# Failures / risks
- Competitor co-governance; risk that big members steer specs; security of the standards (MCP CVEs) is now a shared foundation problem.
