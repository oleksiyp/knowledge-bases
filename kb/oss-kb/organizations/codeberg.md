# Summary
Codeberg is funded by members and donors and had two part-time staff in 2025.[^wiki-codeberg] As of November 2025 it hosted 300,000+ repositories for 200,000+ users and had 1,208 members.[^wiki-codeberg] It became the main destination for projects leaving GitHub over AI concerns: Zig (2025-11-26), Dillo (Nov 2025) and Gentoo (Feb 2026).[^zig-codeberg][^wiki-codeberg] In July 2026 it changed its terms of service to bar vibe-coded and cryptocurrency projects.[^wiki-codeberg]
