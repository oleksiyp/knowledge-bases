# Summary
Zitadel raised a **$9M Series A (Nexus Venture Partners, Floodgate; Nov 2024)** with ~160 customers[^seriesa], then relicensed server components to **AGPL-3.0 with v3 (31 Mar 2025)** to ensure commercial users "contribute back"[^agpl], and moved to quarterly major releases (v4 GA)[^v4].
