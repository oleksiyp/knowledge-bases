# Summary
Bluesky PBC raised a $15M Series A led by Blockchain Capital in Oct 2024[^wiki] and a $100M Series B led by Bain Capital Crypto (with True Ventures, Alumni Ventures, Anthos, Bloomberg Beta and the Knight Foundation) that closed in April 2025 but was only disclosed on 2026-03-19; the valuation was not announced (Business Insider had reported talks at ~$700M in Jan 2025)[^tc-bsky-b][^bi]. (Corrected in pass 2: "~$700M round led by Bain Capital Ventures" → $100M Series B led by Bain Capital Crypto, valuation undisclosed.) With 29 employees (Sept 2025) and revenue limited to custom-domain hosting (as of Apr 2025), it relies on venture funding[^wiki]. Jay Graber stepped down as CEO on 9 March 2026 (to Chief Innovation Officer); Toni Schneider (ex-Automattic, True Ventures) became interim CEO[^ceo] and was named permanent CEO on 2026-07-10[^bsky-ceo-jul]. Mobile DAU fell to ~3.5M in Oct 2025, down 39.8% year on year (Similarweb via Forbes)[^forbes-bsky]. Verdict: struggling (monetization unresolved).
