# Executive summary
- **Platforms bought the OSS building blocks.** Figma acquired Payload (17 Jun 2025)[^payload-figma]; Better Auth absorbed Auth.js (26 Sept 2025)[^authjs] and was bought by Vercel (7 Jul 2026)[^ba-vercel]; Ory bought BoxyHQ (May 2025)[^ory-boxy]. Code stayed MIT/Apache in every case, but managed products (Payload Cloud) were closed to new users and roadmaps now follow the acquirer.
- **A second wave of source-available relicensing hit SaaS alternatives:** NocoDB left AGPL for a Sustainable Use License (9 Jan 2026)[^nocodb-lic], Directus replaced BSL with its own key-enforced MSCL (v12, 10 Jun 2026)[^directus-v12], Medusa added a proprietary Enterprise Edition (11 Aug 2026)[^medusa-ee], and Cal.com went closed source over AI-security fears (Apr 2026)[^calcom]. Zitadel took the gentler AGPL route (Mar 2025)[^zitadel-agpl].
- **The most durable winners were bootstrapped or non-profit.** Ghost passed $10M ARR as a non-profit (Mar 2026)[^ghost-10m], Plausible had its best month ever (Apr 2026)[^plausible-apr], and Bruno grew to 600K+ MAU with no announced funding[^bruno-2025].
- **Foundation and steward stress:** the Drupal Association disclosed a ~$1.15M two-year deficit and changed CEO in July 2026[^da-audit][^da-ceo], even as Drupal CMS 1.0/2.0 successfully relaunched the product[^drupal-cms1][^drupal-cms2]. Hasura pivoted to PromptQL (Jun 2025)[^hasura-promptql], and Nhost answered with Constellation, an open Hasura-compatible engine (Jun 2026)[^nhost-constellation].
- **AI app generation is squeezing low-code internal tools.** ToolJet declared "low-code had its moment"[^tooljet]; Retool's 2026 survey reports that 35% of its customers have already replaced a SaaS tool with a custom build[^retool-report]; Appsmith pivoted to agents and a separate website builder, then had a 9.6-CVSS account-takeover CVE (Jan 2026)[^appsmith-cve].
- **Capital was scarce and selective.** The large raises were Unleash's $35M B (Mar 2026)[^unleash-b], Better Auth's $5M seed[^ba-seed], proprietary Clerk's $50M C[^clerk] and closed Sanity's $85M C[^sanity]. Most 2021–22-funded OSS web startups (Strapi, Appwrite, Medusa, Appsmith, Hasura) announced no new rounds in the window.
- **Government and sovereignty demand created a commons success:** French ministries pushed Grist to 20K monthly users in administration (10× in a year)[^grist-osor].

# Trends
1. **Platform capture of OSS primitives.** Hosting and design platforms buy the libraries their users depend on (Figma–Payload[^payload-figma], Vercel–Better Auth[^ba-vercel], Ory–BoxyHQ[^ory-boxy]; compare Shopify–Tailwind and Cloudflare–VoidZero in other domains). Licenses stay permissive; independence goes.
2. **Fair-code/source-available 2.0.** The successors to BSL are custom, key-enforced licenses with revenue *and* headcount thresholds (Directus MSCL)[^directus-v12] or n8n-style SUL (NocoDB)[^nocodb-lic]; AGPL is the OSI-safe alternative (Zitadel)[^zitadel-agpl]. Cal.com's AI-security rationale for going fully closed is new[^calcom].
3. **AI app generation eats low-code.** Internal-tool vendors (ToolJet, Appsmith, Budibase, Retool) rebuilt around prompts, agents and AI credits[^tooljet][^retool-report]; BaaS players chased "backend for AI builders" (Appwrite Imagine, Supabase).
4. **Agent identity becomes the auth battleground.** Keycloak (MCP authorization)[^keycloak-264], Clerk ($50M on agent identity)[^clerk] and Better Auth's "Agent Auth Protocol" under Vercel[^ba-vercel].
5. **Bootstrapped and non-profit COSS outperformed VC-funded peers** on durability: Ghost[^ghost-10m], Plausible[^plausible-apr], Bruno[^bruno-2025] vs. stalled Strapi/Appwrite/Appsmith fundraising.
6. **Steward funding is the weak link,** not code: Drupal Association deficit[^da-audit], PocketBase's lost grant[^pocketbase-floss], Hasura's pivot leaving v2 users to build replacements[^nhost-constellation].
7. **Sovereignty-driven adoption in Europe** (Grist in French administration[^grist-osor], Baserow and Matomo targeting regulated EU buyers).
8. **"OSS alternative to SaaS X" (the OSS Capital thesis) matured unevenly.** OSS Capital (~80 investments, three ~$50M funds)[^oss-capital] saw portfolio companies split: BoxyHQ exited to Ory[^ory-boxy], Cal.com closed its source[^calcom], Hoppscotch remained a modest open-core business.

# Success patterns
- Default-to-cloud SaaS with an honest self-hosted edition and a simple product (Plausible, Ghost, Umami).
- Local-first/Git-native design that exploits incumbents' lock-in moves (Bruno vs Postman/Insomnia).
- Orderly consolidation: maintainers handing projects to an active successor (Auth.js → Better Auth) rather than abandoning them.
- Vendor-neutral governance for infrastructure-grade components (Keycloak in CNCF; OpenFeature standard).
- Open protocols as distribution (Ghost's ActivityPub).

# Failure patterns
- 2021-era VC rounds sized for hypergrowth that never came, leading to pivots (Hasura → PromptQL), silence (Strapi, Appwrite) or relicensing (Directus, NocoDB).
- Closing the control plane while keeping the engine "open" (Hasura v3), which invites reimplementations (Nhost Constellation).
- Low-code products without an AI-native story losing relevance (Appsmith).
- Nonprofits reliant on events and a single PE-owned sponsor (Drupal Association/Acquia).
- Solo-maintainer dependence with fragile funding (PocketBase).

# Open questions / watchlist for next 6 months
- Does Vercel keep Better Auth framework-neutral, and does Figma reopen Payload Cloud or fold it into Figma Sites?
- Will NocoDB's or Directus's relicensing produce a credible fork (none as of Oct 2026)? Does Teable (AGPL core, ~22K stars)[^teable] or Baserow absorb displaced users after Bending Spoons' Airtable takeover[^airtable]?
- Drupal Association's balanced 2027–28 budget and permanent CEO; Drupal 12 release (late 2026).
- Keycloak CNCF graduation decision.
- Funding or consolidation for Strapi, Appwrite, Medusa and Appsmith — likely acquisition targets for hosting or AI-builder platforms.
- Whether more OSS SaaS alternatives follow Cal.com's AI-security rationale for closing source.
