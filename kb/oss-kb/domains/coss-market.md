# Executive summary

- **Capital is abundant but concentrated.** The LF/COSSA/Serena baseline is $26.4B of COSS funding in 2024 vs a ~$9B/yr 2019–24 average[^lf-coss-2025]; in 2025–26 single COSS rounds rivalled that baseline (Databricks $5B at $134B and $5B at $190B; >$7B run-rate, >80% growth)[^cnbc-dbx-aug26]. No aggregate COSS funding total for 2025 or H1 2026 has been published by LF/COSSA, Crunchbase or OSS Capital (checked 2026-10-03). Globally AI took ~50% of 2025 VC and >70% in Q2 2026[^cb-2025][^cb-h1-2026].
- **The winning archetype is "OSS engine + managed cloud consumed by AI agents"**: Supabase ($2B → $5B → $10.5B in ~12 months; >60% of new databases launched by AI tools)[^supabase-f], ClickHouse (~$15B)[^bbg-clickhouse], Temporal ($12.55B)[^temporal-e].
- **Record COSS M&A**: IBM–HashiCorp ($6.4B EV, Feb 2025)[^tc-ibm-hashicorp], IBM–Confluent (~$11B, closed Mar 2026)[^ibm-confluent-close], and Nvidia–Hugging Face (~$12.9B incl. up to $1B retention awards, announced Sept 2026, close expected H1 2027) — the largest COSS acquisition ever[^nvidia-hf].
- **Public cohort shrank, no US COSS IPO.** HashiCorp, Couchbase and Confluent delisted[^couchbase-close]; MongoDB re-accelerated to +30% then lost its CEO to Meta (stock ~−18%, intraday lows near −26%)[^mdb-q2fy27][^mdb-ceo][^yf-mdb-stock]; GitLab slowed to +21% and cut 14%[^gitlab-q2fy27][^tc-gitlab-cuts]; Elastic steady at +15%[^elastic-q1fy27].
- **AI labs bought the OSS under their agents**: Anthropic–Bun (Dec 2025), OpenAI–Astral (Mar 2026), all pledging to keep MIT/Apache licenses[^bun-anthropic][^astral-openai].
- **AI broke some OSS monetization**: Tailwind Labs laid off 3 of 4 engineers with revenue down ~80% (Jan 2026) and joined Shopify (Sept 2026) despite ~110M weekly installs[^eweek-tw][^tailwind-shopify].
- **Source-available retreat & public money**: Redis followed Elastic back to AGPL (May 2025)[^redis-agpl]; the EU proposed a €2B/7-year open source envelope (June 2026)[^tpp-eu].

# Trends

1. **AI-agent consumption as the new growth engine** — Supabase (>60% DBs by AI tools)[^supabase-f], Databricks (>80% growth)[^cnbc-dbx-aug26], Temporal[^temporal-e]. See [AI capture of COSS funding](/projects/coss-market/ai-capture-of-coss-funding.md).
2. **Consolidation by incumbents** — IBM (HashiCorp, Confluent), Nvidia (HF), Databricks/Snowflake/Supabase (Postgres)[^ibm-confluent-close][^nvidia-hf][^yf-neon]. See [COSS M&A](/projects/coss-market/coss-ma-2024-2026.md).
3. **AI labs internalize OSS toolchains** — Bun, Astral, Stainless, Continue[^bun-anthropic][^astral-openai]. See [AI-lab acquisitions](/projects/coss-market/ai-lab-devtool-acquisitions.md).
4. **Private for longer** — no US COSS IPO; Databricks/Grafana/ClickHouse/Supabase raise privately while public mid-caps get bought[^cb-h1-2026].
5. **License pendulum swings back** — Elastic and Redis to AGPL; fair source accepted; BSL associated with forks and sale[^redis-agpl]. See [Business models](/projects/coss-market/business-models.md).
6. **AI-induced monetization failure** — docs/seat models (Tailwind, GitLab) under pressure[^eweek-tw][^tc-gitlab-cuts].
7. **Open source as industrial policy** — EU €2B strategy, Sovereign Tech Fund; value gap ($8.8T vs $7.7B/yr investment) as rationale[^tpp-eu][^oss-funding-2024].

# Success patterns
- Usage-priced managed service on a permissive or AGPL core, easy for agents to provision programmatically.
- Positioning the OSS project in the hot path of AI workloads (databases, durable execution, runtimes).
- Raising from sovereign/crossover funds (GIC, T. Rowe) to stay private and avoid volatile public markets.
- For exit: enterprise distribution value to IBM-like buyers, or strategic dependency for AI labs.

# Failure patterns
- Seat-based pricing in categories where AI agents replace human seats (GitLab).
- Monetizing human attention (docs, templates) rather than machine usage (Tailwind).
- Sub-scale public listings without an AI growth narrative → take-private or takeover (Couchbase, Confluent, HashiCorp).
- Leadership instability in an AI talent war (MongoDB).
