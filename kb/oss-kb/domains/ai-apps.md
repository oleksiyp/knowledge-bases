# Executive summary
- **Adoption boomed, licences narrowed.** The top AI-app repos are now among GitHub's largest (Open WebUI ~154k stars, ComfyUI ~136k, RAGFlow ~92k, LobeHub ~83k)[^owui-gh][^gnw-comfy], but the category leader Open WebUI left OSI open source in April 2025 with a branding clause + CLA[^owui-license-docs], and copycats followed: LobeHub/FastGPT "Apache + conditions", SurfSense's BSL carve-out (Jul 2026) and Screenpipe's full source-available switch (Jun 2026)[^ss-gh][^sp-license].
- **The money went to cloud add-ons on top of open engines** — Comfy Org's $17M (Sep 2025) and $30M at $500M (Apr 2026), monetised via Comfy Cloud and Comfy API (Sep 2026)[^comfy-17m][^gnw-comfy][^comfy-api] — and to an aggregator-reported Open WebUI Series B (Jul 2026, unconfirmed)[^caplight-owui].
- **Exits came from data-infra buyers, not IPOs:** ClickHouse bought LibreChat (Nov 2025) and Langfuse (Jan 2026), both kept MIT[^bw-lc][^lf-join]; OpenAI bought Promptfoo (Mar 2026)[^pf-blog]; Adobe absorbed Invoke's team (Oct 2025)[^adobe-foundry].
- **2023's pioneers went silent:** AUTOMATIC1111 (no main-branch commits since Jul 2024), GPT4All (no commits since May 2025), Fooocus (LTS), Quivr (pivoted), h2oGPT (archived), Tabby (stalled), Khoj (cloud shut Apr 2026)[^a1111-gh][^g4a-gh][^quivr-pivot][^khoj-sunset][^tabby-gh].
- **Counter-trend — some went *more* open:** Jan relicensed AGPL → Apache-2.0 (May 2025), Langfuse moved most paid features to MIT (Jun 2025), Open Interpreter relaunched as Apache-2.0 Rust (2026), Zylon merged its private fork into PrivateGPT 1.0 (Jun 2026)[^jan-gh][^lf-oss][^oi-pq][^pgpt-10].
- **Plugin ecosystems became an attack surface:** ComfyUI registry malware (Jan 2026) and a 1,000+-instance botnet (Apr 2026)[^comfy-upscaler-pm][^thn-comfy-botnet].
- **Voice and video openness is retreating at the frontier:** Microsoft pulled VibeVoice TTS code after misuse (Sep 2025); Alibaba kept Wan 2.5/2.6 closed after open Wan 2.1/2.2[^vibevoice-byteiota][^wan25-guide].

# Trends
1. **"Branding/SaaS-carve-out" licences are the AI-app default.** Open WebUI (branding >50 users), LobeHub and FastGPT (no derivative/multi-tenant SaaS), SurfSense (BSL dir) and Screenpipe (commercial use paid) show source-available terms arriving at seed stage, not late stage[^owui-license-docs][^sp-license][^ss-gh]. See [/events/2025-04-open-webui-branding-license.md](/events/2025-04-open-webui-branding-license.md).
2. **Infra companies buy the app layer.** ClickHouse (LibreChat, Langfuse), OpenAI (Promptfoo), Adobe (Invoke team), NVIDIA (Hugging Face → Gradio, whisper.cpp) — the AI-app layer is being absorbed as distribution for data/model platforms[^bw-lc][^lf-join][^pf-blog][^adobe-foundry].
3. **Engine + cloud beats polished closed UX.** ComfyUI's GPL engine with Comfy Cloud/API won generative media, while UX-first apps (A1111, Fooocus, Invoke's SaaS) faded[^gnw-comfy][^a1111-gh].
4. **Silent abandonment of 2023 hits.** GPT4All, A1111, Fooocus, Quivr, Tabby — big star counts, dead repos; stars are a lagging indicator[^g4a-gh][^tabby-gh].
5. **Frontier labs eat consumer "second brain" apps.** Khoj's cloud and Quivr's original product died as ChatGPT/Claude added memory, connectors and research modes[^khoj-sunset][^quivr-pivot]; the 2026 breakout instead was private/self-hosted clones (Open Notebook)[^on-gh].
6. **Plugin supply-chain risk moves into AI apps.** ComfyUI node malware and botnets mirror npm/PyPI attacks[^comfy-upscaler-pm][^thn-comfy-botnet].
7. **Chinese teams supply a large share of top apps** (LobeHub, Cherry Studio, RAGFlow, FastGPT, MaxKB, LightRAG, ACE-Step), mostly with dual or conditioned licences.
8. **Frontier voice/video openness retreats** (VibeVoice pulled, Wan 2.5/2.6 closed) even as smaller open models (Kokoro, Chatterbox, HunyuanVideo 1.5, ACE-Step) improve[^vibevoice-byteiota][^wan25-guide][^hunyuan15].

# Success patterns
- **Day-0 support for new models + extensibility** (ComfyUI, Open WebUI, Cherry Studio) keeps users as models churn.
- **Monetise hosting/enterprise, not the local app**: Comfy Cloud/API, Onyx `ee/`, Langfuse Cloud, Open WebUI enterprise licences.
- **Permissive core that a bigger platform wants**: LibreChat and Langfuse kept MIT and were acquired at high strategic value.
- **Upstream-first alignment**: PrivateGPT 1.0 merging the private fork; Langfuse opening paid features.
- **Clean handovers** (Chainlit maintainers, InvokeAI volunteers, Idiap's Coqui fork) preserve user value after company failure[^cl-pypi].

# Failure patterns
- **Being tied to one model family or engine** (Fooocus/SDXL, A1111) as architectures moved to Flux/video.
- **Consumer SaaS on a horizontal OSS app** with no moat against frontier labs (Khoj, Quivr).
- **Vendor-side "open demos"** of a closed product (h2oGPT, GPT4All under Nomic) abandoned once marketing value fades.
- **Feature-level wedges** overtaken by paradigm shifts (Tabby's self-hosted autocomplete vs agentic coding).
- **Non-commercial model licences** that become unusable when the licensor dies (Coqui XTTS).
- Closed competitors show the alternative path: Msty and LM Studio stayed proprietary-but-free and compete on polish[^msty-review].
