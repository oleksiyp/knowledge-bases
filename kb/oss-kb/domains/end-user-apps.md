# Executive summary
- **Geopolitics became the biggest growth driver for end-user OSS.** US sanctions-era fears (the ICC's Microsoft lockout and switch to openDesk[^icc]), state migrations (Schleswig-Holstein: ~€15M/yr savings[^sh]; Denmark's digital ministry) and the EU's June 2026 Open Source Strategy[^ec-oss] turned "sovereignty" into revenue: Nextcloud reported >2M new seats and >50% bookings growth in 2025 and a €250M "Sovereignty 2030" plan[^nc-momentum][^nc-2030].
- **The Linux desktop had its best two years ever, driven by gaming, not office users.** Windows 10's end of support (Oct 2025) and SteamOS/Proton maturity pushed Linux to a record 5.33% of Steam users (Mar 2026), though it fell back to ~4% by July[^steam-mar26][^steam-jul26]. Winners were new, opinionated distros (CachyOS, Bazzite, Omarchy) and KDE Plasma, whose fundraiser hit 276% of goal[^kde-fund].
- **Self-hosting matured into mainstream products.** Immich went stable (v2.0, Oct 2025) and reached ~115k stars[^immich-v2]; Home Assistant doubled to 2M installs by Apr 2025 and claimed 2.7M+ households by Oct 2026, funded by its own nonprofit/commercial combo with no VC[^ha-2m][^ha-home].
- **Browsers: survival, not growth, for the independents.** Mozilla's revenue held (audited 2024: $680.4M, ~86% from one customer, expenses $588.2M) only because the Sept 2025 remedy kept search payments legal[^moz-audit][^mozfin][^remedy]; its AI pivot drew backlash but shipped opt-in with a kill switch. Ladybird is the credible new engine, donor-funded.
- **Governance was the main failure mode.** The Document Foundation expelled ~30 Collabora-affiliated members (Apr 2026)[^tdf-lwn]; Organic Maps was forked into CoMaps; Asahi, Jellyfin and GNOME lost leaders to burnout or churn.
- **Open-core tightened and one flagship left OSS on AI-security grounds.** Cal.com went closed source in April 2026, arguing AI vulnerability scanning makes open code dangerous[^calcom]; Mattermost and Bitwarden restricted free tiers or raised prices.
- **Mobile openness is under platform attack.** Google's Android Developer Verification began enforcement on 30 Sept 2026 in four countries[^adv], threatening F-Droid; the EU DMA (e.g., forcing Android wake-word openness for Home Assistant) is the counterweight[^dma-android].
- **AI money is now a values test.** Blender converted an Anthropic patronage to a one-off donation after backlash[^blender-ai]; Omarchy raised ~$18.5M in pledges and AI-token credits in weeks yet saw patron churn[^omarchy]; Zulip's founder left for Anthropic after moving the company under a nonprofit[^zulip].

# Trends
1. **Sovereignty as a sales channel.** Public-sector migrations (ICC, Schleswig-Holstein, Denmark, Lyon, Austria, French education) and the EU strategy created real demand for Nextcloud, LibreOffice, Euro-Office, Matrix/Element and Thunderbird. See [ICC/openDesk](/events/2025-10-icc-drops-microsoft-for-opendesk.md), [EU strategy](/events/2026-06-eu-tech-sovereignty-package-open-source-strategy.md), [Nextcloud](/projects/end-user-apps/nextcloud.md).
2. **Gaming as the Linux desktop's Trojan horse.** Valve's SteamOS/Steam Machine and Proton made gaming the driver; desktop winners were gaming-first distros and KDE. See [Windows 10 EOL](/events/2025-10-windows-10-end-of-support.md), [Valve](/organizations/valve.md), [CachyOS](/projects/end-user-apps/cachyos.md), [Bazzite](/projects/end-user-apps/bazzite.md).
3. **Office-suite fragmentation.** Instead of consolidating, the "sovereign office" split four ways (LibreOffice/TDF, Collabora's new product, Euro-Office, OnlyOffice) amid public feuds. See [TDF event](/events/2026-04-tdf-expels-collabora-members.md), [Euro-Office](/events/2026-06-euro-office-onlyoffice-fork.md).
4. **AI as both product pressure and governance flashpoint.** Firefox's AI pivot needed a kill switch; Blender rejected an AI-lab patronage; LibreOffice marketed "no AI"; Cal.com used AI-security to justify closing; Ladybird and Omarchy openly build with AI agents. See [Cal.com event](/events/2026-04-cal-com-goes-closed-source.md), [Firefox](/projects/end-user-apps/firefox.md), [Blender](/projects/end-user-apps/blender.md).
5. **Open-core tightening in end-user SaaS alternatives.** Cal.com (closed), Mattermost (limits), Bitwarden (prices), PostHog (ad-platform data sharing) — while fully-open, nonprofit-anchored peers (Zulip, Home Assistant) gained goodwill. See [Mattermost](/projects/end-user-apps/mattermost.md), [Bitwarden](/projects/end-user-apps/bitwarden.md).
6. **Platform gatekeeping vs. regulation.** Google's developer verification vs. EU DMA rulings define the room for F-Droid, GrapheneOS and Home Assistant on Android. See [ADV event](/events/2026-09-android-developer-verification-enforcement.md), [F-Droid](/projects/end-user-apps/f-droid.md).
7. **Supply-chain attacks reach end-user ecosystems.** AUR (1,500+ packages)[^aur], Bitwarden CLI, PostHog npm packages. See [AUR event](/events/2026-06-arch-aur-malware.md).
8. **Nonprofit restructurings and founder exits.** Mastodon (European nonprofit, CEO change), Zulip (foundation owns company), Asahi (board), Bluesky (CEO change), Jellyfin (leader burnout), GNOME (ED churn).

# Success patterns
- **Mission-locked ownership plus a real revenue engine**: Home Assistant (Open Home Foundation + Nabu Casa), Nextcloud (employee-owned, profitable), Zulip (foundation-owned company).
- **Excellent UX that directly replaces a Big Tech service**: Immich (Google Photos), Home Assistant (cloud smart home), Penpot (Figma), Brave (Chrome).
- **Riding a platform shift someone else funds**: KDE/CachyOS/Bazzite on Valve's Linux push; Nextcloud/Element on EU procurement.
- **Asking users directly**: KDE's in-desktop donation prompt quintupled its fundraiser; Thunderbird's appeals sustain a team.
- **Collaboration over fragmentation**: the Open Gaming Collective pooled kernels and input stacks.

# Failure patterns
- **Founder/leader burnout without succession** (Asahi, Jellyfin, GNOME ED churn).
- **Foundation governance entangled with commercial contributors** (TDF/Collabora), or **owners monetizing a community project** (Organic Maps → CoMaps).
- **Dependency on a single payer or platform** (Mozilla/Google; F-Droid/Android; Matrix Foundation/Element).
- **Retroactive restrictions on self-hosted users** (Mattermost message limits) and **license ambiguity** (Bitwarden SDK, OnlyOffice badgeware) — both invite forks.
- **Engagement without monetization** in VC-backed social (Bluesky).
- **Security debt in fast-growing community repos** (AUR, Omarchy).

- Bitwarden under new leadership: further price/licensing changes, or reconciliation with the community? Vaultwarden demand as a signal.
- Ladybird alpha release timing (planned 2026) and whether it reopens to outside contributions.
- Bluesky's first meaningful revenue product under permanent CEO Toni Schneider (since Jul 10, 2026); Mastodon funding under its new nonprofit.
