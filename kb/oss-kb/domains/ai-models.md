# Executive summary
- **China won open-model adoption.** Chinese open models passed US ones in cumulative Hugging Face downloads by Aug 2025 (1.15B vs 723M by Mar 2026); Qwen passed Llama in Sept 2025 and accounted for 69% of new derivatives by Feb 2026; Chinese models took >70% of OpenRouter tokens by Jan 2026[^atom-report]. ~80% of open-model-based Silicon Valley startups reportedly use Chinese models[^mittr-china].
- **DeepSeek-R1 (Jan 2025) was the defining shock**: MIT weights, near-frontier reasoning, and a ~$590–600B one-day Nvidia drawdown[^techspot-nvda]. DeepSeek then raised its first outside money — ~RMB50B (~$7.4B) at ~$52B post-money (June–July 2026) — and hit a ~$1B revenue run rate by Sept 2026[^caixin-ds-52b][^pymnts-ds-1b].
- **Open weights became an IPO story.** Zhipu (8 Jan 2026) and MiniMax (9 Jan 2026, +109% day one) listed in Hong Kong before any US frontier lab[^cnbc-zhipu-ipo][^scmp-minimax]; Moonshot went $4.3B → $20B (May 2026) → $35B (Jul 2026) and confidentially filed for a ~$3B HK IPO (Sept 2026)[^tc-moonshot][^bbg-moonshot-35b][^reuters-moonshot-ipo]; Zhipu's market cap briefly topped HK$1T (~$128B) in June 2026[^caixin-zhipu-1t].
- **Meta left the open frontier.** Llama 4 (Apr 2025) flopped; Muse Spark (8 Apr 2026) shipped closed[^vb-muse-spark]; Meta returned only with the small Apache-2.0 Muse Glimmer 30B (Aug 2026), while its promised Muse Spark 1.2 open weights had not shipped by Oct[^infoq-glimmer][^wiki-muse].
- **The US counter-offensive is real but smaller:** OpenAI's gpt-oss (Aug 2025, Apache-2.0)[^openai-gptoss], Gemma 4 relicensed to Apache-2.0 (Apr 2026; Gemma passed 1B downloads in Aug)[^google-gemma4], NVIDIA Nemotron 3 (Ultra under the Linux Foundation's OpenMDW-1.1)[^mtp-nemotron-ultra], Thinking Machines' Apache-2.0 Inkling (built on DeepSeek/Kimi foundations)[^ghacks-inkling], and heavily funded Reflection AI (~$25B; still no public model as of Oct 2026).
- **Europe's open-weight champion is a business success:** Mistral raised a €3B Series D at €21B (~$24B) post-money (Sept 2026)[^tc-mistral-d]; Black Forest Labs (FLUX) reached $3.25B[^tc-bfl]; Cohere put Command A+ under Apache-2.0 and agreed to combine with Aleph Alpha (~$20B)[^vb-command-a-plus].
- **Licensing is bifurcating**: big-tech US models liberalised (Gemma 4, Muse Glimmer → Apache-2.0) while 2026 Chinese/European flagships added revenue gates (Kimi K3 License: $20M MaaS threshold; Qwen3.8-Max License: $50M; Mistral Medium 3.5 Modified MIT)[^k3-license][^qwen38-license].
- **Consolidation of infrastructure:** NVIDIA agreed to buy Hugging Face for $12.93B (announced 3 Sept 2026; close expected H1 2027)[^tc-nvda-hf].

# Trends
1. **Geographic flip of open-model leadership (US → China).** Evidence: ATOM download and derivative data[^atom-report]; OpenRouter share; US startups and labs (Cursor Composer 2, Inkling) built on Kimi/DeepSeek[^wiki-moonshot][^wiki-tml]. See [Qwen](/projects/ai-models/qwen.md), [DeepSeek](/projects/ai-models/deepseek.md).
2. **Open weights as a capital-markets asset.** HK IPOs of Zhipu/MiniMax, DeepSeek and Moonshot mega-rounds, Mistral €21B — openness is now valued as distribution, talent and policy leverage, not charity. See [HK IPOs](/events/2026-01-zhipu-minimax-hong-kong-ipos.md).
3. **License bifurcation.** Apache-2.0 liberalisation by US big tech (Gemma 4, Muse Glimmer) vs revenue-gated flagship licenses in 2026 (Kimi K3, Qwen3.8-2.4T, Mistral Medium 3.5). See [license tightening](/events/2026-07-kimi-k3-and-flagship-license-tightening.md).
4. **Openness is conditional on not being the frontier leader.** Meta opened when behind OpenAI, closed when it rebuilt for the frontier; OpenAI opened a sub-frontier model; Chinese labs gate their biggest models. See [Muse Spark](/events/2026-04-meta-muse-spark-closed-pivot.md).
5. **Hardware sovereignty.** GLM-5 and DeepSeek V4 trained/served on Huawei Ascend[^reuters-v4]; NVIDIA counters by funding open models (Nemotron, Reflection, Thinking Machines) and buying Hugging Face.
6. **Provenance & distillation as the new battleground.** Anthropic accusations (Feb, Sept 2026), US congressional pressure on Kimi users. See [distillation event](/events/2026-02-anthropic-distillation-accusations.md).
7. **"Open source" vs "open weights".** Only Ai2's OLMo (and partly Nemotron's open data) approach the OSI definition; the EU AI Act gives the label legal weight. See [OSAID](/events/2024-10-osi-open-source-ai-definition.md), [EU AI Act](/events/2025-08-eu-ai-act-gpai-obligations.md).
8. **Open-core in generative media.** FLUX's tiered licensing thrived; Stability survived by pivoting to licensed data and industry partners. See [FLUX](/projects/ai-models/flux.md).

# Success patterns
- **Cadence + full size ladder + permissive license** (Qwen, DeepSeek, GLM) beats occasional large drops.
- **Agentic/coding specialisation** (Kimi, GLM, MiniMax M-series) captured paid API demand via OpenRouter and coding tools.
- **Efficiency innovations shipped as weights** (MoE, MLA, sparse attention) became industry standards and brand.
- **Sovereignty narrative** (Mistral, Cohere+Aleph Alpha, Chinese domestic chips) unlocked state and strategic capital.
- **Open-core tiering** (FLUX: Apache small / non-commercial dev / paid pro) monetised without alienating the community.

# Failure patterns
- **Benchmark gaming and stale releases** (Llama 4 LMArena episode; Grok 2.5) destroy trust.
- **Restrictive custom licenses** (Llama Community License, Gemma ToU until 2026, Cohere CC-BY-NC until Command A+) suppressed derivatives relative to MIT/Apache rivals.
- **Single-vendor dependency**: when Meta's strategy changed, Llama's ecosystem was orphaned.
- **Capability squeeze on first movers** (Stability AI) without a monetisation tier.
- **Thin revenue under rich valuations** (Zhipu, MiniMax losses) — a latent risk if the HK market turns.
