# Executive summary
- **Open standards won; open hardware businesses split.** RISC-V matured into a commercial architecture: RVA23 was ratified in Oct 2024, SiFive was valued at $3.65B in Apr 2026, and Qualcomm bought Ventana in Dec 2025.[^rvi-rva23][^sifive-g][^reg-ventana] OpenTitan became the first commercial-grade open secure chip in production (Chromebooks, Mar 2026).[^google-ot-prod]
- **Foundation-hosted embedded software is the healthiest layer.** Zephyr (930+ contributors per release), NuttX, OpenWrt (25.12 with apk, 2,200+ devices), ROS 2 (Lyrical LTS) and PX4 all grew steadily with no license drama.[^z-44][^hns-2512][^lyrical][^px4-117]
- **The AI-driven DRAM crunch was 2026's biggest shock.** It turned Raspberry Pi's 2025 memory stockpile into a 90% H1 revenue jump, while Pine64 paused all Linux devices until mid-2027 and Framework's CEO warned of "real financial risk".[^tnw-h1][^hwb-pine64][^toms-fw-ram]
- **Chip and platform vendors are buying or open-sourcing the "physical AI" stack.** Qualcomm bought Arduino, Ventana and PickNik/MoveIt; NVIDIA agreed to buy Hugging Face (owner of LeRobot and Pollen); Google's Intrinsic open-sourced Intrinsic Core.[^reg-ventana][^robotreport-picknik][^nv-hf-blog][^intrinsic-core]
- **Open autopilots became defense infrastructure.** ArduPilot guided Operation Spiderweb (Jun 2025). PX4-based Auterion became profitable on Ukraine contracts and was reportedly raising at more than $1.2B (Mar 2026).[^404-spiderweb][^resilience-auterion]
- **Capital-intensive open hardware failed** against Chinese scale: Efabless shut down (Mar 2025), K-Scale Labs shut down (Nov 2025), and Prusa declared open-hardware 3D printing "dead" and moved to a non-OSI license.[^tt-efabless][^kalil-kscale][^hackaday-dead][^prusa-ocl]
- **Regulation cut both ways.** The EU abandoned the RED "radio lockdown" act (Jan 2026), while the US FCC put all new foreign-made drones (Dec 2025) and consumer routers (Mar 2026) on its Covered List.[^fsfe-red][^fcc-drones][^bm-fcc-routers]

# Trends
1. **Open standard, proprietary implementation.** The money is in IP and silicon built on open specs: RISC-V cores (SiFive), ESP32 chips, OpenTitan parts from Nuvoton.[^sifive-g][^esp-ar-2025][^google-ot-prod]
2. **Chip vendors buying open communities.** Qualcomm (Arduino, Ventana, Modular, PickNik), NVIDIA (Hugging Face/LeRobot), Siemens (OpenROAD steward). Each pledges openness, and trust is the open question.[^robotreport-picknik][^nv-hf-blog][^eng-siemens-pi]
3. **The AI-induced component crunch.** DRAM diverted to AI datacenters reversed open hardware's cost advantage. Scale and inventory, not openness, decided the winners.[^tnw-h1][^hwb-pine64][^geerling-dram]
4. **Geopolitics and trade policy as the main external force.** US Covered-List bans on foreign drones and routers, tariffs (Adafruit), China's state RISC-V push and CSIS warnings, and Chinese patent tactics in 3D printing.[^fcc-drones][^bm-fcc-routers][^csis-riscv][^hackaday-dead]
5. **Dual-use and defense money.** Open autopilots power the Ukraine war and Auterion has reached a unicorn-track valuation.[^404-spiderweb][^resilience-auterion]
6. **Memory-safe and AI-native embedded.** Vendor-backed Rust (esp-hal 1.0, Embassy), learned-simulation driving (openpilot 0.11), and community-data robot models (SmolVLA).[^esp-hal-1][^comma-011]
7. **Industrial consortia as OSS.** Automotive (S-CORE, Autoware), datacenter (Caliptra, OpenBMC, OCP's 11,000-attendee summit) and telecom (Duranta open RAN) use open source as shared, pre-competitive infrastructure.[^vda-mou][^ami-openbmc][^ocp-2025][^duranta]
8. **Copyleft as leverage in hardware.** AGPL lineage (PrusaSlicer → Bambu Studio → OrcaSlicer) gave the SFC and the community a lever against device lock-down.[^sfc-bambu]

# Success patterns
- **A neutral host plus multiple competing sponsors** (Zephyr, Dronecode, Autoware, RISC-V International, CHIPS Alliance) keeps projects stable through vendor turbulence.
- **Open software/SDK, monetised hardware** (Espressif, Raspberry Pi, comma.ai, Flipper) is the most reliable business model.
- **Supply-chain scale and foresight** (Raspberry Pi's memory hedge) beat purity during the crunch.[^tnw-h1]
- **Hyperscaler-funded verification** makes open silicon commercially credible (OpenTitan, Caliptra).[^google-ot-prod]
- **Resilience through open toolchains and PDKs**: Tiny Tapeout and LibreLane survived Efabless's collapse.[^tt-efabless]

# Failure patterns
- **Capital-intensive open hardware without a platform parent** (Efabless, K-Scale) dies when VC dries up or Chinese rivals scale.[^tt-efabless][^kalil-kscale]
- **Selling near cost with no buffer** (Pine64) is fatal in component shocks.[^hwb-pine64]
- **Open hardware designs are easily cloned and patented around**, which pushed Prusa to source-available terms.[^prusa-ocl]
- **Device-side lock-down** (Bambu authorisation firmware) neutralises open desktop software.[^sfc-bambu]
- **Community trust incidents** (Arduino ToS, Framework sponsorship, the ESP32 "backdoor" scare) erode goodwill even when the code stays open.[^reg-fw-controversy]
- **Single-vendor stewards being acquired** (PickNik, Precision Innovations, Hugging Face) put roadmaps at the mercy of the acquirer.
