# Net assessment

The strongest window for new OSS momentum: OpenClaw, the vLLM and SGLang spinouts, ClickHouse, and the Hong Kong IPOs. It is also when **AI's negative externalities on OSS businesses became undeniable**: Tailwind's layoffs, curl's bounty, AI-assisted supply-chain attacks and AI relicensing.
