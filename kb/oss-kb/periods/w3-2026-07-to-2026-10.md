**Peak consolidation.** In three months, five OSS-company acquisitions were announced or closed, and the largest COSS deal ever was announced: NVIDIA agreed to buy Hugging Face for ~$12.9B.[^nvidia-hf] Private valuations hit records. At the same time, more single-vendor projects closed down, were sunset, or decayed after acquisition.

# Net assessment

The business side was hot: mega-rounds and deals in every AI-adjacent layer. The OSS side was polarized. Neutral, foundation-held projects (DuckDB, Ray, MCP/A2A, OpenTofu, Valkey) thrived through ownership changes. Single-vendor projects were sunset or closed (Flowise, CockroachDB, Tailwind's independence). See [Executive summary](/executive-summary.md).
