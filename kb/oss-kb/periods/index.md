# Period reviews (newest first)

* [W3 — last 3 months (2026-07-03 → 2026-10-03)](w3-2026-07-to-2026-10.md) - The hottest and most consolidating quarter of the two years. NVIDIA agreed to buy Hugging Face, Databricks reached $190B, Temporal, Mistral and Moonshot raised mega-rounds, and AWS bought DuckLabs. On the failure side: CockroachDB closed its source, Flowise was sunset, the keyv supply-chain wave hit, Automattic's board crisis erupted, and MongoDB's CEO left.
* [W6 — 3 to 6 months ago (2026-04-03 → 2026-07-03)](w6-2026-04-to-2026-07.md) - The quarter of strong private markets and weak public COSS companies. Supabase was valued at $10.5B, SAP invested in n8n at $5.2B, Cloudflare bought VoidZero, Qualcomm agreed to buy Modular, the EU published an open source strategy with €2B, and OpenTelemetry graduated. Meta shipped a closed model, Roo Code and MinIO shut down, GitLab cut 14%, and Mini Shai-Hulud hit TanStack.
* [W9 — 6 to 9 months ago (2026-01-03 → 2026-04-03)](w9-2026-01-to-2026-04.md) - OpenClaw's viral breakout, the first open-weight IPOs (Zhipu, MiniMax), OpenAI buying Astral, IBM closing Confluent, ClickHouse at $15B, the vLLM and SGLang spinouts, and Gemma 4 moving to Apache-2.0. On the failure side: Tailwind Labs laid off 75%, Deno had layoffs, curl ended its bug bounty, the Trivy-to-LiteLLM and axios compromises hit, MinIO was abandoned, and the OSI paused its elections.
* [W12 — 9 to 12 months ago (2025-10-03 → 2026-01-03)](w12-2025-10-to-2026-01.md) - Consolidation began in earnest: IBM–Confluent, Anthropic–Bun, Palo Alto–Chronosphere, Akamai–Fermyon and Cloudflare–Replicate. The Agentic AI Foundation launched with MCP, and n8n, LangChain and Supabase hit unicorn-plus valuations. Shai-Hulud 2.0 and React2Shell hit, ingress-nginx's retirement was announced, MinIO went into maintenance mode, and the PSF withdrew from its NSF grant.
* [W24 — 1 to 2 years ago (2024-10-03 → 2025-10-03)](w24-2024-10-to-2025-10.md) - The year that set the agenda: the DeepSeek-R1 shock, MCP's launch and adoption, Redis's return to AGPL, IBM closing HashiCorp, Databricks–Neon and Snowflake–Crunchy, the Llama 4 flop, gpt-oss, the WP Engine war, Bitnami's paywall, the CVE funding crisis, tj-actions and Shai-Hulud, and Couchbase going private.
