# End-user apps projects

* [Arch Linux](arch-linux.md) - Rolling-release community distro that became the base of the Linux gaming/desktop boom (SteamOS, CachyOS, Omarchy), but suffered a major AUR malware incident (1,500+ packages) in June 2026.
* [Asahi Linux](asahi-linux.md) - Linux on Apple Silicon; survived the Feb 2025 resignation of founder Hector Martin by moving to a seven-person board and Open Collective funding, and by mid-2026 was bringing up M3 hardware.
* [Audacity](audacity.md) - Muse Group-stewarded GPL audio editor; after years of trust repair, shipped Audacity 4.0 (Sept 2026), a full Qt UI rebuild with a new clip model, plus OpenVINO AI effects (2025).
* [Bazzite](bazzite.md) - Universal Blue's immutable Fedora-Atomic gaming distro; survived a 2025 existential threat from Fedora's 32-bit drop proposal and in 2026 anchored the new Open Gaming Collective, becoming one of the most popular new distros.
* [Bitwarden (and Vaultwarden)](bitwarden.md) - Open-source password manager whose 'open' credentials eroded: 2024 SDK license scare, Jan 2026 price doubling, leadership turnover, a supply-chain compromise of its CLI and removal of 'Always free' messaging; the community Rust server Vaultwarden keeps thriving.
* [Blender](blender.md) - GPL 3D suite funded by the Blender Development Fund; shipped Blender 5.0 (Nov 2025) and 5.2 LTS (July 2026), added Netflix Animation as Corporate Patron, and reversed an Anthropic patronage into a one-off donation after community backlash over AI.
* [Bluesky / AT Protocol](bluesky-atproto.md) - VC-backed open-protocol social network; surged past 30M users in early 2025 and 43M+ by Mar 2026 with a $100M Series B (Bain Capital Crypto), but mobile daily activity fell ~40% from its March 2025 peak and founding CEO Jay Graber stepped aside in 2026 with monetization still unresolved.
* [Brave](brave.md) - MPL-2.0 Chromium-based privacy browser from Brave Software; passed 100M monthly active users in Oct 2025, the commercial success story among Chromium forks.
* [CachyOS](cachyos.md) - Performance-tuned Arch-based distro that became the most-used Linux distro among Steam/ProtonDB gamers in 2025–26; a breakout community success of the Windows-10-EOL and Linux-gaming wave.
* [Cal.com (Cal.diy)](cal-com.md) - Formerly the flagship open-source scheduling platform; on 14 April 2026 Cal.com took its production codebase closed, citing AI-accelerated vulnerability discovery, leaving an MIT community edition 'Cal.diy' for non-production self-hosting.
* [CoMaps (Organic Maps fork)](comaps.md) - Community-governed fork of Organic Maps created in 2025 after disputes over KAYAK affiliate links, a hidden server component and shareholder control; by 2026 it had its own momentum, including use by rescuers in Venezuela.
* [F-Droid](f-droid.md) - The FOSS Android app repository; faces an existential threat from Google's Android Developer Verification (enforced from 30 Sept 2026 in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand), while modernizing its build servers and shipping the redesigned F-Droid 2.0 client.
* [Firefox](firefox.md) - Mozilla's Gecko-based browser; OSS engineering remains strong (last major browser keeping full uBlock Origin support) but trust and share eroded amid a ToU backlash, product cuts and an AI pivot, while Google search money (~86% of revenue) survived the US antitrust remedy.
* [GNOME](gnome.md) - The GNOME desktop; technically steady but its Foundation endured deficits, a halved budget and executive-director churn (two EDs gone within a year), now trying a donor-funded Fellowship program.
* [GrapheneOS](grapheneos.md) - Hardened Android OS; grew to ~400k users and in March 2026 signed its first OEM partnership (Motorola) to break its Pixel-only dependence, while relocating infrastructure out of France and becoming a flashpoint in border-search law.
* [Home Assistant](home-assistant.md) - Local-first smart-home platform owned by the Open Home Foundation and funded by Nabu Casa's cloud/hardware sales; doubled to 2M active installs by 2025 and keeps winning, including an EU DMA decision forcing Android wake-word openness in 2026.
* [Immich](immich.md) - Self-hosted Google Photos alternative funded by FUTO; went from beta to stable v2.0 (Oct 2025) and v3.0 (July 2026), reaching ~115k GitHub stars — the breakout self-hosting app of the period.
* [Jellyfin](jellyfin.md) - Volunteer-run, GPL media server (Plex/Emby alternative); delivered the big 10.11 database refactor and Jellyfin 12.0, but lost its project leader and core members to burnout in July 2026.
* [KDE Plasma](kde-plasma.md) - KDE's desktop environment; the default desktop of SteamOS and the gaming distro wave, with record donations (2025 year-end fundraiser €276K vs €100K goal) and a decisive move to Wayland-only in Plasma 6.8.
* [Ladybird](ladybird.md) - Independent from-scratch web browser engine run by a donor-funded nonprofit; strong technical momentum (90%+ WPT parity milestone, Rust adoption) and corporate sponsors, but closed to outside PRs in 2026 ahead of its planned alpha.
* [LibreOffice](libreoffice.md) - The Document Foundation's office suite; adoption boomed with European government migrations (Denmark, Schleswig-Holstein) and 'no AI' positioning, but governance imploded in April 2026 when TDF expelled ~30 Collabora-affiliated members and Collabora announced a cut-down fork.
* [Logseq](logseq.md) - AGPL local-first outliner; after a long DB-version rewrite it split into maintenance-only 'Logseq OG' (Markdown) and a database-based Logseq whose 2.0 beta shipped in July 2026.
* [Mastodon](mastodon.md) - AGPL fediverse server; restructured into a European nonprofit (2025), founder Eugen Rochko stepped down as CEO (Nov 2025, Felix Hlatky succeeding), and won €614K from the Sovereign Tech Fund in 2026 — stable but no longer growing fast.
* [Matrix / Element](matrix-element.md) - Federated secure-messaging protocol and its main commercial implementer Element; government adoption broadened (~35 countries in talks, Bundeswehr, France, ICC via openDesk) but the Matrix.org Foundation ran a 2024 deficit and threatened to shut bridges in 2025.
* [Mattermost](mattermost.md) - AGPL/MIT open-core team chat; v11 (2025) cut the free Team Edition to 250 users and enforced a 10,000-message history limit in the free tier, triggering backlash and Framasoft's 'Mostlymatter' fork.
* [Nextcloud](nextcloud.md) - AGPL self-hosted collaboration suite and the biggest commercial winner of Europe's digital-sovereignty push: 50%+ bookings growth in 2025, 2M new professional users, a €250M 'Sovereignty 2030' plan, and leadership of the Euro-Office fork.
* [Omarchy](omarchy.md) - DHH's opinionated Arch + Hyprland setup turned distro; viral growth (1.2M ISO downloads in year one) and ~$18.5M pledged to its Omacom Foundation by Sept 2026, but dogged by security criticism and political backlash.
* [OnlyOffice](onlyoffice.md) - AGPL web/desktop office suite from Ascensio System SIA; won sovereignty deals (Lyon) but in 2026 was forked by Nextcloud/IONOS as Euro-Office, ended its Nextcloud partnership, and saw its logo-retention ('badgeware') license terms challenged by SFC.
* [Penpot](penpot.md) - MPL-2.0 open-source Figma alternative by Kaleidos; ~60k GitHub stars, a new rendering engine, an MCP server for AI agents, an 'Open Nitrate' governance-based business model and a €6.9M round with Spanish state backing (July 2026).
* [PostHog](posthog.md) - Open-core product-analytics/dev-tools suite (MIT core + proprietary ee); the domain's VC growth story — $70M Series D (June 2025) and $75M Series E at $1.4B (Sept 2025) — while expanding into AI coding tools and data warehousing.
* [Signal](signal.md) - AGPL end-to-end encrypted messenger run by the nonprofit Signal Foundation; usage and political salience rose (Signalgate, ~70M MAU) and it shipped secure backups, but it runs a structural deficit ($29.4M revenue vs $38.0M expenses in 2024).
* [Thunderbird](thunderbird.md) - MZLA-run open source email client; product revival (Android app, native Exchange, government wins like Schleswig-Holstein) with a donation-only model now supplemented by the optional Thunderbird Pro services.
* [Ubuntu](ubuntu.md) - Canonical's Linux distribution; boldest 'oxidization' push in the distro world (Rust coreutils and sudo-rs by default from 25.10, completed in 26.10), shipped 26.04 LTS on time, but hit early sudo-rs CVEs and a 2026 DDoS.
* [Zulip](zulip.md) - Apache-2.0 threaded team chat; its commercial steward Kandra Labs became wholly owned by a new nonprofit Zulip Foundation in May 2026 as founder Tim Abbott left for Anthropic — a model for locking in OSS independence.
