# Summary

The OSPO function professionalized during 2024–26. The 8th annual **LF/TODO Group State of OSPOs 2025** (2025-08-28) describes OSPOs "rapidly maturing from compliance-focused units into strategic governance hubs" for risk management, AI oversight and supply-chain security, and finds organizations with OSPOs report higher upstream contribution, software quality and ecosystem influence; persistent problems are strategy gaps, executive buy-in and ROI justification[^lf-ospo-2025][^lf-research]. In May 2026 the TODO Group launched a working group on **agentic AI for OSPOs**[^lf-todo-agentic]. In Europe, LF research found many firms still use OSS without an OSPO or strategy[^lf-eu-2025], and the EU Open Source Strategy (June 2026) proposes an **EU OSPO Network**[^tpp-eu].

# Lessons
- OSPOs justify themselves via security (SBOMs, CRA compliance) and AI governance rather than "giving back".
- ROI measurement remains the OSPO's weakest point — and the reason corporate OSS cash funding stays at ~14% of contribution value (see [OSS economics](/projects/coss-market/oss-economics-studies.md)).
