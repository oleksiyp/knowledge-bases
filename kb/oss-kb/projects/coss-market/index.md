# COSS market projects

* [COSS venture funding 2024-2026](coss-funding-2024-2026.md) - Venture funding into commercial open source (COSS) companies 2024-2026: a ~$9B/yr baseline (LF/COSSA) swamped from 2025 onward by AI-adjacent mega-rounds (Databricks, Mistral, ClickHouse, Supabase, Temporal); capital is abundant but extremely concentrated.
* [AI's capture of COSS funding](ai-capture-of-coss-funding.md) - From 2025 AI absorbed ~50% and by Q2 2026 >70% of global venture dollars; within COSS, money flowed to open-weight model makers (Mistral) and to data/infra companies that rebranded as AI-agent infrastructure (Databricks, Supabase, ClickHouse, Temporal), squeezing non-AI open source.
* [COSS M&A 2024-2026](coss-ma-2024-2026.md) - Mergers and acquisitions of commercial open source companies, Oct 2024 to Oct 2026: IBM's $6.4B HashiCorp and ~$11B Confluent, Nvidia's ~$12.9B Hugging Face, a Postgres land-grab (Neon, Crunchy, Turso), PE take-privates (Couchbase) and AI-lab acqui-hires of dev tools (Bun, Astral).
* [COSS IPOs and public companies 2024-2026](coss-ipos-and-public-companies.md) - The listed commercial open source cohort shrank (HashiCorp, Couchbase, Confluent taken out) while survivors diverged: MongoDB reaccelerated to 30% growth then lost its CEO to Meta, Elastic steady at ~15-17%, GitLab decelerated and cut 14% of staff; no US COSS IPO happened — the late-stage private cohort (Databricks, ClickHouse, Grafana, Supabase) prefers private mega-rounds.
* [COSS business models 2024-2026](business-models.md) - How commercial open source companies made money 2024-2026: managed cloud consumption beat open core; the 2018-2024 source-available wave partially reversed (Elastic, Redis back to AGPL); fair source/fair-code matured; 'AI-native OSS' (usage by coding agents) became the dominant growth story while docs-funnel models (Tailwind) broke.
* [AI labs acquiring open source developer tools](ai-lab-devtool-acquisitions.md) - 2025-2026 trend in which AI labs and AI-coding companies bought the OSS runtimes and toolchains their agents depend on — Anthropic–Bun, OpenAI–Astral, Anthropic–Stainless, Cursor–Continue — keeping licenses permissive but moving control into AI-lab perimeters.
* [AI disruption of OSS monetization](ai-disruption-of-oss-monetization.md) - How AI coding assistants simultaneously increased OSS usage and broke some OSS revenue models in 2025-2026: Tailwind Labs (record usage, collapsing revenue, 75% engineering layoff, absorbed by Shopify), seat-based open core under pressure (GitLab restructuring), while agent-consumed infrastructure boomed.
* [IBM's open source portfolio](ibm-open-source-portfolio.md) - IBM became the largest owner of commercial open source franchises — Red Hat ($34B, 2019), HashiCorp ($6.4B EV, closed Feb 2025) and Confluent (~$11B, closed Mar 2026) — with Red Hat still growing ~11% in Q2 2026 even as IBM cut jobs and Red Hat exited China R&D.
* [Open source economics studies](oss-economics-studies.md) - Key macro studies on the economic value of and investment in open source: Harvard 'Value of OSS' ($4.15B supply-side, $8.8T demand-side), the 2024 Open Source Software Funding Report ($7.7B/yr, 86% labor), and LF/COSSA/Serena State of Commercial Open Source 2025 (COSS outperforms closed source).
* [European sovereign-tech investment in open source](european-sovereign-tech-oss.md) - Europe turned open source into industrial policy in 2025-2026: Germany's Sovereign Tech Agency/Fund grants (KDE €1M+, Flatpak €500k), the EU Tech Sovereignty Package and Open Source Strategy (June 2026) with a €2B seven-year envelope, plus SUSE's sovereignty pitch amid a possible $6B sale.
* [GitHub Octoverse 2025](github-octoverse-2025.md) - GitHub's Octoverse 2025 (Oct 28, 2025): 180M+ developers, 36M+ new in a year, TypeScript overtakes Python as #1 by contributors, India adds 5.2M developers and is projected to lead by 2030, and 1.1M+ repos use LLM SDKs — the demand side of the open source economy.
* [Open source program office (OSPO) trends](ospo-trends.md) - OSPOs matured 2024-2026 from license-compliance units into strategic hubs for supply-chain security, AI governance and sovereignty; the LF/TODO 2025 survey links OSPOs to more upstream contribution, the TODO Group launched an agentic-AI working group (May 2026) and the EU proposes an EU-wide OSPO network.
