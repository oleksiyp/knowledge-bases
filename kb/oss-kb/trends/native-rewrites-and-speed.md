# Summary

Speed users notice immediately won adoption:

- **uv** reached about 126M monthly downloads by Mar 2026, and OpenAI agreed to acquire Astral.[^astral-openai]
- **TypeScript 7** shipped on a Go compiler with 8–12x faster builds.[^wiki-ts]
- **Vite 8** runs on Rolldown.
- **Polars and DataFusion** grew strongly.

Rust entered the TIOBE top 10 in Jul 2026 and is no longer "experimental" in the Linux kernel.[^wiki-rust]

# Second-order effects

- AI makes porting cheap: Bun's AI-generated Rust port and chardet's AI rewrite. Review capacity becomes the scarce resource, and some projects ban AI code (Zig, Codeberg).
- Rewrites with compatible interfaces win: uv speaks pip, Rspack speaks webpack, and Bun and Deno added Node compatibility.
- Rewrites can also relicense, as chardet's AI rewrite did. See [License pendulum](/trends/license-pendulum.md).
