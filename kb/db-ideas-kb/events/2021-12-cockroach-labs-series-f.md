# What happened

Cockroach Labs announced a $278M Series F at a $5B valuation. It followed a $160M round earlier in 2021.[^crl-f][^pavlo-2021] Pavlo's 2021 review listed it among the year's "eye-popping" rounds alongside Databricks ($1.6B), ClickHouse ($250M), Yugabyte ($188M) and Firebolt ($127M).[^pavlo-2021]

# Why it matters

The valuation set expectations the company then had to grow into. In the following years Cockroach Labs tightened its license step by step: it retired the free Core edition in 2024 and made its source private in 2026. As of October 2026 it has not gone public.
