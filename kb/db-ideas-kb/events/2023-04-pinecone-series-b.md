# What happened

Pinecone raised $100M led by Andreessen Horowitz, with ICONIQ Growth, Menlo Ventures and Wing, at a $750M valuation. Its founder said the valuation had grown more than four-fold in a year.[^cb][^calcalist] In the same weeks Weaviate raised a $50M Series B and Chroma an $18M seed. Pavlo later listed these as the defining rounds of "the year of the vector database".[^pavlo-2023]

# Why it matters

It marks the top of the hype cycle. Within months pgvector had HNSW and MongoDB, Oracle and others shipped vector search. Two years later Pinecone was exploring a sale. The round priced in a new database tier that bundling by incumbents prevented.
