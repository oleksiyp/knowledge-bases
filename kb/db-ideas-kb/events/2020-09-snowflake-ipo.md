# What happened

Snowflake priced its IPO at $120, above a raised range, and raised $3.4B, the largest software IPO up to that time. Berkshire Hathaway and Salesforce bought shares alongside it. The stock opened at $245 and closed at $253.93, a gain of about 112%, valuing the company above $60B.[^cnn][^geekwire]

# Why it matters

It proved that a cloud-only, consumption-priced database could be one of the most valuable software companies. That drove 2021 valuations across the sector. Snowflake later delivered: $4.47B in FY2026 product revenue (+29%) and 125% net revenue retention.[^snow-fy26] Few of the companies funded in its wake came close.
