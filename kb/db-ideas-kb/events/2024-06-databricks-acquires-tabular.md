# What happened

Databricks announced the acquisition of Tabular, a roughly 40-person company founded by Ryan Blue, Daniel Weeks and Jason Reid, who created Apache Iceberg at Netflix.[^cnbc] The price was reported at $1–2B, and Snowflake and Confluent had also bid.[^techtarget][^bnf]

# Why it matters

It was a defensive purchase in the table-format war: Databricks owned Delta Lake, while Iceberg was becoming the industry standard. It shows the high price of a small team that controls an open standard. Pavlo's 2024 review listed it at about $2B.
