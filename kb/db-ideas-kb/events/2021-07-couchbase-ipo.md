# What happened

Couchbase priced an upsized IPO at $24, above its $20–23 range, and began trading on 22 July 2021 under the ticker BASE.[^base-ipo][^rencap]

# Why it matters

It was the last US IPO of an operational-database company in the period. Couchbase grew 16–20% a year with continuing losses and was taken private by Haveli Investments at $24.50 a share in September 2025.[^close] It is the reference case of a mostly self-managed, open-core database company in public markets.
