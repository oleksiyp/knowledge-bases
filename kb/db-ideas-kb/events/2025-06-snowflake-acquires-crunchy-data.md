# What happened

Snowflake announced the acquisition of Crunchy Data, a 13-year-old Postgres company with about 100 employees, for roughly $250M. Crunchy's technology became the basis of Snowflake Postgres.[^cnbc][^sa]

# Why it matters

Together with Databricks–Neon it marked the moment both big data platforms decided they needed transactional Postgres for AI-agent applications. Pavlo wrote that it is "not clear who the next major buyer will be" after these two deals.[^pavlo-2025] The price, about a quarter of Neon's, shows that buyers paid for growth and agent usage more than for enterprise Postgres expertise.
