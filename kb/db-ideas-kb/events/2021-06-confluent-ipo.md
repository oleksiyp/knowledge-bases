# What happened

Confluent sold 23M shares at $36, above its $29–33 range, raising $828M. The shares rose 25% on the first day, valuing the company at $11.4B.[^cnbc]

# Why it matters

It was the peak of the 2021 data-infrastructure IPO window. Confluent's managed Confluent Cloud grew to more than half of subscription revenue, but the stock never sustained its post-IPO highs. IBM agreed in December 2025 to buy it for $31 a share (~$11B enterprise value), below the IPO price.[^ibm]
