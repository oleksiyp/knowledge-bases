# What happened

Supabase announced a $500 million Series F led by GIC on June 4, 2026, at a $10 billion pre-money valuation. The simple pre-money-plus-new-capital figure is $10.5 billion. The company said the financing would support open-source/Postgres tooling, platform growth and employee liquidity.[^round]

# Why it matters

The round shows substantial investor commitment to a Postgres backend platform. It does not establish profitability or the economic value of each new database. Database creation counts, developers and paying production customers are different measures.

The announcement also distinguished roadmap ambitions from production availability: Multigres was an alpha, while OrioleDB production readiness remained a target. Capital can fund deep engineering and reliability work, but a financing announcement is not a general-availability claim. This makes the event positive evidence for the platform business while leaving its storage and scaling bets separately assessable.[^round]
