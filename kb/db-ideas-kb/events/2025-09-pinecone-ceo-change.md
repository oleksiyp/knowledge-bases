# What happened

At the end of August 2025, reports (via The Information) said Pinecone had engaged bankers and was exploring a sale. The reports cited rising competition and the loss of Notion, and named Oracle, IBM, MongoDB and Snowflake as possible buyers.[^calcalist] On 2025-09-08 Pinecone appointed Ash Ashutosh (founder of Actifio, previously at Google) as CEO. Founder Edo Liberty became Chief Scientist.[^pc] Ashutosh later said an acquisition was "definitely not on the table right now".[^tt] Pavlo read it as preparing for an acquisition that, by year-end, had not happened.[^pavlo-2025]

# Why it matters

Two and a half years after a $750M valuation, the category's flagship closed-source company was looking for an exit. Its reported suitors were the incumbents that had made vector search a feature.
