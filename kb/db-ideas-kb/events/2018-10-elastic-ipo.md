# What happened

Elastic N.V. sold 8.05M shares at $36 (above its range), receiving $263.8M in net proceeds. The stock closed its first day at $70, up about 94%.[^cnbc][^elastic-10q]

# Why it matters

Elastic was one of the open-source data-infrastructure IPOs that set expectations for the cohort. It relicensed in 2021, fought AWS over the trademark, returned to AGPL in 2024, and still grew to $1.739B in FY2026 revenue (+17%).[^elastic-fy26] Its path suggests the license mattered less to its revenue than Elastic Cloud and its observability and security products.
