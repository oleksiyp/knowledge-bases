# What happened

Couchbase announced completion of its acquisition by Haveli Investments on September 24, 2025. The approximately $1.5 billion all-cash transaction paid $24.50 per common share. The agreement had been announced June 20, and shareholders approved it September 9. Trading ceased as the company became privately held.[^close]

# Why it matters

The sale price was close to the $24 price of Couchbase’s July 2021 IPO.[^ipo] That comparison illustrates a disappointing path for an investor who bought at the offering price, but it does not establish every shareholder’s return or the buyer’s future performance.

The mixed verdict separates a public-market exit from a product shutdown. Couchbase continued as a company, and the acquisition announcement described plans for further investment.[^close] The analytical lesson is that a database can have durable customers and useful technology without delivering the growth trajectory expected at its public listing. Private ownership changes the financing and governance context; it does not, by itself, prove either a stagnant roadmap or a successful turnaround.
