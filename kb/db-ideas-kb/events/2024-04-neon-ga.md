# What happened
On Apr 15, 2024, Neon moved from technical preview to GA. The platform had grown from about 6,000 to over 700,000 databases within a year[^neon-ga][^neon-ga-hn]. Its architecture separates stock Postgres compute from Safekeepers (a replicated WAL service) and Pageservers (page materialization backed by object storage).

# Why it matters
GA came weeks after PlanetScale ended its free tier and while Aurora Serverless v2 still could not scale to zero. That made Neon the default "serverless Postgres" for developers and later for AI app builders and agents, which set up the ~$1B Databricks acquisition a year later.
