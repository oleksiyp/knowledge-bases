# What happened

On August 26, 2026, Mark Raasveldt and Hannes Mühleisen announced that DuckLabs would join AWS as a subsidiary, with effectiveness expected in early September. They said DuckDB, DuckLake, Quack and related extensions would retain MIT licensing and the stewardship of the nonprofit DuckDB Foundation. The Foundation would establish a stakeholder advisory board, and community-support limitations would be lifted.[^announcement]

The announcement does not disclose a purchase price or establish the actual closing date. This event records the announcement, not an independently verified completion.[^announcement]

# Why it matters

The event illustrates how an embedded open-source engine can become strategically valuable to a hyperscaler without becoming a proprietary cloud-only product. Corporate sponsorship and project licensing are separate decisions. The mixed signal is that engineering resources may become more secure while employment of core developers becomes concentrated in one large vendor. That is an interpretation of the announced structure, not evidence that governance or licensing had changed.
