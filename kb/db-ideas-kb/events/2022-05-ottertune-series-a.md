# What happened

OtterTune announced a $12 million Series A on May 10, 2022, led by Intel Capital and Race Capital with Accel participating. The stated expansion plans included additional database and cloud support.[^otter-investment] The company later closed in 2024, as co-founder Andy Pavlo records in his retrospective.[^pavlo-2024]

# Why it matters

The round is evidence of commercial backing for an independent optimization service, not proof that a tuning model had solved database operations. Our assessment is that the subsequent closure makes the distinction particularly useful: benchmark quality, customer willingness to delegate changes and a sustainable distribution channel are different tests. The event belongs in the funding history, but the later outcome should accompany it whenever the round is used as a signal that ML tuning had reached product-market fit. Funding gave the team resources to pursue that goal; it did not establish the goal had been achieved.
