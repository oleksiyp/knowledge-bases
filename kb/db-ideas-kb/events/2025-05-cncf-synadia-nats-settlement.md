# What happened

CNCF announced resolution of its dispute with Synadia on May 1, 2025. The statement says NATS would remain in CNCF under Apache 2.0 and that Synadia would assign the NATS trademarks to the Linux Foundation.[^cncf-nats] This event is a governance and licensing settlement, not an announcement that a BSL server release had become the project’s settled future.

# Why it matters

The outcome demonstrates the practical value of asset stewardship in a foundation. A major sponsor’s funding and engineering role did not give it uncontested control of project identity. The analytical interpretation is positive for continuity but not proof that sponsor economics were solved. Open licensing, trademark control and sustainable development funding are separate problems. The settlement addressed ownership and community expectations while leaving the longer-term commercial model to the participants.
