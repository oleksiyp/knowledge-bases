# What happened

Quickwit announced that it was joining Datadog on January 9, 2025. The team planned a new Datadog product and an Apache 2.0 release for the open-source community.[^announcement]

# Why it matters

The acquisition validated interest in object-storage search, while changing who controlled product direction. The founders described improving traction and the difficulty of scaling a dispersed team, so the sale should not be classified automatically as a failed engine or failed business.[^announcement]

This illustrates a recurring infrastructure outcome: a specialized engine can be valuable inside a broader platform with existing distribution. For users, the relevant questions differ from the company's exit outcome: maintenance, compatibility and the scope of future releases still matter. The acquisition announcement alone does not supply profitability or purchase-price evidence.
