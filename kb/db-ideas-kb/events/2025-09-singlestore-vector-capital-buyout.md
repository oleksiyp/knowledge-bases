# What happened
SingleStore announced a growth-buyout agreement led by Vector Capital on September 11, 2025. Its CEO later announced that the transaction closed on October 16, 2025.[^ss-agreement][^ss-close] These are the agreement and closing dates of one transaction, not separate acquisitions.

# Why it matters
The business continued under new ownership. Our assessment is that this belongs in the consolidation story rather than the shutdown list: a specialized hybrid database can retain customers and technical value without becoming an independent public-market platform. The official closing statement emphasizes continued investment, but such intentions do not establish future growth. Likewise, an acquisition alone cannot settle whether HTAP is a winning technical architecture. This page avoids treating reported transaction estimates as a disclosed purchase price; the official announcement used here does not supply one.
