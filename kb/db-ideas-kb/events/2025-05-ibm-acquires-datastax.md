# What happened

IBM announced its plan to acquire DataStax in February 2025 and closed in late May 2025, adding Astra DB (Cassandra-based, with vector search) and the Langflow agent builder to watsonx.[^reg][^dbta] DataStax had been valued at $1.6B in June 2022.[^cb] The price was not disclosed; reports of about $3B are unconfirmed.

# Why it matters

It was one of three IBM purchases of data-infrastructure companies in 13 months (HashiCorp, DataStax, then Confluent). A mid-size open-source database vendor found its exit as part of an incumbent's AI data story rather than through an IPO.
