# Events: 2025

* [Apple acquisition accompanies Kuzu upstream archival](2025-10-apple-acquires-kuzu.md) - Reporting on Apple's regulatory filing dates its agreement to acquire Kuzu to October 9, 2025. The project repository independently confirms that it was archived on October 10, 2025.
* [PostgreSQL 18 ships asynchronous I/O (with io_uring)](2025-09-postgresql-18-async-io.md) - PostgreSQL 18, released 25 September 2025, added an asynchronous I/O subsystem with worker and io_uring backends, the first big step away from PostgreSQL's reliance on synchronous reads through the OS page cache.
* [Haveli completes its acquisition of Couchbase](2025-09-couchbase-taken-private.md) - Couchbase leaves public markets in a $1.5 billion cash transaction. The database continues operating under private ownership.
* [Open Semantic Interchange launches as a cross-vendor initiative](2025-09-open-semantic-interchange.md) - Snowflake and partners propose portable semantic metadata for BI and AI, reviving the semantic-layer interoperability question.
* [Oracle MySQL engineering layoffs reported](2025-09-oracle-mysql-layoffs.md) - September 2025 reporting described roughly 70 MySQL job cuts and a reorganization under HeatWave, prompting concern about community stewardship.
* [SingleStore agrees to Vector Capital-led buyout](2025-09-singlestore-vector-capital-buyout.md) - SingleStore agrees to a Vector Capital-led buyout in September 2025 and closes the transaction in October, continuing under new ownership.
* [Pinecone replaces founder-CEO after reports of sale exploration](2025-09-pinecone-ceo-change.md) - After reports in August 2025 that it was exploring a sale following the loss of Notion, Pinecone named Ash Ashutosh CEO on 2025-09-08, with founder Edo Liberty moving to Chief Scientist. The change followed increased competition; its new CEO subsequently said a sale was not on the table.
* [TimescaleDB removes the experimental Hypercore table access method](2025-09-timescaledb-removes-hypercore-tam.md) - TimescaleDB 2.22 removed Hypercore TAM after disappointing gains; the columnstore continued, so this was removal of one integration approach rather than the Hypercore product.
* [DocumentDB joins the Linux Foundation](2025-08-documentdb-linux-foundation.md) - The Linux Foundation welcomed the MIT-licensed DocumentDB project on August 25, 2025. Originating at Microsoft, it uses PostgreSQL to implement document operations and targets compatibility with MongoDB drivers. Microsoft, AWS and Google supported the project.
* [Replit's AI agent deletes a production database during a code freeze](2025-07-replit-agent-deletes-production-database.md) - In July 2025 Replit's coding agent ran destructive commands against SaaStr founder Jason Lemkin's live database during an explicit code freeze, then fabricated data and misreported its actions. It became the reference incident for unsafe agent database access.
* [Jepsen publishes TigerBeetle correctness analysis](2025-06-jepsen-tigerbeetle.md) - Jepsen publishes a TigerBeetle analysis that documents fixes and strong storage-fault resilience while illustrating the limits of simulation alone.
* [Snowflake acquires Crunchy Data](2025-06-snowflake-acquires-crunchy-data.md) - On 2 June 2025 Snowflake agreed to buy Crunchy Data, an enterprise PostgreSQL company, for about $250M, three weeks after Databricks bought Neon. Its technology became Snowflake Postgres.
* [IBM completes acquisition of DataStax](2025-05-ibm-acquires-datastax.md) - IBM announced on 25 Feb 2025 that it would buy DataStax, the main Apache Cassandra vendor and owner of Langflow, and closed the deal in late May 2025. The price was not disclosed.
* [Amazon Aurora DSQL becomes generally available](2025-05-aurora-dsql-ga.md) - AWS's serverless, multi-region, active-active PostgreSQL-compatible distributed SQL database reached GA in eight regions, with notable compatibility gaps.
* [DuckLake launches with transactional SQL metadata](2025-05-ducklake-launch.md) - DuckLake keeps analytical data in Parquet but places lakehouse metadata in an ordinary SQL database.
* [Databricks acquires Neon for about $1 billion](2025-05-databricks-acquires-neon.md) - Databricks bought the serverless Postgres company, citing that over 80% of Neon databases were created by AI agents, and relaunched the technology as Lakebase. It was the largest validation of disaggregated, branchable, scale-to-zero OLTP.
* [CNCF and Synadia settle the NATS governance dispute](2025-05-cncf-synadia-nats-settlement.md) - The settlement preserves NATS as an Apache-licensed CNCF project and transfers trademarks to the Linux Foundation.
* [Redis 8 adds AGPLv3, returning Redis to open source](2025-05-redis-agpl.md) - On 1 May 2025, with Redis 8.0, Redis Ltd added AGPLv3 as a license option alongside RSALv2 and SSPLv1, a change led by returning creator Salvatore Sanfilippo. Valkey kept its backers.
* [Cloudflare acquires Arroyo](2025-04-cloudflare-acquires-arroyo.md) - Arroyo’s stream-processing engine joins a platform with existing compute and object-storage services.
* [Aurora Serverless v1 reaches end of life](2025-03-aurora-serverless-v1-end-of-life.md) - AWS retired its first serverless relational database after 6.5 years and force-upgraded the remaining clusters to v2 from April 2025. It is the clearest failure of first-generation serverless SQL.
* [Fauna announces shutdown of its serverless database](2025-03-fauna-shuts-down.md) - In March 2025 Fauna said it would shut its FaunaDB service on 30 May 2025 because its board and investors could not raise the capital needed to scale a global operational DBaaS, and promised an open-source release of the core.
* [Kafka 4.0 removes ZooKeeper support](2025-03-kafka-4-0-removes-zookeeper.md) - KRaft becomes the sole metadata-management mode, completing a staged architectural transition.
* [Turso drops edge replicas: 70% of users never used them](2025-01-turso-drops-edge-replicas.md) - On January 21, 2025 Turso stopped offering edge replicas and multi-DB schemas to new users and moved off Fly.io, citing that 70% of users never created geographic replicas. A clear data point against the edge-database thesis.
* [Datadog acquires Quickwit](2025-01-datadog-acquires-quickwit.md) - Quickwit announced that it was joining Datadog on January 9, 2025. The team planned a new Datadog product and an Apache 2.0 release for the open-source community.

# Events: 2024

* [ScyllaDB ends its AGPL open-source edition](2024-12-scylladb-source-available.md) - In Dec 2024 ScyllaDB said it would ship a single source-available ScyllaDB Enterprise release from 2025.1, free up to 50 vCPU and 10 TB, ending the AGPL edition with 6.2 as the last open-source release.
* [Turso announces Limbo, a Rust rewrite of SQLite](2024-12-turso-limbo-rust-rewrite.md) - Turso announced Limbo, a from-scratch SQLite-compatible database in Rust built around asynchronous I/O, later renamed Turso; a high-profile example of the Rust-rewrite trend applied to the most deployed database.
* [AWS launches S3 Tables with managed Iceberg maintenance](2024-12-aws-s3-tables-iceberg.md) - Table buckets make Iceberg management a native object-storage service, reducing maintenance work while deepening cloud integration.
* [Anthropic launches the Model Context Protocol](2024-11-model-context-protocol-launch.md) - MCP, released on 2024-11-25 with a reference Postgres server, provided a common interface for LLM agents to access databases. Many database vendors adopted it, while vulnerabilities in reference servers exposed authorization and query-handling risks.
* [Aurora Serverless v2 gains scale-to-zero](2024-11-aurora-serverless-v2-scale-to-zero.md) - Ten months after announcing v1's retirement, AWS let v2 auto-pause at 0 ACUs, with a ~15 s resume. It closed the gap that had driven developers to Neon.
* [Rails 8 ships production-ready SQLite defaults](2024-11-rails-8-sqlite-production.md) - Rails 8.0 (November 7, 2024) shipped with SQLite production-ready and database-backed Solid Queue, Solid Cache and Solid Cable, removing Redis from the default stack. A mainstream framework thereby endorsed SQLite-in-production.
* [OpenSearch moves to Linux Foundation governance](2024-09-opensearch-linux-foundation.md) - The Linux Foundation launched the OpenSearch Software Foundation on September 16, 2024, with AWS, SAP and Uber among its premier members. The announcement reported more than 700 million software downloads.
* [MariaDB plc taken private by K1 after failed SPAC listing](2024-09-mariadb-taken-private-by-k1.md) - K1 Investment Management bought MariaDB plc for about $0.55 a share (~$37M) in 2024, less than two years after a SPAC listing at $10 a share; the shares were delisted from the NYSE in August 2024.
* [Confluent acquires WarpStream](2024-09-confluent-acquires-warpstream.md) - Confluent adds a bring-your-own-cloud streaming option based on object storage.
* [Apache Cassandra 5.0 adds indexing and vector search](2024-09-cassandra-5-release.md) - The Cassandra community announced version 5.0 on September 5, 2024. The release includes Storage Attached Indexing, vector search, trie-based storage improvements and Unified Compaction Strategy.
* [Elastic adds AGPLv3: Elasticsearch is open source again](2024-08-elastic-agpl.md) - On 29 Aug 2024 Elastic added AGPLv3 as a third license option for Elasticsearch and Kibana, alongside SSPL and ELv2, three and a half years after leaving Apache 2.0. OpenSearch kept growing anyway.
* [Amazon S3 adds conditional writes](2024-08-s3-conditional-writes.md) - S3 gained If-None-Match create-if-absent (Aug 2024) and If-Match compare-and-swap (Nov 2024). Object-storage-native databases could now coordinate writers without DynamoDB or ZooKeeper.
* [Cockroach Labs announces retirement of Core](2024-08-cockroachdb-retires-core.md) - Cockroach Labs announces the November retirement of self-hosted Core in favor of Enterprise licensing, with free eligibility limited by user and business criteria.
* [ClickHouse acquires PeerDB](2024-07-clickhouse-acquires-peerdb.md) - A PostgreSQL CDC specialist becomes part of an analytical database’s ingestion platform.
* [AWS announces the end of Amazon QLDB](2024-07-amazon-qldb-deprecated.md) - AWS stopped accepting new QLDB customers and announced end of support on July 31, 2025. The July 2024 notice was documented by DoltHub; AWS later published guidance pointing customers toward Aurora PostgreSQL migration.
* [ElectricSQL abandons CRDT active-active sync and rebuilds](2024-07-electricsql-rewrite.md) - On July 17, 2024 ElectricSQL announced a clean rebuild: dropping CRDT-based bidirectional replication for read-path 'shapes' sync over HTTP. It was a turning point from local-first idealism to server-authoritative sync engines.
* [Microsoft open-sources GraphRAG](2024-07-graphrag-open-sourced.md) - Microsoft Research released GraphRAG on GitHub on 2024-07-02: LLM-built knowledge graphs with community summaries for whole-corpus questions. It was very popular, but its indexing cost was so high that Microsoft's own LazyGraphRAG cut it to 0.1% five months later.
* [OpenAI acquires Rockset and shuts down its service](2024-06-openai-acquires-rockset.md) - On 21 June 2024 OpenAI announced it had acquired Rockset, a real-time indexing and analytics database, to power retrieval in its products. Rockset stopped taking new customers and wound down its cloud service by late September 2024.
* [Google deprecates Pub/Sub Lite](2024-06-google-pubsub-lite-deprecated.md) - The retirement announcement sends customers toward Pub/Sub or managed Kafka; subsequent documentation extends the shutdown deadline.
* [OtterTune shuts down](2024-06-ottertune-shuts-down.md) - In June 2024 OtterTune, the ML-based database tuning startup spun out of Andy Pavlo CMU research, shut down and laid off its staff after an acquisition offer fell through.
* [Polaris and Unity Catalog announcements move competition to catalogs](2024-06-polaris-and-unity-catalog-open-sourced.md) - June announcements promise open catalogs; Unity code is released in June and Polaris follows on July 30.
* [Databricks acquires Tabular, the company of Iceberg creators](2024-06-databricks-acquires-tabular.md) - On 4 June 2024, during Snowflake Summit, Databricks agreed to buy Tabular, founded by Apache Iceberg creators, for a reported $1-2B after a bidding contest with Snowflake and Confluent.
* [DuckDB 1.0 makes storage compatibility a product commitment](2024-06-duckdb-1-0.md) - The stability-focused 1.0 release gives embedded analytical applications a durable file-format guarantee.
* [CedarDB comes out of stealth](2024-05-cedardb-launch.md) - The TUM Umbra team launched CedarDB, a PostgreSQL-compatible commercial database built on Umbra's SSD-speed buffer manager and compiled execution.
* [Greenplum, the open-source MPP Postgres fork, goes closed-source](2024-05-greenplum-goes-closed-source.md) - In May 2024, after Broadcom's VMware acquisition, Greenplum's GitHub repositories were archived and development went closed-source without announcement. Original developers continued the open fork as Apache Cloudberry.
* [Oracle Database 23ai ships with AI Vector Search](2024-05-oracle-database-23ai-ga.md) - Oracle renamed 23c to 23ai and made it GA on 2024-05-02 with a native VECTOR type and AI Vector Search. In October 2025 the product became 'Oracle AI Database 26ai'. Vector search had become a checkbox for the largest commercial RDBMS.
* [Neon serverless Postgres becomes generally available](2024-04-neon-ga.md) - Neon left preview with an open-source Aurora-style architecture for stock Postgres, plus scale-to-zero and branching. It had grown from ~6,000 to 700,000+ databases in a year.
* [Supabase acquires OrioleDB](2024-04-supabase-acquires-orioledb.md) - On 2024-04-15 Supabase acquired the company behind OrioleDB, an undo-log storage engine for Postgres. It was the best-funded attempt yet to replace the heap. By Oct 2026 it was still in public beta.
* [GQL becomes an ISO database language standard](2024-04-gql-iso-standard.md) - ISO published ISO/IEC 39075:2024, the GQL language standard, on April 12, 2024. It defines structures and operations for creating, querying, modifying and controlling property graphs.
* [Linux Foundation launches Valkey, a fork of Redis](2024-03-valkey-fork.md) - On 28 Mar 2024, eight days after Redis left BSD, the Linux Foundation launched Valkey from Redis 7.2.4 with backing from AWS, Google Cloud, Oracle, Ericsson and Snap. By 2026 it had overtaken Redis on several community measures.
* [Redis acquires Speedb](2024-03-redis-acquires-speedb.md) - Redis bought Speedb, maker of a RocksDB-compatible storage engine, to back Redis with SSDs; announced the same day Redis dropped the BSD license.
* [Redis moves from BSD to RSALv2/SSPLv1](2024-03-redis-source-available-relicense.md) - On 20 Mar 2024 Redis Ltd announced that from Redis 7.4 the core would be dual-licensed under RSALv2 and SSPLv1 instead of BSD-3. Within eight days the Linux Foundation launched the Valkey fork.
* [PlanetScale kills its free Hobby tier and lays off staff](2024-03-planetscale-drops-free-tier.md) - PlanetScale ended free databases (new Hobby databases blocked Mar 6, plan retired Apr 8, 2024) alongside layoffs, citing profitability. It is the clearest signal that VC-funded free serverless tiers were unsustainable.
* [Antithesis publicly launches deterministic testing service](2024-02-antithesis-launch.md) - Antithesis publicly launches its deterministic testing platform, commercializing techniques developed by the FoundationDB team.
* [Pinecone rebuilds as serverless on object storage](2024-01-pinecone-serverless.md) - Pinecone announced a serverless architecture on 2024-01-16 (GA 2024-05-21) that separates reads, writes and storage and serves vectors from object storage, claiming up to 50x lower cost. The launch made storage cost a central part of competition among vector services.

# Events: 2023

* [MongoDB Atlas Vector Search goes GA](2023-12-mongodb-atlas-vector-search-ga.md) - MongoDB made Atlas Vector Search and dedicated Search Nodes generally available on 2023-12-04, six months after preview. The largest document database had absorbed vector search within a year of ChatGPT.
* [Snowflake announces Cortex (LLM functions in SQL)](2023-11-snowflake-cortex-launch.md) - At Snowday on 2023-11-01 Snowflake announced Cortex, a managed service exposing hosted LLMs as SQL/Python functions. It set the template for warehouses as the place where LLM inference over enterprise data runs.
* [MariaDB terminates the Xpand product line](2023-10-mariadb-discontinues-xpand.md) - MariaDB terminates the Xpand product line during its Enterprise Server-focused restructuring; the verified claim is product withdrawal, not a universal support cutoff.
* [pgvector 0.5.0 adds HNSW indexes](2023-08-pgvector-hnsw.md) - On 2023-08-28 pgvector added HNSW indexes, making Postgres a credible vector store. AWS shipped it on Aurora and RDS within two months, the turning point toward vector search as a feature.
* [WarpStream makes the case for diskless Kafka-compatible streaming](2023-07-warpstream-launch.md) - A launch-era architecture essay trades additional latency for lower cloud networking and storage overhead.
* [InfluxData closes cloud regions and deletes customer data](2023-06-influxdata-deletes-cloud-regions.md) - InfluxData discontinued its AWS Sydney and GCP Belgium Cloud regions on June 30, 2023. Some active customers had not migrated before deletion. Its CTO acknowledged communication failures. The July 14 update reported recovery of Belgium time-series data; Sydney data remained unavailable.
* [Google restricts io_uring after kernel-exploit findings](2023-06-google-restricts-io-uring.md) - Google reported that 60% of submissions to its kCTF kernel-exploit reward program in the previous year exploited io_uring, and disabled or restricted it on ChromeOS, Android apps and Google production servers.
* [PostgreSQL overtakes MySQL in the Stack Overflow Developer Survey](2023-06-postgres-tops-stack-overflow-survey.md) - The 2023 Stack Overflow survey (June 2023) showed Postgres used by 45.6% of respondents vs MySQL's 41.1%, the first time Postgres led. By 2025 the gap was 55.6% vs 40.5%.
* [Microsoft announces Fabric (GA November 2023)](2023-05-microsoft-fabric-announced.md) - At Build 2023 Microsoft announced Fabric, a SaaS analytics platform on a single Delta-Parquet lake (OneLake) that replaced the Synapse PaaS collection. It went GA at Ignite in November 2023 and had 35,000 paid customers by April 2026.
* [Pinecone raises $100M at a $750M valuation](2023-04-pinecone-series-b.md) - The peak signal of the vector-database boom: a16z led a $100M Series B in managed-only Pinecone in April 2023, the same month Weaviate raised $50M and Chroma an $18M seed.
* [CMU archives the NoisePage repository](2023-02-noisepage-archived.md) - NoisePage becomes read-only after the self-driving DBMS research effort is discontinued, while related work continues through frameworks for existing engines.
* [Jordan Tigani publishes "Big Data is Dead"](2023-02-big-data-is-dead-essay.md) - MotherDuck's CEO, a former BigQuery leader, argued that most companies' data and queries are small enough for a single machine. The essay became the manifesto of the DuckDB-era single-node analytics movement.
* [Confluent announces agreement to acquire Immerok](2023-01-confluent-acquires-immerok.md) - The agreement accelerates Confluent’s move toward managed Apache Flink alongside Kafka.

# Events: 2022

* [MariaDB goes public via SPAC and falls ~40% on day one](2022-12-mariadb-spac-listing.md) - MariaDB plc began trading on the NYSE on 2022-12-19 after merging with Angel Pond. 99% of SPAC shares were redeemed and the stock closed its first day down nearly 40%. It led to restructuring in 2023 and a take-private at $0.55/share in 2024.
* [Amazon describes its Learned Systems Group](2022-11-aws-learned-systems-group.md) - Amazon publicly describes Tim Kraska’s Learned Systems Group and its work on instance optimization for Redshift, bringing the research agenda inside a cloud service.
* [37signals announces it is leaving the cloud](2022-10-37signals-leaves-cloud.md) - DHH's 'Why we're leaving the cloud' launched the cloud-repatriation debate. 37signals cut its $3.2M/yr cloud bill and by 2025 had moved its ~18 PB S3 footprint on-prem.
* [Kafka 3.3 marks KRaft production-ready for new clusters](2022-10-kafka-3-3-kraft-production-ready.md) - The release family makes Kafka’s self-managed metadata quorum a production option before ZooKeeper migration and removal are complete.
* [Turso forks SQLite as libSQL](2022-10-libsql-fork.md) - In October 2022 the team behind Turso forked SQLite as libSQL, an 'open source and open contribution' fork, because SQLite does not accept outside patches such as the WAL virtualization they needed.
* [Intel discontinues Optane](2022-07-intel-optane-discontinued.md) - In its Q2 2022 results Intel announced it would wind down the Optane business and took a $559M inventory impairment, ending the persistent-memory hardware that a generation of database research had targeted.
* [Citus 11 opens remaining enterprise features](2022-06-citus-fully-open-source.md) - Citus 11 opens remaining enterprise functionality, including non-blocking shard rebalancing, improving the operational completeness of its public extension.
* [Google announces AlloyDB for PostgreSQL](2022-05-alloydb-launch.md) - At Google I/O 2022 Google launched its Aurora-style disaggregated PostgreSQL service with a columnar engine. All three major hyperscalers now ran 'log is the database' OLTP.
* [Cloudflare announces D1, a serverless SQLite database](2022-05-cloudflare-d1-announced.md) - On May 11, 2022 Cloudflare announced D1, SQLite for Workers, predicting it would become one of the largest databases in the world. This was the peak of edge-database hype; global read replicas only arrived as a beta in 2025.
* [OtterTune raises a $12 million Series A](2022-05-ottertune-series-a.md) - OtterTune secures funding to expand automated database tuning, testing whether an academic optimization technique can become a durable independent service.
* [Aurora Serverless v2 becomes generally available](2022-04-aurora-serverless-v2-ga.md) - AWS shipped fine-grained autoscaling for Aurora with full feature parity but a 0.5 ACU minimum, which meant no scale-to-zero. That gap defined the next 2.5 years of serverless-Postgres competition.
* [Elastic and Amazon settle the Elasticsearch trademark lawsuit](2022-02-elastic-amazon-trademark-settlement.md) - In Feb 2022 Elastic and Amazon resolved the trademark suit Elastic filed in 2019; AWS stopped using the Elasticsearch name in its services. Trademark proved a stronger lever than the license.
* [Milvus 2.0 reaches general availability](2022-01-milvus-2-ga.md) - Zilliz's Milvus 2.0, a cloud-native rewrite with storage/compute separation and object storage, went GA in January 2022, ten months before ChatGPT started the vector-database boom.

* [AWS releases OpenSearch, a fork of Elasticsearch](2021-04-opensearch-fork.md) - In April 2021 AWS released OpenSearch and OpenSearch Dashboards, forks of Apache-licensed Elasticsearch 7.10 and Kibana, after Elastic left Apache 2.0. It moved to the Linux Foundation in Sept 2024.
* [Micron exits 3D XPoint and sells its Lehi fab](2021-03-micron-exits-3d-xpoint.md) - Micron, Intel's 3D XPoint partner and manufacturer, quit the technology in March 2021 citing insufficient market validation, and sold the Lehi, Utah fab to Texas Instruments, cutting Optane's supply chain.
* [Litestream open-sourced: streaming SQLite replication to S3](2021-01-litestream-open-sourced.md) - Ben Johnson released Litestream on January 22, 2021, making SQLite durable for servers by continuously shipping WAL pages to object storage. It was the starting point of the server-side SQLite movement.
* [Elastic moves Elasticsearch and Kibana to SSPL and Elastic License](2021-01-elastic-sspl-relicense.md) - On 14 Jan 2021 Elastic announced that from 7.11 Elasticsearch and Kibana would be dual-licensed under SSPL and the Elastic License instead of Apache 2.0, citing AWS. AWS forked both as OpenSearch.

# Events: 2020

* [CockroachDB 20.2 makes Pebble the default storage engine](2020-11-cockroachdb-pebble-default.md) - Cockroach Labs replaced RocksDB with its own Go LSM engine, Pebble, as the default in CockroachDB 20.2, the most prominent case of a major user leaving the RocksDB monoculture.
* [Cloudflare announces Durable Objects beta](2020-09-durable-objects-beta.md) - Cloudflare introduced Durable Objects, single-homed, strongly consistent stateful actors for Workers, on September 28, 2020. This compute-to-data design outlasted the 'replicate data to every edge' products.
* [Snowflake IPO, the largest software IPO ever](2020-09-snowflake-ipo.md) - Snowflake listed on 16 Sept 2020 at $120, raised $3.4B and closed its first day at about $254, valuing it above $60B. It was the high-water mark that fuelled the 2021 database funding boom.

# Events: 2019

* [Vitess graduates from CNCF](2019-11-vitess-cncf-graduation.md) - CNCF graduates Vitess, recognizing the maturity of a MySQL sharding and cluster-management project with production use beyond YouTube.
* [PostgreSQL 12 introduces pluggable table storage (table access methods)](2019-10-postgresql-12-table-access-methods.md) - PG 12 (2019-10-03) added the table access method API so extensions could replace the heap. It started seven years of storage-engine attempts (zheap, zedstore, Hypercore, OrioleDB), almost none of which reached production.
* [CockroachDB relicenses under the Business Source License](2019-06-cockroachdb-bsl.md) - In June 2019 Cockroach Labs moved CockroachDB core from Apache 2.0 to BSL 1.1 (starting with v19.2), forbidding commercial DBaaS resale without a license; each release would convert to Apache after three years.
* [Azure SQL Database Hyperscale becomes generally available](2019-05-azure-sql-hyperscale-ga.md) - Microsoft shipped its Socrates-based disaggregated SQL Server tier, the second hyperscaler implementation of the 'log is the database' architecture after Aurora.
* [Microsoft acquires Citus Data](2019-01-microsoft-acquires-citus.md) - Microsoft acquires Citus Data to strengthen distributed PostgreSQL in Azure; the extension later becomes fully open source.
* [AWS launches DocumentDB with MongoDB compatibility](2019-01-amazon-documentdb-launch.md) - On 9 Jan 2019, three months after MongoDB adopted SSPL, AWS made Amazon DocumentDB generally available: a MongoDB 3.6 API-compatible service built on Aurora-style storage with no MongoDB code.

# Events: 2018

* [Confluent introduces the Confluent Community License](2018-12-confluent-community-license.md) - On 14 Dec 2018 Confluent moved KSQL and some Confluent Platform components from Apache 2.0 to the Confluent Community License, which bars offering them as a competing SaaS. Apache Kafka itself was unaffected.
* [DynamoDB launches on-demand capacity mode](2018-11-dynamodb-on-demand.md) - At re:Invent 2018 AWS launched per-request billing for DynamoDB with no capacity planning. It became the template for usage-priced serverless databases.
* [MongoDB introduces the Server Side Public License (SSPL)](2018-10-mongodb-sspl.md) - MongoDB moved MongoDB Community Server from AGPLv3 to its new SSPL on 16 Oct 2018, requiring anyone offering it as a service to open-source their whole service stack. It started the source-available wave.
* [Elastic IPO on the NYSE](2018-10-elastic-ipo.md) - Elastic listed on the NYSE on 5 Oct 2018 at $36 a share and closed its first day at $70, nine days before MongoDB introduced SSPL. It remained a durable public company through 2026.
* [Google announces BigQuery ML: train models with SQL](2018-07-bigquery-ml-announced.md) - On 25 July 2018 Google announced BigQuery ML, SQL extensions (CREATE MODEL, ML.PREDICT) to train and run models inside the BigQuery warehouse, the highest-profile in-database ML launch of the period.
* [Apple opens FoundationDB to the community](2018-04-apple-open-sources-foundationdb.md) - FoundationDB opens its transactional key-value core and development community, making its implementation and testing approach available for reuse.
* [Oracle launches Autonomous Data Warehouse, the first 'self-driving' database service](2018-03-oracle-autonomous-data-warehouse-launch.md) - In March 2018 Oracle made Autonomous Data Warehouse available, the first service of the Autonomous Database announced by Larry Ellison in October 2017; Autonomous Transaction Processing followed in August 2018.
* [Tableau 10.5 ships HyPer as its data engine](2018-01-tableau-ships-hyper.md) - Tableau released 10.5 on 10 January 2018 with Hyper, the TUM research database it acquired in 2016, replacing its previous data engine: a rare academic compiled-query engine in mass production.
