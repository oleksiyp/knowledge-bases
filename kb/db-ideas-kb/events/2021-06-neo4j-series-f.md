# What happened

Neo4j announced a $325M Series F led by Eurazeo, with participation from GV, valuing the company at more than $2B. Neo4j called it the largest investment in a private database company.[^neo4j-pr][^techtarget]

# Why it matters

It is a marker of the 2021 peak: a single-model (graph) database priced as a future public company. Neo4j kept growing, but as of October 2026 it has not gone public, and graph databases stayed a niche relative to relational systems.
