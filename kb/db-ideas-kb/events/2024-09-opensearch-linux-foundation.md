# What happened

The Linux Foundation launched the OpenSearch Software Foundation on September 16, 2024, with AWS, SAP and Uber among its premier members. The announcement reported more than 700 million software downloads.[^announcement]

# Why it matters

This was a governance milestone for the Elasticsearch fork, not a new search engine launch. Foundation participation widened the institutional base beyond the cloud vendor that started the fork.

The reported download count demonstrates distribution activity, not unique deployments or market share. The stronger qualitative conclusion is durability: the fork had a funding and stewardship structure years after the original license dispute. Elastic adding an AGPL source option did not reunify the implementations or erase their separate communities.
