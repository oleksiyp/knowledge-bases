# What happened

Buf announced on May 8, 2026 that CoreWeave had acquired Bufstream. The announcement connects the transaction to CoreWeave’s platform, including W&B Models and Weave, while explaining that Buf would continue concentrating on schema infrastructure and its remaining products.[^cw-buf]

# Why it matters

The event separates a technology’s usefulness from the fate of a standalone product category. A streaming engine can be valuable enough to acquire for a broader platform without remaining an independent choice in the same commercial form. The mixed verdict is an interpretation of that change in distribution, not a claim that its engineering failed. It also warns against treating an acquisition of one product as an acquisition of the whole company: Bufstream’s transaction does not establish that Buf’s schema registry and open-source tooling were sold.
