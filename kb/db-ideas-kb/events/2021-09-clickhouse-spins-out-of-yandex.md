# What happened

On 2021-09-20 ClickHouse, Inc. announced its incorporation in San Francisco with $50M Series A funding led by Index Ventures and Benchmark, with Yandex participating. Alexey Milovidov (creator, CTO), Yury Izrailevsky and Aaron Katz (CEO) were among the founders[^ch-spinout]. In October 2021 it raised a $250M Series B, partly to secure rights to the ClickHouse name from Yandex[^pavlo-2021].

# Why it matters

The project already had a large community. The spin-out gave it a well-funded Western company, a managed cloud (launched Dec 2022) and a clean governance story. By 2026 the company was valued at ~$15B. The case shows that an engine with grassroots adoption can be commercialised late and still win its category.
