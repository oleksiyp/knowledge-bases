# Summary
Restack is the only grant awarded under the WP2025 topic "Open Internet Stack: development of technological commons/open-source 3C building blocks" (HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-DATA-11): a 48-month RIA with an EU contribution of €9,995,999.85, running 1 June 2026 – 31 May 2030 and coordinated by NLnet[^cordis]. The topic received only 4 proposals, 3 passed threshold, and 1 was funded (threshold 14.0/15)[^tt-data11]. NLnet announced the selection at FOSDEM 2026, together with the ELFA and CodeSupply pilots[^nlnet-three-grants]. It is explicitly the successor of the NGI Zero programme[^fsfe-restack].

# Objectives
- Deliver a publicly available, fully operational stack of strategic technology commons implementing an open, trustworthy, human-centric internet[^cordis].
- Keep the NGI Zero bottom-up granting model while bridging it to the needs of European service operators — telecom and hosting providers, shared service centres, research and education networks and civic online utilities[^cordis].
- Connect the 3C Large Scale Pilot roadmap with the Web 4.0 agenda and Virtual Worlds Partnership, build a catalogue of deployable services and feed evidence into policy such as the Digital Networks Act[^cordis].

# Cascade funding
Restack is a cascade (FSTP) programme operated by NLnet: €7M in small to medium R&D grants until 2030, proposals of €5,000–€50,000 that can be scaled up with proven potential; first call opened 3 Sept 2026, deadline **3 Nov 2026 12:00 CET**[^nlnet-restack]. In scope: core internet infrastructure, alternatives to large proprietary online services, secure/privacy-friendly devices and services, full-stack security and supply-chain management, infrastructure decoupling and deployment, standards and interoperability; AI work is out of scope unless already widely deployed (>1M active users)[^nlnet-restack]. Grantees also get support services (accessibility scans, security audits, reproducible packaging, copyright compliance, governance, standardisation)[^nlnet-restack]. NLnet now runs bimonthly deadlines on odd months[^nlnet-call-nov2026]. See [/cascade/nlnet-restack.md](/cascade/nlnet-restack.md).

# Outputs so far
Grant signed; the project started 1 June 2026[^cordis]. FSFE will provide legal and licensing support for potentially over 200 free software projects[^fsfe-restack]. First open call running Sept–Nov 2026[^nlnet-restack].
