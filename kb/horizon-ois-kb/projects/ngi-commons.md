# Summary
NGI Commons is a 38-month CSA (1 Jan 2024 – 28 Feb 2027, EU contribution €2,000,000) under HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-14 "Next Generation Internet Commons Policy", coordinated by Martel Innovate with OpenForum Europe (largest share, €730,125), CNRS and Open Future; Linux Foundation Europe is listed as a terminated participant[^cordis].

# Objectives
- Align NGI with the broader European digital and internet commons landscape on sovereignty, cybersecurity and data self-determination[^cordis].
- Bridge bottom-up commons movements and top-down regulation; ease access to NGI resources and funding for commons initiatives[^cordis].
