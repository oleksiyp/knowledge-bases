# Summary
WISE4 is a 36-month RIA (1 Sept 2026 – 31 Aug 2029, EU contribution €2,999,875) under HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-HUMAN-16, coordinated by Exascale Performance Systems (EXAPSYS, EL) with Politecnico di Torino, BSC, UPC, Eclipse Foundation Europe, SEAT and three SMEs[^cordis]. It covers the open-hardware priority area of HUMAN-16[^tt-human16].

# Objectives
- Deliver an open-source hardware platform with software libraries and middleware for Web 4.0 infrastructure, built on open instruction sets and open hardware IPs[^cordis].
- Integrate RISC-V CPUs, GPUs, AI and security cores with built-in resilience and hardware-rooted AI threat detection[^cordis].
