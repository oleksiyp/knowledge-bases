# Funded projects

Horizon Europe projects funded under the Open Internet Stack topics of WP2025 and the NGI-era topics it continues. Data from CORDIS (as of 2026-10-03). No WP2026-2027 OIS grants are on CORDIS yet: HORIZON-CL4-2026-04-DATA-02 retained 3 proposals and DATA-03 retained 1 (see [/context/who-wins.md](/context/who-wins.md)).

## WP2025 — Open Internet Stack and adjacent Web 4.0 topics (projects started 2026)
* [NOVA](nova.md) - €2.55M CSA (May 2026–Apr 2029) coordinated by PNO Innovation under HUMAN-17, launching the European Partnership on Virtual Worlds and drafting the Web 4.0 SRIA; a 22-beneficiary coordination backbone adjacent to the Open Internet Stack line.
* [Restack](restack.md) - The single project funded under the WP2025 Open Internet Stack topic HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-DATA-11: a ~€10M NLnet-led RIA (Jun 2026–May 2030) that re-runs the NGI Zero cascade model, with €7M of €5k–50k grants for open internet stack building blocks.
* [CodeSupply](codesupply.md) - €3M NLnet-coordinated RIA (Jun 2026–May 2029) under WP2025 Web 4.0 topic HUMAN-16, building an open, federated software-package metadata catalogue for supply-chain security; runs a €400k cascade fund of €5k–50k grants.
* [ELFA](elfa.md) - €3M RIA (Jun 2026–May 2029) under WP2025 Web 4.0 topic HUMAN-16 building an encrypted, local-first open collaboration suite around NextGraph; listed by CORDIS with NLnet as coordinator, but NLnet announced its withdrawal in Aug 2026 and the FSTP calls are postponed.
* [EURO-3C](euro-3c.md) - €75M Telefónica-coordinated RIA (Jul 2026–Jun 2029) under WP2025 topic DATA-08: the 3C (Connected Collaborative Computing) telco-edge-cloud large-scale pilot whose building-block needs the Open Internet Stack topic DATA-11 (Restack) is meant to serve.
* [TWINE](twine.md) - €3M imec-coordinated RIA (Sep 2026–Aug 2029) under WP2025 Web 4.0 topic HUMAN-16: federated, interoperable digital-twin and immersive civic services piloted in Rotterdam, Tampere and Flanders.
* [WISE4](wise4.md) - €3M RIA (Sep 2026–Aug 2029) coordinated by Greek SME EXAPSYS under WP2025 Web 4.0 topic HUMAN-16: an open-source RISC-V-based hardware/software reference platform for Web 4.0 infrastructure.

## WP2023-2024 — NGI Fund, pilots, policy and international (started 2023–2024)
* [TALER](taler.md) - €4.5M TU Eindhoven-coordinated Innovation Action (Dec 2023–Aug 2027) piloting GNU Taler privacy-preserving payments with retail banks; NLnet ran the NGI TALER cascade fund (final call closed 1 Aug 2026).
* [Fediversity](fediversity.md) - €3M NLnet-coordinated Innovation Action (Dec 2023–Nov 2027) building a NixOS-based hosting stack for federated services; its €450k NGI Fediversity fund closed 1 Aug 2026.
* [MOBIFREE](mobifree.md) - €4.4M e Foundation-coordinated Innovation Action (Dec 2023–Nov 2026) scaling an open, privacy-respecting European mobile software ecosystem; NLnet's €670k NGI Mobifree fund closed Dec 2025.
* [Local for local](local-for-local.md) - €1.83M Centric-coordinated Innovation Action (Dec 2023–Nov 2026) integrating NGI building blocks into a local-economy software stack piloted in five European regions, with open calls for NGI innovators.
* [NGI0 Commons Fund](ngi0-commons-fund.md) - The largest NGI Zero grant: €27.5M NLnet-coordinated RIA (Jan 2024–Jun 2027) under the WP2023 'NGI Fund' topic; €21.6M of €5k–50k grants, 13th and final call closed 1 June 2026.
* [NGI TRANSOCEANIC](ngi-transoceanic.md) - €4M SETU-coordinated RIA (planned Jan 2024–Dec 2027) for joint EU–US (NSF) NGI open calls with €3.2M FSTP; CORDIS now lists the project as TERMINATED.
* [NGI Commons](ngi-commons.md) - €2M Martel Innovate-coordinated policy CSA (Jan 2024–Feb 2027) aligning NGI with the European digital/internet commons landscape.

## WP2021-2022 — NGI Zero, search, outreach and transatlantic (started 2022–2023)
* [NGI4ALL.E](ngi4all-e.md) - €2M Martel Innovate-coordinated CSA (Jul 2022–Sep 2025) for NGI communication, marketing and community building — the successor of the NGI Outreach Office.
* [NGI0 Entrust](ngi0-entrust.md) - €12.5M NLnet-coordinated NGI Zero RIA (Aug 2022–Jan 2026) on trust and data sovereignty; funded 242 FOSS projects with €5k–50k grants via seven calls, now closed.
* [NGI0 Review](ngi0-review.md) - €1.5M NLnet-coordinated CSA (Aug 2022–Jul 2026) providing technical reviews, audits and scans for NGI-funded open-source projects.
* [NGI Search](ngi-search.md) - €8.5M Aarhus University-coordinated RIA (Sep 2022–Aug 2025) on trustworthy open search and discovery; ran five open calls funding 50 projects with up to €150k each.
* [OpenWebSearch.eu](openwebsearch-eu.md) - €8.5M University of Passau-coordinated RIA (Sep 2022–Feb 2026) piloting a European Open Web Index on HPC infrastructure; funded 14 third-party projects.
* [NGI Enrichers](ngi-enrichers.md) - €2M GAC-coordinated CSA (Sep 2022–Aug 2025) funding 3–6-month fellowships for European internet innovators in the US and Canada.
* [NGI0 Core](ngi0-core.md) - €11.5M NLnet-coordinated NGI Zero RIA (Jan 2023–Dec 2026) on internet architecture and decentralised technologies; €5k–50k grants via eight calls, last deadline 1 Oct 2024.
* [NGI Sargasso](ngi-sargasso.md) - €6M European Science Foundation-coordinated RIA (Jan 2023–Dec 2025) funding EU–US/Canada NGI teams through five open calls (€4.8M, up to €100k per project).
* [TrustChain](trustchain.md) - €10.6M European Dynamics-coordinated RIA (Jan 2023–Mar 2026) under the internet-architecture topic; distributed €8.8M to third parties via five open calls on identity, privacy, economics, multi-chain and green DLTs.
