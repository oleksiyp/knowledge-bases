# Summary
OpenWebSearch.eu is a 42-month RIA (1 Sept 2022 – 28 Feb 2026, EU contribution €8,502,621.75) under the same topic as NGI Search (HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-05), coordinated by the University of Passau with 11 partners — mostly universities and HPC/research centres (LRZ/Bavarian Academy of Sciences, CERN, DLR, CSC, TU Graz) plus the Open Search Foundation, A1 Slovenija and NLnet[^cordis]. Unlike NGI Zero, budget is spread across the consortium (coordinator ~8%)[^cordis].

# Objectives
- Develop and pilot the core of a European Open Web Index (OWI) and an open Web Search and Analysis Infrastructure (OWSAI)[^cordis].
- Build search verticals and new search paradigms, and a network of HPC, research and business organisations operating the infrastructure[^cordis].
