# Summary
TWINE (Twin-based Web Interoperable Network for Engagement) is a 36-month RIA (1 Sept 2026 – 31 Aug 2029, EU contribution €2,999,137.50) under HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-HUMAN-16, coordinated by imec with OASC, Engineering, the Lisbon Council, three public authorities (Rotterdam, Tampere, Flemish Region) and two SMEs/agencies[^cordis]. It is one of 4 projects funded from 34 HUMAN-16 proposals[^tt-human16].

# Objectives
- Design a federation-ready Web 4.0 architecture with open-source building blocks[^cordis].
- Develop connectors, profiles and a Minimum Interoperability Mechanism (MIM) for immersive civic services[^cordis].
- Demonstrate cross-city federation through pilots in Rotterdam, Tampere and Flanders, and ensure sustainability via open licensing and replication toolkits[^cordis].
