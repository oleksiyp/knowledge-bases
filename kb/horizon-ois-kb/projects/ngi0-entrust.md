# Summary
NGI0 Entrust is a 42-month RIA (1 Aug 2022 – 31 Jan 2026, EU contribution €12,499,375) under HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-04 "Trust & data sovereignty on the Internet", coordinated by NLnet with ten partners[^cordis]. NLnet received €10.92M of the grant, i.e. ~87% — most of it passed on as third-party grants[^cordis].

# Objectives
- Fund open-source and open-hardware solutions to privacy and data-governance problems — trustworthy data management and decentralised identity — through a light-weight granting procedure[^cordis].
- Surround grantees with expert support (security, accessibility, licence compliance, etc.) and connect output to standards bodies such as IETF, W3C and OASIS[^cordis].
- Reach innovators not previously funded by EU R&D (the proposal cites +95% of NGI Zero grantees as new to H2020)[^cordis].

# Cascade funding
€9.6M was allocated to R&D grants of €5,000–€50,000; 242 projects funded and 366 support services delivered; the seventh and last call closed 1 Dec 2023[^nlnet-entrust]. Closed. See [/cascade/ngi-zero-entrust.md](/cascade/ngi-zero-entrust.md).

# Outputs so far
242 FOSS projects funded[^nlnet-entrust]; deliverables include Summer of Nix reports, best-practice guides on security, accessibility, localisation and copyright compliance, and a package repository[^cordis].
