# Summary
NGI Sargasso is a 36-month RIA (1 Jan 2023 – 31 Dec 2025, EU contribution €6,000,000) under HORIZON-CL4-2022-HUMAN-01-07 "NGI International Collaboration – USA and Canada", coordinated by the European Science Foundation (FR) with Sploro, Barcelona Mobile World Capital Foundation and Australo Alpha Lab; US Ignite is an associated partner[^cordis].

# Objectives
- Catalyse EU–US/Canada collaboration on prototypes, standards and open-source contributions in trust, identity, internet architecture and decentralised technologies[^cordis].
- Run five open calls supporting up to 96 projects with equity-free funding plus capacity building[^cordis].

# Cascade funding
€4.8M across five calls for ~90 transatlantic teams, up to €100,000 per project[^sargasso-news]. Closed. See [/cascade/ngi-sargasso-open-calls.md](/cascade/ngi-sargasso-open-calls.md).

# Outputs so far
Ended 31 Dec 2025[^cordis]. First-call winners included APSIDE, D3ICA, DDIP, EBSI-CAN, EvORAN, INTEROP4DID, SBAS and SNDS[^sargasso-news].
