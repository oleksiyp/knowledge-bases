# Summary
MOBIFREE is a 36-month Innovation Action (1 Dec 2023 – 30 Nov 2026; total cost €4.90M, EU contribution €4,421,800) under HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-12, coordinated by e Foundation (FR) with Murena, Waag, Rapid.Space, merlinux, NLnet, UvA, Code for Romania, BioSense Institute and individual developers; Qwant is listed as a terminated participant[^cordis].

# Objectives
- Support European digital sovereignty by scaling open-source, privacy-respecting mobile software across all four key areas of the mobile ecosystem, co-created and piloted with four end-user sectors[^cordis].
