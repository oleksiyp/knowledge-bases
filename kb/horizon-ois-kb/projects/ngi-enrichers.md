# Summary
NGI Enrichers is a 36-month CSA (1 Sept 2022 – 31 Aug 2025, EU contribution €2,000,000) under HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-08 "NGI International Collaboration – Transatlantic fellowship programme", coordinated by GAC (FR) with APRE, SPI and Enrich Global, plus nine associated partners incl. Mitacs and Temple University[^cordis].

# Objectives
- Fund 70–90 European researchers/innovators for 3–6-month fellowships hosted by US and Canadian organisations, across three open calls[^cordis].
