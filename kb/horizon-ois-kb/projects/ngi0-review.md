# Summary
NGI0 Review is a 48-month CSA (1 Aug 2022 – 31 July 2026, EU contribution €1,500,000) under HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-09 "NGI Tech Review", coordinated by NLnet with eight partners; Radically Open Security holds the largest share (€412,500)[^cordis].

All figures in this table come from the CORDIS factsheet[^cordis].

# Objectives
- Offer NGI-funded open-source projects technical assessments, audits and scans by experts (security, accessibility, licence compliance, standardisation, internationalisation)[^cordis].
- Package selected technology through the Nix community for reproducible, verifiable software[^cordis].
