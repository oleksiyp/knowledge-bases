# Summary
EURO-3C is the WP2025 3C large-scale pilot (HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-DATA-08): a 36-month RIA (1 July 2026 – 30 June 2029) with an EU contribution of €74,999,880, coordinated by Telefónica Innovación Digital and including most large European operators (Deutsche Telekom, Orange, Vodafone, TIM, KPN, Telenor, Swisscom, BT, Elisa, MEO), vendors (Ericsson, Nokia, Thales), cloud providers (OVH, IONOS, SUSE, OpenNebula) and research organisations[^cordis]. It is not an Open Internet Stack project, but DATA-11 exists to provide open-source 3C building blocks for "the operation of the 3C large scale pilot"[^tt-data11].

# Objectives
- Pilot end-to-end supply-side infrastructures integrating device, network, computing and communication capabilities for Telco Edge Cloud deployments as a basis for 3C networks[^cordis].
