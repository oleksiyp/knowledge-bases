# Summary
NGI0 Commons Fund is a 42-month RIA (1 Jan 2024 – 30 June 2027) with an EU contribution of €27,499,305.50 under HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-11 "Next Generation Internet Fund", coordinated by NLnet with 12 beneficiaries plus two Swiss associated partners (Internet Society Switzerland Chapter, Free Silicon Foundation)[^cordis]. NLnet holds €23.73M (~86%)[^cordis].

# Objectives
- Contribute to a future technology stack that reinstates humanist values, improves supply-chain security and grows the commons at global scale[^cordis].
- Keep the agile, low-threshold NGI0 funding mechanism and add multidisciplinary support, mentoring on FOSS business models and legal infrastructure[^cordis].

# Cascade funding
€21.6M through 2027 for grants of €5,000–€50,000 (scalable); co-funded by the Swiss SERI; the 13th and final call closed 1 June 2026[^nlnet-commonsfund]. See [/cascade/ngi-zero-commons-fund.md](/cascade/ngi-zero-commons-fund.md).

# Outputs so far
Across all five NGI Zero programmes NLnet reports ~8,500 applications and 1,215 funded projects, plus 215 from adjacent NGI programmes[^nlnet-stocktaking].
