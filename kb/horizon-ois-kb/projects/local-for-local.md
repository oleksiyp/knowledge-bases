# Summary
Local for Local is a 36-month Innovation Action (1 Dec 2023 – 30 Nov 2026; total cost €2.52M, EU contribution €1,827,268) under HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-12, coordinated by Centric Netherlands with Kaunas University of Technology, Aix-Marseille University and TU Delft[^cordis].

# Objectives
- Integrate open-source NGI building blocks into the Local for Local stack (blockchain-based infrastructure, modular platform and the Locasus-B application)[^cordis].
- Test the stack in three pilots across five regions and measure economic impact[^cordis].
