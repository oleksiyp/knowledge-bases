# Summary
TALER is an Innovation Action (1 Dec 2023 – 31 Aug 2027; total cost €5.33M, EU contribution €4,508,355.40) under HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-12 "Pilots for the Next Generation Internet", coordinated by Eindhoven University of Technology with Taler Systems, NLnet, two retail banks (GLS Gemeinschaftsbank, MagNet Bank) and civil-society partners; Bern University of Applied Sciences is an associated partner[^cordis].

# Objectives
- Bring GNU Taler to TRL 9 as a payment system offered via retail banks (GLS, MagNet)[^cordis].
- Address three verticals: publishing/journalism, e-health payments, and donations in the FLOSS supply chain[^cordis].
