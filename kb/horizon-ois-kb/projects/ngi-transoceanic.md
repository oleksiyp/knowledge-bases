# Summary
NGI TRANSOCEANIC is a RIA (planned 1 Jan 2024 – 31 Dec 2027, EU contribution €4,000,000) under HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-13 "NGI International Collaboration – USA", coordinated by South East Technological University (IE) with Trust-IT Services and EURESCOM; Rutgers and UNC Chapel Hill are associated partners[^cordis]. **CORDIS lists the project status as TERMINATED**[^cordis]; the reason is not stated there (unverified).

# Objectives
- Run joint EU–US calls between the EU NGI programme and NSF internet programmes, building on NGIAtlantic.eu (2020–2023)[^cordis].
- Three 18-month open-call cycles with €3.2M FSTP for technology development, joint demonstrators and standards contributions[^cordis].
