# Summary
NGI0 Core is a 48-month RIA (1 Jan 2023 – 31 Dec 2026, EU contribution €11,499,375) under HORIZON-CL4-2022-HUMAN-01-03 "Internet architecture and decentralised technologies", coordinated by NLnet with the same ten partners as NGI0 Entrust[^cordis].

| Beneficiaries | 11 |
| CORDIS status | SIGNED |

All figures in this table come from the CORDIS factsheet[^cordis].

# Objectives
- Fund third parties to review and upgrade the open internet architecture (hardware, software, protocols) for performance, resilience, security, energy efficiency and decentralisation[^cordis].
- Deliver an estimated 150,000–300,000 person-hours of directly funded effort for the public benefit[^cordis].
