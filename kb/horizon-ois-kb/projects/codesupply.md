# Summary
CodeSupply is a 36-month RIA (1 June 2026 – 31 May 2029, EU contribution €3,000,000) funded under HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-HUMAN-16 ("Drive the evolution of the internet towards open and interoperable Web 4.0 and Virtual Worlds: building blocks in priority areas"), coordinated by NLnet with only four beneficiaries[^cordis]. HUMAN-16 drew 34 proposals; 27 passed threshold and 4 were funded (threshold 13.5)[^tt-human16]. NLnet presents it as one of three programmes launched under the "Open Internet Stack" umbrella[^nlnet-three-grants].

# Objectives
- Deliver correct, trusted and verified software metadata (origin, licences, vulnerabilities, project health) as an open-source, federated, digitally sovereign data catalogue[^cordis].
- Use Package-URLs (PURLs) as universal identifiers and distribute curated datasets as open data[^cordis].
- Address cybersecurity, supply-chain management and regulatory/licence-compliance needs that proprietary databases serve opaquely[^cordis].
