# Summary
ELFA (Encrypted Local-First Architecture) is a 36-month RIA (1 June 2026 – 31 May 2029, EU contribution €3,000,000) under HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-HUMAN-16, with 11 beneficiaries — mostly small open-source companies plus two individual developers registered as beneficiaries[^cordis]. CORDIS lists NLnet as coordinator[^cordis], but NLnet's ELFA page states that as of August 2026 NLnet has withdrawn from the consortium and that the open calls are postponed until further notice, to be executed by another organisation[^nlnet-elfa].

# Objectives
- Build a free/open-source, self-hostable and federated suite: document editor, spreadsheet, email, calendar, contacts, project management, video/chat, forms, photo/video sharing, social networking and VR[^cordis].
- Store data locally with CRDTs in an authenticated RDF graph database; sync protocol based on DIDs for portable accounts; end-to-end encryption throughout[^cordis].
- Standardise the protocol and grow an app ecosystem via SDK, app store and an FSTP sub-granting programme[^cordis]. The consortium targets 16 apps[^nextgraph-elfa].

# Cascade funding
The proposal includes an FSTP sub-granting programme[^cordis]; NLnet was to run it but has withdrawn and the calls are postponed[^nlnet-elfa]. The consortium site says the subgranting programme "opens soon"[^elfa-site]. Status tracked in [/cascade/elfa-open-calls.md](/cascade/elfa-open-calls.md).

# Outputs so far
Started 1 June 2026[^cordis]. Partners named on the consortium site: NextGraph, Ura Design, IronCalc, OpenBlocks (BlockNote), Ananas Teknik, Ontola, XWiki (CryptPad), Danube Tech and 2WEBAPP[^elfa-site].
