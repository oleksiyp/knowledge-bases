# Summary
NGI Search is a 36-month RIA (1 Sept 2022 – 31 Aug 2025, EU contribution €8,499,531.25) under HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-05 "Trustworthy open search and discovery", coordinated by Aarhus University with FundingBox, Universidad de Murcia, Linknovate and OW2[^cordis]. Aarhus held €7.21M (~85%), most of it for third-party funding[^cordis].

# Objectives
- Change how people search and discover data and resources on the internet: new interfaces (voice, image), heterogeneous sources (IoT, semantic data, multimedia, social media) and ML/NLP methods[^cordis].
