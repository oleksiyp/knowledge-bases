# Summary
Fediversity is a 48-month Innovation Action (1 Dec 2023 – 30 Nov 2027, EU contribution €3,000,000) under HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-12 "Pilots for the Next Generation Internet", coordinated by NLnet; the largest shares go to the Edsger Institute (€1.19M) and Open Internet Discourse (€1.06M), with Tweag and NORDUnet as smaller partners[^cordis].

# Objectives
- Provide easy-to-use hosted cloud services with personal freedom at their core, without tracking[^cordis].
- Give hosting organisations reproducible deployment configurations, cookbooks and playbooks to deploy and monetise FOSS and open social platforms[^cordis].
