# What it covers

The main WP 2025 Cluster 4 digital call: EUR 359.1M available, 440 proposals submitted[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-11]. It is relevant to OIS for DATA-11 (OIS commons for 3C, 4 proposals, 1 funded) and HUMAN-16 (open Web 4.0 building blocks, 34 proposals, 4 funded)[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-11][^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-human-16]. Adjacent topics in the same call that OIS projects had to coordinate with: DATA-08 (EUR 75M 3C supply-side large-scale pilot), DATA-09 (3C CSA) and HUMAN-17 (Virtual Worlds/Web 4.0 CSA)[^wp2025].
