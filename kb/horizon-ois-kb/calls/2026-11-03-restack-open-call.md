# What it covers

This is the first open call of [Restack](/cascade/nlnet-restack.md), the €7M Open Internet Stack cascade
fund run by NLnet. Proposals of €5,000–€50,000 for free/open-source work across the internet stack.
Individuals may apply. AI projects are out of scope unless already used at scale.[^nlnet-restack]
