# What it covers

WP 2026-2027 digital call with an overall indicative budget of EUR 208.80M after the cancellation of three quantum topics (DIGITAL-EMERGING-11, -12, -18), which moved to the EuroHPC JU[^wp2627][^qt-cancel]. It carried EUR 41.3M of Open Internet Stack topics: DATA-02 Sovereign Solutions (RIA), DATA-03 Support for Scale (CSA, lump sum) and HUMAN-02 Web 4.0/OIS applications (RIA)[^wp2627]. 312 proposals were submitted; the portal published evaluation results on 20 August 2026[^ft-horizon-cl4-2026-04-data-02]. OIS results: DATA-02 31 proposals and 3 retained; DATA-03 6 and 1; HUMAN-02 32 and 2[^ft-horizon-cl4-2026-04-data-02]. China-established entities were not eligible for the RIA topics in both destinations[^wp2627][^ga2627].
