# What it covers

The 2027 digital call of WP 2026-2027: overall indicative budget EUR 272.00M, opening 17 November 2026, deadline 18 March 2027[^wp2627]. **It contains no topic named 'Open Internet Stack'**[^ft-search]. OIS-relevant content: DATA-08 (demand-side 3C pilots, EUR 38M, 2 x ~EUR 19M, restricted eligibility) must reuse open-source and OIS components[^wp2627]. Other forthcoming topics include HUMAN-01 (Virtual Worlds XR hardware, EUR 39M, EUR 4.8–5.6M per project; not OIS), HUMAN-02 (Virtual Worlds/Web 4.0 ecosystem CSA, EUR 3M), HUMAN-07 (international digital standardisation CSA), and DATA-03 (decentralised/federated AI data processing, EUR 35M, 2 x EUR 17.5M, restricted eligibility)[^ft-horizon-cl4-2027-04-human-01][^ft-horizon-cl4-2027-04-human-02][^ft-horizon-cl4-2027-04-data-03][^ft-search].
