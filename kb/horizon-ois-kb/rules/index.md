# Rules

The Horizon Europe rules that govern Open Internet Stack (OIS) proposals and grants, verified against the WP 2026-2027 General Annexes (version of 29 Sep 2026), the Cluster 4 work programme, the Programme Guide V5.1, the Annotated Grant Agreement V2.0, the RIA/IA application form V5.1 and official evaluation statistics (as of 3 Oct 2026).

# Eligibility and consortium

* [Who can participate and who can be funded (countries, association status 2026)](eligibility-countries.md) - Member States, the 23 associated countries (UK, Switzerland, Ukraine, Moldova, Norway, Egypt; Canada/Japan/Korea/NZ for Pillar II), Morocco transitional, Australia from 2027, and hard exclusions (Russia/Belarus, China in RIA/IA).
* [Minimum consortium and participant roles](consortium-composition.md) - 3 independent beneficiaries in 3 MS/AC with at least one in a Member State; how affiliated entities, associated partners and subcontractors count; ineligibility seen in OIS calls.
* [Security restrictions: Article 22(5)/22(6), communication-network restrictions and China rules](security-restrictions.md) - OIS topics carry the high-risk-supplier restriction and the China RIA/IA exclusion plus a 4-year right to object to transfers; full Art. 22(5) country lists apply to the 3C IA.

# Money

* [Action types (RIA, IA, CSA) and funding rates](action-types-and-funding-rates.md) - 100% / 70% (100% for non-profits) / 100%, 25% indirect costs, 160% pre-financing, 5–8% mutual insurance; DATA-02 and HUMAN-02 are actual-cost RIAs, DATA-03 a lump-sum CSA.
* [Lump-sum funding: how it works in Horizon Europe](lump-sum-funding.md) - Shares per work package paid on completion, detailed budget table, no financial audits, how to split work packages, partial completion at the end.
* [Financial support to third parties (FSTP / cascade funding) rules](financial-support-to-third-parties.md) - Default €60k cap and publication/2-month rules; OIS exceptions of €400k per third party with up to 80% (DATA-02 2026) or 70% (DATA-11 2025), €300k with 70%/20% (HUMAN-02).

# Writing and evaluation

* [Proposal structure (Part A / Part B) and page limits 2026-2027](proposal-template-and-page-limits.md) - 40 pages RIA/IA, 25 CSA (45/28 with lump sums), template V5.1 section budget, mandatory D&E plan, AI-use guidance.
* [Evaluation criteria, scoring, thresholds and tie-breaks](evaluation-criteria-and-scoring.md) - Excellence/Impact/Implementation scored 0–5, thresholds 3 and 10, IA impact ×1.5; real OIS cut-offs of 14.0/15.
* [Technology Readiness Levels (TRL 1–9) and how they apply to OIS topics](trl-definitions.md) - Official TRL definitions; OIS topics set no TRL but demand technical maturity, communities and deployer interest.
* [Open source, licensing, open science and standards expectations for OIS](open-source-licensing-and-open-science.md) - Mandatory open access and DMP; OSI/FSF licences recommended for software; OIS expectations on open source "made in Europe", standards, audits, CRA/GDPR/DSA compliance.
* [IPR, exploitation obligations and the consortium agreement](ipr-and-consortium-agreement.md) - AGA Article 16 ownership and access rights, Horizon Results Platform duty, 4-year right to object, mandatory consortium agreement and DESCA 2.1 software clauses.
* [Ethics self-assessment, security appraisal and gender equality plan](ethics-and-security-self-assessment.md) - Part A ethics (incl. AI) and security tables, EUCI rules, GEP eligibility condition for public bodies, research organisations and universities.
