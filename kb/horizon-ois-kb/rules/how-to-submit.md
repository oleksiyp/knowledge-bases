# Before the call: register

1. **EU Login** account for every person who will work in the Portal[^om-registration].
2. **Register the organisation** in the Participant Register (if it has no PIC yet). The person registering becomes the *self-registrant*; on completion the organisation gets a **9-digit Participant Identification Code (PIC)**, initially "declared/non-validated"[^om-registration][^ft-participant-register].
3. **All beneficiaries, affiliated entities and associated partners need a PIC** to be entered in the proposal; one PIC per participant[^ga-2026]. A validated PIC is **not** needed to submit[^ga-2026]. Search the register first — many universities and companies already have a PIC; ask your research office.
4. Incomplete draft registrations are deleted after one year[^om-registration].

# Submitting (six-step wizard)

From the topic page on the F&T Portal ("Start submission")[^af-ria-ia]:
1. Log in to the Portal.
2. Select call, topic and type of action.
3. Create the draft: title, acronym, summary, main organisation and contacts.
4. Add partner organisations (by PIC) and contacts.
5. Complete Part A web forms and upload Part B (PDF) and annexes.
6. Submit.

Rules that matter[^ga-2026]:
- Only electronic submission; paper is not possible. Use the forms inside the system.
- The **coordinator** submits and confirms a mandate to act for all applicants and that all information is correct; each participant later confirms via a declaration of honour.
- Deadlines are **17:00:00 Brussels local time**[^wp7-2026]; deadlines cannot be extended at applicants' request; last-minute technical problems are at the applicant's own risk.
- You can resubmit as often as needed until the deadline; each submission overwrites the previous one and **only the last submitted version is evaluated**; the Portal advises submitting well ahead (e.g. 48 hours) of closure[^it-proposals].
- Page limits are checked automatically; excess pages become invisible after the deadline ([/rules/proposal-template-and-page-limits.md](/rules/proposal-template-and-page-limits.md)).
- Check the topic page regularly — call updates (including deadline changes and results) are published there[^ga-2026].

# After a positive result: validation and LEAR

- **Validation** of beneficiaries and affiliated entities (not associated partners or subcontractors) by the **Central Validation Service**, starting only once the proposal is successful; upload legal entity form, VAT document, registration extract, statutes, etc.[^om-registration][^ga-2026].
- **LEAR (Legal Entity Appointed Representative)** — mandatory for all validated participants; without a LEAR the organisation cannot update legal data or sign grants electronically[^om-lear]. Steps: encode the LEAR in "My Organisations → Authorised users/LEAR", print the generated appointment letter, have the legal representative sign it (ink or qualified e-signature), upload it with ID copies and proof of the representative's authority; the Central Validation Service validates; the LEAR activates the account[^om-lear].
- The LEAR then appoints **legal signatories** (LSIGN) and **financial signatories** (FSIGN) for grant signature and cost claims[^om-lear].
- **Financial capacity** check for coordinators of grants ≥ €500,000 (not public bodies)[^ga-2026].
