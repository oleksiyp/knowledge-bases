# Structure

- **Part A** — filled online in the submission system: administrative data on participants, summarised budget, ethics and security self-assessment tables, researchers table, call-specific questions[^ga-2026][^af-ria-ia].
- **Part B** — technical description downloaded as a template from the submission system, completed and uploaded as PDF; its three sections mirror the three award criteria[^ga-2026][^af-ria-ia].
- Annexes as required (e.g. the **detailed budget table** for lump-sum topics)[^ga-2026].
- Use the forms **inside** the submission system; templates on the topic page are for information only[^ga-2026].

# Page limits (Part B: title, participant list and sections 1–3 together)

| Action | Standard | Lump-sum topic | WP 2025 for comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| RIA / IA | **40** | **45** | 45 (lump sum 50) |
| CSA | **25** | **28** | 30 (lump sum 33) |
| First stage of two-stage | 10 | 10 | 10 |

2026-2027 limits from the General Annexes[^ga-2026]; 2025 limits from the WP 2025 General Annexes[^ga-2025]. For OIS: DATA-02 and HUMAN-02 (RIA, actual cost) = 40 pages; DATA-03 (CSA, lump sum) = 28 pages. Excess pages are automatically made invisible to evaluators after the deadline[^ga-2026]. Layout: reference font Times New Roman (or equivalent), **minimum 11 pt**, standard character spacing, at least single line spacing, A4 pages with margins of at least 15 mm; other fonts may not be used to bypass the page limit[^af-ria-ia]. "Do not consider the page limit as a target"[^af-ria-ia].

# Part B sections and indicative pages (template V5.1, 22 Jan 2026)

| Section | Indicative length |
|---|---|
| List of participants | ~1 page |
| **1. Excellence** — 1.1 Objectives and ambition | ~4 pages |
| 1.2 Methodology (concepts, interdisciplinarity, gender dimension, open science, research data management) | ~14 pages (of which core methodology ~10) |
| **2. Impact** — 2.1 Project's pathways towards impact | ~3 pages |
| 2.2 Measures to maximise impact: dissemination, exploitation, communication | ~3 pages |
| 2.3 Summary canvas | **optional** in V5.x |
| **3. Implementation** — 3.1 Work plan and resources (tables 3.1a–h: WPs, WP descriptions, deliverables, milestones, risks, person-months, subcontracting, purchase costs) | ~12 pages (17 for lump sum) |
| 3.2 Capacity of participants and consortium as a whole | ~3 pages |

Source: template V5.1[^af-ria-ia]. Version 5.0 (5 Nov 2025) "adapted according to WP 2026-2027 simplifications", simplified sections 2.1 and 3.1, reduced the page limit and removed tags[^af-ria-ia]. Impact 2.1 now asks for (a) your project's unique contribution to topic outcomes and destination impacts, quantified where meaningful, with specific target groups, and (b) requirements/barriers beyond the project and mitigation[^af-ria-ia].

# Mandatory content you must not forget

- A **plan for dissemination and exploitation including communication** is an **admissibility condition** (unless the topic says otherwise); a full version is a deliverable within 6 months of signature[^ga-2026][^af-ria-ia]. If exploitation is mainly in non-associated third countries, explain the EU interest[^ga-2026].
- **Open science practices** in 1.2 (or a justification if not appropriate), and a statement that a **Data Management Plan** will be delivered by month 6[^af-ria-ia].
- **Gender dimension** in R&I content (or why not relevant)[^af-ria-ia].
- Ethics and security self-assessments in Part A ([/rules/ethics-and-security-self-assessment.md](/rules/ethics-and-security-self-assessment.md)).
- If the topic allows **financial support to third parties**, describe objectives, expected results and the elements listed in the template ([/rules/financial-support-to-third-parties.md](/rules/financial-support-to-third-parties.md))[^ga-2026].
- Language: any official EU language, but English is strongly advised[^ga-2026].

# Use of AI tools

The template has carried guidance on generative-AI use since V3.3 (27 Sep 2023): applicants are "fully responsible for the content of the proposal (even those parts produced by the AI tool)", must verify accuracy and citations, and must be transparent about which AI tools were used and how[^af-ria-ia].
