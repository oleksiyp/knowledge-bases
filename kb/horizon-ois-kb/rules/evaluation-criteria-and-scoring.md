# The three award criteria (RIA and IA)

| Excellence | Impact | Quality and efficiency of the implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Clarity and pertinence of objectives; extent to which the work is ambitious and **beyond the state of the art** | **Credibility of the pathways** to the expected outcomes and impacts in the work programme | Quality and effectiveness of the **work plan**, risk assessment, appropriateness of effort per work package and resources overall |
| Soundness of methodology: concepts, models, assumptions, interdisciplinarity, **gender dimension** in R&I content, **quality of open science practices** (sharing/management of outputs, engagement of citizens, civil society, end users) | **Suitability and quality of measures to maximise outcomes and impacts** (dissemination & exploitation plan incl. communication) | **Capacity and role of each participant**, and whether the consortium as a whole brings together the necessary expertise |

For **CSAs** Excellence is "clarity and pertinence of objectives" plus "quality of the proposed coordination and/or support measures, including soundness of methodology"; Impact and Implementation are as above[^ga-2026]. Operational capacity of each applicant is assessed under Implementation (researchers table, up to five publications/datasets/**software**/services, up to five previous projects, infrastructure)[^ga-2026].

# Scores and thresholds

- Each criterion scored **0–5** (half points used); scores are given per criterion, not per aspect[^ga-2026].
- **Individual threshold: 3**; **overall threshold: 10** (out of 15)[^ga-2026].
- **Innovation Actions:** for ranking, **Impact is weighted ×1.5**[^ga-2026].
- Two-stage calls (not used by OIS topics): stage 1 scores only Excellence and Impact (bold aspects), each threshold **4**, overall threshold set so that admitted proposals request ~3× (not less than 2×) the budget — "normally 8 or 8.5"[^ga-2026].
- Proposals above threshold are funded in rank order within the budget; others go to a reserve list or are rejected[^ga-2026].

**Passing the threshold is not enough.** In the 2026 OIS evaluation the effective funding cut-off was 14.0/15 for DATA-02 and DATA-03 and 13.5 for HUMAN-02; in 2025 it was 14.0 for DATA-11[^ft-data02][^ft-data11]. Treat **14+/15** as the practical target.

# Ties ("priority order")

For equal scores within a budget envelope, unless the topic sets special rules[^ga-2026]:
1. Proposals covering aspects of the call not covered by higher-ranked proposals come first.
2. Then higher **Excellence** score (for IAs: higher **Impact**, then Excellence).
3. Then **gender balance** among researchers with a leading role named in the researchers table.
4. Then **geographical diversity** (number of MS/AC not otherwise funded by higher-ranked projects).
5. Then other factors (synergies, SME involvement), documented by the panel.

Topic-specific portfolio rules can override pure ranking. HUMAN-02 requires at least one funded proposal per area (Architectural Framework / Applications) and **only one** in the Architectural Framework area[^wp7-2026].

# Lump-sum budget assessment

For lump-sum topics (e.g. DATA-03), the cost estimates are checked under Implementation; overestimation leads to a reduced lump sum (no score penalty), but a budget unfit for purpose lowers the score[^ls-guide].

# Process and redress

- Independent external experts evaluate individually, then in consensus, then in a panel; for CSAs the committee may partly or fully consist of EU-institution representatives if the topic says so[^ga-2026]. Independent observers reported that the HORIZON-CL4-2026-04 evaluation followed the rules[^ft-data02].
- Evaluation review requests (procedural flaws only, not the merits) must be filed within **30 days** of accessing the result, max **7,000 characters**; unopened notifications count as accessed after 10 days[^ga-2026].
- Proposals judged fundable but unfunded may receive a **Seal of Excellence** or a **STEP Seal** if the topic provides for it[^ga-2026].
