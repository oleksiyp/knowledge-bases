# What NCPs are

National Contact Points are national structures (ministries, funding agencies, research promotion offices) appointed by each Member State and Associated Country to give **free** guidance on Horizon Europe: choosing topics, checking eligibility, reviewing ideas, finding partners and explaining legal/financial rules[^ncp-portal][^ideal-ist]. Each country has NCPs per thematic area; for OIS topics you want the **Cluster 4 "Digital"** NCP (often also covering Digital Europe).

# Ideal-ist: the Cluster 4 digital NCP network

- **Ideal-ist** is the international network of Horizon Europe Cluster 4 digital NCPs, describing itself as the "Horizon Europe Digital NCP Network"[^ideal-ist]; it is supported by an EU-funded CSA (IDEAL-IST)[^cordis-idealist].
- Services[^ideal-ist]:
  - **Project Idea Check** — a guided set of questions to validate your project idea with Cluster 4 digital NCPs (free);
  - **Partner Search** — publish a partner request or find consortia;
  - **Topic Tree** (topictree.ideal-ist.eu) — visual links between closed, open and forthcoming Cluster 4 digital topics;
  - **Brokerage events** — an online Face2Face brokerage is being organised for the 2027 digital calls[^ideal-ist].
- **Finding your NCP:** use the Ideal-ist representatives page (/representatives) to locate the digital NCP for your country[^ideal-ist]. The Horizon Europe NCP Portal also lists NCP networks per cluster[^ncp-portal].

# Commission and community events

- **Cluster 4 Info Days 2026** (online, 29–30 Jan 2026; day 2 covered Data, Digital, Human topics incl. OIS); recordings available via HaDEA[^hadea-infodays-2026].
- **Cluster 4 Info Days for 2027 calls**: online, **14–15 October 2026**, organised by the Commission[^ideal-ist-infodays-2027].
- **NLnet OIS matchmaking** (11 Feb 2026): three sessions for DATA-02, HUMAN-02 and DATA-03 where applicants pitched themselves in 5-minute slots; recordings and notes were shared[^nlnet-matchmaking] — a model for how the open-source community self-organises consortia.

# How to use NCPs effectively

- Contact your NCP **3–6 months before the deadline** with a one-page idea; ask specifically about topic fit, eligibility of non-EU partners, and security restrictions.
- Ask whether your national NCP offers proposal pre-reviews or national top-up/"seal of excellence" schemes.
- Use NCP-run brokerage events to find the missing third country for the 3-country rule ([/rules/consortium-composition.md](/rules/consortium-composition.md)).
