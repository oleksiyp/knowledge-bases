**Reading the numbers.** The very small 2025 OIS topic (DATA-11: 4 proposals) was effectively a call for one well-prepared large consortium (won by [Restack](/projects/restack.md)). In 2026 the Sovereign Solutions RIA attracted 31 proposals and 24 cleared every threshold, so quality alone did not win — only proposals at **≥14.0/15** were funded[^ft-data02]. Note also the high ineligibility in the 2025 3C pilot (14 of 18) — eligibility restrictions are a real filter[^ft-data11].

# Takeaways

- Expect **5–10%** for OIS-type RIAs; CSAs and very specialised topics can be higher because fewer consortia apply.
- Above-threshold is common (77% of DATA-02 proposals); the decisive band is 14–15 points[^ft-data02].
- One reserve-list proposal per OIS topic in 2026 — a reserve position occasionally converts if a winner withdraws[^ft-data02].
- Applying to the **cascade calls** run by funded OIS/NGI projects is a different game with its own (often higher-volume) statistics: see [/context/cascade-funding-explained.md](/context/cascade-funding-explained.md).
