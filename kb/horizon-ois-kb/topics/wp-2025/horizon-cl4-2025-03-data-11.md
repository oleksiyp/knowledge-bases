# Summary

HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-DATA-11 is the first Horizon Europe topic to carry the "Open Internet Stack" (OIS) name. It is a single Research and Innovation Action of around EUR 10 million that builds an open-source "stack of strategic commons" for trust, transactions, connectivity and decentralisation, explicitly tied to the 3C (Connected Collaborative Computing / telco-edge-cloud) large-scale pilot funded in the same call[^wp2025]. It sat in call HORIZON-CL4-2025-03 ("DIGITAL - CNECT"), opened 10 June 2025 and closed 2 October 2025[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-11]. One proposal was funded: Restack, coordinated by Stichting NLnet[^cordis-restack].

It is the successor, at a smaller scale, of the Next Generation Internet (NGI) "fund" topics of earlier work programmes. NGI was missing from a July 2024 working draft of WP 2025, which prompted public protest[^register-ngi-cut]. The adopted WP 2025 contains this OIS topic instead[^wp2025].

# Expected outcomes

- A publicly available and operational stack of strategic commons for internet technologies (trust, transactions, connectivity, decentralisation) implementing next-generation digital infrastructures, in particular 3C networks and the wider Web 4.0[^wp2025].
- A library of inclusive, trustworthy, interoperable, human-centric applications built on open-source commons building blocks, integrated and validated in the 3C large-scale pilot[^wp2025].
- A flourishing European ecosystem of digital-commons contributors (individuals, SMEs, academics)[^wp2025].
- Tools, services and insights supporting compliance with the EU legal framework (EUDI, CRA, DMA, DSA, GDPR, Data Act, DGA)[^wp2025].

# Scope

- Three technology areas: (1) trust technologies — privacy-enhancing technologies, AI-based agents, trusted identity technologies usable across multiple 3C networks; (2) network and connectivity technologies matching the needs of the 3C pilot; (3) decentralised technologies for an immersive world based on open standards[^wp2025].
- Proposals must set out concrete cooperation mechanisms with the 3C large-scale pilot (HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-DATA-08): taking in its requirements, prioritising building blocks jointly, enabling swift integration and testing, avoiding duplication; the 3C CSA (DATA-09) monitors duplication risks[^wp2025].
- Software must be operationalised as a stack of open libraries accessible through a common European repository; proposals must include a standardisation strategy, mechanisms for maturing third-party projects (security and accessibility audits, packaging, localisation into EU languages, documentation, licensing advice) and a path to growth for those projects[^wp2025].
- Applicants must demonstrate a proven track record across the full open-source life cycle, including years of experience and volume of open-source projects supported[^wp2025].
- Typical duration 36 months; gender dimension not mandatory[^wp2025].

# Conditions

- **Eligibility:** standard General Annex B, plus "subject to restrictions for the protection of European communication networks" (high-risk 5G suppliers excluded as beneficiaries, affiliated entities and associated partners)[^wp2025].
- **FSTP:** optional ("may"); grants only; max EUR 400 000 per third party (to allow repeat awards and maturity); max 70% of the requested EU contribution may go to FSTP via open calls[^wp2025]. Indicatively third-party projects of EUR 50 000–150 000 lasting 9–12 months; programme management and support cannot be paid from the FSTP envelope[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-11]. Calls should target open-source communities "actively influencing the course of the Internet" — SMEs, research institutes, individual researchers and developers[^wp2025].
- **Funding model:** HORIZON-AG budget-based (actual costs) Model Grant Agreement, not lump sum[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-11].
- **TRL:** none specified in the topic[^wp2025].

# How to read this topic

- This topic is closed and was won by a single consortium; for most developers the practical entry point is now the Restack cascade fund, not the topic itself[^nlnet-restack].
- The 2025 OIS was framed as a supplier of open building blocks to the 3C telco-edge-cloud pilot, which is a narrower, infrastructure-oriented framing than NGI's open-ended "whole internet stack" fund[^wp2025].
- Only 4 proposals were submitted, against 76 for a data-ecosystem topic in the same call; the requirement to show a long open-source-funding track record effectively limited the field to experienced cascade operators[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-11].
