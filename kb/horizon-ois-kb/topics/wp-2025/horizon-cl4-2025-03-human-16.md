# Summary

HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-HUMAN-16 is the Web 4.0 / Virtual Worlds sibling of the 2025 Open Internet Stack topic. Although it does not carry the OIS label, it funds exactly the kind of open-source internet building blocks NGI used to fund — EUDI-wallet tooling, software supply-chain security, open hardware and decentralised alternatives to gatekeeper platforms — and explicitly builds on "NGI building blocks"[^wp2025]. EUR 14.5M was available for about nine RIAs of EUR 1–3M; 34 proposals were submitted and 4 were funded[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-human-16].

# Expected outcomes

- An early demonstration of the future Web 4.0 architectural framework (protocols and standards) made of open-source internet building blocks in identity, software supply chain, open hardware, decentralised solutions and virtual worlds[^wp2025].
- Open, interoperable Web 4.0 and virtual worlds supported by EU-based ecosystems built on digital commons and open standards[^wp2025].
- New business and sustainability models based on open source[^wp2025].

# Scope

Proposals had to pick an area; at least one proposal per area was to be selected[^wp2025]:

1. **Identity management** — open-source tooling for the EU Digital Identity Wallet and eIDAS trust services (e.g. wallets for Linux, cloud and laptop wallets, open mobile OS; issuer/verifier stacks; anonymous digital payments)[^wp2025].
2. **Software supply chain security** — leveraging NGI building blocks for code traceability, collaborative trust models, anomaly detection, SBOMs; explicitly motivated by the XZ Utils attack and CRA compliance[^wp2025].
3. **Open hardware** — prototyping, productisation and integration of open-hardware chips and tools (controllers, processors, network chips)[^wp2025].
4. **Alternatives to centralised platforms** — integrating and operating open-source decentralised/federated messaging, app stores, groupware, given DMA obligations on gatekeepers[^wp2025].
5. **Web 4.0 demonstration for virtual worlds** — integrating existing open-source building blocks for trust, interoperation, transactions (incl. tokens) and cross-provider browsing/search[^wp2025].

Projects must align with the Web 4.0 roadmap/architectural framework produced by the CSA HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-HUMAN-17 and cooperate with each other; typical duration 24–36 months; the topic implements the co-programmed Virtual Worlds Partnership[^wp2025].

# Conditions

- **Eligibility:** General Annex B plus restrictions for the protection of European communication networks[^wp2025].
- **Procedure:** portfolio rule — at least the highest-ranked proposal in each area is funded if above thresholds[^wp2025].
- **FSTP:** optional; max EUR 150 000 per third party; max 15% of requested EU contribution[^wp2025].
- **Funding model:** HORIZON-AG budget-based grant[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-human-16].

# How to read this topic

- Although expected projects were nine, only four grants were made, each close to the EUR 3M ceiling — the budget went to fewer, larger projects[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-human-16].
- Two of four winners are NLnet-coordinated, which shows how strongly the established NGI cascade operators carried over into the Web 4.0 line[^cordis-elfa][^cordis-codesupply].
- Its direct 2026 successor is HORIZON-CL4-2026-04-HUMAN-02, which keeps the "Applications" idea and adds a single "Architectural Framework" project[^wp2025].
