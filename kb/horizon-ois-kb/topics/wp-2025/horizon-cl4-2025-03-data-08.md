# Summary

HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-DATA-08 funds the single EUR 75M supply-side "3C Network" large-scale pilot, which integrates device, network, edge and cloud into an open, multi-vendor telco-edge-cloud platform. It is not an Open Internet Stack topic, but OIS DATA-11 was designed to supply this pilot with open-source building blocks. The pilot in turn must cooperate with the OIS actions and integrate and test their solutions[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-08][^wp2025].

# Expected outcomes

- Stronger European industrial ecosystems for the 3C network, preparing demand-side pilots in WP 2026-2027[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-08].
- Open platforms underpinning an industrial open telco-edge-cloud ecosystem[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-08].
- **Cooperation mechanisms with the Open Internet Stack actions**: defining building-block requirements, integrating them in the pilot, and testing and integrating OIS solutions[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-08].
- Commitment from major European telecom, cloud and edge providers, plus an end-user advisory group[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-08].

# Scope

End-to-end telco-edge-cloud infrastructure prototyping at scale; open orchestration platforms; AI-based resource orchestration; security, privacy and energy efficiency; reuse of open-source frameworks (e.g. Sylva, Nephio, CAMARA) and of results from the Open Internet Stack action; close interaction with OIS constituencies. Activities run from TRL 3 to TRL 7[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-08].

# Conditions

- **Restricted eligibility:** only entities established in EU Member States, Iceland, Norway, Canada, Israel, Republic of Korea, New Zealand, Switzerland and the UK. Entities controlled from elsewhere need guarantees, and high-risk suppliers cannot submit guarantees[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-08].
- HORIZON-AG budget-based grant; no FSTP provision[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-08].
