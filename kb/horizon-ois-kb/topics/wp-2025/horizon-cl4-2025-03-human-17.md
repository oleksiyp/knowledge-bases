# Summary

HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-HUMAN-17 is the coordination backbone of the Virtual Worlds Partnership and the EU Web 4.0 initiative. Its Web 4.0 SRIA and architectural framework are what the open-source building-block projects under HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-HUMAN-16 must align with[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-human-17][^wp2025].

# Expected outcomes

- SRIAs for Virtual Worlds and for **Web 4.0**, the latter "leveraging open-source commons building blocks"[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-human-17].
- An established Virtual Worlds Partnership, a competitive ecosystem, outreach, and standardisation support[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-human-17].

# Scope

Network-building, a Web 4.0 implementation agenda including the architectural framework (standards, protocols, building blocks), ethics/legal/societal analysis, and standardisation representation. Links are encouraged with OIS DATA-11, 3C DATA-08 and HUMAN-14/15/16; typical duration 36 months[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-human-17].

# Conditions

Standard CSA conditions, HORIZON-AG grant[^ft-horizon-cl4-2025-03-human-17].
