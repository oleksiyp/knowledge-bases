# Summary

HORIZON-CL4-2026-04-DATA-02 is the flagship Open Internet Stack topic of the 2026-2027 work programme. It asks for deployable, made-in-Europe open-source solutions that will be "organised under the Open Internet Stack framework built under WP 2025", in three areas: network and transport, sovereign operating systems and firmware (including smartphones), and open-source software productivity and supply-chain tooling[^wp2627]. The topic had EUR 20.5M for about two RIAs of EUR 7–10.25M[^wp2627]. Call HORIZON-CL4-2026-04 closed on 15 April 2026; 31 proposals were submitted and 3 retained for funding, which is more than the 2 indicated[^ft-horizon-cl4-2026-04-data-02].

# Expected outcomes

A large selection of open-source solutions under the OIS framework, serving supply and demand sides of the European 3C provider/vertical ecosystem, that are[^wp2627]:
- open source and made in Europe, supporting trust and sovereignty and offering credible alternatives for citizens, governments and companies;
- packaged for easy deployment by providers, integrators and verticals;
- interoperable, standards-based and decentralised to enable network effects.

# Scope

- Technology areas (one or several): **network and transport** (routing, VPNs, survivable mesh); **sovereign operating systems and firmware (including smartphones)**; **open-source productivity and supply-chain technologies** (federated forges, independent cross-platform development frameworks)[^wp2627].
- Plan for efficient deployment: pre-configured modular components for SMEs, public administrations, research and education, and service providers; development, integration, testing, deployment, uptake and operation activities[^wp2627].
- Proposals must demonstrate: technical maturity (scalability, resilience, advanced cryptography incl. post-quantum, standards alignment incl. software/hardware supply chain, environmental efficiency); an active community with critical mass; evidence of user/deployer interest; and a credible path into the OIS (cataloguing, searchability, availability through app stores, EU-regulation alignment)[^wp2627].
- Must explain relevant previous projects, synergies with WP 2025 OIS, 3C and Virtual Worlds/Web 4.0 topics, and the relationship with the "Open Internet Stack Support for Scale" CSA (DATA-03); plus maintenance, cataloguing, marketing and communication strategy[^wp2627].

# Conditions

- **Eligibility:** General Annex B plus "subject to restrictions for the protection of European communication networks"[^wp2627]. Because it is an RIA in this destination, **legal entities established in China are not eligible** in any capacity (beneficiary, affiliated entity, associated partner, subcontractor, FSTP recipient) under the WP 2026-2027 China restriction[^wp2627][^ga2627].
- **FSTP:** optional; grants only; max EUR 400 000 per third party; FSTP up to **80%** of the requested EU contribution (up from 70% in 2025). If used, open calls should "primarily target internet innovators and adopters of Open Source technologies"; programme management cannot be paid from the FSTP envelope[^wp2627].
- **IPR / exploitation:** the granting authority may object, up to 4 years after the action, to transfer of ownership or exclusive licensing of results (Annex 5 specific provision); beneficiaries must notify DG CNECT/HaDEA in advance[^wp2627].
- **Funding model:** HORIZON-AG budget-based grant (not lump sum)[^ft-horizon-cl4-2026-04-data-02]. **TRL:** none specified[^wp2627].
- FSTP open calls must stay open at least 2 months, be published on the F&T Portal and beneficiaries' websites, and have a clear European dimension (General Annex G)[^ga2627].

# How to read this topic

- With 24 above-threshold proposals for 2–3 slots and a threshold of 14/15, the competition was very strong; an applicant needed a near-perfect score[^ft-horizon-cl4-2026-04-data-02].
- It funds deployable, already-mature solutions with existing communities — not early research. Teams without a user base and deployment evidence would have scored poorly on the "demonstrate" criteria[^wp2627].
- For individual developers and small companies the realistic entry route is the FSTP that the three winners may run (up to EUR 400k per third party)[^wp2627].
- The China exclusion and the high-risk-supplier rule both apply; check every partner, subcontractor and future FSTP recipient[^ga2627].
