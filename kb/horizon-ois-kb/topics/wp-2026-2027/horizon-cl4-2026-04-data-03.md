# Summary

HORIZON-CL4-2026-04-DATA-03 funds a single coordination and support action of about EUR 4M to make the Open Internet Stack visible, trusted and sustainable: a common hub for cataloguing, packaging, reviewing and validating OIS components, plus branding, training, sustainability advice and a policy sandbox[^wp2627]. It is the only lump-sum topic in the OIS family[^ft-horizon-cl4-2026-04-data-03]. Six proposals were submitted and one was retained[^ft-horizon-cl4-2026-04-data-03].

# Expected outcomes

All of the following[^wp2627]:
- a common approach and hub for cataloguing, packaging, reviewing and validating OIS components and projects;
- long-term viability through advice on open-source sustainability and maintenance models (business and foundation), and promotion of the EU and Associated Countries as the location for open-source foundations;
- a common branding, marketing, training and communication plan;
- close interleaving with policy development through a dedicated policy sandbox.

# Scope

The plan must cover[^wp2627]:
- structured, discoverable cataloguing of solutions;
- security and accessibility audits of solutions selected under the OIS;
- screening EU-funded open-source projects (including other Horizon Europe programmes) to make OIS the central hub for them;
- links with national, multi-country and pan-European sovereign open-source initiatives;
- advice on sustainability models, standardisation, licensing and localisation;
- training material per audience (infrastructure operators, integrators, government and vertical IT, end users) stressing compliance with GDPR, DSA/DMA, CRA, security audits and dependency lists;
- attracting adopters (service providers, integrators, OSPOs in governments and verticals) as promoters;
- sandbox tools for compliance-by-design with existing and future EU policy;
- support for an open-source awards scheme and post-project sustainability.
Typical duration 36 months[^wp2627].

# Conditions

- **Eligibility:** General Annex B plus restrictions for the protection of European communication networks[^wp2627]. The WP 2026-2027 China exclusion applies to RIAs and IAs; as a CSA this topic is not covered by that clause, though the general exclusion of MIIT-linked Chinese universities applies to all actions[^ga2627].
- **Lump sum:** costs take the form of a lump sum under the Decision of 7 July 2021 (HORIZON-AG-LS)[^wp2627][^ft-horizon-cl4-2026-04-data-03].
- **IPR:** granting authority may object to ownership transfer/exclusive licensing for 4 years after the action[^wp2627].
- **FSTP:** not provided for in this topic[^wp2627].
