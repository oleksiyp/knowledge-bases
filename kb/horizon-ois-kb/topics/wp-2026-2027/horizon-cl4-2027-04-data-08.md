# Summary

HORIZON-CL4-2027-04-DATA-08 is not an Open Internet Stack topic, but it is the main 2027 Cluster 4 topic that explicitly consumes OIS outputs. It funds up to two large demand-side pilot demonstrators (one on mobility/automotive, one on another vertical) on the converged telco-edge-cloud ("3C") infrastructure built by the WP 2025 supply-side pilot, "in particular through the use of Open Internet Stack components"[^wp2627]. It sits under the WP heading "Telco-Edge-Cloud continuum/ 3C Network (Connected Collaborative Computing) and Open Internet Stack"[^wp2627]. Status on 3 October 2026: forthcoming, opening 17 November 2026, deadline 18 March 2027[^ft-horizon-cl4-2027-04-data-08].

# Expected outcomes

- Demand-side validation of open orchestration platforms across the telco-cloud-edge continuum, unlocking AI value for European businesses[^wp2627].
- Sustainability and competitiveness of vertical sectors using 3C features (API aggregation, slicing, automation, latency, security, ISAC, reconfigurability)[^wp2627].
- One pilot on smart mobility including automotive; a vibrant SME/start-up ecosystem around 3C[^wp2627].

# Scope

- Up to two pilots: (1) mobility (transport, logistics, automotive), supporting the Connected and Autonomous Vehicle Alliance strategy; (2) another vertical such as energy, smart communities, industrial virtual worlds, health, agrifood or manufacturing[^wp2627].
- Consortia combine demand-side users and infrastructure suppliers; must include a robust data-governance model (data sovereignty, Common European Data Spaces, Gaia-X principles) and post-quantum-resistant security[^wp2627].
- Must reuse open APIs and open-source components from the supply-side pilot and projects such as Sylva, Anuket, Nephio and CAMARA; open-source frameworks used "should be made available to the Open Euro Stack Support for Scale" (the WP's wording for the OIS Support for Scale CSA)[^wp2627].

# Conditions

- **Restricted eligibility:** participation limited to entities established in EU Member States, Iceland, Norway, Canada, Israel, Republic of Korea, New Zealand, Switzerland and the UK, plus newly associating countries if listed for this topic. Entities controlled from non-eligible countries need positively assessed guarantees, and entities controlled by China or a Chinese entity cannot submit guarantees[^wp2627].
- China-established entities are excluded from all Innovation Actions[^ga2627].
- **TRL:** expected to achieve TRL 6-8 by the end of the project[^wp2627].
- Funding model: HORIZON-AG budget-based grant (not lump sum)[^ft-horizon-cl4-2027-04-data-08].
