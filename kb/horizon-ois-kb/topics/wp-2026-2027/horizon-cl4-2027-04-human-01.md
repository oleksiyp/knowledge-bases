# Summary

This file is here to correct a common confusion. HORIZON-CL4-2027-04-HUMAN-01 is sometimes cited as the 2027 "Open Internet Stack" topic, with "EUR 4.8–5.6M per project". The per-project range is right, but the topic is about **XR hardware for virtual worlds**: headsets, screens, wearables, haptics, sensors, actuators, chips and non-invasive brain-computer interfaces. It is not about open internet software[^ft-horizon-cl4-2027-04-human-01][^wp2627]. A search of the Funding & Tenders Portal for "Open Internet Stack" finds no topic with a 2027 deadline[^ft-search].

# Expected outcomes

Innovative XR hardware (headsets, screens, wearables, haptic components, sensors, actuators, chips) giving closer-to-reality immersion that stimulates all human senses[^ft-horizon-cl4-2027-04-human-01].

# Scope

New XR equipment, wearables, haptics and non-invasive BCIs. Proposals must integrate components into fully tested devices demonstrated in industrial and societal scenarios, focus on performance, miniaturisation, interoperability and energy efficiency, involve SSH expertise, and use and contribute to open standards[^ft-horizon-cl4-2027-04-human-01]. Typical duration 36 months; implements the Virtual Worlds Partnership[^ft-horizon-cl4-2027-04-human-01].

# Conditions

- Eligibility: General Annex B plus restrictions for the protection of European communication networks[^wp2627]; as an RIA in the human-centric destination, China-established entities are not eligible[^wp2627][^ga2627].
- TRL 3 at start → TRL 5 at end[^wp2627].
- 4-year right of objection to ownership transfer/exclusive licensing[^ft-horizon-cl4-2027-04-human-01]. No FSTP provision[^ft-horizon-cl4-2027-04-human-01].
