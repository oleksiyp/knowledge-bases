# Summary

HORIZON-CL4-2026-04-HUMAN-02 carries the Open Internet Stack into the Virtual Worlds / Web 4.0 line. It has two areas: an "Architectural Framework" area (only one project selected) building a human-centric Web 4.0 framework from open hardware up to immersive decentralised applications, and an "Applications" area for open-source decentralised services usable in virtual worlds[^wp2627]. EUR 16.8M for about three projects of EUR 2.8–8.4M; 32 proposals, 2 retained[^ft-horizon-cl4-2026-04-human-02].

# Expected outcomes

- Common: the emergence of Web 4.0 standards, protocols, components and a framework for their interaction, plus virtual-world solutions[^wp2627].
- Architectural Framework: a cross-platform, interoperable, secure Web 4.0 framework relying on open-source software, and a structured community driving commons based on open source, open standards and open hardware[^wp2627].
- Applications: open-source, made-in-Europe, standards-based decentralised solutions exploiting XR, compliant-by-design with EU rules and packaged for easy deployment[^wp2627].
- Synergies with Virtual Worlds, OIS and 3C actions and with Member-State initiatives[^wp2627].

# Scope

- **Area 1 — Architectural Framework:** interoperable layers from open hardware, web and AI agents up to immersive decentralised applications, using cross-platform digital commons from European SMEs, start-ups and academia; must demonstrate immersive multimodal interaction, enhance trust, privacy, portability and identity management, facilitate EU Wallet deployment, and balance decentralisation, security and energy efficiency with verifiable metrics[^wp2627].
- **Area 2 — Applications:** one or more of: alternative decentralised apps (messaging, videoconferencing, groupware, social media) exploitable via virtual-world technologies; app stores (incl. web-based) for virtual worlds; shared cross-technology virtual-world user and attribute management. Must show technical maturity, critical-mass communities and evidence of user/deployer interest[^wp2627].
- Cross-cutting: maturing mechanisms (security and accessibility audits, packaging, localisation, documentation, licensing advice), a standardisation strategy, community animation, collaboration with Virtual Worlds/Web 4.0, 3C and OIS initiatives, and a proven open-source track record[^wp2627].

# Conditions

- **Eligibility:** General Annex B plus restrictions for the protection of European communication networks[^wp2627]. As an RIA in the human-centric destination, **entities established in China are not eligible**[^wp2627][^ga2627].
- **Procedure:** at least the top-ranked proposal per area is funded if above threshold; **only one** Architectural Framework proposal will be selected[^wp2627].
- **FSTP:** grants only, max **EUR 300 000** per third party; up to **70%** of the proposed budget in the Architectural Framework area, up to **20%** in the Applications area; open calls should target European open-source communities (SMEs, research institutes, individual developers)[^wp2627].
- **IPR:** 4-year right of objection to transfer/exclusive licensing[^wp2627]. **Funding model:** HORIZON-AG budget-based[^ft-horizon-cl4-2026-04-human-02]. **TRL:** none specified[^wp2627].
