# Open Internet Stack and NGI call topics (Horizon Europe Cluster 4)

One file per topic, grouped by work programme. The Open Internet Stack topics have the deadlines 2 Oct 2025 (DATA-11) and 15 Apr 2026 (DATA-02, DATA-03, HUMAN-02). On 2026-10-03 no OIS-branded topic is open or forthcoming; see [Cluster 4 structure](/context/cluster-4-structure.md).

# Work programmes

* [Work Programme 2026-2027](wp-2026-2027/) - OIS Sovereign Solutions, OIS Support for Scale, Web 4.0 + OIS applications (all deadline 15 Apr 2026), plus 2027 adjacent/non-OIS topics
* [Work Programme 2025](wp-2025/) - First 'Open Internet Stack' topic (DATA-11) and Web 4.0 building blocks (HUMAN-16), plus the adjacent 3C pilot and Web 4.0 CSA
* [Work Programme 2023-2024](wp-2023-2024/) - Last NGI-branded topics: NGI Fund, Pilots, USA, Commons Policy (deadline 29 Mar 2023)
* [Work Programme 2021-2022](wp-2021-2022/) - First Horizon Europe NGI topics (deadlines 21 Oct 2021 and 5 Apr 2022)

# All topics at a glance

* [HORIZON-CL4-2026-04-DATA-02](wp-2026-2027/horizon-cl4-2026-04-data-02.md) - Main WP 2026-2027 Open Internet Stack topic: EUR 20.5M RIA (EUR 7–10.25M per project) for deployable open-source sovereign solutions in networking, operating systems/firmware and software supply chain, FSTP up to 80% / EUR 400k; deadline 15 Apr 2026, closed, 3 proposals retained from 31.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2026-04-DATA-03](wp-2026-2027/horizon-cl4-2026-04-data-03.md) - EUR 4M lump-sum CSA (one project) to run the Open Internet Stack hub: cataloguing, security/accessibility audits, branding, training, sustainability advice and a policy/compliance sandbox; deadline 15 Apr 2026, closed, 1 proposal retained from 6.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2026-04-HUMAN-02](wp-2026-2027/horizon-cl4-2026-04-human-02.md) - EUR 16.8M RIA (EUR 2.8–8.4M per project, 3 expected) for one Web 4.0 'Architectural Framework' project (FSTP up to 70%) and open-source decentralised 'Applications' for virtual worlds (FSTP up to 20%), max EUR 300k per third party; deadline 15 Apr 2026, closed, 2 retained from 32.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2027-04-DATA-08](wp-2026-2027/horizon-cl4-2027-04-data-08.md) - Adjacent (not OIS-branded) forthcoming topic: EUR 38M IA, ~EUR 19M per pilot, 2 pilots (mobility + one other vertical) on the 3C telco-edge-cloud infrastructure, expected to use Open Internet Stack components; TRL 6-8, restricted eligibility; opens 17 Nov 2026, deadline 18 Mar 2027.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2027-04-HUMAN-01](wp-2026-2027/horizon-cl4-2027-04-human-01.md) - NOT an Open Internet Stack topic (often confused with one): EUR 39M RIA for XR/haptics/BCI hardware, EUR 4.8–5.6M per project, ~8 projects, TRL 3→5, Virtual Worlds Partnership; opens 17 Nov 2026, deadline 18 Mar 2027.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-DATA-08](wp-2025/horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-08.md) - Adjacent (not OIS-branded) WP 2025 topic: the EUR 75M 3C telco-edge-cloud supply-side large-scale pilot (one RIA, restricted eligibility, TRL 3→7) that the Open Internet Stack topic DATA-11 must feed with open building blocks; deadline 2 Oct 2025, closed, funded EURO-3C.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-DATA-11](wp-2025/horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-11.md) - The founding Open Internet Stack topic: one EUR 10M RIA (budget-based, up to 70% FSTP, max EUR 400k per third party) to build an open-source commons stack feeding the 3C telco-edge-cloud pilot; deadline 2 Oct 2025, closed, funded the NLnet-led Restack project.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-HUMAN-16](wp-2025/horizon-cl4-2025-03-human-16.md) - NGI-style open-source building-block topic in the Virtual Worlds Partnership: EUR 14.5M RIA, EUR 1–3M per project across five areas (EUDI identity, software supply chain, open hardware, alternatives to centralised platforms, Web 4.0 demos), FSTP up to 15% / EUR 150k; deadline 2 Oct 2025, closed, 4 projects funded.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-HUMAN-17](wp-2025/horizon-cl4-2025-03-human-17.md) - Adjacent WP 2025 CSA (EUR 2.5M, one project) to launch the Virtual Worlds Partnership and write the Virtual Worlds and Web 4.0 SRIAs, incl. a Web 4.0 architectural framework built on open-source commons; deadline 2 Oct 2025, closed, funded NOVA.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-11](wp-2023-2024/horizon-cl4-2023-human-01-11.md) - The last NGI cascade 'fund' topic: one EUR 27M RIA that had to put at least 80% into FSTP (max EUR 500k per third party) for open-source internet commons across the whole stack; deadline 29 Mar 2023, closed, funded NGI0 Commons Fund (NLnet).
* [HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-12](wp-2023-2024/horizon-cl4-2023-human-01-12.md) - NGI pilots: EUR 14M IA, EUR 3–5M per project, applying NGI open-source technologies in at least two vertical use cases with at least 15% FSTP (max EUR 60k per third party); deadline 29 Mar 2023, closed; funded TALER, MOBIFREE, Fediversity and Local for local.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-13](wp-2023-2024/horizon-cl4-2023-human-01-13.md) - EUR 4M RIA for EU-US (NSF) NGI collaboration via three coordinated open calls, at least 80% FSTP, max EUR 150k per third party; deadline 29 Mar 2023, closed; funded NGI TRANSOCEANIC.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-14](wp-2023-2024/horizon-cl4-2023-human-01-14.md) - EUR 2M CSA to connect NGI with digital-commons policy (mapping commoners, strategic agenda, expert group, one-stop-shop path); deadline 29 Mar 2023, closed; funded NGI Commons.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-04](wp-2021-2022/horizon-cl4-2021-human-01-04.md) - WP 2021-2022 NGI cascade RIA (EUR 12M, at least 80% FSTP, max EUR 500k per third party) on trust and data sovereignty; deadline 21 Oct 2021, closed; funded NGI0 Entrust.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-05](wp-2021-2022/horizon-cl4-2021-human-01-05.md) - WP 2021-2022 NGI RIA on open search (EUR 17M, two research areas: a cascade fund with at least 80% FSTP and an open web index pilot with at least 15% FSTP); deadline 21 Oct 2021, closed; funded NGI Search and OpenWebSearch.EU.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-07](wp-2021-2022/horizon-cl4-2021-human-01-07.md) - WP 2021-2022 NGI outreach office CSA (EUR 2M); deadline 21 Oct 2021, closed; funded NGI4ALL.E.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-08](wp-2021-2022/horizon-cl4-2021-human-01-08.md) - WP 2021-2022 CSA for 3–6-month EU-US/Canada NGI fellowships (EUR 2M, at least 70% FSTP, max EUR 60k each); deadline 21 Oct 2021, closed; funded NGI Enrichers.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-09](wp-2021-2022/horizon-cl4-2021-human-01-09.md) - WP 2021-2022 CSA (EUR 1.5M) for independent technical review of NGI outputs; deadline 21 Oct 2021, closed; funded NGI0 Review.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2022-HUMAN-01-03](wp-2021-2022/horizon-cl4-2022-human-01-03.md) - WP 2021-2022 NGI cascade RIA (EUR 22M, 2 x EUR 11M, at least 80% FSTP, max EUR 500k per third party) on internet architecture and decentralised technologies; deadline 5 Apr 2022, closed; funded NGI0 Core and NGI TrustChain.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2022-HUMAN-01-07](wp-2021-2022/horizon-cl4-2022-human-01-07.md) - WP 2021-2022 NGI RIA for EU-US/Canada collaboration (EUR 6M, at least 80% FSTP); deadline 5 Apr 2022, closed; funded NGI Sargasso.
