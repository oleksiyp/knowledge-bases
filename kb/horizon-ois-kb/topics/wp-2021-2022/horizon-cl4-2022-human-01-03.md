# Summary

The topic behind NGI0 Core (NLnet) and NGI TrustChain, two of the largest Horizon Europe NGI cascade funds[^ft-horizon-cl4-2022-human-01-03][^cordis-ngi0-core][^cordis-trustchain].

This is a **predecessor** topic from the Next Generation Internet era, kept here for context. It is closed and has no further deadlines.

# Expected outcomes

- A more decentralised and trustworthy internet[^ft-horizon-cl4-2022-human-01-03].

# Scope

- Each RIA ran at least five open calls; typical third-party projects EUR 50 000–150 000 over 9–12 months[^ft-horizon-cl4-2022-human-01-03].

# Conditions

- At least 80% FSTP; max EUR 500 000 per third party[^ft-horizon-cl4-2022-human-01-03].
