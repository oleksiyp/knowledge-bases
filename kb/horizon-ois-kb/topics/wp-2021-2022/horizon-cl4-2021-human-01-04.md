# Summary

An early Horizon Europe NGI cascade topic on trust and data sovereignty. It funded NGI0 Entrust, one of the NGI Zero funds run by NLnet[^ft-horizon-cl4-2021-human-01-04][^cordis-ngi0-entrust].

This is a **predecessor** topic from the Next Generation Internet era, kept here for context. It is closed and has no further deadlines.

# Expected outcomes

- Increased trust and data sovereignty on the internet via outstanding open-source innovators (see portal topic text)[^ft-horizon-cl4-2021-human-01-04].

# Scope

- Cascade funding to open-source innovators, research groups, start-ups and SMEs, with at least five open calls; typical third-party projects EUR 50 000–150 000 over 9–12 months; apps without a research component are excluded[^ft-horizon-cl4-2021-human-01-04].

# Conditions

- FSTP at least 80% of the EU contribution; max EUR 500 000 per third party[^ft-horizon-cl4-2021-human-01-04].
