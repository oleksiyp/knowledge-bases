# Work Programme 2021-2022 — topics


# NGI topics (predecessors)

* [HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-04 — Trust & data sovereignty on the Internet (RIA)](horizon-cl4-2021-human-01-04.md) - WP 2021-2022 NGI cascade RIA (EUR 12M, at least 80% FSTP, max EUR 500k per third party) on trust and data sovereignty; deadline 21 Oct 2021, closed; funded NGI0 Entrust.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-05 — Trustworthy open search and discovery (RIA)](horizon-cl4-2021-human-01-05.md) - WP 2021-2022 NGI RIA on open search (EUR 17M, two research areas: a cascade fund with at least 80% FSTP and an open web index pilot with at least 15% FSTP); deadline 21 Oct 2021, closed; funded NGI Search and OpenWebSearch.EU.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-07 — Next Generation Internet community-building and outreach (CSA)](horizon-cl4-2021-human-01-07.md) - WP 2021-2022 NGI outreach office CSA (EUR 2M); deadline 21 Oct 2021, closed; funded NGI4ALL.E.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-08 — NGI International Collaboration - Transatlantic fellowship programme (CSA)](horizon-cl4-2021-human-01-08.md) - WP 2021-2022 CSA for 3–6-month EU-US/Canada NGI fellowships (EUR 2M, at least 70% FSTP, max EUR 60k each); deadline 21 Oct 2021, closed; funded NGI Enrichers.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-09 — NGI Tech Review (CSA)](horizon-cl4-2021-human-01-09.md) - WP 2021-2022 CSA (EUR 1.5M) for independent technical review of NGI outputs; deadline 21 Oct 2021, closed; funded NGI0 Review.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2022-HUMAN-01-03 — Internet architecture and decentralised technologies (RIA)](horizon-cl4-2022-human-01-03.md) - WP 2021-2022 NGI cascade RIA (EUR 22M, 2 x EUR 11M, at least 80% FSTP, max EUR 500k per third party) on internet architecture and decentralised technologies; deadline 5 Apr 2022, closed; funded NGI0 Core and NGI TrustChain.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2022-HUMAN-01-07 — NGI International Collaboration - USA and Canada (RIA)](horizon-cl4-2022-human-01-07.md) - WP 2021-2022 NGI RIA for EU-US/Canada collaboration (EUR 6M, at least 80% FSTP); deadline 5 Apr 2022, closed; funded NGI Sargasso.
