# Summary

A policy CSA to integrate NGI and internet commons into national and EU digital-commons policy, and the conceptual forerunner of the 2026 'OIS Support for Scale' CSA[^wp2324][^wp2627].

This is a **predecessor** topic from the Next Generation Internet era, kept here for context. It is closed and has no further deadlines.

# Expected outcomes

- Integration of NGI with digital-commons policy; a long-term strategy for sovereignty-critical commons; a more coherent funding landscape[^wp2324].

# Scope

- Map communities of commoners; measure NGI leverage; define a strategic agenda for the MFF timeframe; consult on commons priorities; plan a European one-stop shop; run a commons expert group; design governance models; organise strategic events with the NGI outreach office; typical duration 36 months[^wp2324].

# Conditions

- No FSTP; standard conditions[^wp2324].
