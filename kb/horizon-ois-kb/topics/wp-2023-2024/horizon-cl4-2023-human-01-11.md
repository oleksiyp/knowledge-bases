# Summary

The direct predecessor of the Open Internet Stack topics. It funded one large consortium to run continuous open calls ('NGI Zero'-style) for top value-driven open-source innovators who create, mature and grow internet commons across the whole stack, server and device side alike[^wp2324].

This is a **predecessor** topic from the Next Generation Internet era, kept here for context. It is closed and has no further deadlines.

# Expected outcomes

- A human-centric internet aligned with European values (privacy, inclusiveness, transparency, openness, decentralisation)[^wp2324].
- A flourishing internet based on common building blocks created within NGI[^wp2324].
- A structured ecosystem of contributors driving new and existing internet commons based on open-source software, open standards and open hardware[^wp2324].
- Synergies with NGI pilots and like-minded actions[^wp2324].

# Scope

- Commons covering the whole internet stack: open hardware, networking/transport, firmware, OS and virtualisation, electronic identities and middleware, decentralised ledgers, software productivity tools, traffic supervision, over-the-top and vertical applications[^wp2324].
- Continuous open-call environment with transparency, fair treatment and conflict-of-interest handling. Projects are matured through security and accessibility audits, packaging, localisation, documentation and licensing advice. Sustainability is addressed through legal hosting, funding models (e.g. foundations), governance and maintenance strategies[^wp2324].

# Conditions

- **FSTP mandatory:** at least 80% of the requested EU contribution goes to third parties via open calls; max EUR 500 000 per third party[^wp2324].
- **Award criteria addition:** 'capacity and proven experience of the consortium to create and grow internet commons based on open source software, hardware and standards' is assessed under Implementation[^wp2324].
- No communication-network security restriction is listed in the topic's specific conditions[^wp2324].

# How to read this topic

- The cascade (NGI0 Commons Fund via NLnet) is how most developers took part. Its open calls on the portal ran every two months from February 2024 to April 2025[^ft-search].
- The 80% FSTP minimum is much higher than the 70%/80% maxima in the OIS topics, which make FSTP optional. Under NGI, cascade funding was the purpose of the topic; under OIS it is a design choice[^wp2324][^wp2025].
