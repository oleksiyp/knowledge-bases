# Summary

Funds joint projects between European NGI teams and teams funded by the US NSF through three open calls coordinated with NSF[^wp2324].

This is a **predecessor** topic from the Next Generation Internet era, kept here for context. It is closed and has no further deadlines.

# Expected outcomes

- Reinforced NGI–NSF synergies, joint technologies, standards and demonstrators, and a transatlantic open-source ecosystem[^wp2324].

# Scope

- Themes: trust and PETs, data sharing and portability, sustainable internet, eIDs, internet architecture renovation, decentralised technologies; typical third-party projects ~EUR 150 000 over 18 months; typical duration 48 months[^wp2324].

# Conditions

- **FSTP mandatory:** at least 80% to third parties; max EUR 150 000 per third party; only EU/Associated-Country organisations can receive EU FSTP[^wp2324].
