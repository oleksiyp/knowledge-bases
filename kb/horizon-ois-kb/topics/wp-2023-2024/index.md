# Work Programme 2023-2024 — topics


# NGI topics (predecessors)

* [HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-11 — Next Generation Internet Fund (RIA)](horizon-cl4-2023-human-01-11.md) - The last NGI cascade 'fund' topic: one EUR 27M RIA that had to put at least 80% into FSTP (max EUR 500k per third party) for open-source internet commons across the whole stack; deadline 29 Mar 2023, closed, funded NGI0 Commons Fund (NLnet).
* [HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-12 — Pilots for the Next Generation Internet (IA)](horizon-cl4-2023-human-01-12.md) - NGI pilots: EUR 14M IA, EUR 3–5M per project, applying NGI open-source technologies in at least two vertical use cases with at least 15% FSTP (max EUR 60k per third party); deadline 29 Mar 2023, closed; funded TALER, MOBIFREE, Fediversity and Local for local.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-13 — Next Generation Internet International Collaboration - USA (RIA)](horizon-cl4-2023-human-01-13.md) - EUR 4M RIA for EU-US (NSF) NGI collaboration via three coordinated open calls, at least 80% FSTP, max EUR 150k per third party; deadline 29 Mar 2023, closed; funded NGI TRANSOCEANIC.
* [HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-14 — Next Generation Internet Commons Policy (CSA)](horizon-cl4-2023-human-01-14.md) - EUR 2M CSA to connect NGI with digital-commons policy (mapping commoners, strategic agenda, expert group, one-stop-shop path); deadline 29 Mar 2023, closed; funded NGI Commons.
