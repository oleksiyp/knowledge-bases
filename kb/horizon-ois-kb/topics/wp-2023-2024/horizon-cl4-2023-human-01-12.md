# Summary

Innovation Actions that take NGI technologies into real-life, multi-vertical use cases close to operational conditions[^wp2324]. 'NGI Taler' (the TALER project) came out of this topic, not out of a separate call[^cordis-taler].

This is a **predecessor** topic from the Next Generation Internet era, kept here for context. It is closed and has no further deadlines.

# Expected outcomes

- NGI technologies applied in industrial and societal use cases, enabling internet ecosystems for key verticals[^wp2324].
- New business and sustainability models based on open source[^wp2324].
- Support for the community of European top internet innovators[^wp2324].

# Scope

- Integrate NGI technologies into use cases in sectors such as public services, health, supply chain, transport, finance, creative industries, tourism and energy, with at least two verticals per proposal[^wp2324].
- Development, integration, testing, deployment, uptake and operation, focused on open-source software and hardware; SSH experts and user organisations involved; typical duration 24–36 months[^wp2324].

# Conditions

- **FSTP mandatory:** at least 15% of the requested EU contribution to third parties; max EUR 60 000 per third party; typical third-party projects EUR 10 000–50 000 lasting 6–9 months[^wp2324].
