Each step links to the rule page with the details. The [rules index](/rules/index.md) lists them all.[^rules-index]

# 1. Check that you can apply at all (week 1)

- [ ] Each partner is established in a Member State or an [Associated Country](/rules/eligibility-countries.md).
  Canada, Japan, Korea and New Zealand are associated for Pillar II only, which covers Cluster 4.
- [ ] No partner is caught by the [security restrictions](/rules/security-restrictions.md):
  - OIS topics exclude high-risk 5G suppliers.
  - In WP 2026–27, entities established in China cannot join RIAs or IAs in these destinations, in any role.
- [ ] The minimum consortium is met: [3 independent entities from 3 different MS/AC](/rules/consortium-composition.md).
- [ ] Every partner has a **PIC** and an appointed **LEAR** ([how to submit](/rules/how-to-submit.md)).
  Registration can take weeks.

# 2. Read the topic as an evaluator would (weeks 1–2)

- [ ] Map the topic's *expected outcomes* one by one to your work packages. Evaluators score
  against them.
- [ ] Note the [action type and funding rate](/rules/action-types-and-funding-rates.md): RIA 100%,
  IA 70% (100% for non-profits), CSA 100%.
- [ ] Note the budget model: [lump sum](/rules/lump-sum-funding.md) or actual costs.
- [ ] Note the cascade-funding (FSTP) rules if the topic allows them: per-third-party cap and
  maximum share of the budget ([FSTP rules](/rules/financial-support-to-third-parties.md)).
- [ ] Check the [open-source and licensing expectations](/rules/open-source-licensing-and-open-science.md)
  and the standardisation angle.

# 3. Build the consortium (weeks 2–6)

- [ ] Cover every expected outcome with at least one partner who has a track record in it
  ([who wins](/context/who-wins.md)).
- [ ] Decide early who runs any cascade funding, and how its management is paid for. In DATA-02 it
  must be paid outside the FSTP budget.
- [ ] Draft the [consortium agreement and IPR plan](/rules/ipr-and-consortium-agreement.md).

# 4. Write (weeks 4–10)

- [ ] Respect the [page limits](/rules/proposal-template-and-page-limits.md): 40 pages for RIA/IA,
  25 for CSA (45/28 with lump sums).
- [ ] Score yourself on each [criterion](/rules/evaluation-criteria-and-scoring.md) (0–5, threshold 3
  per criterion and 10 overall). Aim for **14 or more**: the 2026 OIS cut-offs were 13.5–14.0
  ([success rates](/rules/success-rates.md)).
- [ ] Complete the [ethics and security self-assessment](/rules/ethics-and-security-self-assessment.md).
- [ ] Ask your [National Contact Point](/rules/national-contact-points.md) for a pre-submission review.
- [ ] Apply the [practical tips](/rules/practical-tips.md), including what the Commission said it
  does *not* want.

# 5. Submit (final week)

- [ ] Submit through the Funding & Tenders portal **at least 48 hours early**. Deadlines are
  17:00 Brussels time.
- [ ] Afterwards, expect results in about 4–5 months and a signed grant in about 8
  ([time to grant](/rules/timeline-and-time-to-grant.md)).
