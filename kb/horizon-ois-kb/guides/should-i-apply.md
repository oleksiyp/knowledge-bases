# The situation in October 2026

- **Only four topics have carried the "Open Internet Stack" label**, and all are closed.[^topics-index]
  - [HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-DATA-11](/topics/wp-2025/horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-11.md), deadline 2 Oct 2025.
  - [2026-04-DATA-02 Sovereign Solutions](/topics/wp-2026-2027/horizon-cl4-2026-04-data-02.md), [DATA-03 Support for Scale](/topics/wp-2026-2027/horizon-cl4-2026-04-data-03.md) and [HUMAN-02](/topics/wp-2026-2027/horizon-cl4-2026-04-human-02.md), all with a deadline of 15 Apr 2026.
- **No dedicated OIS topic is planned for 2027.** The Commission's topic briefings say so.[^keydates]
- **Direct competition is extreme.** DATA-02 funded 3 of 31 proposals, HUMAN-02 funded 2 of 32,
  and the 2025 DATA-11 topic funded 1 of 4. Real cut-offs are 13.5–14 out of 15, against a formal threshold of 10.[^success]
- **The money now flows mainly through cascade funding (FSTP).** Funded projects re-grant up to
  70–80% of their budgets to third parties, in pieces of €5k–€400k.

# Pick your route

| You are… | Best route | Why | Next date |
|---|---|---|---|
| An individual developer or a small FOSS team, anywhere | **[NLnet Restack](/cascade/nlnet-restack.md)** or **[CodeSupply](/cascade/nlnet-codesupply.md)** | No consortium needed, a short form, €5k–€50k first grants (Restack allows up to €150k per proposal), milestone payments[^restack-fund][^codesupply-fund] | **3 Nov 2026, 12:00 CET**, then roughly every 2 months |
| An SME or open-source company with a product in the OIS technology areas | Watch the **[Sovereign Solutions cascade calls](/cascade/ois-sovereign-solutions-fstp.md)** | The three DATA-02 winners can re-grant up to €400k per third party[^sovsol] | Not announced. Grants are expected to be signed around Dec 2026, so calls are likely in 2027 |
| A research or industry organisation able to lead or join a large consortium in EU/EEA, CA, IL, KR, NZ, CH or UK | **[2027-04-DATA-08](/topics/wp-2026-2027/horizon-cl4-2027-04-data-08.md)**, the 3C pilot that must reuse OIS components | The only 2027 topic tied to OIS: €38M for about two ~€19M Innovation Actions[^data08] | Opens 17 Nov 2026, deadline **18 Mar 2027** |

# If you go for cascade funding

1. Read [how cascade funding works](/context/cascade-funding-explained.md). The money is usually paid
   by milestone after delivery, not upfront.
2. Check [Restack's scope](/cascade/nlnet-restack.md). AI projects are out of scope unless they
   already have more than 1M users.
3. Write for **reuse by the stack**: interoperability, standards, packaging and security
   hardening.

# If you go for a consortium topic

1. Work through the [proposal checklist](/guides/proposal-checklist.md), starting with
   [eligibility](/rules/eligibility-countries.md) and the [security restrictions](/rules/security-restrictions.md).
2. Study [who wins](/context/who-wins.md). NLnet coordinates 8 of the 21 core grants, and the
   NGI Zero partner set recurs in every grant.
3. Plan for **about 8 months from deadline to signature**
   ([timeline](/rules/timeline-and-time-to-grant.md)).
