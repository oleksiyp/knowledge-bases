# Guides

* [Deadline calendar](deadlines.md) - Every date that matters for Open Internet Stack applicants from October 2026 to mid-2027: info days, cascade deadlines, topic openings and closings, and expected grant signatures.
* [Proposal checklist for a consortium topic](proposal-checklist.md) - A step-by-step checklist from deciding to apply to submitting, linking the rule pages: eligibility, security, consortium, budget model, page limits, scoring and submission.
* [Should I apply, and through which route?](should-i-apply.md) - A decision guide for October 2026. All four Open Internet Stack topics are closed, and no dedicated OIS topic is planned for 2027. The realistic routes are NLnet cascade grants (deadline 3 Nov 2026), future Sovereign Solutions cascade calls, the 2027 3C pilot consortium, or complementary programmes.
