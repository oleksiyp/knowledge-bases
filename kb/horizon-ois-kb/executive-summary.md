# Bottom line

1. **"Open Internet Stack" is the successor to Next Generation Internet (NGI)**, and it is small.
   After the [2024 controversy](/context/ngi-funding-controversy-2024.md) over cutting NGI,[^controversy]
   only **four OIS-branded topics** were opened:
   - [2025-03-DATA-11](/topics/wp-2025/horizon-cl4-2025-03-data-11.md): €10M
   - [2026-04-DATA-02 Sovereign Solutions](/topics/wp-2026-2027/horizon-cl4-2026-04-data-02.md): €20.5M
   - [DATA-03 Support for Scale](/topics/wp-2026-2027/horizon-cl4-2026-04-data-03.md): €4M
   - [HUMAN-02](/topics/wp-2026-2027/horizon-cl4-2026-04-human-02.md): €16.8M

   **All four are closed**, and no dedicated OIS topic is planned for 2027.
2. **Winning directly is very hard.**
   - DATA-02 funded 3 of 31 proposals, HUMAN-02 2 of 32, DATA-03 1 of 6, and DATA-11 1 of 4.
   - Cut-offs were 13.5–14 out of 15.[^success]
   - Winners are concentrated: **NLnet** coordinates 8 of the 21 core NGI/OIS grants and receives
     42% of their €132M. The same NGI Zero partner set recurs in every grant.[^who]
3. **For most developers the real door is cascade funding.**
   [NLnet Restack](/cascade/nlnet-restack.md) (€7M to 2030) and
   [CodeSupply](/cascade/nlnet-codesupply.md) (€400k) both close on **3 Nov 2026, 12:00 CET**.
   - Grants are €5k–€50k, open to individuals, with no consortium needed.
   - Further NLnet deadlines follow roughly every two months.
4. **The next large cascade wave** should come from the three
   [Sovereign Solutions](/cascade/ois-sovereign-solutions-fstp.md) winners: up to €400k per third
   party and up to 80% of €20.5M. Winners aren't public yet; grants are expected to be signed
   around December 2026.
5. **For consortia, the only 2027 OIS-linked topic** is
   [2027-04-DATA-08](/topics/wp-2026-2027/horizon-cl4-2027-04-data-08.md), the 3C pilot.
   - €38M for about two Innovation Actions, which must reuse OIS components.
   - It opens 17 Nov 2026 and closes **18 Mar 2027**.
   - Only EU/EEA, CA, IL, KR, NZ, CH and UK entities are eligible.
6. **Policy momentum is stronger than the current budget.** The
   [June 2026 Open Source Strategy](/context/tech-sovereignty-package-2026.md) makes "scale up the
   Open Internet Stack" its first measure and targets about €2bn over seven years.[^oss-strategy]
   Expect the next framework programme (FP10, from 2028) to carry it.
