# Summary

Horizon Europe (2021-2027) publishes its Pillar II funding in multi-year **work programmes (WPs)** adopted by Commission decision and amended several times. For Cluster 4 there have been four WPs: 2021-2022, 2023-2024 (later relabelled "2023-2025"), a single-year WP 2025, and 2026-2027. The list below gives each adoption and amendment with the open-internet calls it produced.

# Notes

- The Commission decision number of the WP 2026-2027 adoption is not printed on the Cluster 4 PDF retrieved on 2026-10-03, and no source checked here confirms it. Treat it as unverified.
- WP 2026-2027 introduced the China eligibility restriction (RIAs/IAs) under Article 22(6) of the Horizon Europe Regulation. This matters for OIS consortia and FSTP recipients[^ga2627].
- Under the WP 2026-2027 General Annex F, evaluation results arrive about 5 months after the deadline and grants are signed about 8 months after it. For the 15 April 2026 OIS deadline that means projects start from about December 2026[^ga2627].
