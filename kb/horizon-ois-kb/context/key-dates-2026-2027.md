# What is (and is not) on offer in 2027

- **No dedicated Open Internet Stack topic** in the 2027 calls: the Commission's briefings say follow-up funding for DATA-02, DATA-03 and HUMAN-02 is "Not planned for 2027"[^si-fiches].
- Adjacent 2027 topics in HORIZON-CL4-2027-04 (deadline 18 Mar 2027)[^wp7-2026]:
  - **HORIZON-CL4-2027-04-DATA-08** Demand-side 3C pilot demonstrators on converged Telco Edge Cloud Infrastructure (IA, €38M, ~€19M each) — builds on Open Internet Stack components and the "Open Euro Stack Support for Scale"; **restricted** under Art. 22(5) to MS, IS, NO, CA, IL, KR, NZ, CH, UK; TRL 6–8.
  - **HORIZON-CL4-2027-04-HUMAN-02** Create a thriving and competitive Virtual Worlds and Web 4.0 ecosystem (CSA, €3M).
  - **HORIZON-CL4-2027-04-HUMAN-07** Facilitate the engagement of European stakeholders in international digital standardisation (CSA, €7M) — relevant to open-standards communities.
  - **HORIZON-CL4-2027-04-DATA-03** New approaches for decentralized, federated and sustainable AI data processing (RIA, €35M).
- For most open-source developers, 2027 money will flow through **cascade calls** of the projects funded in 2025–2026 (Restack, CodeSupply, ELFA, and the DATA-02/HUMAN-02 winners) — see [/context/cascade-funding-explained.md](/context/cascade-funding-explained.md).

# Beyond 2027

The Commission proposed on 16 July 2025 a €175 billion **FP10** (2028–2034), tightly connected to a new **European Competitiveness Fund**[^sb-fp10]; the Open Source Strategy says open-source business support "will be expanded under the new programming period" ([/context/tech-sovereignty-package-2026.md](/context/tech-sovereignty-package-2026.md)).
