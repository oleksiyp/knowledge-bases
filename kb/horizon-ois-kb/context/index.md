# Context

Background on programme structure, history, policy and funding mechanics behind the Horizon Europe Cluster 4 Open Internet Stack (OIS) calls.

# Programme structure and mechanics

* [Horizon Europe Cluster 4 structure — where the Open Internet Stack sits](cluster-4-structure.md) - How Cluster 4 (Digital, Industry and Space) is organised into destinations and calls in WP 2021-2022, 2023-2024, 2025 and 2026-2027, and how the NGI line became the Open Internet Stack in the data/3C destination and the Virtual Worlds/Web 4.0 line.
* [Horizon Europe Cluster 4 work programme timeline (2021–2027) for Open Internet Stack applicants](work-programme-timeline.md) - Adoption and amendment dates (with Commission decision numbers) of the Cluster 4 work programmes 2021-2022, 2023-2024, 2025 and 2026-2027, and the NGI/OIS call deadlines they produced.
* [Cascade funding (FSTP) in Horizon Europe — how it works for Open Internet Stack applicants](cascade-funding-explained.md) - How Financial Support to Third Parties (FSTP / "open calls" / cascade funding) works in Horizon Europe — the €60,000 default cap and the OIS exceptions (€150k, €400k), publication and openness rules, sub-grant contracts, milestone payments, reporting — and how it differs from joining a consortium grant.
* [Who wins Open Internet Stack and NGI grants](who-wins.md) - Data analysis of the 21 Horizon Europe NGI-era and Open Internet Stack grants (€132.4M EU contribution) plus WP2025/2026 evaluation statistics: who coordinates, from where, with what consortium size and budget split, which organisations recur, and what the winners have in common.
* [Complementary programmes for Open Internet Stack applicants](complementary-programmes.md) - Brief pointers to non-OIS funding an open-source internet project should also consider in late 2026 — Digital Europe (ECCC cybersecurity call, deadline 14 Jan 2027), EIC Accelerator/Pathfinder, Sovereign Tech Agency, Prototype Fund (DE/CH), NLnet's own non-EU funds — and how to reach National Contact Points.
