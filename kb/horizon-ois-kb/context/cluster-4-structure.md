# Summary

Cluster 4 "Digital, Industry and Space" is one of the six Pillar II clusters of Horizon Europe. Each work programme part is split into **destinations** (thematic headings with expected impacts), and destinations hold **topics**, which are bundled into **calls** that share an opening date and a deadline[^wp2627][^wp2025]. Open-internet funding lived for years in the "human-centred" destination under the NGI label. From WP 2025 it is split: the **Open Internet Stack (OIS)** sits in the **data/3C infrastructure destination**, while an OIS-flavoured Web 4.0 strand sits in the **human-centric / Virtual Worlds destination**[^wp2025][^wp2627].

# How the OIS is framed in WP 2026-2027

- The data destination says a sovereign Open Internet Stack, "rooted in open-source, interoperable and standard-based solutions", will reinforce trust and resilience and keep critical digital infrastructure under European control. It is paired with the 3C telco-edge-cloud continuum[^wp2627].
- The human-centric destination calls for a Web 4.0 architectural framework "underpinned by open-source solutions and shared standards"[^wp2627].
- Both destinations exclude legal entities established in China from RIAs and IAs, following the WP-wide General Annex B rule adopted for 2026-2027. OIS topics are additionally "subject to restrictions for the protection of European communication networks" (high-risk 5G suppliers excluded)[^wp2627][^ga2627].

# From NGI to OIS (lineage)

| Period | Branding | Typical instrument | Example winners |
|---|---|---|---|
| WP 2021-2022 | NGI (trust, search, architecture, international, outreach, review) | RIAs with at least 80% FSTP, max EUR 500k per third party | NGI0 Entrust, NGI Search, NGI0 Core, TrustChain, NGI Sargasso[^ft-search] |
| WP 2023-2024 | NGI Fund / Pilots / International / Commons Policy | Fund RIA with at least 80% FSTP; pilot IAs with at least 15% FSTP | NGI0 Commons Fund, TALER, MOBIFREE, Fediversity[^wp2324] |
| WP 2025 | "Open Internet Stack" (first use) plus Web 4.0 building blocks | One EUR 10M RIA, FSTP optional up to 70%, max EUR 400k per third party | Restack (NLnet); WISE4, ELFA, TWINE, CodeSupply[^wp2025][^ft-search] |
| WP 2026-2027 | OIS Sovereign Solutions, OIS Support for Scale, Web 4.0 + OIS applications | RIAs with FSTP up to 80% (DATA-02) or 70%/20% (HUMAN-02); lump-sum CSA | announced around end-2026[^wp2627] |

In July 2024 a working draft of WP 2025 no longer mentioned NGI, which triggered an open letter and press coverage[^register-ngi-cut][^netzpolitik-ngi]. A May 2025 report prepared for DG CNECT, "From NGI to Open Internet Stack", set out the policy case and budget scenarios (EUR 0–50M per year) for operationalising the stack[^mackintosh-ois].

# Where OIS money goes now

- All three OIS-branded WP 2026-2027 topics had their deadline on 15 April 2026. A portal search finds no OIS-branded topic with a 2027 deadline[^ft-search].
- Remaining practical routes: cascade (FSTP) calls run by the winners (Restack and CodeSupply have deadlines on 3 November 2026; the 2026 winners are expected to open calls from 2027), and adjacent 2027 topics such as HORIZON-CL4-2027-04-DATA-08[^ft-search][^wp2627].
- Watch the planned WP 2026-2027 amendment, expected in autumn 2026[^accelopment-amend], and the next framework programme. Neither has been checked here for new OIS topics.
