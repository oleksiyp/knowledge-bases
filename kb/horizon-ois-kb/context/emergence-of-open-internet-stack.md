| Nov 2024 – Jan 2025 | NGI coordinators' consultation and "Next Generation Internet 2026 – Suggestions for future pilot programmes" (23 Jan 2025) call for expanding "the vision of an Open Internet Stack beyond the current 3C proposal"[^mackintosh-2025]. |

# How OIS is framed

**In the WP 2025 topic (DATA-11)** — as summarised in the DG CNECT report: technological commons of modular, scalable, secure **3C (connectivity, computing, communication) building blocks** to reduce vendor lock-in and enhance interoperability; a library of building blocks (specifications, hardware designs, software, middleware, data assets, tools); a **"compliance by design"** mechanism and a **regulatory test bed** (sandboxes, interoperability test bed); explicitly complementary to top-down AI/HPC/data/cloud/quantum programmes and aimed at "a population of developers that are not usually associated with EU funding"[^mackintosh-2025].

**In the WP 2026-2027 destination text**: "Building a sovereign Open Internet Stack, rooted in open-source, interoperable and standard-based solutions, will reinforce trust, resilience and innovation, while ensuring Europe retains control over critical digital infrastructures"[^wp7-2026]. Topics emphasise "Open source and made in Europe", ease of deployment, interoperability, cataloguing, app-store availability, alignment with EU regulation, security/accessibility audits, branding and a policy sandbox[^wp7-2026].

**In the Commission's topic briefings**: "Open Internet Stack is a new initiative starting from WP25, that intends to leverage the strong and active communities of European Open Source innovators which were supported in previous NGI topics"[^si-fiches].

**In the 2026 Open Source Strategy**: OIS is "a shared catalogue, a one-stop shop for open source building blocks and sovereign solutions in Europe"; the Commission says it mobilised **€41.3 million** in WP 2026-2027 through three OIS calls (Sovereign Solutions, Support for Scale, Web 4.0/OIS applications)[^com-2026-503].

# What changed versus NGI

| NGI (2018–2024) | OIS (2025–) |
|---|---|
| Research-and-experimentation, "Internet of humans" | Deployment, sovereignty, alternatives to non-EU platforms |
| Many small grants (often €5–50k) via cascade | Larger RIAs with cascade tickets up to €400k per third party (DATA-02) |
| Broad topical scope | Three named technology areas in DATA-02; Web 4.0 areas in HUMAN-02 |
| Little support for maintenance/business | Explicit maintenance, cataloguing, sustainability, foundations, compliance |

Cascade sizes: DATA-02 conditions[^wp7-2026]; NGI Zero-style ranges as offered by NLnet's Restack (€5,000–50,000, with possibility to scale up)[^nlnet-ngi0] — see [/context/cascade-funding-explained.md](/context/cascade-funding-explained.md).
