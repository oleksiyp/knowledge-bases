# What happened

- **July 2024.** A leaked draft of the Horizon Europe **Work Programme 2025** for Cluster 4 no longer mentioned Next Generation Internet. FSFE reported that under the 2023-2025 Cluster 4 programme about **€27 million** had been allocated to NGI and that the draft did not renew this cascade funding[^fsfe-2024]. The Register reported the change on 17 July 2024; OW2 CEO Pierre-Yves Gibello called it "incomprehensible ... when NGI has proven efficient and economical to support free software", and the Commission declined to comment[^register-2024].
- The shift was widely read as a reallocation toward **AI**, leaving internet infrastructure underfunded[^fsfe-2024][^edri-2024].

# The open letter

- Published around **17 July 2024**, initiated by the association **petites singularités** with OW2 and the Matrix.org Foundation, and backed by projects across the NGI funding consortia[^matrix-letter]. Matrix says the letter was supported by "500+ funded projects across 18 European funding consortia"[^matrix-letter].
- Key arguments: NGI showed its strength "as a generic funding instrument to fund digital commons"; free and open-source software "is by design the opposite of potential vectors for foreign interference"; Europe needs its own software infrastructure[^matrix-letter].
- Widely mirrored by NGI-funded projects, e.g. F-Droid[^fdroid-letter], XWiki[^xwiki-letter], CryptPad[^cryptpad-letter], Deuxfleurs[^deuxfleurs-letter] and NextGraph[^nextgraph-letter].
- Evidence used by campaigners: NGI's July 2024 impact figures — over 1,000 projects funded; 57% offering viable alternatives to market solutions; 74% continuing after funding ends[^fsfe-2024].
- FSFE followed up in August 2024 asking the Commission for a **dedicated budget for Free Software**[^fsfe-2024-08] and urged supporters to lobby national representatives while the WP was still in negotiation[^edri-2024].

# Outcome

- No formal Commission reply to the letter was found. The answer came through the work programmes: the final **WP 2025** (call HORIZON-CL4-2025-03, deadline 2 Oct 2025) included **HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-DATA-11 "Open Internet Stack: development of technological commons/open-source 3C building blocks"** (RIA, €10M) and **HUMAN-16 "Drive the evolution of the internet towards open and interoperable Web 4.0 and Virtual Worlds: building blocks in priority areas"** (RIA, €14.5M)[^wp7-2025].
- Internally, a report prepared for DG CNECT in May 2025 records NGI coordinators' November 2024 consultation calling for more budget, a bottom-up dimension, operational infrastructure and sustainable business models under an "Open Internet Stack"[^mackintosh-2025].
- The rebranding continued with dedicated OIS topics in WP 2026-2027 and the OIS's central role in the June 2026 Open Source Strategy — see [/context/emergence-of-open-internet-stack.md](/context/emergence-of-open-internet-stack.md) and [/context/tech-sovereignty-package-2026.md](/context/tech-sovereignty-package-2026.md).

# Lessons for applicants

- Community mobilisation measurably shapes Cluster 4 content; demonstrating impact (users, policy alignment, alternatives to Big Tech) remains the currency.
- The money moved from many small NGI calls to fewer, larger, deployment-oriented OIS grants (often with bigger cascade tickets), which changes who can compete directly ([/context/who-wins.md](/context/who-wins.md)).
