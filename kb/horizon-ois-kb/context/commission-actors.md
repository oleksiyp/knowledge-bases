# Policy owner: DG CONNECT (DG CNECT)

- The **Directorate-General for Communications Networks, Content and Technology** is responsible for the Next Generation Internet initiative[^whoiswho-e3] and is named, together with HaDEA, as the **granting authority** in the OIS topics (e.g. for notifications of ownership transfers)[^wp7-2026].
- **Directorate E "Future Networks"**, **Unit E.3**: historically called "Next-Generation Internet" — Olivier Bringer was introduced in 2018 as Deputy and Acting Head of the NGI Unit[^ngi-bringer-2018], and a 2023 Commission vacancy notice concerns Unit E3[^e3-call-2023]. The EU Whoiswho directory currently lists the unit as **"Future Internet (CNECT.E.3)"** with **Thibaut Kleiner as (acting) Head of Unit**, under the Deputy Director-General in charge of Directorates A, C and E[^whoiswho-e3]. Thibaut Kleiner spoke as a DG CONNECT Director at the NGI Forum 2025[^ngi-forum-2025]. Personnel listings change often — verify in Whoiswho before citing names.
- The May 2025 strategy report "From NGI to Open Internet Stack" was written for "Senior DG CNCT & EU Commission decision makers" (marked "For EN-CNECT")[^mackintosh-2025].

# Political level

- **Henna Virkkunen**, Executive Vice-President for **Tech Sovereignty, Security and Democracy**, committed at her November 2024 hearing to developing tech sovereignty and reducing dependencies; the OIS was acknowledged there as "open services and platforms which are an alternative to proprietary solutions"[^mackintosh-2025].
- The 3 June 2026 Tech Sovereignty Package and Open Source Strategy ([/context/tech-sovereignty-package-2026.md](/context/tech-sovereignty-package-2026.md)) set the political mandate; the Commission's internal open-source policy is run by the **EC Open Source Programme Office (OSPO)**, with code.europa.eu and the EU Open Source Solutions Catalogue[^ec-news-0603].

# Implementation: HaDEA

- The **European Health and Digital Executive Agency (HaDEA)** runs Cluster 4 digital calls: it publishes submission statistics and results and prepares grant agreements with successful consortia (e.g. the HORIZON-CL4-2025-04 results)[^hadea-2025-04-results]. The OIS topic texts name both DG CNECT and HaDEA as the granting authority[^wp7-2026], i.e. HaDEA manages the grants while DG CONNECT steers policy.
- Questions on a topic go to the **Research Enquiry Service** (link on the topic page)[^ft-data11].

- **NGI Outreach Office** — its Director, Monique Calisti (CEO, Martel Innovate), spoke at the NGI Forum 2025[^ngi-forum-2025]; see [/organizations/martel-innovate.md](/organizations/martel-innovate.md).
