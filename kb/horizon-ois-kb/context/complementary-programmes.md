# Why look beyond OIS

OIS cascade money comes in small slices (≤€50k for a first grant) on bimonthly cycles, and the big
consortium topics only open once a year. Most sustainable open-source internet projects stack several
sources. EU rules forbid funding the same costs twice, so split the work cleanly between funders.

# Digital Europe Programme (DIGITAL)

- **ECCC cybersecurity call DIGITAL-ECCC-2027-DEPLOY-CYBER-11**: open 1 September 2026 – **14 January 2027,
  17:00 CET**, €96M over seven topics. Relevant ones: secure AI tools (€15M), AI-powered solutions for
  SMEs (CYBER-11-AI4SME, €20M), and EU cybersecurity legislation compliance (CYBER-11-EULEG, €20M), which
  is relevant to CRA-compliance tooling for open source.[^eccc-cyber11] The previous call (CYBER-09,
  €50M) closed 31 March 2026.[^eccc-cyber09]
- DIGITAL grants are deployment-oriented and usually need a consortium and co-financing. Some topics
  (e.g. SME uptake) themselves run FSTP, so watch the F&T Portal "cascade funding" filter.[^ft-portal]

# European Innovation Council (EIC)

- **EIC Accelerator** for start-ups and SMEs, including natural persons who intend to set one up. It
  offers a grant below €2.5M (lump sum, 24 months) plus optional equity. 2026 full-proposal cut-offs:
  7 Jan, 4 Mar, 6 May, 8 Jul, 2 Sep and **4 Nov 2026**. Short proposals (12-page form, pitch deck,
  3-minute video) can be submitted any time.[^eic-acc] Suits an open-source company with a business
  model, not a pure commons project.
- **EIC Pathfinder Open 2026** (deep-tech research consortia, up to €4M) closed 12 May 2026. Pathfinder
  Challenges close 28 October 2026.[^eic-2026-summary] These figures come from a secondary source; check
  them on eic.ec.europa.eu.

# Sovereign Tech Agency (Germany)

- **Sovereign Tech Fund**: takes applications on a rolling basis via apply.sovereign.tech. Minimum project
  size €50k. It funds maintenance and security of open-source *infrastructure* (libraries, package
  managers, crypto) worldwide, but not user-facing apps or prototypes. You cannot hold concurrent public
  funding for the same activities.[^sta-fund]
- Also: **Sovereign Tech Resilience** (audits, bug and fix bounties), **Sovereign Tech Fellowship**
  (maintainer funding) and **Sovereign Tech Standards** (standards participation pilot,
  June 2026–June 2027).[^sta-programs]
- A natural complement to Restack: NLnet funds new development, and STA funds maintenance and hardening
  of what is already widely used.

# Prototype Fund

- **Germany**: open-source prototypes in public-interest tech. Up to €95k for teams over 6 months, with a
  second stage possible. Applicants need a registered presence in Germany, and German tax and bank details
  are required.[^ptf-wiki] Check prototypefund.de for the current round dates.[^ptf-de]
- **Switzerland**: Prototype Fund Switzerland runs a 2026–2027 edition. See its schedule.[^ptf-ch]

# NLnet's own (non-EU) funds

NLnet also runs funds that are not EU money, such as the Research and Higher Education Technology Fund
(with the Vietsch Foundation)[^nlnet-nren] and the Open Social Fund (private money, currently not
accepting proposals).[^nlnet-opensocial] They use the same submission form and the same deadline
rhythm, the 3rd of every odd month.[^nlnet-funding]

# National Contact Points (NCPs) and partner search

- Every country has Horizon Europe Cluster 4 NCPs who give free advice on eligibility, consortium
  building and proposal checks. Find yours on the NCP Portal Cluster 4 page.[^ncp-c4]
- **Ideal-ist** is the ICT NCP network and runs partner search for digital topics. Use it if you want to
  move from cascade funding into a consortium proposal (e.g. future OIS RIA/CSA topics).[^idealist]
