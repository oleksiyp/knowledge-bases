# Track record and current programmes
- Across five NGI Zero programmes NLnet reports nearly 8,500 applications and 1,215 funded projects, plus 215 projects from adjacent NGI programmes, and "over a million hours" of FOSS development[^nlnet-stocktaking].
- It paused its open calls in June 2026 for a stocktaking while moving "from NGI to Open Internet Stack"; the NGI Zero Commons Fund's final call closed 1 June 2026[^nlnet-stocktaking][^nlnet-commonsfund].
- In January 2026 it announced selection for three Horizon Europe grants — Restack, ELFA and CodeSupply[^nlnet-three-grants]. As of August 2026 NLnet has withdrawn from the ELFA consortium and ELFA's open calls are postponed[^nlnet-elfa].
- Open now: Restack (€7M to 2030) and CodeSupply (€400k) calls, both €5k–50k per project, deadline 3 Nov 2026 12:00 CET[^nlnet-restack][^nlnet-codesupply]. NLnet runs deadlines every two months on odd months[^nlnet-call-nov2026].
- Its model: very large coordinator share (78–87% of the EU contribution in the NGI Zero/Restack grants) because the budget is passed on as small grants, with a fixed circle of support partners (security audits, accessibility, licensing, packaging, outreach)[^cordis-101299072][^cordis-101135429].

## Additional notes (cascade funding)

NLnet runs the two Open Internet Stack cascade funds that are open now. Both have a deadline of **3 November 2026, 12:00 CET**:
[Restack](/cascade/nlnet-restack.md) (€7M; €5k–€50k first grants, up to €150k per proposal and €500k per third party over the
programme) and [CodeSupply](/cascade/nlnet-codesupply.md) (€400k; max €60k per third party). NLnet withdrew from
[ELFA](/cascade/elfa-open-calls.md) in August 2026, so ELFA's open calls are postponed. Closed NLnet-run NGI funds:
[Commons Fund](/cascade/ngi-zero-commons-fund.md) (final 2026-06-01), [TALER](/cascade/ngi-taler.md) and
[Fediversity](/cascade/ngi-fediversity.md) (final 2026-08-01), [Mobifree](/cascade/ngi-mobifree.md) (final 2025-12-01),
[Core](/cascade/ngi-zero-core.md), [Entrust](/cascade/ngi-zero-entrust.md). How NLnet grants are paid (milestone-based
donations under an MoU): [cascade funding explained](/context/cascade-funding-explained.md).
