# Organizations

Coordinators and recurring partners of Open Internet Stack (WP2025) and NGI-era Horizon Europe grants, ranked by number of coordinations then participations. Figures from CORDIS.

* [NLnet Foundation (Stichting NLnet)](nlnet.md) - Amsterdam-based public-benefit foundation that has coordinated every NGI Zero fund and now runs the Open Internet Stack cascade funds (Restack, CodeSupply). (11 grant(s), 8 as coordinator.)
* [Martel Innovate BV](martel-innovate.md) - Amsterdam SME of the Martel group; coordinated the NGI4ALL.E outreach CSA and the NGI Commons policy CSA. (2 grant(s), 2 as coordinator.)
* [imec](imec.md) - Belgian research centre; coordinator of TWINE and partner in NOVA and EURO-3C. (3 grant(s), 1 as coordinator.)
* [Aarhus University](aarhus-university.md) - Danish university; coordinator of NGI Search. (1 grant(s), 1 as coordinator.)
* [Centric Netherlands BV](centric-netherlands.md) - Dutch IT company; coordinator of the Local for Local NGI pilot. (1 grant(s), 1 as coordinator.)
* [Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e)](eindhoven-university-of-technology.md) - Dutch technical university; coordinator of the TALER pilot (GNU Taler). (1 grant(s), 1 as coordinator.)
* [European Dynamics Luxembourg SA](european-dynamics.md) - IT company (Luxembourg entity with a Greek affiliated entity); coordinator of NGI TrustChain. (1 grant(s), 1 as coordinator.)
* [European Science Foundation (ESF)](european-science-foundation.md) - Strasbourg-based organisation; coordinator of NGI Sargasso. (1 grant(s), 1 as coordinator.)
* [Exascale Performance Systems (EXAPSYS)](exapsys.md) - Greek SME; coordinator of WISE4 (open RISC-V hardware for Web 4.0). (1 grant(s), 1 as coordinator.)
* [GAC Group](gac-group.md) - French SME (Issy-les-Moulineaux); coordinator of NGI Enrichers. (1 grant(s), 1 as coordinator.)
* [PNO Innovation SL](pno-innovation.md) - Barcelona company; coordinator of NOVA (Virtual Worlds Partnership CSA). (1 grant(s), 1 as coordinator.)
* [South East Technological University (SETU)](south-east-technological-university.md) - Irish university (Waterford); coordinator of NGI TRANSOCEANIC (and earlier NGIAtlantic.eu). (1 grant(s), 1 as coordinator.)
* [Telefónica Innovación Digital SL](telefonica-innovacion-digital.md) - Telefónica subsidiary; coordinator of the €75M EURO-3C large-scale pilot that the OIS topic DATA-11 (Restack) is meant to serve. (1 grant(s), 1 as coordinator.)
* [University of Passau](university-of-passau.md) - German university; coordinator of OpenWebSearch.eu. (1 grant(s), 1 as coordinator.)
* [e Foundation](e-foundation.md) - Paris-based foundation; coordinator of MOBIFREE. (1 grant(s), 1 as coordinator.)
* [Association for Progressive Communications (APC)](association-for-progressive-communications.md) - Association for Progressive Communications, registered in Gurb, Spain; partner in every NGI Zero consortium and in Restack. (5 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [Center for the Cultivation of Technology gGmbH](center-for-the-cultivation-of-technology.md) - Berlin non-profit; partner in every NGI Zero consortium and in Restack. (5 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [Commons Caretakers BV](commons-caretakers.md) - Amsterdam SME; recurring NGI Zero and Restack partner. (5 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [Free Software Foundation Europe (FSFE)](fsfe.md) - Berlin-based free-software advocacy charity; licensing and REUSE-compliance partner in every NGI Zero consortium and in Restack. (5 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [HAN University of Applied Sciences](han-university-of-applied-sciences.md) - Dutch university of applied sciences (Arnhem/Nijmegen); partner in every NGI Zero consortium and in Restack. (5 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [NixOS Foundation](nixos-foundation.md) - Foundation behind Nix/NixOS; partner in every NGI Zero consortium and in Restack. (5 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [Radically Open Security BV](radically-open-security.md) - Dutch security-consultancy SME; partner in every NGI Zero consortium and in Restack, where it holds one of the largest partner budgets. (5 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [Tolerant Networks Ltd](tolerant-networks.md) - Irish SME; recurring NGI Zero and Restack consortium partner. (5 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [Brno University of Technology (VUT)](brno-university-of-technology.md) - Czech technical university; partner in NGI0 Entrust, Core and Commons Fund. (3 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [OW2](ow2.md) - Paris-based open-source community association; partner in NGI Search, NGI0 Commons Fund and Restack. (3 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [OpenForum Europe (OFE)](openforum-europe.md) - Brussels open-technology policy organisation; partner in NGI0 Commons Fund, NGI Commons and Restack. (3 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [Petites Singularités](petites-singularites.md) - Brussels-based organisation; partner in NGI0 Entrust, NGI0 Core and the TALER pilot. (3 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [Stichting Edsger Institute](edsger-institute.md) - Dutch foundation; major beneficiary in Fediversity, CodeSupply and Restack. (3 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [APELL (European Open Source Software Business Association)](apell.md) - Brussels-based association of European open-source business federations; partner in NGI0 Commons Fund and Restack. (2 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [EURESCOM GmbH](eurescom.md) - Heidelberg telecom research institute; partner in NGI TRANSOCEANIC and EURO-3C. (2 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [FundingBox](fundingbox.md) - Polish/Spanish group (FundingBox Accelerator Sp. z o.o. / FundingBox Communities SL); partner in NGI Search and NGI4ALL.E. (2 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [AboutCode Europe ASBL](aboutcode-europe.md) - Belgian non-profit (ASBL) of the AboutCode open-source community; largest beneficiary of CodeSupply. (1 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [Eclipse Foundation Europe GmbH](eclipse-foundation-europe.md) - European open-source foundation; partner in WISE4. (1 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [GÉANT Association](geant.md) - Association of the pan-European research and education network; new partner in Restack. (1 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [NextGraph](nextgraph.md) - French organisation behind the NextGraph local-first platform; technical core of ELFA. (1 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [Stichting Open Internet Discourse](open-internet-discourse.md) - Dutch foundation; second-largest beneficiary of the Fediversity pilot. (1 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
* [Taler Systems SA](taler-systems.md) - Luxembourg SME behind GNU Taler; second-largest beneficiary in the TALER pilot. (1 grant(s), 0 as coordinator.)
