# Summary

NGI TALER is a Horizon Europe pilot (grant **101135475**, HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-12 "Pilots for the
Next Generation Internet"). It runs 1 December 2023 – 31 August 2027 with an EU contribution of about
€4.5M, coordinated by Technische Universiteit Eindhoven.[^cordis-taler] NLnet ran its open calls for
FOSS work around **GNU Taler**: new capabilities, tools, UX, integrations into FOSS applications and
infrastructure. At least **€676,000** was available in €5k–€50k grants.[^nlnet-taler]

During NLnet's mid-2026 pause the Taler calls stayed open on a bimonthly schedule.[^nlnet-stocktaking]
Then **the 14th and final call closed on 1 August 2026**.[^nlnet-taler]
