# Summary

NGI Local for Local integrates open-source NGI building blocks into the "Local for Local" software
stack, which includes a blockchain-based infrastructure, a modular platform and the Locasus-B app. The
stack is piloted in five European regions. The project finances, mentors and supports NGI innovators
picked through open calls.[^ngi-l4l] Coordinator: Centric Netherlands. Partners include KTU, Aix-Marseille
University and TU Delft.[^ngi-l4l]

The EU F&T Portal lists two cascade calls: "Local for Local Cascade Funding" (1 May – 31 July 2024) and
"NGI Local for Local Currency Ecosystem" (1 December 2024 – 1 February 2025). Both are
closed.[^ft-l4l-1][^ft-l4l-2] No further calls were found. The project ends in November 2026 (see the
project page).
