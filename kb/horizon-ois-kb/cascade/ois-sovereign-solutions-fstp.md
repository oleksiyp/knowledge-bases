# Summary

The 2026–2027 Work Programme contains the OIS follow-up topic **HORIZON-CL4-2026-04-DATA-02 "Open
Internet Stack Sovereign Solutions" (RIA)**: €20.50M in total, €7.00–10.25M per project, about two
projects. The call opened 15 January 2026 and closed **15 April 2026**.[^wp2627] The topic lets
beneficiaries pass **up to 80% of the requested EU contribution** to third parties, **up to €400,000 per
third party**. That high cap is there so one entity can receive FSTP several times, including across
grants, and so projects can reach maturity.[^wp2627]

The expected outcome is "a large selection of Open-Source solutions that will be organised under the
Open Internet Stack framework built under WP 2025".[^wp2627] If applicants use FSTP, the calls should
"primarily target ... internet innovators and adopters of Open Source technologies". Management and
support tasks cannot be paid from the FSTP budget.[^wp2627]

Evaluation is finished: 31 proposals were submitted, 24 were above threshold, **3 were retained** and 1
is on the reserve list. The winners had not been published on CORDIS as of 2026-10-03.[^tt-data02] See
[who wins](/context/who-wins.md).

# Eligibility

Not yet defined. It will be set in the winning projects' open calls. The topic carries "restrictions for
the protection of European communication networks".[^wp2627]

# What it funds

Expected: maturing, adopting and scaling open-source OIS components, for both the supply side and the
demand side (3C providers and verticals).[^wp2627]

# How to apply

Not yet possible. A reasonable expectation, not confirmed: grant agreements in late 2026 or early 2027,
then first open calls in 2027. Watch the F&T Portal cascade-funding listings and ngi.eu/opencalls.

# Deadlines

None published.

# Fit

The biggest new OIS cascade pot in the pipeline. It is likely aimed at projects with a working product
that need adoption or scale funding, rather than first prototypes.
