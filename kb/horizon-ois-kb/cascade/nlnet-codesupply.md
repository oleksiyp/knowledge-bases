# Summary

**CodeSupply** is a Horizon Europe pilot (grant **101298846**, topic **HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-HUMAN-16**,
1 June 2026 – 31 May 2029, €3.0M). It is building "an open source, federated, and digitally sovereign
database of software package metadata" covering origin, licensing, vulnerabilities and quality, using
Package-URLs as identifiers.[^cordis-codesupply] NLnet coordinates it. The other partners are AboutCode
Europe ASBL (BE), Stichting Edsger Institute (NL) and Universidad Rey Juan Carlos
(ES).[^cordis-codesupply]

**€400,000** of the budget goes to open calls for "auxiliary free and open source efforts" aligned with
the programme.[^nlnet-codesupply] **First call: opened 3 September 2026, deadline 3 November 2026,
12:00 CET**, listed on the F&T Portal as "CodeSupply open call (2026-11Y)".[^nlnet-codesupply][^ft-codesupply]

# Eligibility

- Independent researchers, developers and organisations. Results must be free/open source under a
  recognised licence and published as open access.[^nlnet-codesupply][^codesupply-guide]
- The proposal must fit CodeSupply's and the Open Internet Stack's goals, and research and development
  must be its primary objective. Call-specific hard criteria such as a European dimension
  apply.[^codesupply-guide]

# What it funds

New capabilities for CodeSupply software, auxiliary tools, user-experience improvements, integration
into FOSS applications, and improvements to the underlying stack.[^nlnet-codesupply] Eligible work
types include software development, research, security audits, formal proofs, documentation and
standardisation related to supply-chain security, metadata and licence compliance.

# Amounts and limits

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Per proposal | €5,000–€50,000[^nlnet-codesupply] |
| Max per third party (lifetime) | **€60,000**[^codesupply-guide] |
| Topic ceiling (WP 2025 HUMAN-16) | €150,000 per third party; FSTP ≤15% of EU contribution[^wp2025] |

CodeSupply chose the default €60k cap even though the topic would allow up to €150k.

# How to apply

Use the same NLnet proposal form as Restack, choosing the CodeSupply call. The evaluation also matches
Restack: criteria weighted 30/40/30, a 5.0/7.0 threshold, clarifying questions, then an independent
committee. Grants are paid as charitable donations against completed milestones, and projects over €50k
may need an independent security audit.[^codesupply-guide]

# Fit

Fits SBOM/SPDX/CycloneDX tooling, package-ecosystem metadata, vulnerability databases, licence scanners,
reproducible-build verification and PURL integrations. The €400k pot and €60k cap make it a small,
focused fund, so if your work is broader infrastructure, consider [Restack](/cascade/nlnet-restack.md).
