# Summary

NGI TransOcEanic planned three coordinated EU–NSF open calls worth **€3.2M**, funding at least 21
projects at about €150k each. EU partners would have been funded by NGI and US partners by
NSF.[^transoceanic-news] The project site still says "the 1st Open call has not launched yet", with
dates "TBC". EU proposals were to be capped at €150k.[^transoceanic-oc1] CORDIS lists the project as
**terminated**.[^cordis-transoceanic] Treat it as unavailable.
