# Summary

NGI Sargasso is grant **101092887** under HORIZON-CL4-2022-HUMAN-01-07 "NGI International Collaboration
– USA and Canada". It ran 1 January 2023 – 31 December 2025 with €6.0M, coordinated by the European
Science Foundation.[^cordis-sargasso] It ran five open calls and planned to support up to 96 projects
with up to €100k of equity-free funding plus up to 9 months of capacity building.[^ngi-sargasso] Each
project had to pair an EU team with a US and/or Canadian team.[^cascadefunding-sargasso5]

The **5th and final call** opened 23 September 2024 and **closed 25 November 2024**. It distributed up to
€1.2M in €50k–€100k grants and selected 13 teams from 104 applications.[^cascadefunding-sargasso5][^sargasso-oc5-results]
