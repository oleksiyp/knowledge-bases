# Summary

**Restack** is the main cascade fund of the Open Internet Stack and the successor to NGI Zero. NLnet
runs it. It funds "deliver, mature and scale" work on operational internet commons across the whole
stack, "from libre chips to middleware without a vendor lock-in, from productivity tools to 'local-first'
infrastructure", plus the end-user applications.[^nlnet-restack] NLnet will award **€7 million** in small
and medium R&D grants between now and 2030.[^nlnet-restack]

The parent grant is Horizon Europe **101299072** under topic **HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-DATA-11**. It runs
1 June 2026 – 31 May 2030, total cost €10.0M, coordinated by Stichting NLnet with partners including
Radically Open Security, Stichting Edsger Institute, Commons Caretakers, HAN University of Applied
Sciences and GÉANT.[^cordis-restack] NLnet's announcement also names FSFE, OW2 and the NixOS Foundation
among the partners.[^nlnet-three-grants]

**First call: open 3 September 2026, deadline 3 November 2026, 12:00 CET (noon)**, listed on the EU F&T
Portal as "Restack Open Call (2026-11R)".[^nlnet-restack][^ft-restack]

# Eligibility

- Anyone can apply: individuals, informal teams, companies, NGOs and research institutes. No legal
  entity or institutional affiliation is needed.[^restack-faq]
- Proposers from the EU and Horizon Europe associated countries get priority when proposals are
  otherwise equal. Proposals from elsewhere are eligible if they are of exceptional quality, the
  proposer has unique expertise, and there is a clear European dimension.[^restack-elig]
- Minors may apply. Guardian consent is only needed once negotiations start.[^restack-elig]
- All outputs must be released under a recognised free/open-source licence, and the work must have
  research and development as its primary objective.[^restack-guide]
- **AI-related projects are out of scope**, unless they already have more than 1 million active human
  users and are directly relevant to the stack.[^nlnet-restack]

# What it funds

Eligible activities include FOSS software and open hardware development, validation and security work
(audits, formal proofs, testing), documentation, standardisation (including membership fees), user
research and inclusive design, scientific research, attendance at events such as IETF or FOSDEM, and
project management.[^restack-elig] Besides money, grantees get support services: accessibility scans,
security audits, reproducible packaging, licence-compliance help and standardisation
assistance.[^nlnet-restack]

The DATA-11 topic asks that the funded portfolio feed the **3C (Connected Collaborative Computing)
large-scale pilot**. The thematic emphasis is trust technologies (PETs, identity), network and
connectivity, and decentralised technologies. The topic also expects maturing services: audits,
packaging, localisation and licensing advice.[^wp2025]

# Amounts and limits

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| First proposal | €5,000–€50,000[^restack-guide] |
| Any proposal > €50k | only after a successfully concluded earlier project in Restack or an equivalent (e.g. NGI) programme; may require a full independent security audit[^restack-guide] |
| Max per proposal | €150,000[^restack-guide] |
| Max per third party (lifetime) | €500,000, for a person or entity across all roles[^restack-guide][^restack-faq] |
| Topic ceiling (WP 2025) | €400,000 per third party; up to 70% of the EU contribution as FSTP[^wp2025] |

The €500k lifetime cap in NLnet's guide is higher than the €400k in the DATA-11 topic conditions. This
KB could not explain the difference, so confirm with NLnet before planning beyond €400k.

# How to apply

1. Read the Guide for Applicants and the eligibility page.
2. Submit through NLnet's proposal form (nlnet.nl/propose), choosing the Restack call, before 12:00 CET
   on the deadline.[^nlnet-restack]
3. Evaluation has two stages. First, a score on technical excellence/feasibility (30%),
   relevance/impact/strategic potential (40%) and cost-effectiveness (30%), with a 5.0/7.0 threshold.
   Second, shortlisted proposals get clarifying questions and a re-rating. An independent review
   committee validates the selection.[^restack-guide]
4. Expect **3–5 months** from the call deadline to a decision.[^restack-faq]
5. Selected projects sign a **Memorandum of Understanding** with milestones. Payment is made **after
   each milestone is completed**, not in advance. The default project length is 12 months, and the grant
   is a donation.[^restack-faq]

# Fit

Good fit for maintainers and small teams working on infrastructure-level FOSS: protocols, networking,
identity, email/DNS, operating-system and packaging, local-first tools, open hardware. It also suits
former NGI Zero grantees who want to scale up, since earlier NGI work counts as track
record.[^restack-guide] Poor fit for AI-centric projects, proprietary or open-core work, and
pure-commercialisation proposals.
