# Summary

NGI Enrichers is grant **101070125** under HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-08 and ran 1 September 2022 –
31 August 2025 with €2.0M, coordinated by GAC (FR). It was a transatlantic fellowship programme placing
70–90 European researchers in 3–6-month fellowships at US and Canadian institutions.[^cordis-enrichers]
Fellows received a living allowance (€3,800 per full month), travel and visa costs, plus bootcamps and
mentoring.[^euraxess-enrichers] The **3rd and last round closed on 10 May 2024**.[^enrichers-linkedin]
