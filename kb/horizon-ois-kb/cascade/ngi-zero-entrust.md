# Summary

NGI Zero Entrust is grant **101069594** under HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-04 and ran 1 August 2022 –
31 January 2026.[^cordis-entrust] It awarded **€9.6M** in €5k–€50k grants, supporting 242 FOSS projects
and providing 366 support services such as audits and accessibility reviews.[^nlnet-entrust] The 7th
and final call closed on 1 December 2023, and the programme ended in January 2026.[^nlnet-entrust]
