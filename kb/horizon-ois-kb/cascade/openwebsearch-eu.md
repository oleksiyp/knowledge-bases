# Summary

OpenWebSearch.EU is grant **101070014** under HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-05. It ran 1 September 2022 –
28 February 2026 with about €8.5M, coordinated by Universität Passau, and built the pilot **Open Web
Index (OWI)**.[^cordis-ows][^ows-end] Its "Community Programme" ran three third-party calls. Calls #2
(applications on the OWI) and #3 (compute/data-centre infrastructure) ran **8 February – 4 April 2024,
17:00 CET**, offering **€50k–€150k** for up to 12 months to individuals or organisations in EU Member
States or associated countries.[^nlnet-ows-call] Across the three calls, 15 third-party projects were
funded: nine technical, two infrastructure, one economic and three legal.[^ows-tag]

The project ended on 28 February 2026. The work continues as a broader "European Open Web Search
initiative", but no new third-party calls have been announced. OWI data is available through the
project portal (research use), and enquiries go to ows@openwebsearch.eu.[^ows-end]
