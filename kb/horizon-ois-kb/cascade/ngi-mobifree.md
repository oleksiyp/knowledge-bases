# Summary

NGI Mobifree is a Horizon Europe pilot (grant **101135795**). NLnet ran its open calls and offered
**€670,000** in €5k–€50k grants over nine calls. 26 projects with participants from 21 countries were
funded.[^nlnet-mobifree] **The final call (2025-12M) closed on 1 December 2025.**[^nlnet-mobifree][^ft-mobifree]
