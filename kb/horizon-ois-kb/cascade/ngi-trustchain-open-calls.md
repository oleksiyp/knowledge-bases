# Summary

NGI TrustChain is grant **101093274** under HORIZON-CL4-2022-HUMAN-01-03. It ran 1 January 2023 –
31 March 2026 with €10.6M, coordinated by European Dynamics Luxembourg SA.[^cordis-trustchain] It ran
**five open calls** (through F6S) on decentralised digital identity, trustworthy data and DLT, offering up
to €117k per call and up to €200k per applicant across calls. Funded projects lasted 9
months.[^trustchain-faq]

The **5th and final call** ("Green, scalable and sustainable DLTs") ran 11 November 2024 – **15 January
2025, 17:00 CET**, and 15 winners were announced.[^trustchain-oc5][^trustchain-faq]

# Eligibility

Historical: individuals, or applicants linked to a legal entity, from EU Member States, overseas
territories and associated countries.[^trustchain-faq]

# What it funds

Historical: decentralised identity, trust and DLT.

# How to apply
