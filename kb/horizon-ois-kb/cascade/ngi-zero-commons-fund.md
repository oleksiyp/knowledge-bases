# Summary

The NGI Zero Commons Fund was NLnet's flagship NGI cascade programme. It is Horizon Europe grant
**101135429** under HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-11 ("Next Generation Internet Fund"), running 1 January 2024
– 30 June 2027 with an EU contribution of about €27.5M.[^cordis-commons] NLnet set aside **€21.6M** for
grants through 2027, with extra money from the Swiss State Secretariat for Education, Research and
Innovation (SERI).[^nlnet-commons]

**The 13th and final call closed on 1 June 2026.** No new proposals are accepted.[^nlnet-commons]
NLnet then paused all open calls to take stock of ten years of NGI Zero: over 10,000 applications and
about 1,215 projects funded across five NGI Zero programmes. It said regular calls would resume after
summer 2026 under three Open Internet Stack programmes: Restack, CodeSupply and ELFA.[^nlnet-stocktaking]

# Eligibility

Historical: anyone, from individuals to organisations. Grants of €5k–€50k, scalable for proven
projects. Results had to be FOSS.[^nlnet-commons]

# What it funds

Historical: any FOSS, open-hardware or open-standards work that strengthens the internet commons.

# How to apply

Closed. Use [Restack](/cascade/nlnet-restack.md) instead. Successfully completed Commons Fund projects
count as "equivalent" track record for Restack proposals above €50k.
