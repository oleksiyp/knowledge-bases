# Summary

NGI Search is grant **101069364** under HORIZON-CL4-2021-HUMAN-01-05 "Trustworthy open search and
discovery". It ran 1 September 2022 – 31 August 2025 with €8.5M, coordinated by Aarhus University (DK).
The other partners were FundingBox Accelerator (PL), OW2 (FR), Linknovate (ES) and Universidad de Murcia
(ES).[^cordis-ngisearch] It ran **five open calls** and distributed up to **€6.8M**. Selected teams got
up to €150k plus a support programme of up to 9 months.[^ngisearch-oc5][^ngi-ngisearch]

The **5th and final call ran 1 June – 1 August 2024 (17:00 CEST)**.[^ngisearch-oc5] The project has
ended and there will be no further calls.

# Eligibility

Historical (OC5): natural persons, or entities registered in an EU Member State or Horizon Europe
associated country. Consortia could have at most 3 members. Caps were €50k per natural person and €150k
per entity, summed across all calls. Consortium partners and their affiliates were
excluded.[^ngisearch-oc5]

# What it funds

Historical: new search and discovery technology, results under open licences.[^ngisearch-oc5]

# How to apply

Closed. Search and discovery work now fits [Restack](/cascade/nlnet-restack.md) or uses the Open Web
Index (see [OpenWebSearch.eu](/cascade/openwebsearch-eu.md)).

# Deadlines

Final call: 2024-08-01, closed.[^ngisearch-oc5]
