# Summary

**ELFA (Encrypted Local-First Architecture)** is a three-year research programme that started in 2026.
It is building "a collaborative and decentralized open platform capable of providing private workspaces
and healthy social networking".[^nlnet-elfa] The project is grant **101298715** under
HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-HUMAN-16 (Area 4, alternatives to centralised platforms), starting 1 June 2026. It
targets a local-first, end-to-end-encrypted suite of about 16 applications: documents, spreadsheets,
email, calendar, chat, video, social, storage, forms and project management.[^elfa-site][^cordis-cl4-human16]
The consortium site lists NextGraph, Ura Design, IronCalc, OpenBlocks (BlockNote), Ananas Teknik,
Ontola, XWiki, Danube Tech and 2WEBAPP.[^elfa-site]

10% of the budget (**€300,000**) was reserved for open calls of €5k–€50k for auxiliary FOSS work, which
NLnet was to run as one of its three OIS programmes.[^nlnet-elfa][^nlnet-stocktaking]

# Status (October 2026)

In **August 2026 NLnet withdrew from the ELFA consortium**. "Open calls are postponed until further
notice, to be executed by another organisation."[^nlnet-elfa] The consortium site says only that
sub-granting "opens soon".[^elfa-site] CORDIS still listed NLnet as coordinator when this KB checked it,
so a change of coordinator may still be under way.[^cordis-cl4-human16]

# Eligibility

To be set by the new operator. Under the HUMAN-16 topic, support can be up to €150k per third party and
FSTP up to 15% of the EU contribution (see the [explainer](/context/cascade-funding-explained.md)).

# What it funds

Expected scope: FOSS work around local-first sync, CRDTs, end-to-end encryption, decentralised identity
(DID) and app integrations with the ELFA platform.[^nlnet-elfa][^elfa-site]

# How to apply

Not possible yet. Watch elfaconsortium.eu and the EU F&T Portal "cascade funding" listings, which every
FSTP call must appear on.
