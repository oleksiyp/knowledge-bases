# Cascade funds (FSTP / open calls)

Open calls run by Horizon Europe Open Internet Stack and NGI projects. This is the route for individuals,
SMEs and small open-source teams who will not join a consortium. Status as of 2026-10-03. How the
mechanism works (the €60k default cap and the OIS exceptions of €150k/€400k, contracts, milestone
payments) is in [/context/cascade-funding-explained.md](/context/cascade-funding-explained.md). Other
funders are in [/context/complementary-programmes.md](/context/complementary-programmes.md).

## Open now (next deadline 3 November 2026, 12:00 CET)
* [Restack (NLnet)](nlnet-restack.md) - €7M OIS fund (HORIZON-CL4-2025-03-DATA-11). €5k–€50k first grants, up to €150k per proposal and €500k per third party; individuals welcome; AI out of scope. Call 2026-11R.
* [CodeSupply (NLnet)](nlnet-codesupply.md) - €400k for software supply-chain security FOSS (HUMAN-16). €5k–€50k, max €60k per third party. Call 2026-11Y.
