# Summary

NGI Fediversity is grant **101136078** under HORIZON-CL4-2023-HUMAN-01-12. It runs 1 December 2023 –
30 November 2027 with a €3.0M EU contribution, coordinated by NLnet.[^cordis-fediversity] Its open calls
offered **€450,000** in €5k–€50k grants for service portability, personal data control, federated
services, hosting infrastructure, and open-standard, reproducible deployment.[^nlnet-fediversity]
**The 12th and final call closed on 1 August 2026.**[^nlnet-fediversity]

# Eligibility

Historical. Results had to be FOSS, libre hardware or open standards.[^nlnet-fediversity]

# What it funds

Historical: Fediverse hosting, reproducible packaging and service portability.

# How to apply

Closed. Restack's support services include reproducible packaging, and its scope covers this area (see
[Restack](/cascade/nlnet-restack.md)).
