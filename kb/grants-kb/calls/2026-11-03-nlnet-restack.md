# What the call funds

Innovations and enhancements to core internet infrastructure, alternatives to closed online technologies, security, reproducibility, standards, and devices to run them — all released under recognised FOSS licences. AI projects are excluded unless already in wide use (>1M users).[^nlnet-restack]
