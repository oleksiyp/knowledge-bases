# What the call funds

Scoping, establishing or securing community-governed open-source ecosystems around research products. It does not fund product development.[^nsf-26-506]

# Key dates

- Full proposals due September 1, 2026, 5 p.m. submitter local time.[^nsf-26-506]

# How to apply

Research.gov; titles start with "PESOSE: [Track]:"; I-Corps for PESOSE participation for Tracks 1–2.[^nsf-26-506]
