# What the call funds
Infrastructure and bandwidth costs of established, large-scale open-source VPN and anti-censorship tools serving users in censored countries such as China, Cuba, Iran, Myanmar and Russia. Staff costs are not covered.[^otf-sas]

# Key dates
- Opens: 15 Sep 2026
- Questions due: 28 Sep 2026
- Concept notes due: 15 Oct 2026
- Anticipated contract start: 1 Apr 2027[^otf-sas]

# How to apply
Stage 1 concept note through the OTF application system. Stage 2 is an invited proposal with user metrics and a cost model.[^otf-sas]
