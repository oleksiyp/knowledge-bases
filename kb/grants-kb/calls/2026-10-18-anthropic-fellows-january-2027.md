# What the call funds

Four months of full-time, mentored empirical research on scalable oversight, robustness, AI control, interpretability, AI security and model welfare. Fellows receive a stipend and a compute budget.[^fellows-post]

# How to apply

Apply through the track listings on Anthropic's Greenhouse board (London, Ontario, remote US, San Francisco).[^fellows-greenhouse] Applications are rolling, so a later application is considered for a later cohort.[^fellows-post]
