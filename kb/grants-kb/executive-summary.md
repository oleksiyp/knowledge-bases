# Bottom line

1. **There is real money for software in 2026, but it has moved.** The largest stable sources
   are now German and EU public funds:
   - [Sovereign Tech Fund](/programs/oss-infrastructure/sovereign-tech-fund.md)
   - [NLnet Restack](/programs/oss-infrastructure/nlnet-restack.md)
   - [Prototype Fund](/programs/oss-infrastructure/prototype-fund.md)
   - [EIC Accelerator](/programs/startup-rnd/eic-accelerator.md)

   AI labs and hyperscalers also pay for open-source security:
   - [Alpha-Omega](/programs/oss-infrastructure/alpha-omega-grants.md)
   - [OpenAI Daybreak](/programs/ai/openai-daybreak-frontline-defenders.md)
   - [Claude for Open Source](/programs/ai/anthropic-claude-for-open-source.md)

   US federal research and internet-freedom funding shrank and is now volatile.[^changes]
2. **130 of 247 programs accept applications now** (rolling or open), and 48 dated deadlines
   fall between now and July 2027.[^deadlines] Of the rest, 81 are between rounds and 26 are
   paused or discontinued.
3. **Security and maintenance framing wins.** New 2026 money is aimed at hardening, audits,
   supply chain and EU Cyber Resilience Act compliance, not new features.
4. **For a single open-source maintainer, the fastest path** is to:
   - apply to NLnet Restack or CodeSupply (€5k–€50k, closing 3 Nov);
   - apply to FLOSS/fund (rolling, via `funding.json`);
   - claim free AI tooling for maintainers;
   - if the project is critical infrastructure, pitch to the Sovereign Tech Fund or Alpha-Omega.[^start-here]
5. **For a software startup:**
   - **EU:** the EIC Accelerator (cut-off 4 Nov), national tools such as WBSO (NL), Ścieżka SMART (PL) and SPRIND (DE), and free EuroHPC GPU time.
   - **US:** NSF SBIR is back after its 2025–26 lapse (4 Nov).
   - **Everywhere:** stack the cloud and AI credit programs.
