# Summary

Through DG CONNECT and Horizon Europe the European Commission is the largest public funder of open internet software. Since 2018 its Next Generation Internet (NGI) initiative re-granted money to thousands of small FOSS projects via operators like NLnet; in 2026 this continues as the "Open Internet Stack" initiative.[^ngi-home][^nlnet-3grants] The 2026 Cluster 4 calls (HORIZON-CL4-2026-04, €221.8M across 15 topics; -05, €85.5M) included Open Internet Stack topics.[^hadea-2026]

# Programs

- [Horizon Europe Open Internet Stack calls](/programs/oss-infrastructure/horizon-europe-open-internet-stack.md) — large consortium grants.
- Cascade funds via operators: [NLnet Restack](/programs/oss-infrastructure/nlnet-restack.md), [CodeSupply](/programs/oss-infrastructure/nlnet-codesupply.md).[^ngi-opencalls]

# 2025–2026 changes

- NGI Zero funds wound down (Commons Fund final call June 2026); Open Internet Stack cascade calls opened Sept 2026.[^ngi-opencalls][^nlnet-3grants]
