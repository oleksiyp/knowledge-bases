# Summary
Through the Digital Democracy Initiative (DDI), CIVICUS runs rolling digital-resiliency grants of up to $10k ($20k for joint applications) and a cohort-based Digital Action Lab ($10k–$20k). Both are backed by the European Union, Danida and Norway.[^civicus-drg][^civicus-dal]

# 2025–2026 changes
- One of the European-funded channels still open while US democracy and media assistance was cut in 2025. Carnegie reports that 21% of affected media groups saw more donor funding in 2025, mainly from European donors, while 48% saw less.[^carnegie-media-2026]
