# Summary

The Ethereum Foundation funds protocol research, client development and ecosystem projects. For individuals, its Ethereum Protocol Fellowship cohort 7 runs from June to November 2026 and pays stipends to selected fellows.[^epf7]

## Additional notes (web3)
### Summary
The Ethereum Foundation (EF) funds Ethereum public goods mainly through its Ecosystem Support Program (ESP), which allocated ≈$9.86M in Q1 2026 and ≈$5.5M in Q2 2026 to client, ZK, cryptography, security and tooling work. ([source](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/ethereum-foundation-announces-9-85m-q1-2026-ecosystem-funding)) ([source](https://blog.ethereum.org/2026/08/18/allocation-q2-26)) It also runs an annual PhD Fellowship ($24K). ([source](https://esp.ethereum.foundation/rounds/phdfp26))

### 2025–2026 changes
- Aug 2025: ESP paused open grant applications to reassess priorities. ([source](https://blog.ethereum.org/category/ecosystem-support-program))
- Nov 2025: relaunched with a Wishlist + RFP model (no unsolicited proposals). ([source](https://blog.ethereum.org/category/ecosystem-support-program))
- From 10 Jun 2026: new grants paid in ETH by default. ([source](https://ethdaily.io/ef-esp-pays-grants-in-eth))
- EF described as entering "mild austerity" in 2026 (per press coverage of Vitalik Buterin's separate $45M commitment). ([source](https://www.opensourceforu.com/2026/02/ethereum-co-founder-vitalik-buterin-puts-45m-into-open-source-privacy-and-verifiable-systems/))
