# Summary

NASA's Science Mission Directorate (SMD) funds research through the annual ROSES omnibus. Under its Open-Source Science Initiative it created elements for open-source tools (F.7 OSTFL), high-priority open-source science (HPOSS), supplements (SOSS) and Transform to Open Science training (TOPS-T).[^nasa-os-funding]

# Programs

- [OSTFL and other open-source science elements](/programs/research-software/nasa-roses-ostfl.md): not currently solicited

# 2025–2026 changes

- NASA lists every open-source science element as not currently or no longer solicited.[^nasa-os-funding]
- ROSES-2026 was planned for July 2026 but was still pending at the end of August 2026.[^roses26-july][^granted-roses26]
