# Summary
DDP runs incident-response, regional, sustainable-protection and community funds for defenders under digital threat. It is hosted by Hivos and funded by around ten governments, which have included the US State Department.[^hivos-ddp][^ddp-home]

# 2025–2026 changes
- In October 2026 the Incident Response, Regional Partnership and Sustainable Protection funds were marked CLOSED, and the Community & Network Fund was invitation-only.[^ddp-home]
- Sweden (Sida) has committed SEK 32M for 2024–2028.[^openaid-ddp]
