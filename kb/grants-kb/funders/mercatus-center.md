# Summary

Mercatus runs Emergent Ventures, which has awarded rolling, low-paperwork grants to individuals since 2018.[^ev-home] It also housed Fast Grants, the COVID-era science grants program.[^fast-grants-wiki]

# Programs

- [Emergent Ventures](/programs/individuals/emergent-ventures.md)

# 2025–2026 changes

- Emergent Ventures stayed very active through 2026, with cohorts 51 to 59 announced.[^ev-cohort-59]
- Fast Grants (2020) raised over $50M and made about 260 grants before suspending. It is not an open program.[^fast-grants-wiki]
