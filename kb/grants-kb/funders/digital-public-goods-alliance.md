# Summary
The DPGA does **not** make grants. It maintains the DPG Standard and a Registry of 255 recognised digital public goods (open-source software, open data, open AI systems and open content) and has 56 members.[^dpga-home] It advocates for financing DPGs.[^dpga-financing] It co-founded the Co-Develop DPI fund.[^codev-faq]

# Programs
- None directly. Funding pathways: (1) get your open-source project recognised in the DPG Registry; (2) use that status with DPI funders such as [Co-Develop](/programs/public-interest/co-develop.md), the [UNICEF Venture Fund](/programs/public-interest/unicef-venture-fund.md) and [Gates Grand Challenges](/programs/public-interest/gates-grand-challenges.md); (3) contact hello@digitalpublicgoods.net about member matchmaking.[^dpga-home]

# 2025–2026 changes
- Canada, through the Canadian Digital Service, joined the DPGA in June 2026.[^cds-dpga-2026]
