# Summary

The Software Sustainability Institute (founded 2010) was renamed the **Institute for Research Software** on September 1, 2026, with a launch event on October 20, 2026. Its motto stays "Better Software, Better Research".[^irs-rename] It runs the annual Fellowship Programme and delivers the UKRI-funded Research Software Maintenance Fund.[^ssi-fellows-2027][^ssi-rsmf-r2-awards]
