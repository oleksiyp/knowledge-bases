# Summary

Google's Open Source Programs Office runs Google Summer of Code (GSoC), the largest paid-mentorship program for new open-source contributors. In 2026 GSoC had 185 organizations[^gsoc-2026-orgs] and 1,141 contributors.[^gsoc-2026-contributors]

# Programs

- [Google Summer of Code](/programs/individuals/google-summer-of-code.md): $750–$6,600 stipends.
- [Google Open Source Peer Bonus](/programs/oss-infrastructure/google-open-source-peer-bonus.md): small awards, by nomination only.

# 2025–2026 changes

- Google Season of Docs, which paid technical writers to work on open-source docs, "has concluded after six great years" (2019–2024).[^gsod-ended]
- GSoC continued in 2026 at a record scale of 23,371 proposals.[^gsoc-2026-contributors]

## Additional notes (ai)

- [TPU Research Cloud](/programs/ai/google-tpu-research-cloud.md) gives free Cloud TPU quota on a rolling basis, provided results are shared openly.[^trc-faq]
- [Gemini Academic Program](/programs/ai/google-gemini-academic-program.md) gives Gemini API credits and higher rate limits to academics, reviewed monthly.[^gemini-research]
- [Google for Startups Cloud Program](/programs/ai/google-for-startups-cloud-ai.md) offers up to $350k in credits for AI-first startups.[^gfs-devto]
- [Google.org AI Impact Challenges](/programs/ai/google-org-ai-impact-challenges.md): $30M AI for Science and AI for Government calls in 2026, both closed in April.[^gorg-science]
