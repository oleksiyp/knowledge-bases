# Summary

The eScience Center funds research mainly with **in-kind research software engineers** rather than cash. It runs the annual Open eScience Call and, from 2026, a €5.4M Research Software Sustainability Programme funded by Open Science NL.[^esc-calls][^osnl-esc]
