## Additional notes (research-software)

NSF is the largest U.S. funder of academic research software outside biomedicine. Within CISE, the Office of Advanced Cyberinfrastructure (OAC) runs CSSI and other cyberinfrastructure programs. The Directorate for Technology, Innovation and Partnerships (TIP) runs the open-source ecosystem program, renamed PESOSE in NSF 26-506.[^nsf-26-506][^nsf-cssi-page]

Research-software programs:

- [PESOSE (formerly POSE)](/programs/research-software/nsf-pesose.md): open; next deadline 2027-03-02
- [CSSI](/programs/research-software/nsf-cssi.md): paused, waiting for a new solicitation
- [IDSS](/programs/research-software/nsf-oac-idss.md): next deadline 2027-07-27
- [CICI](/programs/research-software/nsf-oac-cici.md): next deadline 2027-01-20
- [BSSw Fellowship](/programs/research-software/bssw-fellowship.md): co-funded with DOE

- In April–May 2025 NSF terminated more than 1,000 active grants it judged out of line with new priorities, and added DEI-related award certifications.[^cen-nsf-terminations] The Carpentries withdrew a POSE proposal that had been recommended for funding rather than accept those terms.[^carpentries-pose]
- FY2026 appropriations were enacted in January 2026.[^aip-fy26-nsf]
- POSE became PESOSE, adding a security track. CSSI did not get a 2026 deadline and is waiting for a new solicitation.[^nsf-26-506][^nsf-cssi-page]
