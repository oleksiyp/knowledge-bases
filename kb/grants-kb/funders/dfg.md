# Summary

DFG treats research software as a research infrastructure topic. Its permanent *Forschungssoftwareinfrastrukturen* programme (2024–) has March and August submission dates.[^dfg-fsi][^dfg-rs-context] In 2026 DFG also issued a call for projects at the interface between research and scientific information provision (Information for Researchers No. 39, June 15, 2026).[^dfg-ifr-26-39]
