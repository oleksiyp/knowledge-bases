# Summary
The ISOC Foundation runs about eleven grant programmes: Beyond the Net, BOLT, Chapter Admin, Community-Centered Connectivity, the Common Good Cyber Fund, Global Encryption Day, IGF events, Research, Resiliency, SCILLS and Sustainable Peering Infrastructure.[^isocf-areas]

# 2025–2026 changes
- The Common Good Cyber Fund moved from its 2025 pilot (six grants) to a 2026 open call of at least $3.5M. It is part of a Safer Internet Initiative of more than $40M for 2026–2029.[^cgcf-2026-pr]
- BOLT is no longer accepting applications. Applicants are pointed to Community-Centered Connectivity instead.[^isocf-bolt]
