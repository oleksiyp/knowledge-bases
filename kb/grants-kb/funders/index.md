# Funders

* [1517 Fund](1517-fund.md) - 1517 Fund is a venture fund backing young and non-credentialed founders. Its Medici Project gives no-strings $1,000+ microgrants to students and dropouts in North America.
* [28DIGITAL (formerly EIT Digital)](28digital.md) - Former EIT Knowledge and Innovation Community for digital, independent since Oct 2025 under the 28DIGITAL brand; runs venture incubation and education programs.
* [Aave DAO](aave-dao.md) - Governance DAO of the Aave protocol; its Aave Grants DAO is sunset and funding now flows to Aave Labs under the 'Aave Will Win' framework.
* [Access Now](access-now.md) - Digital-rights NGO (organiser of RightsCon) that regrants to grassroots digital-rights groups in low- and middle-income countries.
* [Amazon Web Services (AWS)](amazon-web-services.md) - Provides promotional AWS credits for academic research and startups, including the Generative AI Accelerator (up to $1M in credits).
* [Anthropic](anthropic.md) - AI lab funding open-source maintainers (Claude Max, Project Glasswing), science researchers (API credits), AI-safety fellows and startups, mostly in kind.
* [APNIC Foundation](apnic-foundation.md) - Foundation of the Asia-Pacific Regional Internet Registry; runs ISIF Asia grants for Internet development in 56 Asia-Pacific economies.
* [Aptos Foundation](aptos-foundation.md) - Supports the Aptos network with milestone-based Ecosystem Grants, Payments Grants (paused) and the Aptos Assembly sprint.
* [Arbitrum Foundation / ArbitrumDAO](arbitrum-foundation.md) - Supports the Arbitrum ecosystem; DAO-funded programs include the D.A.O. (ex-Questbook) grants, the $10M Audit Program (closed Jul 2026), ArbiFuel and partner credits.
* [ARIA (Advanced Research + Invention Agency)](aria.md) - UK R&D funding agency. Its £59M Safeguarded AI programme funds AI-assisted formal verification, currently through a £20M rolling cybersecurity call.
* [Astera Institute](astera-institute.md) - Astera Institute is a philanthropic institute in Emeryville, California. It runs a fully funded residency for open, non-proprietary science and AI projects, with large compute access.
* [Australian Department of Industry, Science and Resources (DISR)](australia-disr.md) - Australian department co-administering the R&D Tax Incentive (with ATO) and the Industry Growth Program.
* [Avalanche Foundation](avalanche-foundation.md) - Supports Avalanche; ended the $40M Retro9000 program and now lists Team1 grants, research grants, dev credits and audit subsidies.
* [Bezos Earth Fund](bezos-earth-fund.md) - Climate and nature philanthropy; runs the $100M AI for Climate and Nature Grand Challenge.
* [Bloomberg](bloomberg.md) - Financial data company whose OSPO runs the employee-voted FOSS Contributor Fund ($10k grants, quarterly, since 2023).
* [German Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy (BMWE)](bmwe.md) - German ministry funding EXIST founder grants, ZIM SME R&D grants (paused since July 2026) and overseeing the Forschungszulage tax allowance.
* [Borealis Philanthropy](borealis-philanthropy.md) - US intermediary that hosts pooled funds such as the Disability Inclusion Fund, including the Ford/MacArthur-backed Disability x Tech initiative.
* [Bpifrance](bpifrance.md) - French public investment bank delivering innovation grants (Bourse French Tech) and operating France 2030 contests such as i-Nov.
* [Brink](brink.md) - Bitcoin development non-profit funding established protocol developers with year-long grants and onboarding fellows in London.
* [Canada Revenue Agency (SR&ED)](canada-revenue-agency.md) - Administers Canada's SR&ED tax incentive program.
* [Chainlink Labs](chainlink-labs.md) - Developer of Chainlink oracles; its BUILD program exchanged services for token commitments (not grants) and was restructured in June 2026.
* [Chan Zuckerberg Initiative (CZI) / Biohub](chan-zuckerberg-initiative.md) - Philanthropy that ran Essential Open Source Software for Science (EOSS, 2019–2024); CZI science work now runs through Biohub, which co-seeded the Open Source for Science Fund in 2026.
* [CIVICUS](civicus.md) - Global civil-society alliance that delivers EU/Danida/Norway-funded Digital Democracy Initiative grants (rapid digital resiliency grants, Digital Action Lab) to Global South CSOs.
* [Climate Change AI](climate-change-ai.md) - Nonprofit (fiscally sponsored by Future Earth) connecting ML and climate; ran Innovation Grants 2022–2025, none planned now.
* [Cloudflare](cloudflare.md) - Network/CDN company offering Project Alexandria recurring credits to non-profit OSS projects (and startup programs covered in the ai category).
* [Co-Develop](co-develop.md) - Pooled global fund for digital public infrastructure, founded by the Rockefeller Foundation, Norway MFA and DPGA; country-demand-driven, no open calls.
* [Coefficient Giving (formerly Open Philanthropy)](coefficient-giving.md) - The largest philanthropic funder of AI safety. Renamed from Open Philanthropy in Nov 2025; runs technical AI safety, governance and capacity-building RFPs and rolling career grants.
* [Coinbase / Base](coinbase-base.md) - Coinbase's Base L2 team funds builders via retroactive Base Builder Grants (1–5 ETH), the Base Batches accelerator and the Base Ecosystem Fund (equity).
* [Cooperative AI Foundation (CAIF)](cooperative-ai-foundation.md) - Charity backed by a $15M commitment from Macroscopic Ventures that funds cooperative and multi-agent AI research and PhD fellowships.
* [Craig Newmark Philanthropies](craig-newmark-philanthropies.md) - Craigslist founder's philanthropy; rolling grants to US 501(c)(3)s with a cybersecurity ('cyber civil defense') priority.
* [Datadog](datadog.md) - Observability company offering free accounts to qualifying OSS projects and co-funding the GitHub Secure Open Source Fund.
* [Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG)](dfg.md) - Germany's central research funding organisation; runs the permanent Research Software Infrastructures programme (LIS) and earlier calls on research software sustainability and quality.
* [Digital Defenders Partnership](digital-defenders-partnership.md) - Hivos-hosted multi-donor fund (est. 2012 by the Freedom Online Coalition) for digital protection of human-rights defenders; most funds closed or invite-only in Oct 2026.
* [Digital Public Goods Alliance](digital-public-goods-alliance.md) - Multi-stakeholder alliance (56 members) that maintains the DPG Standard and Registry (255 DPGs) — NOT a grantmaker; DPG status is a credential used by funders such as Co-Develop and UNICEF.
* [Digital Research Alliance of Canada](digital-research-alliance-canada.md) - Canadian national digital research infrastructure organisation; runs the Research Software Maintenance Fund (Small ≤CA$150K, Large ≤CA$500K).
* [Django Software Foundation](django-software-foundation.md) - The Django Software Foundation (DSF) pays Django Fellows, contract maintainers at $82.26/hour as of 2025. The Fellowship is its biggest expense.
* [U.S. Department of Energy Office of Science (ASCR)](doe-office-of-science.md) - DOE Office of Science, via Advanced Scientific Computing Research (ASCR), funds HPC scientific software stewardship (CASS) and co-funds the BSSw Fellowship.
* [Drips (Radworks)](drips.md) - Open-source funding protocol (Drip Lists, dependency splitting, RetroPGF tooling, Drips Wave sprints) built within the Radworks ecosystem.
* [EA Funds](ea-funds.md) - Grantmaking funds of the effective altruism community. Closed the Long-Term Future Fund in Aug 2026 and launched the Transformative AI Fund.
* [Enterprise Estonia (EIS)](enterprise-estonia.md) - Estonian business and innovation agency offering start-up, R&D-employee and development grants.
* [Enterprise Singapore](enterprise-singapore.md) - Singapore agency running Startup SG grants (Founder, Tech) and equity schemes.
* [Ethereum Foundation](ethereum-foundation.md) - Nonprofit supporting the Ethereum ecosystem. For individuals it runs the Ethereum Protocol Fellowship (stipends for core-dev trainees) and a PhD fellowship. Project grants go through the Ecosystem Support Program (covered in the web3 category).
* [EuroHPC Joint Undertaking](eurohpc-ju.md) - EU body that runs European supercomputers and AI Factories, offering free GPU access for research, SMEs and startups.
* [European Commission (DG CONNECT — NGI / Open Internet Stack)](european-commission.md) - The EU executive funds open source internet technology through Horizon Europe — directly via Cluster 4 calls and indirectly via cascade funds such as NLnet's Restack; NGI is transitioning into the Open Internet Stack initiative in 2026.
* [European Innovation Council (EIC)](european-innovation-council.md) - Horizon Europe body funding breakthrough innovation via EIC Pathfinder, Transition and Accelerator (grant + EIC Fund equity); 2026 work programme ~€1.4bn.
* [Fast Forward](fast-forward.md) - US-based accelerator and funder for tech nonprofits; $25k+ unrestricted grants per cohort member.
* [Fastly](fastly.md) - Edge cloud company whose Fast Forward program has given free CDN/edge services to OSS projects since 2014.
* [Filecoin Foundation](filecoin-foundation.md) - Non-profit supporting Filecoin/IPFS; runs open devgrants (≤$50K) and RFPs; sister org FFDW funds decentralized-web public-interest projects.
* [Financial Privacy Foundation](financial-privacy-foundation.md) - Administers the Zcash Coinholder-Directed Retroactive Grants Program (quarterly, coinholder-voted, paid in shielded ZEC).
* [Ford Foundation](ford-foundation.md) - Major US foundation whose Technology and Society programme funds public-interest technology and digital rights; invitation-only.
* [Foresight Institute](foresight-institute.md) - Nonprofit funding open-source AI-for-science and AI-safety projects with grants, compute and node spaces in SF and Berlin.
* [Frontier Model Forum](frontier-model-forum.md) - Industry body (Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, OpenAI) that manages the AI Safety Fund for independent frontier-AI safety research.
* [Funding the Commons](funding-the-commons.md) - Convener of public-goods funding events, builder residencies, fellowships and hackathons for open-source digital public goods.
* [FUTO](futo.md) - FUTO funds software that frees users from Big Tech control, through large 'legendary' grants (over $5M to 20 projects) and $1,000–$5,000 microgrants (52 projects).
* [Gates Foundation](gates-foundation.md) - Global health and development foundation; runs Grand Challenges open RFPs and funds digital public goods / DPI (e.g. via Co-Develop partners).
* [gem.coop (Gem Fellowship)](gem-coop.md) - Community Ruby gem cooperative that runs the Gem Fellowship, funded with $100k/year for three years by Contributed Systems (Sidekiq).
* [Gitcoin](gitcoin.md) - Public-goods funding organisation behind Gitcoin Grants rounds (quadratic funding and, since GG24, multi-mechanism domains); operating under budget constraints in 2025–26.
* [GitHub](github.md) - GitHub (Microsoft) funds developers through GitHub Sponsors ($40M+ paid out) and ran the GitHub Accelerator (2023–2024).
* [Giveth](giveth.md) - Crypto donation platform for public goods running quadratic-funding rounds (often partner-funded) and GIVbacks; also manages milestone payouts for TheDAO Security Fund.
* [Golem Foundation (Octant)](golem-foundation.md) - Golem Foundation funds Ethereum public goods through Octant, distributing staking yield from 100k ETH via community-allocated epochs.
* [Google](google.md) - Google funds open source through Google Summer of Code stipends, the Open Source Peer Bonus and other programs; GSoC 2026 ran with 185 organizations and 1,141 contributors.
* [Helmholtz Association](helmholtz.md) - Germany's largest research organisation; funds internal research software improvement via ScienceServe (Initiative and Networking Fund) and HIFIS services.
* [HeroDevs](herodevs.md) - Company selling extended support for end-of-life open source. It runs a $20M Open Source Sustainability Fund that pays maintainers to adopt lifecycle and EOL best practices.
* [HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) — R&D tax relief](hmrc.md) - UK tax authority administering R&D tax relief (merged RDEC and ERIS since April 2024).
* [Hugging Face](hugging-face.md) - AI model hub that gives in-kind GPU hardware to community Spaces (community GPU grants, ZeroGPU).
* [Human Rights Foundation](human-rights-foundation.md) - NYC non-profit whose Bitcoin Development Fund grants bitcoin to open-source freedom-tech developers and educators; also runs an AI for Individual Rights program.
* [Institute for Research Software (formerly Software Sustainability Institute)](institute-for-research-software.md) - UK national centre for research software (Edinburgh, Manchester, Oxford, Southampton…), renamed from SSI on 1 Sep 2026; runs the Fellowship Programme and delivers the UKRI Research Software Maintenance Fund.
* [Internet Society Foundation](internet-society-foundation.md) - Grantmaking arm of the Internet Society; funds Internet research, connectivity, chapters, and (with government donors) the Common Good Cyber Fund.
* [Internews](internews.md) - Global media-development nonprofit heavily dependent on US government funding; digital-safety programmes largely halted after 2025 US aid cuts.
* [Israel Innovation Authority (IIA)](israel-innovation-authority.md) - Israeli government agency funding tech R&D via conditional grants (Tnufa, Startup Fund, R&D Fund).
* [Kanro (Vitalik Buterin)](kanro.md) - Vitalik Buterin's scientific-funding and direct-gifting vehicle (with Balvi) for open-source biosecurity, health and related work; no public application.
* [John S. and James L. Knight Foundation](knight-foundation.md) - US journalism, arts and communities funder; funds news technology mainly via Press Forward open calls and periodic Knight challenges.
* [Korea Ministry of SMEs and Startups (MSS)](korea-mss.md) - Korean ministry running TIPS and other startup programs.
* [LACNIC](lacnic.md) - Latin American and Caribbean Internet Address Registry; funds Internet development via the FRIDA programme.
* [Lambda](lambda.md) - GPU cloud company offering research grants of up to $5k in cloud credits.
* [Investment and Development Agency of Latvia (LIAA)](liaa.md) - Latvian agency running startup support, science-intensive startup and innovation voucher programmes.
* [Linux Foundation](linux-foundation.md) - The Linux Foundation (and its CNCF) runs LFX Mentorship, three paid 12-week mentorship terms a year with PPP-based stipends of $1,000–$6,600.
* [Major League Hacking (MLH)](major-league-hacking.md) - MLH runs hackathons and the MLH Fellowship, a remote 12-week internship alternative with need-based stipends in some tracks.
* [Manifund](manifund.md) - Nonprofit platform for public grant proposals and AI-safety regranting by independent regrantors.
* [MATS (ML Alignment & Theory Scholars)](mats.md) - Organization that runs fully funded AI-safety research fellowships in Berkeley and London ($1,600 a week, plus compute, housing and meals).
* [Mercatus Center (George Mason University)](mercatus-center.md) - Mercatus Center at George Mason University houses Emergent Ventures, Tyler Cowen's rolling grant and fellowship program for individuals, and formerly Fast Grants.
* [Meta](meta.md) - Ran Llama Impact Grants for social-impact projects built on Llama (2023–25). No open round in 2026.
* [Microsoft](microsoft.md) - Microsoft funds OSS through its employee-nominated FOSS Fund (up to $12,500/quarter) and as a co-funder of the GitHub Secure Open Source Fund and Alpha-Omega.
* [Mozilla Foundation](mozilla-foundation.md) - Mozilla Foundation funds public-interest technologists. Its Fellows Program returned in 2026 with up to 10 fellows at $100k–$125k each.
* [NASA Science Mission Directorate](nasa.md) - NASA SMD funds open-source science software through ROSES open-science elements (OSTFL, HPOSS, TOPS) — all currently unsolicited.
* [NCBR — National Centre for Research and Development (Poland)](ncbr.md) - Polish R&D agency running the consortium/large-enterprise tracks of FENG Ścieżka SMART.
* [NEAR Foundation](near-foundation.md) - Supports the NEAR ecosystem; funding now emphasises metrics-based Protocol Rewards plus DevHub, Potlock and Meta Pool grants.

* [Netherlands eScience Center](netherlands-escience-center.md) - Dutch national centre for research software (NWO/SURF), funding projects mainly with in-kind RSE time: Open eScience Call, Open & Sustainable Research Software call, RSSF fellowship.

* [NLnet Foundation](nlnet.md) - Dutch foundation that re-grants EU (NGI / Open Internet Stack) and private money as small €5k–€50k grants to free and open source internet technology; 2026 funds Restack and CodeSupply open until 3 Nov 2026.
* [National Research Council Canada (NRC IRAP)](nrc-canada.md) - Canadian federal R&D organisation running the Industrial Research Assistance Program for SMEs.
* [U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF)](nsf.md) - US federal science agency. Leads the National AI Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot, which gives free AI compute allocations.
* [NumFOCUS](numfocus.md) - U.S. 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsor for scientific open-source projects (NumPy, pandas, Julia, Bioconductor…); runs Small Development Grants and channels earmarked donations (e.g., SciML small grants).
* [NVIDIA](nvidia.md) - Provides GPU-hour and hardware grants to faculty (Academic Grant Program, currently paused) and $60k PhD fellowships. NVIDIA Inception for startups is a perks program, not a grant.
* [Okta for Good](okta-for-good.md) - Okta's philanthropy arm; Nonprofit Technology Initiative and Nonprofit Technology Fellowship ($25k per fellow's organisation).
* [Omidyar Network](omidyar-network.md) - Philanthropic investment firm working on responsible tech, economic power and democracy; does not accept unsolicited proposals.
* [Open Knowledge Foundation Deutschland (Prototype Fund)](open-knowledge-foundation-deutschland.md) - Berlin non-profit that runs the Prototype Fund on behalf of the German Federal Ministry of Research, Technology and Space (BMFTR), funding public-interest open source prototypes by Germany-based developers.

* [O'Shaughnessy Ventures](oshaughnessy-ventures.md) - O'Shaughnessy Ventures (OSV) funds up to 20 equity-free $100,000 fellowships a year plus $10,000+ grants for independent builders, researchers and creatives.
