# Summary

Sloan's Technology program (led by Joshua Greenberg) is the most consistent private funder of the *institutions* around research software: OSPOs, RSE careers, software citation and archiving.[^sloan-ossci][^unf-sloan]

# Programs

- [Open Source in Science](/programs/research-software/sloan-open-source-in-science.md): rolling LOIs
- [Better Software for Science](/programs/research-software/sloan-better-software-for-science.md): no longer accepting proposals

# 2025–2026 changes

- Better Software for Science stopped taking proposals; the Open Source in Science line continues, with 2026 focus areas of career paths, institutional support and publication/archiving.[^sloan-bss][^sloan-ossci]
