# Summary

The EIC is the EU's deep-tech innovation agency; the Accelerator offers grants below €2.5M and €1–10M equity to startups/SMEs[^eic-acc].

# 2025–2026 changes

2026: shorter templates (~20 pages), evaluations every two months, new Advanced Innovation Challenges (~€300K lump sums) and STEP Scale-Up equity (€10–30M)[^hezelburcht-wp26].
