# Summary

UKRI's Digital Research Infrastructure programme funds the Research Software Maintenance Fund, delivered by the Institute for Research Software (formerly SSI).[^ukri-rsmf-r2] EPSRC ran earlier dedicated calls, including *Software for research communities* (£4.5M, 2021) and *Support the development of research software engineering* (2021). Both are closed.[^ukri-sfrc][^ukri-rse-dev]

# Programs

- [Research Software Maintenance Fund](/programs/research-software/uk-research-software-maintenance-fund.md): rounds 1–2 awarded; no round 3 announced

# 2025–2026 changes

- RSMF Round 2 ran January–February 2026 (EOI) with awards in August 2026.[^ukri-rsmf-r2]
