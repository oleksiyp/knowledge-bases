# Summary

The Sovereign Tech Agency (STA; formerly Sovereign Tech Fund) is Germany's public funder for open source digital infrastructure. It reports supporting 118 technologies with €41.1 million of commissioned work since October 2022.[^sta-tech] Unlike most grantmakers it is open to applicants worldwide and pays for maintenance, not novelty.[^sta-programs]

# Programs

- [Sovereign Tech Fund](/programs/oss-infrastructure/sovereign-tech-fund.md) — rolling, ≥€50k investments.[^sta-programs]
- [Sovereign Tech Fellowship](/programs/oss-infrastructure/sovereign-tech-fellowship.md) — paid maintainer fellowships (2026 round closed).[^sta-programs]
- [Sovereign Tech Resilience](/programs/oss-infrastructure/sovereign-tech-resilience.md) — free audits, CRA compliance, memory-safety and PQC services, bug bounties.[^sta-resilience-relaunch]
- [Sovereign Tech Standards](/programs/oss-infrastructure/sovereign-tech-standards.md) — pilot paying maintainers to participate in standards bodies.[^sta-programs]
- Sovereign Tech Challenge — closed (2023).[^sta-programs]

# 2025–2026 changes

- 2026: Standards pilot launched; Fellowship expanded to up to 12 fellows; Resilience relaunched 28 Sep 2026 with CRA-compliance, memory-safety, PQC and supply-chain services.[^sta-programs][^sta-resilience-relaunch]
