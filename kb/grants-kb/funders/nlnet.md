# Summary

NLnet is the most important small-grant funder for open internet software in Europe and globally: it operates EU cascade funds and its own private funds, with lightweight applications and milestone payments.[^nlnet-funding] In 2026 it transitioned from the NGI Zero funds to the European Commission's Open Internet Stack initiative after winning three Horizon Europe grants (Restack, ELFA, CodeSupply).[^nlnet-3grants]

# Programs

- [Restack](/programs/oss-infrastructure/nlnet-restack.md) — €5k–€50k; deadline 3 Nov 2026.[^nlnet-funding]
- [CodeSupply](/programs/oss-infrastructure/nlnet-codesupply.md) — supply-chain metadata; deadline 3 Nov 2026.[^nlnet-funding]
- [NGI Zero Commons Fund](/programs/oss-infrastructure/nlnet-ngi-zero-commons.md) — final call closed 1 Jun 2026.[^nlnet-commons]

# 2025–2026 changes

- NGI Zero Commons (€21.6M) stopped taking applications after 1 June 2026.[^nlnet-commons]
