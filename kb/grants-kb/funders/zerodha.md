# Summary

Zerodha, India's largest retail broker, created FLOSS/fund in October 2024 to give $1M a year to free and open source projects worldwide.[^floss-home] In 2025 it distributed the full $1M to 40 projects and committed another $1M for 2026.[^floss-tranche2]

# 2025–2026 changes

- Two 2025 tranches ($325k, $675k); working on GitHub Sponsors as payout partner; fresh $1M for 2026.[^floss-tranche2]
