# Summary

DoD runs SBIR/STTR through the Defense SBIR/STTR Innovation Portal (DSIP) with component topics from the services, DARPA and other agencies; since 2026 topics pre-release monthly[^precision-cal].

# 2025–2026 changes

SBIR authority lapsed 1 Oct 2025 – 13 Apr 2026; AFWERX reopened in April 2026, replaced broad Open Topics with Focused Open Topics and introduced Strategic Breakthrough Awards up to $30M[^grantengine-afwerx].
