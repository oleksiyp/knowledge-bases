# Summary

The Linux Foundation hosts many open-source foundations, including CNCF and OpenSSF. Its LFX Mentorship platform pays mentees to work on LF projects across three terms a year.[^cncf-lfx-readme]

# Programs

- [LFX Mentorship](/programs/individuals/lfx-mentorship.md)

# 2025–2026 changes

- From July 2026 a refreshed PPP stipend schedule applies: the $6,600 maximum stays, and the minimum drops to $1,000 so more mentees can be funded.[^cncf-stipend-2026]

# Related

- Other categories may also cover LF security funding (OpenSSF/Alpha-Omega). See the oss-infrastructure guide.

## Additional notes (oss-infrastructure)

- **[Alpha-Omega](/programs/oss-infrastructure/alpha-omega-grants.md)** (LF/OpenSSF) — open quarterly intake (Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct), typical grants $50k–$100k; Q4 2026 window 1–31 Oct 2026.[^ao-apply-oi] 2025: 12 grants totalling $5.15M (Eclipse $800k, Ruby Central $700k, Rust Foundation $695k, PSF $640k, OpenJS $580k, ASF $500k…).[^ao-recipients-oi]
- **OpenSSF** — March 2026: Anthropic, AWS, GitHub, Google, Google DeepMind, Microsoft and OpenAI committed $12.5M to Alpha-Omega and OpenSSF; the OpenSSF TAC distributes funding (>$660k across 14 Technical Initiatives) to OpenSSF's own initiatives — not an open grant program for outside projects.[^openssf-12-5m-oi]
- **LFX Crowdfunding** — 0% fees (LF underwrites until $10M raised) for LF-ecosystem projects, mentorships, events and security research.[^lfx-crowd-oi]
- **OpenJS Foundation** (LF-affiliated): see [/funders/openjs-foundation](/funders/openjs-foundation.md).
