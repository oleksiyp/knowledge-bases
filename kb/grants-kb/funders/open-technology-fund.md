# Summary
The Open Technology Fund (OTF) is the largest dedicated funder of open-source internet-freedom tools. Congress appropriated $43.5M for it in 2025, about 98% of its budget, channelled through the US Agency for Global Media (USAGM).[^heise-otf-2025] It runs rolling, annual and rapid-response funds, plus in-kind Labs.[^otf-funds]

# Programs
- [Internet Freedom Fund](/programs/public-interest/otf-internet-freedom-fund.md): rolling, $10k–$900k
- [FOSS Sustainability Fund](/programs/public-interest/otf-foss-sustainability-fund.md): annual
- [Rapid Response Fund](/programs/public-interest/otf-rapid-response-fund.md): rolling
- [Surge and Sustain Fund](/programs/public-interest/otf-surge-and-sustain-fund.md): concept notes due 2026-10-15
- [Information Controls Research Program](/programs/public-interest/otf-information-controls-research.md): fellowship
- [OTF Labs](/programs/public-interest/otf-labs.md): in-kind

# 2025–2026 changes
- On 15 March 2025, after Executive Order 14238, USAGM terminated OTF's grant. OTF sued, and USAGM rescinded the termination before the 27 March hearing but then withheld later payments.[^wiki-otf]
- In June 2025 a federal judge ordered the FY2024 funds released. In October 2025 OTF asked the court to compel the overdue FY2025 funds (reportedly about $25M).[^wiki-otf] The government filed a notice of appeal on 26 January 2026.[^courtlistener-otf]
- The administration's FY2026 budget request proposed an orderly shutdown of USAGM while still listing $43.5M for OTF.[^usagm-cbj-2026]
- In October 2026 OTF's funds page still lists the IFF, Rapid Response, Surge & Sustain, FOSS Sustainability and ICRP programmes.[^otf-funds]
