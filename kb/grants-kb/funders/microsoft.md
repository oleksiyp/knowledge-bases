Microsoft runs the [FOSS Fund](/programs/oss-infrastructure/microsoft-foss-fund.md) (Fund 36 in March 2026) and co-funds the GitHub Secure Open Source Fund and, since March 2026, the $12.5M Alpha-Omega/OpenSSF coalition.[^ms-fossfund][^gh-sosf][^openssf-12-5m] AI-related programs are covered in the ai category.
