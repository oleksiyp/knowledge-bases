# Summary

Schmidt Sciences is one of the largest non-lab funders of AI safety research. It runs the Science of Trustworthy AI program[^ss-tai], AI2050 fellowships (127 fellows, up to $80M committed)[^ai2050-2026], and co-leads the $10M multi-agent safety fund.[^mas-portal]

## Additional notes (research-software)

Schmidt Sciences (formerly part of Schmidt Futures) funds VISS, a $40M, five-year network of scientific software engineering centers at Cambridge, Georgia Tech, JHU and UW.[^viss-page][^cam-viss] It also funds Eric and Wendy Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellowships at partner universities. These are not software grants, though fellows get software-engineering support.[^ucsd-schmidt-ai]

Research-software programs:

- [VISS](/programs/research-software/schmidt-viss.md): no open call; engage the centers directly

- VISS published a 2025 impact report and continues.[^viss-page]
