# Summary

Wellcome supports open research software mainly through partnerships. It is a co-anchor of the $20M Open Source for Science Fund with Biohub.[^rp-os4s] Researchers can also include software work in Wellcome's standard schemes such as Discovery Awards.[^wellcome-discovery] No standalone Wellcome research-software call was found for 2026.
