# Summary

Mozilla Foundation makes grants and runs fellowships for trustworthy AI and an open internet. The 2026 Fellows cohort (up to 10 people) started in May 2026.[^moz-oppdesk][^moz-2026-fellows]

# Programs

- [Mozilla Foundation Fellows Program](/programs/public-interest/mozilla-fellows.md)

# 2025–2026 changes

- The 2026 fellowship was nomination-based (deadline 30 January 2026) and offered two tracks worth $100k and $125k.[^moz-oppdesk]

## Additional notes (oss-infrastructure)

- **[Mozilla Open Source Support (MOSS)](/programs/oss-infrastructure/mozilla-moss.md)** — "As a result of the 2020 restructuring at Mozilla, the MOSS program is on indefinite hiatus and is not currently accepting applications."[^moss-oi] Mozilla currently has no OSS-maintenance grant program.

[^moss-oi]: https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/moss/

## Additional notes (ai)

- [Democracy x AI Cohort](/programs/ai/mozilla-democracy-x-ai.md): $50k grants for 10 working AI-for-democracy tools, with a $250k Sustain Track for 2. Partial open source is required, and the 2026 call closed on 16 March 2026.[^moz-dxai]

[^moz-dxai]: Opportunity Desk — https://opportunitydesk.org/2026/02/20/mozilla-foundation-incubator-democracy-x-ai-cohort-2026/

## Additional notes (public-interest)

- **Incubator:** launched in late 2025. Its first open call, Democracy x AI ($50k × 10 plus two $250k Sustain awards), closed on 16 March 2026. See [/programs/ai/mozilla-democracy-x-ai.md](/programs/ai/mozilla-democracy-x-ai.md).[^pi-moz-dxai-pr]
- **Mozilla Technology Fund** ran themed $50k cohorts (Auditing AI; AI & Environmental Justice in 2024).[^pi-moz-mtf-github] No MTF call has been seen since the Incubator launched, so it appears to be superseded.

## Additional notes (startup-rnd)

- [Mozilla Builders Accelerator](/programs/startup-rnd/mozilla-builders.md) (a wider-Mozilla initiative): 2024 local-AI cohort with up to $100K non-dilutive per project; no newer cohort listed as of Oct 2026, only bespoke collaborations.[^mozb-programs-srd]
