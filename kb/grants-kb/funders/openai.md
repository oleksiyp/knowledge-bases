# Summary

OpenAI funds software and security work mainly in kind, through API credits, ChatGPT Pro seats and subsidised model access. Its 2026 activity centres on cyber defence: Trusted Access for Cyber came with a $10M credit pool in February[^oai-tac] and Daybreak for Frontline Defenders with a $1B pledge in September.[^register-daybreak] Its nonprofit arm, the OpenAI Foundation, makes unrestricted cash grants to US community nonprofits.[^pfaf-2026]
