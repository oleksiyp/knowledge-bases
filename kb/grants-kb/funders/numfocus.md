# Summary

NumFOCUS is the fiscal home for much of the scientific open-source stack. It runs the Small Development Grants microgrant program for its projects and circulates outside funding calls to projects, such as OS4LS in 2026.[^numfocus-sdg][^numfocus-fundraising-161]

# Programs

- [Small Development Grants](/programs/research-software/numfocus-small-development-grants.md)
- [SciML Small Grants](/programs/research-software/sciml-small-grants.md) (donations via NumFOCUS)

# 2025–2026 changes

- The SDG process moved to public GitHub issues with lottery selection in 2025. A 2026 round schedule has not been published.[^sdg-repo]
