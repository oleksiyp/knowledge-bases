# Web3 / crypto ecosystem grants & retroactive public-goods funding

* [Aave Grants DAO (sunset)](aave-grants-dao.md) - Former community grants program of the Aave DAO; wound down from Aug 2024 and confirmed sunset (no applications accepted or reviewed) in July 2026; Aave DAO now funds Aave Labs directly under the "Aave Will Win" framework.
* [Aptos Foundation Ecosystem Grants](aptos-foundation-grants.md) - Milestone-based Aptos Foundation grants (typically $5K–$50K) for founders building apps, infrastructure and tools on Aptos, reviewed on a rolling basis; Payments Grants (up to $150K) not accepting applications; Aptos Assembly sprint offers up to $50K.
* [Arbitrum Audit Program (AAP)](arbitrum-audit-program.md) - $10M-in-ARB, 12-month program subsidising up to 100% of third-party smart-contract audits for promising teams on Arbitrum One/Orbit; applications closed 31 Jul 2026 (≈$1.2–2M committed).
* [Arbitrum D.A.O. Grant Program (Domain Allocator Offerings, ex-Questbook)](arbitrum-dao-domain-allocator-grants.md) - ArbitrumDAO's entry-level grants (up to 25K USDC, 50K with second reviewer) across five domains incl. Dev Tooling on One & Stylus, run on Questbook; Season 3 was approved to run to March 2026 — no Season 4 confirmed as of Oct 2026.
* [Avalanche Builder Hub grants (Team1 grants, research, audit subsidies)](avalanche-foundation-builder-grants.md) - Current Avalanche funding options listed on the Builder Hub — Team1 fast builder grants, up to $50K academic research grants, developer credits and up-to-75% subsidised security audits; replaces Retro9000 and infraBUIDL.
* [Avalanche Retro9000 (ended)](avalanche-retro9000.md) - Avalanche Foundation's $40M retroactive grant program for Avalanche L1s, infrastructure tooling and (from Mar 2026) usage-scored C-Chain rounds; the Retro9000 site is now an archive and the program no longer accepts applications.
* [Base Batches (accelerator with grants)](base-batches.md) - Base's annual accelerator — Startup Track gives $10K grants to ~15 pre-seed teams (<$250K raised) with ≥3 receiving $50K investment; student and robotics tracks; 2026 applications closed (Startup Track 9 Mar 2026).
* [Base Builder Grants (Coinbase)](base-builder-grants.md) - Retroactive 1–5 ETH grants from the Base team to builders who have already shipped on Base (apps, dev tooling, content), found via discovery and community nominations; no formal application; ongoing in cohorts since 2024.
* [Brink grants & fellowship (Bitcoin protocol development)](brink-grants-fellowship.md) - Brink awards year-long grants to established Bitcoin protocol developers (global) and runs a salaried one-year London fellowship to onboard engineers into Bitcoin Core development; applications via online forms.
* [Cardano Project Catalyst](cardano-project-catalyst.md) - Cardano's treasury-funded community grants; Fund15/Fund16 voting was paused in Jan 2026 and the funds returned to treasury, replaced in Aug 2026 by a curated ~2–2.5M ADA "Catalyst Pilot" (50K–200K ADA grants, adoption-linked payouts; closed 20 Aug 2026).
* [Prezenti Grants for Celo (Season 3)](celo-prezenti-grants.md) - Celo community grants operator Prezenti funds Celo builders via Frontier (AI/agent infra), Anchor (traction ≥5,000 daily tx, from $25K) and referral-only Boost pools; Season 3 window 12 Aug – 29 Dec 2026; KYC required; paid 20%/80% on milestones.
* [Chainlink BUILD (not a grant)](chainlink-build.md) - Chainlink Labs' BUILD program traded enhanced service access/support for a share of project token supply — not a grant; restructured June 2026 into case-by-case commercial agreements paid in LINK, with BUILD token rewards ending 7 Jul 2026.
* [Deep Funding (SingularityNET)](deep-funding-singularitynet.md) - SingularityNET's community-voted and RFP-based grants for decentralized AI / AGI software (OpenCog Hyperon, MeTTa, SingularityNET platform); grants up to ~$100K, milestone-based; activity in early 2026 (BGI Nexus round, Hyperon RFPs) but no open round verified for Oct 2026.
* [Drips (Drip Lists, RetroPGF rounds and Drips Wave)](drips.md) - Ethereum-based protocol for streaming/splitting funds to open-source repos and their dependencies; ecosystems run RetroPGF rounds and, since 2026, "Wave" contribution sprints (e.g. Stellar Wave 7, Jul 2026) on it — maintainers claim GitHub repos to receive funds.
* [Ethereum Foundation Ecosystem Support Program (ESP) — Wishlist & RFP grants](ethereum-foundation-esp.md) - EF grants for free, open-source Ethereum infrastructure, tooling, cryptography and research, now awarded only against EF-curated Wishlist items and RFPs (open applications ended Aug 2025); paid in ETH by default since June 2026; rolling.
* [Ethereum Foundation PhD Fellowship](ethereum-foundation-phd-fellowship.md) - $24,000 one-year stipend supplement for 9–10 PhD students researching Ethereum-related topics with open publication; annual call (2026 window closed 22 Apr 2026).
* [Filecoin Foundation Open Grants (devgrants)](filecoin-foundation-open-grants.md) - Filecoin Foundation open grants up to $50K for open-source work advancing the Filecoin ecosystem (storage tooling, FVM, developer tools), proposed as GitHub issues; RFP track currently empty; rolling.
* [Funding the Commons residencies & fellowships](funding-the-commons.md) - Fully funded residencies/fellowships and hackathons for builders of open-source digital public goods (e.g. El Salvador Fellowship, 12–17 Oct 2026; applications closed 18 Sep 2026); covers costs rather than cash grants.
* [Gitcoin Grants (GG rounds)](gitcoin-grants.md) - Gitcoin's community funding rounds for open-source and Ethereum public goods; GG24 (Oct 2025) moved to multi-mechanism "domains" (~$1.8M); a smaller partner-funded GG25 was planned for 2026 but no dates were published as of Oct 2026.
* [Giveth Quadratic Funding Rounds](giveth-qf-rounds.md) - Giveth's donation platform runs recurring quadratic-funding rounds (often partner-sponsored) for verified public-goods projects, plus GIVbacks rewards; e.g. Ethereum Security QF (Apr–May 2026, 637 ETH match); rounds announced irregularly.
* [HRF Bitcoin Development Fund](hrf-bitcoin-development-fund.md) - Human Rights Foundation grants paid in bitcoin for open-source Bitcoin/Nostr software, privacy tools, freedom-tech education and research that help human-rights defenders; announced in rounds several times a year (2026: Jan 1.3B sats, Apr 1.5B sats, Aug 500M+ sats).
* [NEAR Protocol Rewards & DevHub grants](near-protocol-rewards.md) - NEAR's grants have shifted to metrics-based Protocol Rewards (up to ~$10K/month for launch-stage projects, scored on GitHub and on-chain activity) plus community-run DevHub grants for tooling, Potlock funding rounds and Meta Pool grants.
* [Octant (Golem Foundation) public-goods epochs](octant.md) - Golem Foundation stakes 100k ETH and distributes yield to Ethereum public goods via community-allocated epochs with matching; Epoch 13 (privacy focus, 100 ETH matching) opens 14 Oct 2026; Octant v1 sunsetting in favour of v2.
* [OpenSats grants (Bitcoin, Nostr, Long-Term Support)](opensats-grants.md) - US 501(c)(3) funding free and open-source Bitcoin and Nostr projects and developers worldwide (nym-friendly) via General Fund grant waves, the Nostr fund, Long-Term Support grants for load-bearing contributors and fast RED reimbursements for red-teaming; rolling.
* [Optimism Grants Council (Governance Fund growth grants)](optimism-grants-council.md) - Upfront OP grants from the Optimism Governance Fund; Season 9 (Feb–May 2026, 3.89M OP) narrowed to DEX-liquidity/TVL growth on Superchain chains — little relevance for pure software public goods; Season 9 submissions closed 20 May 2026.
* [Optimism Retro Funding (RetroPGF)](optimism-retro-funding.md) - Optimism Collective's retroactive public-goods rewards in OP (e.g. 2025 Dev Tooling mission up to 8M OP, algorithmically scored); PAUSED from Jan 2026 for at least 12 months, with no missions in Seasons 9–10.
* [Polygon Community Grants Program (Community Treasury Board)](polygon-community-grants.md) - Polygon's treasury-funded grants (1B POL over 10 years; Season 2: 35M POL); for Season 3 the Community Treasury Board shifted toward investments, liquidity incentives and founder support (max 80M POL); open-grant availability in late 2026 unverified.
* [Protocol Guild](protocol-guild.md) - Collective that pools donations (incl. the 1% token pledge) and streams them with a 4-year vest to ~190 Ethereum L1 core contributors; membership by peer curation after 6 months of near-full-time OSS protocol work; ongoing.
* [Solana Foundation Grants (milestone, convertible, RFP)](solana-foundation-grants.md) - Solana Foundation funds open-source public goods for Solana via milestone-based grants, convertible grants (for public goods with a commercial component) and RFPs; rolling, ~3-week decisions; $100M+ to 500+ projects historically.
* [Spiral (Block) open-source Bitcoin grants](spiral-grants.md) - Spiral, Block's bitcoin open-source unit, gives year-long renewable grants (paid in BTC) to developers and designers improving Bitcoin privacy, security, scalability and UX (e.g. LDK/BDK, Lightning security); applications by email year-round.
* [Starknet Foundation Seed & Growth Grants](starknet-foundation-grants.md) - Starknet Foundation grants paid in STRK — Seed Grants up to $25K for early teams with an MVP (rolling, ~4-week decision, mandatory KYC/KYB) and Growth Grants $25K–$1M for teams with a product in market; open in 2026.
* [Stellar Community Fund (SCF) Build Award](stellar-community-fund.md) - SDF's community-reviewed awards of up to $150K (paid in XLM, four tranches) to take Stellar/Soroban projects from MVP to mainnet, via Open, Integration and RFP tracks; rounds every ~6 weeks — SCF #46 submissions due 8 Nov 2026.
* [Sui Foundation grants (RFPs, developer grants, academic research awards)](sui-foundation-grants.md) - Sui Foundation funds Sui builders mainly via rolling and "Flash" RFP cohorts (a $50M grants pool announced Feb 2026), plus $25K academic research awards and the Hydropower accelerator; open-ended developer grants de-emphasised.
* [Superteam Earn Instagrants (Solana regional micro-grants)](superteam-instagrants.md) - Fast (decision in ~48h–1 week), equity-free Solana micro-grants of up to ~$10–15K in USDC, often regional (e.g. Solana Foundation USA, India/CoinDCX) via Superteam Earn; availability varies by region — the US grant was paused as of Oct 2026.
* [TheDAO Security Fund (ETHSecurity Initiatives)](thedao-security-fund.md) - Staking-yield endowment (~69–75k ETH from the 2016 DAO) funding Ethereum security tooling, research and response via QF, RFPs and retro grants; Round Two (RFP-based) opened 15 Sep 2026, decisions mid-Nov 2026, submissions open to late Jan 2027.
* [Uniswap Foundation Grants](uniswap-foundation-grants.md) - Open, rolling Uniswap Foundation grants for protocols, tooling, research, governance and community around Uniswap v4/Unichain (reported range $7.5K to $1M+), plus small Unichain grants (≤$7.5K); open as of Oct 2026.
* [Uniswap Foundation Security Fund (UFSF) audit subsidies](uniswap-foundation-security-fund.md) - Monthly cohorts subsidising up to 100% of smart-contract audit costs for Uniswap-ecosystem (v4 hooks) teams via Areta's auditor marketplace; October 2026 cohort deadline 7 Oct 2026.
* [Vitalik Buterin personal giving (Kanro / Balvi and 2026 open-source commitment)](vitalik-buterin-kanro-giving.md) - Discretionary, no-application giving by Vitalik Buterin via Kanro/Balvi (open-source biosecurity, health, AI safety) and a 2026 pledge of 16,384 ETH (~$45M) over several years for privacy-first, verifiable open-source software and hardware; invite-only.
* [Web3 Foundation Grants Program & Polkadot Open Source Developer Grants (discontinued/closed)](web3-foundation-grants.md) - W3F's long-running Polkadot/Substrate OSS grants (up to $30K initial, $100K follow-up) were discontinued; the successor treasury-funded Polkadot Open Source Developer Grants bounty (up to $30K in DOT, KYC) is also closed to submissions; Polkadot funding now flows via OpenGov treasury and ecosystem programs.
* [Zcash Coinholder-Directed Retroactive Grants](zcash-coinholder-retroactive-grants.md) - Quarterly retroactive grants for completed, verifiable Zcash work, voted by shielded-ZEC holders and administered by the Financial Privacy Foundation; valued in USD, paid in shielded ZEC; KYC above $50K; Q4 2026 proposals 30 Oct – 13 Nov 2026.
* [Zcash Community Grants (ZCG)](zcash-community-grants.md) - Committee-run grants funded by 8% of Zcash block rewards (dev fund through Nov 2028; ~100K ZEC available) for independent teams building Zcash wallets, SDKs, infrastructure, interoperability and education; rolling via GitHub/forum; plus RFPs.
* [ZKsync Community Activation Pilot (ZK Nation RFPs)](zksync-community-activation-rfps.md) - ZK Nation token program (TPP-8) allocating 20M ZK over 12 months (2025–26), including a 13.6M ZK RFP pool run by the ZKsync Foundation for awareness, developer adoption and education initiatives; not a general software grant.
