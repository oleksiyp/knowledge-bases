# Summary
OpenSats funds Bitcoin, nostr and free/open-source projects; all programs are "open to applicants worldwide" and "nym-friendly".[^os-apply] It offers General Fund grants, **Long-Term Support (LTS)** grants for critical contributors such as Bitcoin Core maintainers, and fast-tracked **RED** reimbursements for security researchers red-teaming Bitcoin software.[^os-apply] Grants are announced in waves — the 17th Bitcoin wave (31 Mar 2026: 3 new grants, 6 renewals) and 17th Nostr wave (20 May 2026: 5 projects).[^os-btc17][^os-nostr17] The blog remained active through September 2026.[^os-blog]

# Eligibility
- Projects/people with direct impact on Bitcoin (or nostr); output must be publicly available under open-source licenses; commitment to transparency and education.[^os-apply]

# What it funds
- Protocol and library development, wallets, privacy, payments, education, nostr clients/infrastructure, security research.[^os-btc17][^os-nostr17]

# Amounts & terms
- Amounts per grant (not published as a range); on approval OpenSats may request tax info and Bitcoin addresses depending on location.[^os-apply]

# How to apply
- Concise (≈one-page) application reviewed by the board.[^os-apply]

# Deadlines
Rolling.

# Track record
- Funds rust-bitcoin among many others; 17 Bitcoin waves to date.[^os-rustbtc][^os-btc17]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you maintain OSS Bitcoin/nostr software, including pseudonymously.
- **Poor fit if:** your project is altcoin- or token-centric.
