# Summary
The Solana Foundation funds public goods for the Solana ecosystem in three ways: milestone-based grants, milestone-based **convertible grants** for public goods with a commercial component, and RFPs for specific needs.[^sol-grants] Convertible grants convert to investment only if the project hits defined milestones such as raising a priced round.[^sol-conv] The Foundation reports 500+ projects and $100M+ funded; first review takes about a week and decisions within ~3 weeks.[^sol-grants]

# Eligibility
- Projects that are a public good for Solana and committed to open-sourcing their work and learnings.[^sol-grants]

# What it funds
- Developer tooling, infrastructure, research, education that benefit the Solana ecosystem broadly.[^sol-grants]
- Commercial products fit only via convertible grants or investments.[^sol-conv]

# Amounts & terms
- Applicant proposes a structured budget with milestones; payouts on milestones.[^sol-grants]
- Payment currency and KYC are not stated on the page (regional Superteam grants pay in USDC).

# How to apply
- Online form: project overview, why it is a public good, milestone budget.[^sol-grants]

# Deadlines
Rolling.

# Track record
- 500+ projects, $100M+ across 6 continents.[^sol-grants]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you build OSS Solana SDKs, tooling or infra.
- **Poor fit if:** you want a no-strings grant for a revenue-generating product (expect a convertible).
