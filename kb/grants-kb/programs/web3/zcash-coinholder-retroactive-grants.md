# Summary
The Coinholder-Directed Retroactive Grants Program pays for **fully completed** Zcash work chosen by ZEC holders, who register by moving ZEC into the shielded Orchard pool and vote each quarter.[^zch-q2] It is administered by the Financial Privacy Foundation (FPF); grants are valued in USD but disbursed in shielded ZEC, with KYC required for awards above $50,000.[^zch-q2] Each quarter has a two-week call for proposals, a mandatory 30-day review and a ~12-day vote; the Q4 2026 call runs 30 Oct – 13 Nov 2026.[^zch-dates]

# Eligibility
- Completed, publicly verifiable work benefiting Zcash.[^zch-q2]

# What it funds
- Wallet integrations, SDKs, naming, swaps, core protocol work (e.g. ZcashNames, THORSwap/Metro in Q2).[^zch-q2]

# Amounts & terms
- USD-denominated, paid in shielded ZEC at disbursement-time rates; KYC >$50K.[^zch-q2]

# How to apply
- Post a proposal in the forum's Retroactive Grants category during the call window.[^zch-dates]
