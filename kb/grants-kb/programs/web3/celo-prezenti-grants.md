# Summary
Prezenti runs Celo's community grants. Season 3 (applications 12 Aug – 29 Dec 2026) has three pools: **Frontier** for AI and agent-economy infrastructure deployed on Celo mainnet (agent identity, transaction infra, developer tooling, verification), **Anchor** for projects with proven traction (≥5,000 daily transactions on any chain) that grow Celo transactions/TVL/MiniPay usage, and referral-only **Boost**.[^pz-s3] Payments are 20% on execution and 80% after verified milestones, to Celo addresses; KYC is required (Prezenti is a Canadian entity).[^pz-s3] Season 2 (Feb–Jun 2026, $346K capacity) funded 19 of 75 applicants with $132K committed.[^pz-s2-retro]

# Eligibility
- Celo mainnet deployment with verifiable on-chain activity (Frontier); traction thresholds (Anchor); referral (Boost).[^pz-s3]
- KarmaGAP profile mandatory; disclose jurisdiction and prior funding.[^pz-s3]

# What it funds
- Growth-oriented products and AI/agent infra on Celo; not pure research.[^pz-s3]

# Amounts & terms
- Anchor from $25K; paid in stablecoin (USDm) — $2M granted to date.[^pz-site][^pz-s2-retro]

# How to apply
- Apply via Prezenti during the Season 3 window with verifiable evidence of deployments and users.[^pz-s3]
