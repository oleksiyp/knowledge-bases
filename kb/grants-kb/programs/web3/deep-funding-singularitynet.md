# Summary
Deep Funding is SingularityNET's community-driven grants program for decentralized, beneficial AI: anyone can submit proposals and the community votes, alongside foundation RFP pools.[^df-intro] A 2025 RFP round awarded over $1.25M across 14 challenge areas around OpenCog Hyperon, MeTTa and the SingularityNET platform.[^df-agi] Grants are milestone-based and recipients keep their IP.[^df-intro] Note: this is distinct from the "Deep Funding" dependency-graph experiment used in Gitcoin GG24.

# Eligibility
- Individuals, teams and researchers worldwide building on or for the SingularityNET/ASI ecosystem.[^df-intro]

# What it funds
- AGI/Hyperon tooling, MeTTa, AI services on SingularityNET, BGI (beneficial AGI) projects.[^df-agi][^df-bgi]

# Amounts & terms
- Up to ~$100K per project depending on pool; BGI Nexus round: $500K total, max $50K per project.[^df-intro][^df-bgi]
- Payment currency and KYC rules: unverified — check each round's terms.

# How to apply
- Submit through the DEEP Projects portal during a round or in response to an RFP.[^df-rfps]

# Track record
- 16 winners of $1M+ AGI grants announced Feb 2025.[^df-vktr]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you build AI/AGI software aligned with Hyperon/MeTTa.
- **Poor fit if:** you want general OSS funding outside this stack.
