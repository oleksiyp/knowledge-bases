# Summary
The Web3 Foundation Grants Program funded open-source Polkadot, Kusama and Substrate software and research for years, but its README now states W3F "has decided to discontinue the general Grants Program" and is not accepting new applications, citing alignment with Polkadot's product-centric vision.[^w3f-gh] The treasury-funded **Open Source Developer Grants** bounty — up to $30,000 per project payable in DOT, milestone-based, with mandatory KYC/KYB — is also closed to further submissions.[^pos-apply][^pos-forum] Remaining routes are Polkadot/Kusama OpenGov treasury proposals, parachain programs and hackathons.[^w3f-gh]

# Eligibility
- Closed.[^w3f-gh]

# What it funded
- PoCs, general-purpose libraries and dApps for Polkadot; tooling and research.[^pos-forum]

# Amounts & terms
- Bounty: up to $30K in DOT, paid after milestone review; KYC/KYB required.[^pos-forum]

# How to apply
- Not possible; see OpenGov treasury and ecosystem alternatives listed in the W3F README.[^w3f-gh]

# Deadlines
None.

# Track record
- Hundreds of grants historically (W3F GitHub repository).[^w3f-gh]

# Fit
- **Poor fit:** discontinued. Polkadot builders should look at OpenGov treasury proposals.
