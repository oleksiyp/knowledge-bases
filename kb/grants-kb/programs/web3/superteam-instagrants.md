# Summary
Superteam Earn hosts "equity-free crypto grants that help you turn ideas into real products in the Solana ecosystem," including regional instagrant pools co-funded by the Solana Foundation.[^st-grants] Examples: Solana Foundation USA Grants ($1–$10K USDC, US-only, average approved grant $8,307 across 22 recipients, roughly monthly decisions, currently **paused**) and the Solana Foundation & CoinDCX India pool (up to $15K USDC).[^st-usa][^st-india]

# Eligibility
- Usually region-specific; "Proof of Work > Credentials".[^st-usa]

# What it funds
- Developer tooling, DAO tools, education, payments, cause-driven projects on Solana.[^st-usa]

# Amounts & terms
- Paid in USDC; weekly community updates required on some pools.[^st-usa]

# How to apply
- Short application on Superteam Earn for the relevant regional pool.[^st-grants]

# Deadlines
Rolling per pool.

# Track record
- USA pool: $182.8K approved across 22 recipients.[^st-usa]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you can ship fast and want a small, quick Solana grant.
- **Poor fit if:** your region has no active pool or you need >$15K.
