# Summary
Sui Foundation's grants hub lists the Hydropower accelerator, $25K Academic Research Awards, direct foundation investment and Requests for Proposals, and stresses "connecting serious founders with meaningful, long-term support - not grants."[^sui-hub] The RFP program runs **rolling RFP cohorts** for larger long-term needs and **Flash RFPs** for short projects.[^sui-rfp] In February 2026 the Foundation announced $50M in new grants to be distributed in cohorts targeting developer tooling and SDKs, DeFi infrastructure, gaming and on-chain data tools.[^sui-50m]

# Eligibility
- Startups, developers and research teams building on Sui whose work matches current RFPs.[^sui-rfp]

# What it funds
- Tooling/SDKs, DeFi infra, data tools, research (academic awards).[^sui-50m][^sui-hub]

# Amounts & terms
- Per RFP; academic research award $25K.[^sui-hub] Payment currency/KYC not stated on the hub.

# How to apply
- Submit proposals via the Sui Grants Hub during each RFP's window.[^sui-hub]

# Deadlines
Per RFP (check hub).

- **Good fit if:** you can deliver a scoped Move/Sui tooling RFP.
- **Poor fit if:** you want an open-ended grant for your own idea.
