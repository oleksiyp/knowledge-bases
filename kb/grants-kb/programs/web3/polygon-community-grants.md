# Summary
Polygon's Community Grants Program draws on 1B POL unlocked over a decade from the Community Treasury.[^pol-1b] Season 2 allocated 35M POL with AI, DePIN and consumer projects prominent.[^pol-s2] The Community Treasury Board's Season 3 strategy (PFP-4, Oct 2025) set a maximum 80M POL budget and moved beyond grants into a Liquidity & Incentives Fund, a Capital Investments & Infrastructure Fund and a Founder Support Fund.[^pol-pfp4] We could not verify an open grant application window as of October 2026; the forum shows mostly individual grant requests and impact reports.[^pol-forum]

# Eligibility
- Builders, teams and creators contributing to the Polygon ecosystem.[^pol-1b]

# What it funds
- dApps, infrastructure, tooling; Season 3 increasingly investments and liquidity.[^pol-pfp4]

# Amounts & terms
- Paid in POL; Season 3 may use investment instruments (not equity-free).[^pol-pfp4]

# How to apply
- Check the Polygon forum Community Treasury category and CTB announcements.[^pol-forum]

# Deadlines
Unverified.

# Track record
- Season 2: 35M POL.[^pol-s2]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you build a product on Polygon PoS/AggLayer and accept POL or investment.
- **Poor fit if:** you want a predictable OSS grant.
