# Summary
Base Builder Grants are small, fast, ETH-denominated awards (1–5 ETH) from the Base team at Coinbase.[^base-para] Recipients are identified from live work — via the team's monitoring of X and Farcaster plus a public nomination form — rather than proposals.[^base-para][^base-gitcoin] Over 20 cohorts have been distributed since March 2024; selected recipients complete W-8/W-9 tax forms before payment.[^base-gitcoin]

# Eligibility
- Builders with shipped, live contributions to Base: apps, developer tooling, education, community, creative projects.[^base-gitcoin]

# What it funds
- Already-shipped work that is unique/fun, brings users onchain, and is making an impact.[^base-para]

# Amounts & terms
- 1–5 ETH; tax documentation (W-8/W-9) required.[^base-gitcoin]

# How to apply
- No application; get nominated via the form or be discovered. The team only contacts selected recipients.[^base-para]

# Deadlines
None (rolling cohorts).

# Track record
- 20+ cohorts since March 2024.[^base-gitcoin]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you shipped something visible on Base, including OSS tooling.
- **Poor fit if:** you need funding before building.
