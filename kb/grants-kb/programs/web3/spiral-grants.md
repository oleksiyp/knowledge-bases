# Summary
Spiral is a Block Inc. division that builds and funds free, open-source projects improving bitcoin's privacy, security, UX and scalability.[^spiral-home] It gives (often renewed) grants to individual developers — e.g. renewals for Bitcoin Zavior (LDK/BDK) and Matt Morehouse (Lightning security) — and to programs such as Summer of Bitcoin.[^spiral-renew][^spiral-sob] A grant directory reports Spiral pays grants in BTC, accepts applications year-round and has funded 100+ OSS projects.[^spark-dir] Design grants moved to the Bitcoin Design Foundation, whose 2026 impact initiatives fund full-time designers.[^bdf-design]

# Eligibility
- Developers/designers with OSS Bitcoin contributions; global.[^spark-dir]

# What it funds
- Lightning (LDK), wallets (BDK), payments, privacy, security testing, education programs.[^spiral-renew]

# Amounts & terms
- Paid in BTC; amounts unpublished.[^spark-dir]

# How to apply
- Email a detailed proposal to grants@spiral.xyz.[^spark-dir]

# Deadlines
Rolling.

# Track record
- 100+ OSS projects funded (directory figure).[^spark-dir]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you are an established OSS Bitcoin/Lightning contributor.
- **Poor fit if:** you need a formal application process with predictable timelines.
