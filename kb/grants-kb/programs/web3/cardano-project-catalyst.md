# Summary
Project Catalyst has funded 2,200+ Cardano projects through community-voted treasury rounds.[^pc-update] Fund15 voting, scheduled for January 2026, was paused, and in February 2026 Input Output, the Cardano Foundation and Intersect said they would "reassess the strategy, operations and best path forward"; previously committed funds (Fund14 and earlier) were unaffected.[^pc-f15][^pc-update] In August 2026 a **Catalyst Pilot** opened: ~2–2.5M ADA (from undistributed prior-fund ADA) for 10–15 teams with working products integrating oracles, stablecoins, programmable tokens or on-chain identity, curated by community experts instead of public vote; it closed 20 Aug 2026.[^pc-pilot]

# Eligibility
- Pilot: teams with a working product on Cardano; compliance checked by community curators on arrival.[^pc-pilot]

# What it funds
- Pilot focus: integrations of new on-chain tools; earlier funds covered dev tooling, dApps, community.[^pc-pilot]

# Amounts & terms
- Pilot: 50K–200K ADA; 40% upfront after onboarding, 40% on usage targets, 20% on sustained adoption.[^pc-pilot]

# How to apply
- Watch projectcatalyst.io for the post-pilot model.[^pc-update]
