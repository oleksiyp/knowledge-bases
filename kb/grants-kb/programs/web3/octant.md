# Summary
Octant is the Golem Foundation's public-goods funding platform: the Foundation stakes 100,000 ETH from its treasury and a portion of the yield funds recurring "epochs" in which GLM lockers allocate rewards to approved Ethereum public-goods projects, with a matching pool.[^octant-oak] Octant v1 is being sunset and future rounds run on Octant v2.[^octant-gitcoin] Epoch 13 is a privacy-focused round with a 100 ETH matching pool and a new voting app, announced August 2026.[^octant-e13]

# Eligibility
- Public-goods projects (typically Ethereum-aligned, open source) approved by the Golem Foundation for each epoch.[^octant-oak]

# What it funds
- Ethereum public goods; Epoch 13: privacy on Ethereum and the open internet.[^octant-e13]

# Amounts & terms
- Paid in ETH from staking yield; amount depends on community allocations and matching.[^octant-oak]

# How to apply
- Projects propose themselves before an epoch and are curated by the Golem Foundation; check Octant announcements for the Epoch 13 timetable.[^octant-oak]
