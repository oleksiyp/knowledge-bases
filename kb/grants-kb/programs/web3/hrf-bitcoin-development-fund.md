# Summary
HRF's Bitcoin Development Fund (BDF) supports "individuals and projects that make Bitcoin and related freedom technologies more powerful tools for human rights defenders operating in challenging political environments" — open-source software, research, education and financial privacy — and pays grants in bitcoin.[^hrf-ff] In 2026 it announced 1.3B sats to 22 projects (Jan), 1.5B sats to 26 projects (1 Apr) and 500M+ sats to 16 projects (Aug).[^hrf-jan26][^hrf-apr26][^hrf-ff] Since 2020 BDF has granted $11.8M in BTC to 367 projects in 68 countries.[^hrf-apr26]

# Eligibility
- Open-source Bitcoin/freedom-tech developers, educators and researchers worldwide, especially serving people under authoritarian regimes.[^hrf-ff]

# What it funds
- Bitcoin protocol and privacy work, payments for dissidents, Nostr, self-custody, education hubs, research.[^hrf-apr26]

# Amounts & terms
- Paid in sats; amounts vary per grant.[^hrf-ff]

# How to apply
- Submit HRF's online application form.[^hrf-ff]

# Deadlines
Rolling; rounds announced periodically.

# Track record
- $11.8M to 367 projects (2020–Apr 2026).[^hrf-apr26]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** your OSS tool improves privacy/censorship-resistance for at-risk users.
- **Poor fit if:** your work is commercial or not Bitcoin/freedom-tech related.
