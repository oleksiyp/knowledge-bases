# Summary
ZKsync has no standing open developer-grant program comparable to ESP; its main 2025–26 funding vehicle is the Community Activation Pilot (TPP-8), approved by ZK token holders, allocating 20M ZK over 12 months to existing community initiatives and a competitive RFP process for global awareness, developer adoption and education.[^tpp8] The RFP pool is 13.6M ZK (≈$680K at $0.05/ZK), evaluated by the ZKsync Foundation on strategic alignment, feasibility and experience, with new RFPs posted continuously.[^tpp8][^tpp8-rfp]

# Eligibility
- Respondents to specific RFPs (organisations or individuals).[^tpp8-rfp]

# What it funds
- In-person/online growth, education and developer-adoption initiatives — not core protocol software.[^tpp8]

# Amounts & terms
- Paid in ZK tokens.[^tpp8]

# How to apply
- Respond to RFPs posted on the ZK Nation forum.[^tpp8-rfp]

# Deadlines
Per RFP.

# Track record
- Not compiled.

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you run developer education/community programs for ZKsync.
- **Poor fit if:** you want to fund OSS tooling development.
