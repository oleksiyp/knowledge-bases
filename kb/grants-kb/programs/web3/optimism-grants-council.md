# Summary
The Grants Council allocates the Optimism Governance Fund. In Season 9 its budget fell to 3.89M OP (from 6.29M in Season 8), with a separate 0.98M OP for the Developer Advisory Board, and both were measured on DEX TVL in collateral-borrow pairs and trading volume per TVL.[^s9-missions] Applications ran 11 Feb – 20 May 2026 and focused on fewer projects with larger allocations, prioritising OP Mainnet.[^s9-open] It is a growth/incentive program, not a public-goods software grant.

# Eligibility
- Projects that deploy their own contracts on eligible OP Chains contributing revenue to the Collective.[^s9-missions]

# What it funds
- Season 9: DEX liquidity and fees growth in priority pairs; DAB budget for developer-facing work with the same metrics.[^s9-missions]

# Amounts & terms
- Paid in OP; each council publishes its rubric before selection.[^s9-missions]

# How to apply
- Via the Grants Council application process announced on the Optimism forum each season.[^s9-open]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you run a DeFi protocol on OP Mainnet able to grow liquidity.
- **Poor fit if:** you build OSS libraries, tooling or research.
