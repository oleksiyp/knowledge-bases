# Summary
Announced January 2026, TheDAO Security Fund stakes the ~75,000 dormant ETH left from the 2016 DAO (≈$150M endowment) and spends the yield on Ethereum security, via quadratic funding, retroactive grants and ranked-choice voting, while keeping original claims open indefinitely.[^coindesk-dao][^gitcoin-dao] Round One was the Ethereum Security QF round on Giveth (23 Apr – 14 May 2026, 637 ETH matching).[^giveth-qf] Round Two, "ETHSecurity Initiatives", launched 15 Sep 2026 around RFPs (formally verified compiler, client-verified ENS resolution, local-first Safe interfaces, shared fuzzing tooling) with Giveth-managed milestone payouts.[^ethdaily-r2]

# Eligibility
- Teams/individuals doing Ethereum security work; anyone can also submit their own RFP.[^ethdaily-r2]
- Teams must dedicate at least one-third of the requested budget to measurable adoption milestones.[^ethdaily-r2]

# What it funds
- Security tooling, formal verification, fuzzing, wallet/Safe safety, incident response, research.[^ethdaily-r2][^giveth-qf]

# Amounts & terms
- Yield ≈$8M/year; Round Two examples: $600K for a formally verified Vyper compiler, $300K for Auditware's privacy-preserving detection system; up to 100 proposals eligible.[^cb-r2]
- Milestone payouts managed by Giveth after independent technical review; paid in crypto.[^ethdaily-r2]

# How to apply
- Respond to a published RFP or propose your own via thedao.fund / Giveth; ETHSecurity badge holders allocate capital in mid-November.[^ethdaily-r2]

# Fit

- **Poor fit if:** your work is not security-related.
