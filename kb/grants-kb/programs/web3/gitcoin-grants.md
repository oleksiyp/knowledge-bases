# Summary
Gitcoin Grants has run community funding rounds since 2019, best known for quadratic funding (QF) of open-source and Ethereum public goods.[^gg-program] GG24 (Oct–Nov 2025) was the first "Gitcoin 3.0" round: six thematic domains run by independent "Domain Allocators" with different mechanisms (QF, MACI private voting, hypercerts peer review, direct milestone grants, conviction voting + retro rewards), distributing ≈$1.8M ($1.175M from Gitcoin, $632.5K from partners).[^gg24-case] Gitcoin's GG25 planning (Nov 2025) aimed at a small, partner-funded "targeted round" in Q2 2026 to "preserve runway and limit direct spend"; as of 2026-10-03 no GG25 round dates were published on Gitcoin's site.[^gg25-early][^gg-home]

# Eligibility
- Projects create a profile in Gitcoin's Builder and must meet each round's eligibility criteria; applications are reviewed per round.[^gg-program]
- Most rounds target open-source and Ethereum-aligned public goods.[^gg24-case]

# What it funds
- GG24 domains: Developer Tooling & Infrastructure, Interop Standards/Infra/Analytics, Privacy, Public Goods R&D, Solutions Development, Targeted Development & Adoption.[^gg24-case]

# Amounts & terms
- GG24 Dev Tooling domain: $200K QF matching (78 OSS projects); Interop: $100K matching.[^gg24-case]
- GG24 Public Goods Tooling Development sub-round: $123,800 total ($83,800 proactive via conviction voting to 12 projects, $40,000 performance-based retro).[^gg-tooling-retro]
- Payouts are on-chain in crypto (stablecoins/ETH depending on round).[^gg-program]

# How to apply
- Watch Gitcoin's site/forum for round announcements; apply via Builder; then rally donors during the donation window.[^gg-program]

# Track record
- ≈$1.8M distributed in GG24 with ~1,300 unique donors in QF rounds.[^gg24-case]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you run an OSS Ethereum public good with a community willing to donate.
- **Poor fit if:** you need predictable, sizeable funding or a fixed calendar — round cadence became irregular after 2025.
