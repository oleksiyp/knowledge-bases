# Summary
Aptos Foundation Ecosystem Grants fund founders and startups building applications, infrastructure and tools that expand the Aptos network, typically $5,000–$50,000 and milestone-based.[^aptos-eco] The Payments Grant (up to $150,000 for wallets, APIs, compliance layers and payment infrastructure) is currently not accepting applications.[^aptos-pay] The Aptos Assembly is a four-week sprint with up to $50K milestone grants plus accelerator access (as described in search listings of aptosnetwork.com).[^aptos-eco]

# Eligibility
- Teams building on Aptos (Move).[^aptos-eco]

# What it funds
- Apps, infrastructure, developer tools.[^aptos-eco]

# Amounts & terms
- $5K–$50K, milestone-based; payment currency/KYC not verified (site rate-limited our fetch).[^aptos-eco]

# How to apply
- Online application with roadmap, milestones and budget; rolling review.[^aptos-pay]

# Deadlines
Rolling.

# Track record
- Not compiled.

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you build Move tooling or infra on Aptos.
- **Poor fit if:** you need >$50K without traction.
