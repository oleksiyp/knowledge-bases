# Summary
UFSF subsidises third-party security audits for teams building in the Uniswap ecosystem, including v4 hooks, covering up to 100% of audit cost.[^ufsf-oct] Applications run in monthly cohorts through Areta, which operates a curated auditor marketplace and provides early security scanning.[^ufsf-oct] The October 2026 cohort closes 7 October 2026, 23:59 UTC; 30+ projects were supported in earlier cohorts.[^ufsf-oct]

# Eligibility
- Teams building within the Uniswap ecosystem (v4 hooks etc.).[^ufsf-oct][^uf-build]

# What it funds
- Smart-contract audits by vetted firms (not cash to the team).[^ufsf-oct]

# Amounts & terms
- Up to 100% subsidy of audit costs.[^ufsf-oct]

# How to apply
- Apply via areta.fillout.com/ufsf-projects before the monthly deadline.[^ufsf-oct]

- 30+ ecosystem projects supported.[^ufsf-oct]

- **Good fit if:** you have audit-ready v4 hook or Uniswap-integrated contracts.
- **Poor fit if:** you need cash for development.
