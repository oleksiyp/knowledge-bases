# Summary
Vitalik Buterin funds open-source work directly through Kanro and Balvi — "scientific investment and direct gifting funds" focused on free and open-source biosecurity, pandemic prevention and indoor air quality — which have distributed over $400M.[^tekedia-kanro] Kanro gave Varro 20M USDC of non-dilutive support to develop open-source pathogen-detection hardware.[^varro] In February 2026 Buterin committed 16,384 ETH (~$45M) of personal funds, to be deployed over several years, to privacy tools, encrypted communications, open silicon/secure hardware, verifiable software stacks, ZK/FHE and local-first systems — separately from the Ethereum Foundation.[^vb-45m]

# Eligibility
- No open call; recipients are selected at the donor's discretion.[^vb-45m]

# What it funds
- Open-source biosecurity/health hardware and software; privacy and verifiable computing; open hardware.[^vb-45m][^tekedia-kanro]

# Amounts & terms
- Discretionary; typically paid in crypto (e.g. USDC).[^varro] Open-source release is a stated condition in public examples.[^varro]
