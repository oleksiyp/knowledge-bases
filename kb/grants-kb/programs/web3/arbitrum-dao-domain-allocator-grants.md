# Summary
The Arbitrum D.A.O. (Domain Allocator Offerings) Grant Program, formerly the Questbook program, is ArbitrumDAO's entry point for small grants. Season 3 covered five domains — New Protocols & Ideas; Education, Community Growth & Events; Gaming; Dev Tooling on One & Stylus; Orbit Chains — and was approved for one year until March 2026 or until funds ran out.[^arb-s3] Grants are paid in USDC on milestones.[^arb-s3] As of 2026-10-03 the forum shows final reports and ongoing DAO programs but no confirmed Season 4.[^arb-forum-cat]

# Eligibility
- Anyone with a Questbook wallet/profile submitting to a domain template.[^arb-s3]

# What it funds
- Notably "Dev Tooling on One and Stylus" (Rust/C++ smart contracts), plus new protocols, education, gaming and Orbit chains.[^arb-s3]

# Amounts & terms
- Up to 25,000 USDC reviewed by one Domain Allocator; up to 50,000 USDC needs a second allocator; milestone payouts in USDC.[^arb-s3]
- Season 3 had 128 approved grants by late 2025.[^arb-s3]

# How to apply
- Via Questbook, choosing the domain; allocator reviews proposals.[^arb-s3]

# Deadlines
| Season | Window | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Season 3 | Mar 2025 – Mar 2026 | Ended (scheduled); Season 4 unconfirmed[^arb-s3] |

# Track record
- Grew from $250K across four domains (S1) to $1M per domain (S2) and five domains (S3).[^arb-s3]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** a Season 4 launches and you build Stylus/Arbitrum dev tooling.
- **Poor fit if:** you need funding now.
