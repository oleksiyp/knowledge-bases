# Summary
The Stellar Community Fund is SDF's community-governed grants program. Its **Build Award** provides up to $150,000 worth of XLM to move validated projects from concept to mainnet, through an Open Track (community voting), Integration Track and RFP Track.[^scf-awards] Funds arrive in four tranches (10% on acceptance, then 20% MVP, 30% testnet, 40% mainnet).[^scf-hb] Smaller Instawards (via ambassador chapters) and post-launch Grow support also exist; SCF reports $42M awarded across 656 submissions.[^scf-home] The current round, SCF #46, has a submission deadline of 8 November 2026.[^scf-awards]

# Eligibility
- Projects building on Stellar/Soroban; submit an interest form to confirm eligibility first.[^scf-awards]
- SDF may require KYC/KYB at any point in the award process.[^scf-hb]

# What it funds
- Ecosystem infrastructure, DeFi, developer tools and real-world-use applications.[^scf-awards]

# Amounts & terms
- Budget in USD, paid in XLM; lifetime cap $150K per project; Integration Track final tranche tied to a ratified on-chain metric.[^scf-hb]

# How to apply
- Interest form → full submission by round deadline → panel/community review (Neural Quorum Governance) → award.[^scf-awards][^scf-home]
