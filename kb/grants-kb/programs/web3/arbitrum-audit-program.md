# Summary
The Arbitrum Audit Program allocated $10M in ARB over 12 months to subsidise third-party smart-contract audits for teams with strong product-market-fit potential launching on Arbitrum One, deploying an Orbit chain or migrating from elsewhere; up to 100% of audit cost could be covered.[^aap-blog] A committee from the Arbitrum Foundation, Offchain Labs and a DAO-elected expert reviewed applications on a rolling basis.[^aap-blog] By 30 April 2026 only ≈$1.23M (12%) had been committed, and applications closed on 31 July 2026.[^aap-tr3]

# Eligibility
- Teams building on Arbitrum One/Orbit with technical readiness and ecosystem alignment.[^aap-tr3]

# What it funds
- Audits from approved firms (DeFi was 54% of applications).[^aap-tr3]

# Amounts & terms
- Subsidy paid toward audit costs; budget denominated in ARB.[^aap-blog]

# How to apply
- Closed; the Arbitrum Foundation grants page still lists other programs (ArbiFuel gas sponsorship, Alchemy-Arbitrum credits).[^arb-grants]
