# Summary
With Retro9000 archived, the Avalanche Builder Hub lists active support: **Team1 Builder Grants** ("fast, focused funding for builders on Avalanche"), a **Call for Research Proposals** (up to $50,000 for academic research on cryptoasset and decentralized-network economics), **Developer Credits** (with Space & Time) and **Security Audits** subsidised up to 75% via approved providers; the Blizzard Fund invests separately.[^avax-grants][^r9k-site] The infraBUIDL program no longer appears on the grants page.[^avax-grants]

# Eligibility
- Builders on Avalanche; academic researchers for the research call.[^avax-grants]

# What it funds
- Avalanche apps/L1s (Team1), research, audits, dev credits.[^avax-grants]

# Amounts & terms
- Research up to $50K; audits up to 75% subsidy; Team1 amounts not published.[^avax-grants]

# How to apply
- Via the Builder Hub forms for each program.[^avax-grants]

# Deadlines
Rolling.

# Track record
- Not compiled.

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you build on Avalanche and need small fast funding or an audit subsidy.
- **Poor fit if:** you want a large public-goods grant.
