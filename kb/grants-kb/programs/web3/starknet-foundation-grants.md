# Summary
Starknet Seed Grants provide up to $25,000 in STRK, non-dilutive, to early-stage teams with an MVP or proof of concept that are active in the Starknet community (e.g. hackathon participants).[^sn-seed] Applications are rolling, reviewed in rounds with responses in about four weeks, and KYC/KYB is legally required before funds are released.[^sn-seed] Growth Grants ($25K–$1M in STRK) target later-stage teams with a product in market, in Innovation, Ecosystem Integration, Content & Creator and "Next Billion" categories.[^sn-grants][^sn-growth-x]

# Eligibility
- Seed: MVP/PoC, active in Starknet community, uses existing Starknet tools.[^sn-seed]
- Growth: working product in market.[^sn-grants]

# What it funds
- Apps, infrastructure and tooling on Starknet.[^sn-grants]

# Amounts & terms
- Paid exclusively in STRK; KYC/KYB mandatory; three-month check-in required for Seed.[^sn-seed]

# How to apply
- Grants form → committee evaluation → decision (~4 weeks) → KYC + grant agreement → payout.[^sn-seed]

# Deadlines
Rolling.

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you have a Cairo/Starknet MVP and can accept STRK.
- **Poor fit if:** you are idea-stage or need fiat.
