# Summary
The EF PhD Fellowship gives 9–10 doctoral students $24,000 over one year as a supplement to their existing stipend, for Ethereum-related research across computer science, economics, political science, policy and related fields.[^phd26] Research must be published in academic venues and openly accessible. The 2026 window closed on 22 April 2026; decisions came ~6 weeks later.[^phd26]

# Eligibility
- Actively enrolled in a PhD program at a university or academic institute.[^phd26]
- Disciplines: economics, political science, business, public policy, computer science, urban planning, sociology.[^phd26]

# What it funds
- Doctoral research relevant to Ethereum; outputs must be openly accessible.[^phd26]

# Amounts & terms
- $24,000 over one year; fellows may qualify for a "continuity stipend" depending on project quality.[^phd26]

# How to apply
- Apply via the ESP rounds page during the annual window.[^phd26]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you are a PhD student whose thesis touches Ethereum protocol, cryptography, mechanism design or governance.
- **Poor fit if:** you are not enrolled in a PhD program or need full salary-level funding.
