# Summary
Retro9000 was a $40M Avalanche Foundation program rewarding developers who had already shipped Avalanche L1s and infrastructure on mainnet, later adding monthly C-Chain rounds scored on real usage (Round 1 from 2 March 2026, Round 2 from 30 March 2026).[^r9k-launch][^r9k-c2] As of October 2026 the Retro9000 site states the program is no longer active and functions as an archive, pointing builders to the Builder Hub grants page.[^r9k-site]

# Eligibility
- Closed.[^r9k-site]

# What it funded
- Avalanche L1 launches, infra tooling, C-Chain activity — retroactively, with advisory community voting.[^r9k-launch][^r9k-site]

# Amounts & terms
- Fourth L1 & infra cohort: $160K+ across six projects.[^r9k-c4]

# How to apply
- Not possible; see [/programs/web3/avalanche-foundation-builder-grants.md](/programs/web3/avalanche-foundation-builder-grants.md).[^r9k-site]

# Deadlines
None.

# Track record
- Multiple cohorts 2025–2026.[^r9k-c4]
