# Summary
The Uniswap Foundation (UF) runs an open grants program for teams building protocols, tooling, research, governance and community across the Uniswap ecosystem, at any stage, and lists it as open.[^uf-build] It also offers Unichain grants (up to $7,500 plus product/GTM support for DeFi projects with $250K+ TVL and infra projects), audit subsidies via UFSF, and router subsidies.[^uf-build] Third-party listings put UF grants at $7,500 to $1M+.[^uf-web3grants]

# Eligibility
- Teams building on or for Uniswap (v4 hooks, Unichain, tooling, research); see UF Open Grants eligibility criteria.[^uf-build]

# What it funds
- Protocol innovation, developer tooling, research, governance, security, community.[^uf-dev]

# Amounts & terms
- Scope-based; payment currency/KYC not specified on the page (verify with UF).[^uf-build]

# How to apply
- Submit via the form linked from UF's Build page after reviewing eligibility criteria.[^uf-build]
