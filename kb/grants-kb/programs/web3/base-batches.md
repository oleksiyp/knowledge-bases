# Summary
Base Batches is a program to help onchain builders kickstart a business through mentorship, networking and funding.[^bb-site] In 2026 the Startup Track admitted the top 15 pre-seed teams (under $250K raised), each receiving a $10K grant, with at least three getting $50K investment from the Base Ecosystem Fund and consideration by Coinbase Ventures; Demo Day was in San Francisco in late May.[^bb-site]

# Eligibility
- Startup Track: pre-seed teams with <$250K raised; Student Track: undergraduates worldwide; Robotics Track: robotics builders.[^bb-site]

# What it funds
- Early-stage onchain startups on Base.[^bb-site]

# Amounts & terms
- $10K grant (non-dilutive) per Startup Track team; investments are separate and dilutive.[^bb-site]

# How to apply
- Online application during the annual window.[^bb-site]
