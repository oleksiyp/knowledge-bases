# Summary
The Ecosystem Support Program (ESP) is the Ethereum Foundation's grant arm for "free and open-source projects that strengthen Ethereum's foundations" — builder tools, infrastructure, research and other public goods.[^esp-home] After pausing open applications in August 2025, ESP relaunched in November 2025 with a proactive model: money is offered only against EF-curated **Wishlist** items (open-ended gaps) and **RFPs** (prescriptive, time-bound problems), plus non-financial Office Hours.[^esp-blog][^esp-applicants] Since applications submitted on or after 10 June 2026, grants are paid **in ETH by default**.[^ethdaily-eth] ESP continues to allocate several million dollars per quarter (≈$9.86M in Q1 2026, ≈$5.5M in Q2 2026).[^kucoin-q1][^esp-q2-2026]

# Eligibility
- Individuals, teams, nonprofits, academics and for-profit companies may apply, but the funded work must be "free, open-source, non-commercial" and freely accessible.[^esp-applicants]
- The proposal must map to a live Wishlist item or RFP; one application per opportunity.[^esp-applicants]
- Priority is infrastructure, developer tools, cryptographic research and ecosystem education rather than end-user applications.[^esp-applicants]

# What it funds
- Protocol/client work, zero-knowledge proofs, cryptography, security and formal verification, developer tooling, privacy, community resources.[^esp-q2-2026]
- Does **not** fund commercial products, token launches or closed-source work.[^esp-applicants]

# Amounts & terms
- Amount is set by scope and negotiated with the Grant Management team; RFPs may carry budget guidance.[^esp-applicants]
- Milestone-based payments; recipients complete identity verification and sign a formal grant letter (KYC).[^esp-applicants]
- Paid on-chain in ETH by default, with limited exceptions where legal/operational constraints prevent receiving ETH; applies to applications from 10 June 2026 onward.[^esp-applicants][^ethdaily-eth]

# How to apply
- Browse Wishlist/RFPs → apply → review (typically 3–6 weeks) → decision → execute → publish results.[^esp-applicants]
- Use Office Hours first to check alignment with a Wishlist/RFP item.[^esp-home]
- As of 2026-10-03 the RFP page lists **no active RFPs**; Wishlist items remain the main entry point.[^esp-rfp]

# Track record
- Q2 2026: ≈$5.50M allocated; grantees include Lodestar and Lighthouse (consensus clients), Gean (Go consensus client), Steward (wallet), and ZK proving work by Matter Labs, Brevis, Succinct.[^esp-q2-2026]
- Q1 2026: ≈$9.86M allocated; cumulative ESP giving is reported at $172M+ to 1,700+ projects.[^kucoin-q1][^ethdaily-eth]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you maintain or can build OSS Ethereum infrastructure, tooling, cryptography or research that matches a Wishlist item; you can receive ETH and pass KYC.
- **Poor fit if:** you are building a commercial dApp or token project, need fiat, or want to pitch an idea outside EF's published priorities.
