# Summary
Funding the Commons (FtC) convenes public-goods funders and builders and runs builder residencies, hackathons and fellowships.[^ftc-home] It reports $790K+ in public-goods funding facilitated and 88 open-source projects built in residencies.[^ftc-home] The 2026 El Salvador Fellowship was a fully funded six-day residency (12–17 Oct 2026) for founders of digital public goods; applications closed 18 Sep 2026.[^ftc-es]

# Eligibility
- Builders/founders of open-source digital public goods and mission-driven tech.[^ftc-es]

# What it funds
- Residency costs, mentorship, connections to funders; hackathon prizes.[^ftc-res]

# Amounts & terms
- "Fully funded" residencies; no published cash grant sizes.[^ftc-es]

# How to apply
- Apply per residency call on fundingthecommons.io.[^ftc-home]
