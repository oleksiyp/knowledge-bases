# Summary
Craig Newmark Philanthropies (CNP) accepts brief proposals on a **rolling basis** through a web form. It releases money for multi-year efforts annually, based on progress.[^cnp-apply] Current priorities are cybersecurity, veterans and military families, and effective communications and collaboration.[^cnp-apply] In 2022 CNP pledged $50M to "cyber civil defense".[^cnp-50m] It is also a donor to ISOC's [Common Good Cyber Fund](isoc-common-good-cyber-fund.md).[^cgcf-page]

# Eligibility
- US 501(c)(3) public charities (509(a)(1) or (a)(2)) active with the IRS and their state. Fiscally sponsored projects can apply with their sponsor agreement.[^cnp-apply]

# What it funds
- Cybersecurity, including clinics with extra requirements, plus veterans' work and trustworthy communications.[^cnp-apply]

# Amounts & terms
- No published range.[^cnp-apply]

# How to apply
- Fill in the Information Request and Checklist form and submit a short proposal covering the problem, solution, budget, metrics, communications and collaboration.[^cnp-apply]

# Deadlines
Rolling.

# Track record
- The $50M cyber civil defense pledge (2022).[^cnp-50m]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you are a US nonprofit building open security tooling or training for under-resourced organisations.
- **Poor fit if:** you are outside the US or not a 501(c)(3).
