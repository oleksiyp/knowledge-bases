# Summary
The General Support Fund (GSF) gives general or programme support of at least $10,000 to individuals, groups, affiliates and organisations whose work is 100% focused on Wikimedia projects.[^wm-gsf] Maximum amounts vary by region (for example up to $500k a year in the US & Canada), and the average first-time grant in FY2024–25 was $33k.[^wm-gsf] Round 1 of FY26–27 closed on 1 September 2026 (30 September for Northern & Western Europe), with decisions on 13 and 27 November. **Round 2 closes on 1 March 2027.**[^wm-gsf]

# Eligibility
- Applicants must be from the Wikimedia movement and aligned with its mission, must not be blocked on any project, must comply with the Universal Code of Conduct, and must be in a country where funding is legally allowed.[^wm-gsf]
- You must contact your Regional Program Officer at least one month before the deadline.[^wm-gsf]

# What it funds
- Ongoing programmes and organisational costs for Wikimedia work, which can include technical staff and tool development.[^wm-gsf]

# Amounts & terms
- Minimum $10k. Multi-year grants are possible, with annual reporting.[^wm-gsf]

# How to apply
- Talk to your Regional Program Officer, then submit your proposal by the round deadline.[^wm-gsf]
