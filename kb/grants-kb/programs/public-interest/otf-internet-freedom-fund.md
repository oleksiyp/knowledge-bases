# Summary
The Internet Freedom Fund (IFF) is the Open Technology Fund's main, always-open grant line. It pays for technology development, applied research, digital-security ecosystem work and community events that help people living under repressive censorship or surveillance.[^otf-iff] Awards range from $10,000 to $900,000 over up to 24 months, but OTF says the ideal range is $50,000–$200,000 for 6–12 month projects.[^otf-iff] As of October 2026 the fund page still says it accepts applications on a rolling basis, even though OTF's US federal grant has been in litigation since March 2025.[^otf-iff][^wiki-otf]

# Eligibility
- Individuals aged 18+ and for-profit or nonprofit organisations of any nationality.[^otf-iff]
- Applicants in countries under US (OFAC) trade restrictions are not eligible.[^otf-iff]

# What it funds
- Technology development (e.g. circumvention, secure communications, privacy tools), applied research, digital-security ecosystem improvements, and events or community convenings.[^otf-iff]
- It does **not** fund basic connectivity or ICT4D projects. It also does not fund legal or policy advocacy unless that work is narrowly focused technical research.[^otf-iff]

# Amounts & terms
- $10,000–$900,000, up to 24 months. Typical awards are $50,000–$200,000.[^otf-iff]
- OTF grantees can also use OTF's Labs for free security audits, UX work and engagement support (see [OTF Labs](otf-labs.md)).

# How to apply
1. Submit a concept note through OTF's online system.[^otf-iff]
2. OTF invites selected applicants to submit a full proposal.[^otf-iff]
3. OTF's Advisory Council does the final review. Expect a reply to the concept note within about 6–8 weeks.[^otf-iff]

# Deadlines
| Deadline | Note |
|---|---|
| Rolling | Concept notes are accepted at any time.[^otf-iff] |

# Track record
- In 2025 heise reported that OTF supported roughly 50 projects. Annual support included about $800k for Let's Encrypt, about $500k for Tor and $396k for F-Droid, and OpenVPN was also among them.[^heise-otf-2025]
- **2025–26 disruption:** USAGM terminated OTF's grant on 15 March 2025, and OTF sued.[^wiki-otf][^otf-lawsuit] A June 2025 preliminary injunction ordered the FY2024 funds released. OTF went back to court in October 2025 over withheld FY2025 money.[^wiki-otf] The government filed a notice of appeal to the DC Circuit on 26 January 2026.[^courtlistener-otf] Applicants should expect possible delays in disbursement.

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you build or maintain open tools used by people in censored or surveilled environments (circumvention, encrypted messaging, anti-surveillance), or you do measurement research on those environments.
- **Poor fit if:** your project is general civic tech, connectivity or ICT4D, or advocacy, or you are located in an OFAC-sanctioned country.
