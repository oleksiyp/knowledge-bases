# Summary
The UNICEF Venture Fund makes **equity-free** investments in **open-source** frontier-technology startups from emerging economies. Early-stage awards are $50k–$100k and growth-stage awards $200k–$400k, from a $35M fund with 147 investments to date.[^unicef-vf] Calls are themed. In 2026 there was a **Blockchain Ventures** call (up to $100k paid in ETH, BTC or USDC; closed 10 March 2026) and a **Climate Ventures** call for climate × child-health tech (up to $100k; closed 17 May 2026). The Climate Ventures call is the first cohort of a five-year programme.[^od-unicef-bc-2026][^unicef-vf-climate-2026]

# Eligibility
- Private companies registered in a UNICEF programme country, with a working prototype or pilot results.[^unicef-vf-climate-2026][^od-unicef-bc-2026]
- Open-source licensing is mandatory (e.g. GPL, MIT, BSD, CERN or CC-BY).[^od-unicef-bc-2026] Woman-led startups and young founders are encouraged.[^unicef-vf-climate-2026]

# What it funds
- AI, ML, blockchain, data science and other frontier tech that benefits children, including through climate, health and education.[^unicef-vf][^unicef-vf-climate-2026]

# Amounts & terms
- Equity-free. Early-stage awards come with 12–18 months of mentorship.[^unicef-vf][^od-unicef-bc-2026]

# How to apply
- Online form during each themed call.[^od-unicef-bc-2026]

# Track record
- 147 investments, 81 of them in startups in UNICEF programme countries.[^unicef-vf]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you are an open-source startup in an emerging market with a working product and impact metrics.
- **Poor fit if:** you are based in a high-income country or your code is closed.
