# Summary
Fast Forward is the main US-based accelerator for **tech nonprofits**: nonprofit organisations whose core product is software or AI.[^ffwd-acc] Each selected organisation gets an unrestricted grant of $25,000 or more, mentorship, peer support and a Demo Day.[^ffwd-acc] The 2027 cohort application window ran from 17 August to 7 September 2026. Interviews run until December, and the programme runs from late February to early June 2027.[^ffwd-acc]

# Eligibility
- A nonprofit model with a minimum viable product already built, at an early stage. Applicants are judged on leadership, technical talent, impact, scalability, lived experience and alignment.[^ffwd-acc]
- International applicants are accepted if they can participate in English (see the FAQ).[^ffwd-faq]

# What it funds
- Unrestricted support for scaling tech-nonprofit products.[^ffwd-acc]

# Amounts & terms
- $25k+ unrestricted. Equity-free (nonprofits).[^ffwd-acc]

# How to apply
- Online application, then interviews, then selection by year end.[^ffwd-acc] An application workshop was held on 12 August 2026.[^ffwd-acc]

# Track record
- 112 portfolio organisations since 2014, with $1.4B in follow-on funding. Alumni include SIRUM, Dollar For, Koko and Lenny Learning.[^ffwd-acc]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you have turned (or will turn) an open civic or health app into a nonprofit and need your first institutional backing.
- **Poor fit if:** you are a for-profit startup or have no product yet.
