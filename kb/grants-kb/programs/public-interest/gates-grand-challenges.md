# Summary
Grand Challenges is the Gates Foundation's open-RFP channel. Calls are themed and time-limited, and they increasingly target AI and digital tools for health and development.[^gcgh-home][^gcgh-ai] The most recent software-relevant call, **AI-Enabled Consumer Engagement to Advance Family Planning**, offered up to $500k for 12 months. It required an existing user base in sub-Saharan Africa and closed on 25 August 2026.[^ofy-gc-fp] In October 2026 the portal said "There are no open Grant Opportunities at this time".[^gcgh-opps]

# Eligibility
- Usually open worldwide to nonprofits, companies, universities, international organisations and government agencies. Each call adds its own geographic and operational requirements.[^ofy-gc-fp]

# What it funds
- Call-specific: for example, the use of AI in health, equitable AI in low- and middle-income countries, and diagnostics.[^gcgh-ai][^ofy-gc-fp]

# Amounts & terms
- Varies by call. The family-planning call offered up to $500k for 12 months with indirect costs capped at 15%.[^ofy-gc-fp]

# How to apply
- Short online proposals via the Gates submission portal while a call is open.[^ofy-gc-fp]

- In January 2026 Grand Challenges announced awards for "Innovations for Gram-Negative Antibiotic Discovery".[^gcgh-home]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you have a deployed digital-health or AI tool in low- and middle-income countries and can run a 12-month evaluation.
- **Poor fit if:** you have no operations in the target countries.
