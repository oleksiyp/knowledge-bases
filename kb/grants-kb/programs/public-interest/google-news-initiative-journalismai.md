# Summary
The Google News Initiative (GNI) is Google's journalism-support arm. Its main open grant line for news technology is the **JournalismAI Innovation Challenge**, run by Polis at the LSE.[^jai-2025] The 2025 edition, announced on 11 November 2025, funded up to 12 small and medium publishers with $50k or $100k each to build AI for audience intelligence and revenue.[^jai-2025] Applications closed on 3 December 2025, and the 9-month programme runs from February 2026.[^jai-2025] A 2026 edition has not been announced; if the pattern holds, a call would come around November 2026 (unverified).

# Eligibility
- Small and medium news publishers (10–200 staff) that have operated for at least 12 months, have a verifiable digital presence and are already exploring AI. Previous winners are excluded.[^latam-jai]

# What it funds
- AI experiments and implementations for audience intelligence, business innovation and revenue.[^jai-2025]

# Amounts & terms
- $50k or $100k over a 9-month programme.[^jai-2025]

# How to apply
- An online application during the call window. Results came out at the end of January 2026.[^jai-2025]
