# Summary
Wellcome's Mental Health Data Prizes fund multidisciplinary teams, with people with lived experience involved, to build **data tools** for mental health.[^wellcome-mhdp] The UK 2026–28 edition, run with Social Finance, targets early intervention for anxiety, depression and psychosis. Applications closed on 8 May 2026.[^wellcome-mhdp] The Africa 2024–26 edition, run with APHRC, gave £200k to each of 10 teams.[^wellcome-mhdp]

# Eligibility
- Teams in the edition's geography (UK; Africa; earlier UK and South Africa).[^wellcome-mhdp]

# What it funds
- Data tools and analysis that improve mental-health understanding and intervention.[^wellcome-mhdp]

# Amounts & terms
- Africa: £200k per team. The original 2022–23 prize totalled £1.4M across three phases.[^wellcome-mhdp]

# How to apply
- Through the delivery partner (Social Finance for the UK edition).[^wellcome-mhdp]
