# Summary
Tech To The Rescue (TTTR) matches nonprofits with tech companies for pro bono work and has delivered more than €18M in pro bono value.[^ttr-home] Its 2026 programmes are the **AI Impact Lab** and the **AI Impact Scaling Program**.[^ttr-home] The Lab is a free 10-week online programme that produces a testable AI prototype, a solution blueprint and team upskilling. Validated prototypes can move on to the Scaling Program.[^ttr-lab] Lab applications were closed in October 2026, with a waitlist open.[^ttr-lab]

# Eligibility
- Social-impact organisations with a clearly defined problem and an active intervention, and a team of 2–3 people including two decision-makers and one technical member.[^ttr-lab]

# What it funds
- In-kind AI prototyping, strategy and mentoring. It gives **no cash**.[^ttr-home][^ttr-lab]

# Amounts & terms
- Pro bono. There is no participation fee.[^ttr-lab]

# How to apply
- Join the waitlist on the AI Impact Lab page.[^ttr-lab]

# Deadlines
No published deadline.

# Track record
- More than €18M in pro bono value delivered across its ecosystem.[^ttr-home]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you are a nonprofit without in-house engineers that wants an AI pilot built.
- **Poor fit if:** you are a developer looking for money for your own project.
