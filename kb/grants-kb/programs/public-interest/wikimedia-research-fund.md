# Summary
The Wikimedia Research Fund supports established researchers, and researchers who want to start working on Wikimedia.[^wm-research] Type 1 grants are $2k–$50k for 12 months. Type 2 extended research is up to $150k over 24 months ($75k a year maximum). Type 3 events and community-building grants are up to $50k.[^wm-research] In the 2025–26 cycle, letters of intent closed on 16 January 2026 and final decisions came on 1 May 2026.[^wm-research]

# Eligibility
- Established researchers, or established researchers moving into Wikimedia research. Individuals can hold up to three open grants and organisations up to five.[^wm-research]

# What it funds
- Research about Wikimedia projects and communities.[^wm-research]
- It does **not** fund "proposals to develop a product or technology in support of the Wikimedia projects".[^wm-research] Use the [Rapid Fund](wikimedia-rapid-fund.md) for tools instead.

# Amounts & terms
- See the summary above. Work may start from 1 July 2026 for the current cycle.[^wm-research]

# How to apply
- Two stages: an LOI, then a full proposal. All deadlines are 23:59 AoE.[^wm-research]
