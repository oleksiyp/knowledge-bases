# Summary
Technology and Society is one of Ford's programmes under "Challenging Inequality".[^ford-opps] Ford "does not accept unsolicited grant proposals by mail, email or phone". Program officers find and invite grantees.[^ford-opps] For outsiders, the practical entry points are **intermediary regranting funds** it backs, such as the [Disability Inclusion Fund x Tech](disability-inclusion-fund-tech.md) (co-funded with MacArthur).[^dif-tech-2026]

# Eligibility
- By invitation only.[^ford-opps]

# What it funds
- Ford's grant-opportunities page lists only the programme name, Technology and Society, under "Challenging Inequality".[^ford-opps] It describes no detailed scope or public range.
- Through intermediaries, its funding reaches disability-led tech work, among other areas.[^dif-tech-2026]

# Amounts & terms
- No public range.

# How to apply
- No open applications. Build visibility with Ford program officers and grantees, or apply to Ford-backed intermediaries.[^ford-opps]

# Deadlines
None.

# Track record
- Co-funder of Disability Inclusion Fund x Tech (2026–27 round).[^dif-tech-2026]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you lead an established US or global public-interest-tech nonprofit that is already in Ford's network.
- **Poor fit if:** you are an individual developer or a company.
