# Summary
The Bezos Earth Fund launched its **AI for Climate and Nature Grand Challenge** in April 2024 with a $100M commitment, and it drew more than 1,000 proposals from nonprofits and universities.[^bef-ai] Phase I grants were announced in May 2025 (24 grants).[^bef-ai] On 23 October 2025 the Fund announced **Phase II**: $30M to 15 teams, up to $2M each, to scale AI for biodiversity, climate and food security.[^bef-phase2] No further open call had been announced as of October 2026.[^bef-ai][^bef-phase2]

# Eligibility
- Nonprofits and academic institutions (Phase II winners include TNC, Yale, Cornell and the New York Botanical Garden).[^bef-phase2]

# What it funds
- AI for sustainable proteins, power-grid optimisation, biodiversity conservation, plus a wildcard category.[^bef-ai][^bef-phase2]

# Amounts & terms
- Phase II: up to $2M per team.[^bef-phase2]

# How to apply
- No open call at present.[^bef-ai]

# Deadlines
None open.

# Track record
- 39 grants totalling $29.6M under the initiative so far, per the initiative page.[^bef-ai]
