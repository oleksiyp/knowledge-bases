# Summary
Co-Develop is a global nonprofit fund that helps countries adopt safe, inclusive digital public infrastructure (DPI).[^codev-faq] It was founded by the Rockefeller Foundation, the Norwegian Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Digital Public Goods Alliance, and it pools money from several donors.[^codev-faq] It runs **no open calls**. Investment ideas are co-created with countries and technical-assistance providers, then approved by its Investment Committee.[^codev-faq] Grantees include open-source DPGs such as Mojaloop, OpenCRVS, OpenSPP, OpenG2P and Mifos.[^codev-inv]

# Eligibility
- In practice: proven DPG builders deployed at population scale, technical-assistance providers, capacity-building initiatives and research institutions.[^codev-inv]

# What it funds
- Open-source code bases for DPI (ID, payments, civil registration, social protection), plus technical assistance, governance and inclusive design.[^codev-faq][^codev-inv]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you maintain a registered DPG already running national-scale DPI deployments.
- **Poor fit if:** you are early-stage or have no government adopters.
