# Summary
Climate Change AI (CCAI), fiscally sponsored by Future Earth, ran Innovation Grants of up to about $150k for one-year AI/ML climate projects. Projects had to produce **publicly available datasets and tools**.[^ccai-ig] Funders included the Quadrature Climate Foundation, Schmidt Futures, DeepMind and the Global Methane Hub.[^ccai-ig] CCAI's page now says it "does not currently have any future calls of this program planned". The 2025 call was the last.[^ccai-ig] Some aggregators list a 15 September 2026 deadline; that date matches the closed **2024** call, not a 2026 one.[^ccai-ig]

# Eligibility
- Earlier calls set their own eligibility rules, which are not restated on the current page. Check the archived call texts linked from CCAI's page.[^ccai-ig]

# What it funds
- Research, deployment, datasets and tools for climate mitigation, adaptation and climate science.[^ccai-ig]

# Amounts & terms
- Up to about $150k for one year. Public data and tool release required.[^ccai-ig]

# How to apply
- No open call. Prospective sponsors can contact partnerships@climatechange.ai.[^ccai-ig]
