# Summary
The Patrick J. McGovern Foundation (PJMF) is one of the largest funders of AI and data for social good. It reports nearly $500M in contributions since inception, and its public grants database shows 2026 awards mostly between $300k and $750k.[^pjmf-grants] PJMF does not take unsolicited proposals. Grantees are identified through a strategy-driven, invitation-only process. It has occasionally run open calls such as the 2023 Data & Society Accelerator.[^ip-pjmf]

# Eligibility
- By invitation. Grantees are typically nonprofits, research institutions and social enterprises.[^ip-pjmf]

# What it funds
- Building AI and data capacity in nonprofits and public institutions, and AI for climate, health equity, human rights and journalism (based on its grants database).[^pjmf-grants]

# Amounts & terms
- Recorded grants range from $30k to $2M.[^pjmf-grants]

# How to apply
- No open applications. Watch for occasional open calls and accelerators.[^ip-pjmf]

# Deadlines
None.

# Track record
- The grants database (filterable and exportable) lists awards since 2017.[^pjmf-grants]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you are a nonprofit with a data or AI team doing visible public-interest work.
- **Poor fit if:** you are a for-profit startup or an individual.
