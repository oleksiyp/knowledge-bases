# Summary
FRIDA is LACNIC's grants programme for Internet development in Latin America and the Caribbean.[^frida-home] The 2026 call had two categories: **Internet Stability and Security**, and **AI applied to Internet and Networks**. It funded projects with up to $20,000 for up to 12 months, plus technical accompaniment.[^frida-2026-upch] Applications closed on 12 July 2026, and FRIDA's site now lists preselected projects.[^frida-2026-upch][^frida-home]

# Eligibility
- Research projects, case studies, proofs of concept, prototypes and implementations from the LAC region. See the call's rules ("bases") for applicant types.[^frida-2026-upch][^frida-home]

# What it funds
- Network architecture, traffic engineering, cryptography, security resilience, IPv6, and AI/ML for network operations and security.[^frida-2026-upch]

# Amounts & terms
- Up to $20k over at most 12 months.[^frida-2026-upch]

# How to apply
- Two stages: a simplified pre-selection application, then a full proposal for technical evaluation.[^frida-2026-upch]
