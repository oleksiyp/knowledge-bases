# Summary
ICRP funds researchers studying the "technical orchestration of repressive information controls", meaning censorship, filtering and surveillance.[^otf-icrp] Fellows receive $7,000/month for 3–12 months, plus travel and equipment allowances, and work full time at a host organisation.[^otf-icrp] The 2026 concept-note window closed on 7 September 2026. Invited full proposals are due on 1 November 2026, and projects start around March 2027.[^otf-icrp]

# Eligibility
- Postdocs, doctoral students and experienced practitioners from any discipline, of any nationality, except people in OFAC-sanctioned countries.[^otf-icrp]
- PhD students need departmental support documentation unless they justify opting out.[^otf-icrp]
- Fellows must embed with a host organisation they are not already affiliated with. OTF can help find a host.[^otf-icrp]

# What it funds
- Applied research on censorship and surveillance techniques, measurement and circumvention.[^otf-icrp]

# Amounts & terms
- $7,000/month. Up to $5,000 for travel and up to $5,000 for equipment, both subject to OTF approval.[^otf-icrp]

# How to apply
- Submit a concept note. Invited applicants then submit a full proposal.[^otf-icrp]
