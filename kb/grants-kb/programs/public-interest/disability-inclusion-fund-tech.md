# Summary
The Disability Inclusion Fund (DIF), hosted by Borealis Philanthropy, runs a **Disability x Tech** initiative funded by Ford's Technology & Society programme and MacArthur's Technology in the Public Interest programme.[^dif-tech-2026] The 2026–27 round offered $25k–$70k grants for 1–2 years to US disability-led groups with budgets under $1M. Applications closed on 20 May 2026.[^dif-tech-2026]

# Eligibility
- US or US-territory organisations with an annual budget under $1M. Applicants can be 501(c)(3)s, fiscally sponsored groups, or LLCs and for-profits restricted to charitable use.[^dif-tech-2026]
- The organisation must be **disability-led**: more than 50% of management or boards are people with disabilities.[^dif-tech-2026]

# What it funds
- Disabled people teaching tech skills, disability-justice tech-policy work, disabled-led design and development of technology, equitable digital-infrastructure access, partnerships with makerspaces, and nonpartisan policy education.[^dif-tech-2026]
- It does **not** fund hardware or software product development as the primary focus, non-US organisations, hospitals or medical research, or commercial activity.[^dif-tech-2026]

# Amounts & terms
- $25k–$70k over 1–2 years, typically no more than 25% of the organisation's budget.[^dif-tech-2026]

# How to apply
- Online form during the call window.[^dif-tech-2026]

# Track record
- Current grantees work on 3D-printed wheelchairs, right-to-repair for assistive tech, video-conferencing improvements for disfluent speakers, and AAC policy advocacy.[^dif-tech-2026]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you are a small US disability-led group doing tech access, design or policy work.
- **Poor fit if:** your main deliverable is a software product, or you are outside the US.
