# Summary
OTF runs three "Resource Labs" that pay external experts to deliver services to internet-freedom projects. They are the **Security Lab** (audits), the **User Experience & Discovery Lab** (design and usability) and the **Impact & Engagement Lab** (communications with users).[^otf-labs] The Security Lab serves qualifying internet-freedom projects as well as all OTF-funded technology projects.[^otf-labs] All three labs were listed as accepting applications in October 2026.[^otf-labs]

# Eligibility
- Internet-freedom technology projects. OTF grantees qualify automatically for the Security Lab.[^otf-labs]

# What it funds
- Security audits, UX/accessibility and design improvements, and communications or engagement strategy.[^otf-labs]

# Amounts & terms
- In-kind only. OTF pays the service providers directly.

# How to apply
- Each lab has its own form: apply.opentech.fund/security-lab/, /user-experience-discovery-lab/ and /impact-engagement-lab/.[^otf-labs]

# Deadlines
Rolling. Some lab cohorts have dated intakes, so check each form.

# Track record
Not summarised on the labs page.

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you maintain an open-source privacy or circumvention tool and need an independent audit or usability review.
- **Poor fit if:** you need cash for development.
