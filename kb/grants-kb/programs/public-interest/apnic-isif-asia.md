# Summary
ISIF Asia is the APNIC Foundation's flagship grants programme. It funds Asia-Pacific organisations working on connectivity and on Internet-based solutions for underserved communities.[^isif-asia] There are three grant tiers: up to $20k, $50k and $75k.[^isif-asia] The 2026 call covered IPv6, digital health, essential services, inclusive access, environment and energy, climate impacts and disaster risk.[^ptc-isif-2026] Concept notes closed on 24 April 2026, winners are announced in October, and the next round opens in 2027.[^isif-asia]

# Eligibility
- Organisations only (government bodies, companies, NGOs). Individuals are not eligible.[^isif-asia]
- The organisation must be registered in one of the 56 economies in APNIC's service region, and the work must happen in the Asia-Pacific.[^isif-asia]

# What it funds
- Infrastructure, inclusion and innovation projects. Software or platform work qualifies when it serves these goals.[^isif-asia][^ptc-isif-2026]

# Amounts & terms
- Tiers of $20k, $50k and $75k.[^isif-asia]

# How to apply
- Two stages: a concept note (March–April), then an invited full application (due early July).[^isif-asia]
