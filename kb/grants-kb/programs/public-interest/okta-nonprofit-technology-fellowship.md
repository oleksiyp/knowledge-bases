# Summary
Okta for Good's Nonprofit Technology Fellowship develops technology leaders at nonprofits through convenings and coaching. Each fellow's organisation receives a **$25,000 grant** from the Okta for Good Fund, held at Tides Foundation.[^okta-ntf] The 2026 cohort applied from 6 to 31 October 2025 and started in February 2026.[^okta-ntf] Going by that annual October pattern, the next call is likely in fall 2026, but the page showed no new dates on 3 October 2026.[^okta-ntf]

# Eligibility
- Full-time employees of a 501(c)(3) or international equivalent whose role is primarily technology. International applicants are welcome.[^okta-ntf]

# What it funds
- Leadership development plus unrestricted support for the organisation.[^okta-ntf]

# Amounts & terms
- $25k to the organisation, pending Tides approval.[^okta-ntf]

# How to apply
- Online application in October, then finalist interviews in November–December.[^okta-ntf]
