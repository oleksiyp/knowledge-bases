# Summary
The ISOC Foundation's Research Grant Program funds applied, open research in four areas: an inclusive Internet, greening the Internet, measuring meaningful connectivity, and a trustworthy Internet.[^isocf-research] Independent researchers can receive up to $200,000 and organisations up to $500,000.[^isocf-research] Outputs must be openly published at no cost.[^isocf-research] The 2026 window ran from 7 April to 22 May 2026.[^isocf-research]

# Eligibility
- Public research institutions with 501(c)(3) or equivalent status, public universities, and independent researchers with advanced degrees and peer-reviewed publications.[^isocf-research]
- Applicants must register in Fluxx at least 24 hours before the deadline.[^isocf-research]

# What it funds
- Applied research addressing real-world Internet questions, including measurement work that often produces open datasets and tools.[^isocf-research]

# Amounts & terms
- Up to $200k for individuals and $500k for organisations. Open publication is required.[^isocf-research]

# How to apply
- Apply through Fluxx during the annual window.[^isocf-research]
