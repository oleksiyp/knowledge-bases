# Public-interest technology grants

* [Access Now Grants](access-now-grants.md) - Flexible project, core and discretionary/emergency grants (average $7k–$54k) to grassroots digital-rights organisations, mainly in low- and middle-income countries; not an open-application fund — contact the team.
* [ISIF Asia (APNIC Foundation)](apnic-isif-asia.md) - APNIC Foundation's annual Information Society Innovation Fund grants of $20k / $50k / $75k for organisations in the Asia-Pacific building Internet infrastructure, inclusion and resilience solutions; 2026 concept notes closed 24 Apr; next round 2027.
* [AWS Imagine Grant (US)](aws-imagine-grant.md) - Annual AWS program for US 501(c) nonprofits giving cash (up to $50k / $150k / $200k) plus AWS credits (up to $20k–$100k) for mission-critical cloud and AI projects; 2026 round closed 5 Jun 2026.
* [Bezos Earth Fund AI for Climate and Nature Grand Challenge](bezos-earth-fund-ai-grand-challenge.md) - $100M challenge (launched Apr 2024) funding nonprofits/academia to apply AI to sustainable proteins, grid optimisation, biodiversity; Phase I (May 2025) and Phase II ($30M, 15 teams up to $2M each, Oct 2025) awarded; no new open call (Oct 2026).
* [CIVICUS DDI Digital Resiliency Grants](civicus-ddi-digital-resiliency-grants.md) - Rolling rapid-response grants (up to $10k solo / $20k joint, ≤6 months) from the CIVICUS Crisis Response Fund and the EU/Denmark/Norway-backed Digital Democracy Initiative for Global South civil society facing digital and civic-space threats.
* [CIVICUS Digital Action Lab (DDI)](civicus-digital-action-lab.md) - 9-month cohort programme giving up to ten Global South CSOs $10k–$20k plus technical support to build and test digital civic-tech solutions; EU/Denmark/Norway-funded; Cohort 3 applications closed 30 Apr 2026.
* [Climate Change AI Innovation Grants](climate-change-ai-innovation-grants.md) - Up to ~$150k, one-year grants for AI/ML climate projects that release public datasets and tools; ran four calls 2022–2025, but CCAI states no future calls are planned (Oct 2026).
* [Co-Develop (Digital Public Infrastructure fund)](co-develop.md) - Pooled nonprofit fund (founded by Rockefeller Foundation, Norway MFA and DPGA) making country-demand-driven grants to DPGs and DPI technical-assistance providers (e.g. MOSIP, Mojaloop, OpenCRVS, OpenSPP, Mifos); no open calls.
* [Craig Newmark Philanthropies — rolling grants (cybersecurity, trustworthy information)](craig-newmark-philanthropies.md) - Rolling, brief-proposal grants from Craig Newmark Philanthropies to US 501(c)(3)s, with cybersecurity ("cyber civil defense") a top priority; also co-funds the ISOC Common Good Cyber Fund.
* [Digital Defenders Partnership (DDP) funds](digital-defenders-partnership.md) - Hivos-hosted, multi-government fund for digital protection of human-rights defenders (incident response, sustainable protection, regional and community funds); most funds marked CLOSED or invitation-only on its site in Oct 2026.
* [Disability Inclusion Fund x Tech (Borealis Philanthropy)](disability-inclusion-fund-tech.md) - $25k–$70k, 1–2 year grants to small US disability-led organisations working on disability and technology (tech skills, tech policy, disabled-led design, digital access); Ford and MacArthur-funded; 2026 deadline was 20 May 2026.
* [Fast Forward Tech Nonprofit Accelerator](fast-forward-accelerator.md) - Three-month accelerator for early-stage tech/AI nonprofits with an MVP, giving $25,000+ unrestricted grants, mentorship and Demo Day; 2027 cohort applications closed 7 Sep 2026; open to international applicants.
* [Ford Foundation Technology and Society](ford-technology-and-society.md) - Ford Foundation's technology-and-justice programme; invitation-only (no unsolicited proposals); also co-funds open regranting such as Disability Inclusion Fund x Tech.
* [Gates Foundation Grand Challenges](gates-grand-challenges.md) - Gates Foundation's open RFP series for global health and development innovations (often AI/digital), e.g. 2026 "AI-Enabled Consumer Engagement to Advance Family Planning" (up to $500k, deadline 25 Aug 2026); no calls open as of Oct 2026.
* [JournalismAI Innovation Challenge (Google News Initiative × LSE Polis)](google-news-initiative-journalismai.md) - GNI-funded grants of $50k or $100k for small/medium news publishers to build and test AI for audience and revenue; 2025 edition (12 grants) closed 3 Dec 2025, program running Feb–Oct 2026.
* [ISOC Foundation Beyond the Net Grant Program](isoc-beyond-the-net.md) - Grants up to $55,000 (max two concurrent) for Internet Society Chapters' local projects on community connectivity, resilient access, online safety and open-Internet advocacy; chapters only; page shows Closed (Oct 2026).
* [Common Good Cyber Fund (Internet Society Foundation)](isoc-common-good-cyber-fund.md) - Pooled multi-donor fund (AU, CA, DE, NL, UK governments, Craig Newmark Philanthropies, ISOC) giving ~15 two-year grants of $100k–$300k to nonprofits doing cybersecurity for the common good and protection of civil society; 2026 call closed 4 Aug 2026.
* [Internet Society Foundation Research Grant Program](isoc-foundation-research-grant.md) - Applied, openly-published Internet research grants — up to $200k for independent researchers and $500k for institutions — on inclusion, greening, measuring connectivity and trustworthiness; annual call (2026 deadline 22 May).
* [LACNIC FRIDA Program](lacnic-frida.md) - LACNIC's annual call funding up to USD 20,000 for 12-month Internet stability/security and AI-for-networks projects in Latin America & the Caribbean; 2026 call closed 12 Jul 2026 (preselection 7 Aug).
* [Patrick J. McGovern Foundation — AI for public purpose grants](mcgovern-foundation-ai-for-public-purpose.md) - Major AI-for-social-good funder (grants typically $300k–$750k in 2026; ~$500M given since inception) supporting nonprofits' AI/data capacity, climate, health, journalism and rights; invitation-only, with occasional open calls.
* [Microsoft AI for Accessibility grants (status check)](microsoft-ai-for-accessibility.md) - Microsoft's 2018 five-year, $25M AI for Accessibility program (Azure credits + grants for disability tech); Microsoft's current accessibility innovation page lists no open grant program (Oct 2026) — treat third-party "open until Dec 2026" listings as unverified.
* [Mozilla Fellows Program](mozilla-fellows.md) - 12-month Mozilla Foundation fellowship for builders of alternatives to dominant tech (privacy, AI auditing, open infrastructure, climate justice, data); $100k–$125k incl. $25k project budget; 2026 cycle closed 30 Jan 2026.
* [Okta for Good Nonprofit Technology Fellowship](okta-nonprofit-technology-fellowship.md) - Six-month fellowship for full-time nonprofit technology leaders worldwide; each fellow's organisation gets a $25,000 grant; annual call each autumn (2026 cohort applications closed 31 Oct 2025; next call expected fall 2026).
* [OTF Free and Open Source Software Sustainability Fund](otf-foss-sustainability-fund.md) - Annual OTF fund for maintenance, security and resilience of mature FOSS that internet-freedom tools depend on; ~$7k/month for individual maintainers up to $400k/24 months for larger efforts; 2026 round (co-funded by GitHub) closed 7 May 2026.
* [OTF Information Controls Research Program (ICRP)](otf-information-controls-research.md) - OTF research fellowship paying $7,000/month for 3–12 months (plus travel/equipment) to study the technical side of censorship and surveillance while embedded at a host organization; 2026 concept notes closed 7 Sep 2026.
* [OTF Internet Freedom Fund](otf-internet-freedom-fund.md) - Open Technology Fund's flagship rolling fund for technology, applied research and digital-security work that helps people facing censorship and surveillance; $10k–$900k (typically $50k–$200k); open and accepting concept notes despite 2025–26 US funding litigation.
* [OTF Labs (Security, UX & Discovery, Impact & Engagement)](otf-labs.md) - In-kind services from OTF — free security audits, UX/design and communications support — for internet-freedom projects (not only OTF grantees); applications open.
* [OTF Rapid Response Fund](otf-rapid-response-fund.md) - Short-term emergency funding and technical services (audits, forensics, DDoS mitigation, hosting) for activists, journalists and groups facing state-sponsored digital emergencies; typically up to $50k for ≤6 months; rolling.
* [OTF Surge and Sustain Fund](otf-surge-and-sustain-fund.md) - Per-user infrastructure funding for established, large-scale open-source VPN and anti-censorship tools serving users in China, Iran, Russia, Myanmar, Cuba and other censored countries; concept notes due 15 Oct 2026.
* [Tech To The Rescue — AI Impact Lab & AI Impact Scaling Program](tech-to-the-rescue.md) - Pro bono (non-cash) AI prototyping and scaling support for social-impact organisations, matched with tech companies (€18M+ pro bono value delivered); AI Impact Lab applications closed with a waitlist (Oct 2026).
* [Twilio.org Impact Fund](twilio-org-impact-fund.md) - Twilio.org grants for nonprofits using digital communications for digital transformation, humanitarian assistance and climate action ($14M to 70+ organisations in 2025); invitation-only rounds — register interest.
* [UNICEF Venture Fund](unicef-venture-fund.md) - Equity-free investments ($50k–$100k early-stage, $200k–$400k growth) in open-source frontier-tech startups (AI, blockchain, climate/health) from UNICEF programme countries; themed calls — 2026 Blockchain (closed 10 Mar) and Climate & Health (closed 17 May).
* [Wellcome Mental Health Data Prize](wellcome-mental-health-data-prize.md) - Wellcome-funded prize series for teams building data tools for mental health (UK 2026–28 edition on early intervention for anxiety, depression and psychosis, applications closed 8 May 2026; Africa 2024–26 edition awarded £200k per team).
* [Wikimedia General Support Fund](wikimedia-general-support-fund.md) - Wikimedia Foundation's general/operating support (min $10,000, regional caps up to $500k/yr; multi-year option) for groups and organisations 100% focused on Wikimedia projects; Round 2 deadline 1 Mar 2027.
* [Wikimedia Rapid Fund](wikimedia-rapid-fund.md) - Small ($500–$5,000) Wikimedia Foundation grants for 1–12 month community projects, including small-scale technical/tool projects for Wikimedia; five cycles a year (next deadlines 1 Nov 2026, 1 Feb 2027, 1 Apr 2027).
* [Wikimedia Research Fund](wikimedia-research-fund.md) - Wikimedia Foundation research grants ($2k–$50k; extended up to $150k over 2 years; events up to $50k) for established researchers studying Wikimedia projects; annual LOI cycle (2025–26 LOIs closed 16 Jan 2026); excludes product/tech development.
