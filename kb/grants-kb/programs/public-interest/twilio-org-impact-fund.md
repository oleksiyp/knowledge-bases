# Summary
The Twilio.org Impact Fund supports tech projects in digital transformation, humanitarian assistance and climate action. It reports $14M in grants and donations to more than 70 organisations in 2025.[^twilio-if] **Active grant rounds are invitation-only.** Organisations can share their contact details to be considered for future rounds.[^twilio-if]

# Eligibility
- Public-benefit nonprofits (501(c)(3)s or equivalents, including registered non-US NGOs) and certain international organisations.[^twilio-if]

# What it funds
- Digital transformation, humanitarian work, climate, displaced communities and tech-based social enterprises.[^twilio-if]

# Amounts & terms
- Not disclosed.[^twilio-if]

# How to apply
- Register interest via the Impact Fund page.[^twilio-if]

# Deadlines
None public.

# Track record
- $14M to more than 70 organisations in 2025.[^twilio-if]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you are a nonprofit running SMS, WhatsApp or voice-based services, especially in crises.
- **Poor fit if:** you need an open, competitive application.
