# Summary
The Surge and Sustain Fund covers the user costs of deploying large-scale, open-source VPN and anti-censorship tools.[^otf-funds] Payment is based on verified unique users in specific censored countries, multiplied by an agreed cost per user and capped per contract. Only infrastructure and bandwidth are covered, not staff.[^otf-sas] The current call opened on 15 September 2026, and concept notes are due on **15 October 2026**.[^otf-sas]

# Eligibility
- Individuals aged 18+ and nonprofit or for-profit organisations of any nationality. Applicants in OFAC-restricted countries and US-debarred entities are excluded.[^otf-sas]
- The fund targets "established, large-scale circumvention technologies" serving users in China, Cuba, Iran, Myanmar, Russia and other censored environments.[^otf-sas]

# What it funds
- Server, bandwidth and other infrastructure costs, scaled to verified active users.[^otf-sas] It does not cover staff costs.[^otf-sas]

# Amounts & terms
- Monthly payments equal verified active users × cost-per-user, up to a ceiling set per contract.[^otf-sas]

# How to apply
1. Stage 1: submit a concept note covering the tool's resilience, scale and compliance with the qualifying conditions.[^otf-sas]
2. Stage 2: invited applicants submit a detailed proposal with user metrics and a cost model.[^otf-sas]
