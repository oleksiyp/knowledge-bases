# Summary
CIVICUS's Crisis Response Fund and the Digital Democracy Initiative (DDI) jointly offer rapid-response "digital resiliency" grants. They are for local civil society facing imminent civic-space or digital threats.[^civicus-drg] Grants are up to $10,000 for one organisation or $20,000 for joint applications, and must be spent within six months.[^civicus-drg] The European Union, Denmark (Danida) and Norway back the programme, which makes it one of the European-funded options that remained while US democracy aid shrank in 2025–26.[^civicus-drg] Applications are accepted all year.[^civicus-drg]

# Eligibility
- Local CSOs, groups or movements, formal or informal, based in an ODA-eligible Global South country and facing a verifiable imminent threat.[^civicus-drg]
- Applicants need a bank account that can receive US transfers and must not have received CRF funding in the previous two years.[^civicus-drg]

# What it funds
- Responses that mitigate digital threats or threats to digital civic rights, and wider civic-space threats that digital resilience can counter.[^civicus-drg]

# Amounts & terms
- Up to $10k single or $20k joint, spent within six months or less.[^civicus-drg]

# How to apply
- Online application at any time. CIVICUS acknowledges within about a month, and funds typically arrive 6–8 weeks after a complete application.[^civicus-drg] See the guidelines PDF.[^civicus-drg-guidelines]

# Deadlines
Rolling.

# Track record
Not published on the page.

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you are a Global South civil-society group that needs quick money for digital security, secure tools or continuity during a crackdown.
- **Poor fit if:** you are based in the Global North or you are building a product.
