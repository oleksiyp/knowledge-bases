# Summary
The Rapid Response Fund (RRF) gives short-term, urgent digital-security help to high-risk people and groups facing a clear, time-sensitive digital emergency.[^otf-rrf] Help comes in two forms: technical services from about nine partner organisations, or direct money (usually up to $50,000 for six months or less).[^otf-rrf] Applications are reviewed as they arrive.[^otf-rrf]

# Eligibility
- Activists, journalists, human-rights defenders and organisations aged 18+ facing a digital emergency.[^otf-rrf]

# What it funds
- **Services:** security audits, forensic analysis, secure hosting, DDoS mitigation and localization.[^otf-rrf]
- **Direct funding:** circumvention solutions, personal digital protection, emergency tool development and decentralized communication networks.[^otf-rrf]
- It does **not** fund long-term capacity building or device-replacement claims.[^otf-rrf]

# Amounts & terms
- Typically $1–$50,000 for six months or less. OTF considers larger requests. Payments are made in USD when contract objectives are completed.[^otf-rrf]

# How to apply
- Fill in a single online form at apply.opentech.fund and read OTF's Applicant Guidebook first.[^otf-rrf]

# Track record
OTF publishes no aggregate figures on the fund page.

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** a tool or community you run is under active attack (DDoS, blocking, compromise) and you need fast help.
- **Poor fit if:** you need general development funding. Use the [Internet Freedom Fund](otf-internet-freedom-fund.md) instead.
