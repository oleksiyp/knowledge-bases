# Summary
The Digital Defenders Partnership (DDP) is hosted by Hivos. It funds digital protection for human-rights defenders, journalists and activists through four mechanisms: an Incident Response Fund (up to $5,000), a Regional Partnership Fund, a Sustainable Protection Fund and a Community & Network Fund (up to €15,000).[^ddp-home] When checked in October 2026, the site showed the first three as **CLOSED** and the Community & Network Fund as **invitation only**.[^ddp-home] Donors over time include about ten European and allied foreign ministries and the US State Department.[^hivos-ddp] Sweden's Sida has committed SEK 32m for 2024–2028.[^openaid-ddp]

# Eligibility
- Human-rights defenders, journalists, activists and their organisations facing digital threats. The regional and sustainable funds are for organisations in specific countries or regions.[^ddp-home]

# What it funds
- Emergency responses to digital attacks, long-term organisational security capacity, regional protection initiatives, and rapid responders or digital-security facilitators.[^ddp-home]
- It is not a software-development fund. Tool builders should look at [OTF](otf-internet-freedom-fund.md).

# Amounts & terms
- Incident Response Fund up to $5,000. Community & Network Fund up to €15,000. Other funds are sized by region.[^ddp-home]

# How to apply
- Check the funds page for status, or email team@digitaldefenders.org.[^ddp-home]

# Deadlines
None open at last check.

# Track record
- DDP was formed in 2012 by Freedom Online Coalition governments and has been funded since then by ministries of Australia, Canada, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, Germany, Latvia, the Netherlands, the UK, plus Sida and the US State Department.[^hivos-ddp]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you are a civil-society group or rapid responder needing protection funds. Watch for reopening.
- **Poor fit if:** you want to fund software development.
