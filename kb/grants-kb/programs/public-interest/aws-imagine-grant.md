# Summary
The AWS Imagine Grant funds US nonprofits that treat technology as mission-critical.[^od-aws-2026] There are three 2026 award tiers:
- **Momentum to Modernize:** up to $50k cash plus $20k in AWS credits.
- **Go Further, Faster:** up to $150k plus $100k in credits.
- **Pathfinder (frontier AI):** up to $200k plus $100k in credits and help from the AWS Generative AI Innovation Center.[^od-aws-2026]

The 2026 portal was open from 19 March to 5 June 2026, and Round 2 is by invitation.[^aws-us-guidelines]

# Eligibility
- US nonprofits with active 501(c) status of any budget size. Schools and universities are excluded.[^od-aws-2026]

# What it funds
- Cloud modernisation, new cloud-native solutions and frontier-AI projects in any cause area.[^od-aws-2026]

# Amounts & terms
- See the tiers above. Awards also include training and possible marketing support.[^od-aws-2026]

# How to apply
- Round 1 proposal on YouNoodle. Round 2 is invitation-only.[^aws-us-guidelines][^od-aws-2026]
