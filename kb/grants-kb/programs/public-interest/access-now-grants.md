# Summary
Access Now Grants gives flexible, grantee-driven funding to grassroots and frontline digital-rights groups, mostly in low- and middle-income countries.[^an-grants] There are three types: project grants (average $29k), core grants (average $54k) and discretionary grants for emergencies (average $7k, maximum $10k).[^an-grants] The page does not describe an open application window, and enquiries go to the grants team.[^an-grants]

# Eligibility
- Grassroots and frontline organisations working on digital rights, focused on low- and middle-income countries.[^an-grants]

# What it funds
- Work against internet shutdowns, digital-security help for at-risk groups, documenting and preventing online violence, and bringing digital rights into wider human-rights movements.[^an-grants]
- Building technology is not an explicit focus.[^an-grants]

# Amounts & terms
- See the averages above.[^an-grants]

# How to apply
- Contact the Access Now Grants team via the grants page. Most grants appear to be sourced proactively.[^an-grants]

# Deadlines
No public deadlines.

# Track record
- Access Now's 10-year retrospective covers its grantmaking volume and geography.[^an-10yrs]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you are a small digital-rights group in the Global Majority.
- **Poor fit if:** you are a software project looking for development funding.
