# Summary
The Digital Action Lab is a 9-month cohort programme for up to ten civil-society organisations. They co-design, test and scale digital solutions that strengthen civic space and inclusive democracy.[^civicus-dal] Each organisation receives a $10,000–$20,000 grant plus training, peer learning and technical guidance. The European Union, Danida and Norway fund it.[^civicus-dal] The Cohort 3 call closed on 30 April 2026.[^od-dal3]

# Eligibility
- CSOs in OECD DAC ODA-eligible countries, preferably working in obstructed or repressed civic space. Applicants need democracy or civic-space experience, some experience with digital tools, capacity to manage a grant, and working English.[^civicus-dal]
- Women, youth, LGBTQI+, Indigenous and grassroots groups get priority.[^civicus-dal]

# What it funds
- Developing and piloting digital tools for civic engagement, together with CIVICUS teams.[^civicus-dal]

# Amounts & terms
- $10k–$20k per organisation over 9 months.[^civicus-dal]

# How to apply
- Through an open call. Selected organisations then co-design their projects with CIVICUS.[^civicus-dal]

- **Poor fit if:** you are a tech company or need more than $20k.
