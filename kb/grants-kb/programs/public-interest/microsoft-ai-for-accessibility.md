# Summary
Microsoft launched **AI for Accessibility** in 2018 as a five-year, $25M program. It gave grants and Azure credits to developers, NGOs and researchers building AI for people with disabilities.[^tc-ai4a-2018][^ms-ai4a-winners] The five-year term has passed. As of October 2026, Microsoft's accessibility innovation page shows no active grant program, application form or deadline, only product, Ability Summit 2026 and resource content.[^ms-a11y-innov] Several grant aggregators still list an "open until December 2026" AI for Accessibility or "Accessibility Innovation" grant. This KB could not confirm that on any microsoft.com page, so the status here is **discontinued (unverified)**.

# Eligibility
- Historically global: developers, NGOs, academics and inventors.[^ms-ai4a-winners]

# What it funds
- Historically: AI for communication, employment and daily life for people with disabilities.[^tc-ai4a-2018]
