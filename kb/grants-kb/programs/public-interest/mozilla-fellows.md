# Summary
Mozilla Fellows lead a 12-month project building alternatives to dominant technology systems.[^od-moz-fellows] **Track I** pays $100k ($75k stipend plus $25k project budget). It is for embedded fellows in Kenya, South Africa, Brazil, MENA, Mexico, the UK, the US or the EU. **Track II** pays $125k ($100k stipend plus $25k project budget).[^od-moz-fellows] The 2026 cycle closed on 30 January 2026, and the 2026–27 cohort has been announced.[^od-moz-fellows][^moz-fellows-2026]

# Eligibility
- Individuals in countries where Mozilla, a US 501(c)(3), can legally make grants, with an emphasis on the Global Majority. Fellows must be able to receive ACH or wire payments from the US.[^od-moz-fellows]

# What it funds
- Six themes: user privacy, AI auditing, climate justice, protecting creators, democratising data and open infrastructure.[^od-moz-fellows]

# Amounts & terms
- $100k–$125k for 12 months. Fellows must take part in the cohort and share their work publicly.[^od-moz-fellows]

# How to apply
- Through the nomination/application form during the open window.[^od-moz-fellows]

# Track record
- 2026 fellows include work on open-sourcing Adalat AI's public-sector adoption methodology, community-governed motion-capture datasets (Paqarina), and mapping civil-society infrastructure dependencies in the WANA region (Euqda).[^moz-fellows-2026]

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you are an individual technologist with a 12-month open-infrastructure, AI-accountability or privacy project.
- **Poor fit if:** you are an organisation looking for core funding.
