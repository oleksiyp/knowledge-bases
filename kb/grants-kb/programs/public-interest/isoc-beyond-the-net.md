# Summary
Beyond the Net funds local projects by Internet Society Chapters. Projects cover community-centred connectivity, affordable and resilient access, online safety for newly connected users, and open-Internet advocacy.[^isocf-btn] A chapter can hold at most two grants at once, totalling up to $55,000.[^isocf-btn] The page showed the programme as **Closed** in October 2026.[^isocf-btn]

# Eligibility
- ISOC chapters in good standing only, with a bank account and all previous reports filed.[^isocf-btn]
- Requests above $35k need a prior successful Foundation grant of $20k or more. Requests above $20k need an external partner.[^isocf-btn]

# What it funds
- Local connectivity, resilience, safety training and advocacy projects.[^isocf-btn]

# Amounts & terms
- Up to $55k total. Apply at least 8–10 weeks before the project starts.[^isocf-btn]

# How to apply
- Apply through the Foundation's grants system when the programme is open.[^isocf-btn]

None open at last check.

# Fit
- **Good fit if:** you are active in an ISOC chapter and have a local connectivity or open-source tooling project.
- **Poor fit if:** you are not a chapter.
