# Summary
The Rapid Fund pays $500–$5,000 for short (1–12 month) projects run by individuals, groups or affiliates active in the Wikimedia movement.[^wm-rapid] Technical projects are eligible under dedicated guidelines, with an emphasis on small-scale development such as bots, tools and gadgets.[^wm-rapid] There are five fixed cycles per fiscal year. The next deadline is **1 November 2026**.[^wm-rapid]

# Eligibility
- Individuals, groups and affiliates engaged in Wikimedia, with a current contribution history on the target projects and organising or training experience.[^wm-rapid]
- Organisations can hold up to two open grants. Individuals must finish their previous grant before applying again.[^wm-rapid]

# What it funds
- Short, low-cost projects: editathons, workshops, events and small technical projects.[^wm-rapid]

# Amounts & terms
- $500–$5,000 for 1–12 months of future work.[^wm-rapid]

# How to apply
- Apply on Meta-Wiki or Fluxx by the cycle deadline. The review cycle takes about 53 days: 7 days of initial review, 28 days of review and 18 days for the decision and agreement.[^wm-rapid]
