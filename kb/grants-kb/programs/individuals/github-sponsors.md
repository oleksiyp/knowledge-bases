# Summary

GitHub Sponsors lets anyone with a GitHub account receive monthly or one-time sponsorships through a Sponsor button on their profile and repositories. GitHub reports $40M+ paid to maintainers, 103 supported regions and 4,200+ sponsoring organizations.[^gh-sponsors] Sponsorships from personal accounts carry no fee. Organizations paying by credit card pay up to 6% (3% card plus 3% GitHub), and invoiced billing avoids the card fee.[^gh-sponsors-fees] PayPal payouts were dropped in 2023, so payouts go through Stripe Connect.[^gh-sponsors-paypal]

# Eligibility

- Individuals or organizations in supported regions, with a completed profile, bank and tax information, and 2FA enabled.[^gh-sponsors]

# What it funds

- Whatever sponsors choose to support, usually ongoing maintenance.

# Amounts & terms

- Income depends entirely on sponsors. Maintainers set their own tiers.[^gh-sponsors]

# How to apply

- Join GitHub Sponsors, create tiers, then submit banking and tax documents.[^gh-sponsors]

# Deadlines

None.

# Track record

- $40M+ distributed.[^gh-sponsors]

# Fit

- **Good fit if** you have users on GitHub, and especially if companies depend on your code. Pair it with [thanks.dev](/programs/oss-infrastructure/thanks-dev.md) to reach dependency-based funders.
- **Poor fit if** you are in an unsupported region. Look at [Open Collective](/programs/individuals/open-collective.md) or [Liberapay](/programs/individuals/liberapay.md).
