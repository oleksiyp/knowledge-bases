# Summary

Astera Institute's residency pays individuals to build open, non-proprietary projects for 12–18 months on its Emeryville, California campus.[^astera-first][^astera-fall26-x] Residents get a salary of $125k–$250k, a project budget and access to large GPU clusters. Astera requires all outputs to be open: open-source software, open data and open publications.[^astera-first] The Fall 2026 call closed on 19 April 2026, with offers in early June and a start date of 17 August 2026.[^astera-fall26-x][^astera-fall26-granted] It is one of the best-funded individual programs for open AI or science software, but it requires relocation.

# Eligibility

- Individuals with an ambitious open project. Relocation and visa sponsorship were offered in the first cohort.[^astera-first]
- The Fall 2026 focus areas were neuro and AI, life sciences and open science, plus an open track.[^astera-fall26-x]

# What it funds

- Early-stage, open-first projects with societal-scale impact. Earlier focus areas included ML-assisted data curation and metascience tooling.[^astera-first]

# Amounts & terms

- Salary is $125,000–$250,000.[^astera-first] The Fall 2026 project budget was reported at up to $1.5M, with access to H100 and B200-class compute (third-party summary).[^astera-fall26-granted] An October 2025 post cites budgets of up to $2M.[^astera-next]
- Residents work on-site in Emeryville, with periodic reporting. All outputs must be open.[^astera-first]

# How to apply

- Apply through Astera's posted call (Ashby). Calls have been announced on Astera's Substack.[^astera-next]

# Track record

- The first cohort had about 20 residents.[^astera-first] Three more joined in October 2025, working on fusion cost analysis, space telescopes and bio-inspired fibers.[^astera-next]

# Fit

- **Good fit if** you want to build open AI or science infrastructure full-time and can move to the Bay Area.
- **Poor fit if** your project is proprietary or you cannot relocate.
