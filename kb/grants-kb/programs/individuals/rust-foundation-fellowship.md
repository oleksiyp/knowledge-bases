# Summary

From 2022 to 2024 the Rust Foundation ran yearly fellowships paying Rust contributors a monthly stipend, plus support for training and travel.[^rf-fellows-2024] In 2023 the stipend was $1,000 a month, with up to $2,000 each for travel and training.[^rf-fellowship-2023-x] The 2024 cohort, announced in October 2024, had 21 fellows across Project, Community and Project Goal tracks.[^rf-fellows-2024] The fellowship page says the application is closed and asks applicants to "stay tuned".[^rf-fellowship-page] In 2026 longer-term maintainer funding moved to the [Maintainers Fund](/programs/individuals/rust-maintainers-in-residence.md).

# Eligibility

- Applicants must be over 18 and able to receive US transfers. People paid to work on Rust full-time are not eligible (part-time paid contributors are). Foundation staff are excluded.[^rf-fellowship-page]

# What it funds

- Contributor time on Rust Project work, community-building and project goals.[^rf-fellows-2024]

# Amounts & terms

- A monthly stipend ($1,000 in 2023), plus travel and training budgets.[^rf-fellowship-2023-x] Project and Community fellowships last 12 months, and Project Goal fellowships 6 months.[^rf-fellows-2024]

- 21 fellows in the 2024 cohort.[^rf-fellows-2024]
