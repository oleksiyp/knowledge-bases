# Summary

Summer of Bitcoin is a GSoC-style global summer internship. University students spend 12 weeks contributing to bitcoin open-source projects and are paid in bitcoin.[^sob-what] Stipends are $3,000–$6,600, set by purchasing power parity for the student's location.[^sob-paid] The program is listed among OpenSats-funded projects.[^sob-opensats] The 2026 cycle has finished, with final evaluations from 16 to 30 August 2026.

# Eligibility

- The program targets university students.[^sob-what]
- Payment is in BTC, but participants can ask to be paid in local currency where regulations make BTC impractical.[^sob-paid]

# What it funds

- 12 weeks of summer work on bitcoin open-source projects, such as wallets, Lightning and libraries.[^sob-what]

# Amounts & terms

- The stipend is $3,000–$6,600 by PPP. 40% is paid after the midpoint evaluation (2026: 29 June–3 July) and 60% after the final evaluation (2026: 16–30 August).[^sob-paid]

# How to apply

- Apply online in early spring. Applicants then complete a selection process with the projects. Details are in the program guide.[^sob-what]
