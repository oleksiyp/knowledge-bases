# Summary

O'Shaughnessy Ventures (OSV) awards up to 20 fellowships a year. Each pays $100,000 for one year, with no equity taken, to "researchers, builders and creatives advancing civilization".[^osv-fellowship][^osv-llc] Other shortlisted applicants can receive grants of at least $10,000.[^osv-oppdesk] The 2026 cycle closed on 30 April 2026 after 11,813 applications.[^osv-fellowship] The 2027 timing has not been published, though the 2026 cycle opened on 1 January.[^osv-oppdesk]

# Eligibility

- Adults anywhere in the world, with no credentials required.[^osv-fellowship]
- Fellows are expected to work at least 40 hours a week, ideally 60+. Quitting your job is optional.[^osv-fellowship]
- Projects planning venture funding are directed to OSV's VC arm instead.[^osv-fellowship]

# What it funds

- Independent, ambitious projects. Recent fellows have worked on AI language preservation, quantum computing, biomedical devices and biological data storage.[^osv-llc]

# Amounts & terms

- The fellowship pays $100,000 for 12 months. Grants are $10,000 or more. Fellows keep 100% ownership of their work.[^osv-llc][^osv-oppdesk]

# How to apply

- Stage 1 is the application, reviewed on a rolling basis with most updates within 30 days. Stage 2 is a call with shortlisted applicants. Final selections are announced by 1 June (2026 cycle).[^osv-oppdesk]
- Tip: applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, so applying early in the window helps.

- More than 75 people funded over the past three years.[^osv-fellowship]

# Fit

- **Good fit if** you are an independent builder who wants a year of runway without equity strings.
- **Poor fit if** you are building a VC-track startup, or need team or organizational funding.
