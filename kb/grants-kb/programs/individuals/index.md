# Funding for individual developers

* [1517 Fund Medici Project Grants](1517-medici-grants.md) - No-strings microgrants (minimum $1,000) from 1517 Fund for high-school and college students, dropouts and deep-tech scientists building passion projects; rolling, North America only, apply with a 5-minute Loom video.
* [Astera Institute Residency](astera-residency.md) - Fully funded 12–18 month on-site residency in Emeryville, CA for open, non-proprietary science/AI projects — $125k–$250k salary, project budget up to ~$1.5M and large GPU access; Fall 2026 cohort selected (deadline was Apr 19, 2026).
* [Django Fellowship (DSF Fellows)](django-fellowship.md) - Paid contractor roles ($82.26/hour as of 2025) funded by the Django Software Foundation to maintain Django — triage, reviews, security releases; three Fellows in 2026, new positions opened only when fundraising allows.
* [Emergent Ventures (Mercatus Center)](emergent-ventures.md) - Tyler Cowen's rolling, low-paperwork grants and fellowships for individuals with "zero to one" ideas, from a few thousand to ~$50k (occasionally more), open to anyone 13+ worldwide; actively awarding in 2026 (cohorts 51–59 announced Jan–Sep 2026).
* [Ethereum Protocol Fellowship (EPF)](ethereum-protocol-fellowship.md) - Ethereum Foundation's ~5-month mentored fellowship for aspiring Ethereum core/protocol developers, with monthly stipends for selected fellows; cohort 7 runs June–November 2026 (applications closed 13 May 2026).
* [FUTO Microgrants and Legendary Grants](futo-grants.md) - FUTO funds open-source and user-freedom software with one-time microgrants of $1,000–$5,000 (often unsolicited) and larger multi-year "legendary" grants; applications accepted year-round by email.
* [GitHub Accelerator](github-accelerator.md) - GitHub's 10-week program giving open-source maintainers non-dilutive funding (up to $40,000 per project in 2024) plus mentorship; ran 2023 and 2024 (AI-focused) cohorts, no cohort announced since — treat as inactive.
* [GitHub Sponsors](github-sponsors.md) - GitHub's built-in recurring/one-time sponsorship platform for individual developers and OSS organizations in 103 regions; 0% fee on sponsorships from personal accounts (up to 6% on org sponsorships by card); $40M+ paid out; always open.
* [Google Summer of Code (GSoC)](google-summer-of-code.md) - Google-paid stipends ($750–$6,600, PPP-adjusted) for students and open-source beginners aged 18+ to spend 90–350 hours on a mentored open-source project; 2026 cycle under way, 2027 dates not yet announced.
* [HeroDevs Open Source Sustainability Fund](herodevs-sustainability-fund.md) - $20M HeroDevs fund giving OSS maintainers grants (page states $2,500–$15,000) to adopt end-of-life and support-lifecycle best practices (versioning, EOL timelines, security process, migration guides); accepting applications.
* [Ko-fi](ko-fi.md) - Tip-jar style creator platform with 0% platform fee on one-off donations on the free plan (5% on memberships/shop/commissions; paid Gold plan removes these); payment processor fees apply.
* [LFX Mentorship (Linux Foundation, CNCF and others)](lfx-mentorship.md) - Linux Foundation platform running three 12-week paid mentorship terms a year on LF/CNCF projects, with PPP-based stipends of $1,000–$6,600; Term 3 2026 is running, 2027 Term 1 applications expected around late January.
* [Liberapay](liberapay.md) - French nonprofit recurring-donation platform for open-source and free-culture creators that takes no platform cut (only processor fees) and caps per-donor gifts to limit influence; always open.
* [MATS (ML Alignment & Theory Scholars)](mats.md) - Fully funded 12-week AI-safety research fellowship in Berkeley/London — $1,600/week stipend ($19,200), $8k–$16k/month compute, housing and meals; Winter 2027 cohort (Jan 19–Apr 10, 2027) applications closed Sep 6, 2026.
* [MLH Fellowship](mlh-fellowship.md) - Major League Hacking's remote 12-week "internship alternative" where fellows work 20 hrs/week on industry or open-source projects in mentored pods, with a need-based stipend in some tracks; Fall 2026 batch running (Sep 14–Dec 4), next batch Spring 2027.
* [Open Collective / Open Source Collective (fiscal hosting)](open-collective.md) - Transparent fundraising platform plus fiscal hosts (e.g., Open Source Collective, 10% host fee, 3,000+ projects) that let OSS projects and their contributors receive donations, corporate sponsorships and grants and pay out to individuals without forming a legal entity; always open.
* [O'Shaughnessy Fellowships & Grants](oshaughnessy-fellowships.md) - O'Shaughnessy Ventures' annual, equity-free $100,000 one-year fellowships (up to ~20/yr) plus $10,000+ grants for independent builders, researchers and creatives worldwide; 2026 cycle closed (Apr 30, 11,813 applicants), 2027 cycle not yet announced.
* [Outreachy Internships](outreachy.md) - Paid, remote 3-month open-source internships ($7,000 stipend) for people facing under-representation or discrimination in tech; December 2026 cohort is in its contribution phase (final applications due 2026-11-02).
* [Patreon](patreon.md) - General creator-membership platform sometimes used by developers for recurring support; flat 10% platform fee for new creators (since Aug 2025) plus processing and payout fees.
* [Polar (polar.sh)](polar.md) - Open-source merchant-of-record billing platform for developers selling subscriptions, licenses and digital products (with pay-what-you-want pricing); its original issue-funding and donations features were sunset in v2 — now a monetization tool rather than a donation platform. Fees from 5% + 50¢ (2026 Starter).
* [PSF Developers-in-Residence](psf-developers-in-residence.md) - Python Software Foundation staff roles funded by corporate sponsors (Alpha-Omega, Bloomberg, Meta, anonymous donors) to maintain CPython, PyPI and Python security; hired as PSF job openings, not a grant call.
* [Recurse Center Living-Expense Grants](recurse-center-grants.md) - Need-based living-expense grants (historically up to $7,000 for a 12-week batch) for under-represented programmers attending the free Recurse Center retreat in NYC; paused for budget reasons as of 2026.
* [Rust Foundation Fellowship](rust-foundation-fellowship.md) - Rust Foundation fellowships (monthly stipend ~$1,000–$1,500 plus travel/training budget) for Rust contributors; last cohort (21 fellows) announced Oct 2024, application currently closed — longer-term maintainer funding has shifted to the 2026 Maintainers Fund.
* [Rust Foundation Maintainers Fund — Maintainers in Residence & maintainer grants](rust-maintainers-in-residence.md) - New (2026) Rust Foundation fund ($350k raised from Google, AWS, OpenAI and the Rust Leadership Council) paying existing Rust Project maintainers full- or half-time as Maintainers in Residence, plus shorter maintainer grants; selected by the Rust Project Funding Team, no open call.
* [Summer of Bitcoin](summer-of-bitcoin.md) - Annual 12-week remote internship paying university students $3,000–$6,600 (PPP-adjusted, in BTC) to contribute to bitcoin open-source projects; 2026 cycle complete, 2027 applications expected early in the year.
* [Thiel Fellowship](thiel-fellowship.md) - $250,000 over two years, equity-free, for builders aged 22 or younger without a university degree who leave school to build; rolling applications year-round.
