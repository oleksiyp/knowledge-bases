# Summary

The Thiel Fellowship gives young people a $250,000 grant over two years to build something new instead of going to university.[^thiel-home][^thiel-faq] It takes no equity.[^thiel-faq] Applications are accepted year-round. Fellows can live anywhere, and relocating to San Francisco is not required.[^thiel-faq] Software and AI startups are common, but software is not required.

# Eligibility

- Applicants must be 22 or younger and must not hold a university degree (undergraduate or graduate).[^thiel-faq]
- You can apply while still in school, but must drop out to accept the fellowship.[^thiel-faq]
- An idea alone is not enough. You need to show "meaningful progress toward a concrete vision".[^thiel-faq]

# What it funds

- The fellow personally, to work full-time on a venture, research or project.

# Amounts & terms

- $250,000 is spread over two years, with no equity taken.[^thiel-faq] Fellows also get access to the foundation's network of founders and investors.[^thiel-home]

# How to apply

- Apply through the online portal. There is no fixed deadline.[^thiel-faq]

# Deadlines

Rolling.

# Track record

- The fellowship has run since 2011.[^thiel-home] Annual cohort size is not published on the FAQ.

# Fit

- **Good fit if** you are 22 or younger, already shipping, and ready to commit full-time.
- **Poor fit if** you have a degree, or want to stay in school.
