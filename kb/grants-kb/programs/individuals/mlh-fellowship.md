# Summary

The MLH Fellowship calls itself "a 12-week internship alternative for aspiring software engineers". Fellows work on real projects from industry partners in small mentored groups.[^mlh-swe] The Fall 2026 Software Engineering batch runs from 14 September to 4 December 2026, three days a week at about 20 hours a week.[^mlh-swe] A third-party listing describes the stipend as need-based, depending on track and country, and usually $1,000–$5,000. MLH's own program page did not state an amount when checked.[^osprograms-mlh]

# Eligibility

- Applicants must be 18 or older, able to commit 20+ hours a week for 12 weeks, communicate well in English, and be available during ET, PT, GMT or IST business hours.[^mlh-swe]
- Applicants cannot live in a US-embargoed country. Both students and non-students are accepted.[^mlh-swe]

# What it funds

- Mentored team work on partner projects, some of them open source.
- It is not a grant for your own project.

# Amounts & terms

- Some tracks pay a need-based stipend. The amount was not published on the official page checked, and a third-party listing gives about $1,000–$5,000.[^osprograms-mlh]

# How to apply

- Apply online, interview with coordinators and mentors, then complete enrollment paperwork.[^mlh-swe]
- Admissions are rolling, and applications close several weeks before each batch.[^mlh-swe] Apply early.

# Track record

- MLH reports 500+ graduates, 50% from under-represented groups and 50% from non-CS majors, across 30+ countries.[^mlh-swe]

# Fit

- **Good fit if** you are an early-career developer who wants structured team experience.
- **Poor fit if** you need a reliable income. The stipend is not guaranteed.
