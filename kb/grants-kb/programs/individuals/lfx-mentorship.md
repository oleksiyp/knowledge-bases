# Summary

LFX Mentorship is the Linux Foundation's platform for paid, mentored contributions to LF-hosted projects. CNCF is the biggest user, with dozens of projects each term. There are three 12-week terms a year (March–May, June–August, September–November).[^cncf-lfx-readme] From July 2026 stipends follow a refreshed purchasing-power-parity schedule: still a $6,600 maximum, but the minimum drops to $1,000 so the program can fund more mentees.[^cncf-stipend-2026] Term 3 2026 runs from 7 September to 27 November 2026, and the next mentee window should be for 2027 Term 1.[^cncf-term3-2026]

# Eligibility

- Mentees apply on the LFX platform with a statement of purpose. Mentors review applications and may interview.[^cncf-lfx-readme]
- Stipends are paid only after satisfactory evaluations.[^lfx-mentee-docs]
- Detailed eligibility rules (including limits on repeat participation) are in the LFX docs. They were not fully verified for this entry.

# What it funds

- Defined mentorship projects proposed by LF and CNCF projects (Kubernetes ecosystem, observability, security tooling and others).
- It does not fund your own project ideas.

# Amounts & terms

- Stipends are PPP-adjusted by the mentee's country: maximum $6,600, minimum $1,000, for terms starting on or after 1 July 2026. Mentees accepted earlier keep the old amounts.[^cncf-stipend-2026]
- Payment comes in two tranches after the midterm and final evaluations (Term 3 2026: 21 October and 25 November).[^cncf-term3-2026]

# How to apply

1. Projects post proposals (Term 3 2026: 1–28 July).[^cncf-term3-2026]
2. Mentees apply in a roughly two-week window (Term 3 2026: 3–18 August; Term 1 2026: 26 January–10 February).[^cncf-term3-2026][^cncf-term1-2026]
3. Mentors review for about two weeks, then the term kicks off.[^cncf-term3-2026]

# Track record

- CNCF has run LFX terms every year with published project lists in the cncf/mentoring repo.[^cncf-lfx-readme]

# Fit

- **Good fit if** you want paid cloud-native or infrastructure experience, three chances a year.
- **Poor fit if** you are in a high-cost country and expected the old flat amounts. Check the new PPP table.
