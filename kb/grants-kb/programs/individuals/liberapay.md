# Summary

Liberapay is a nonprofit founded in France in 2015 for recurring donations to "people whose work you appreciate". It focuses on open-source software and free knowledge.[^liberapay-about] Liberapay takes no cut. The service is funded by donations to its own account, though payment-processor fees still apply.[^liberapay-about] Donations are capped per donor per week to limit any single donor's influence, and donors are anonymous by default.[^liberapay-about]

# Eligibility

- Creators and contributors, especially in open source and free culture.[^liberapay-about]

# What it funds

- A stable, crowdfunded basic income for creators.[^liberapay-about]

# Amounts & terms

- No platform fee, plus processor fees.[^liberapay-about]

# How to apply
