# Summary

Emergent Ventures funds "highly scalable, 'zero to one' ideas for meaningfully improving society". Economist Tyler Cowen has run it at George Mason University's Mercatus Center since 2018.[^ev-home] It is one of the fastest ways for an individual to get a no-strings grant: a short online form, rolling review and no deadlines.[^ev-home] It is clearly active in 2026, with winners of cohorts 51 to 59 published on Marginal Revolution between January and September 2026.[^ev-cohort-51][^ev-cohort-59] Grants are not tied to software, but many recipients are coders, AI builders or founders of early tech projects.[^ev-cohort-56]

# Eligibility

- Anyone aged 13 or older, anywhere in the world. Applicants may submit one application per person and project.[^ev-home]
- Dedicated tracks exist for India, Africa, the Caribbean and Ukraine.[^ev-home][^ev-india-16]

# What it funds

- Individuals and very early projects across science, tech, policy, writing and building. Examples from 2026 cohorts include a payments and compute layer for AI apps, decentralized AI systems, and payments systems built by a 17-year-old.[^ev-cohort-56][^ev-cohort-51]
- Some awards are travel or "career development" grants rather than project funding.

# Amounts & terms

- No range is published.[^ev-home] Third-party listings report roughly $1,000–$50,000.[^ev-merge]
- Grants are equity-free, with no IP or licensing conditions known.

# How to apply

- Use the online application form. Mercatus does not give individual feedback or status updates.[^ev-home]
- Tip: be concrete about what you will do with the money and why you are the person to do it. The bar is talent and ambition more than polish.

# Deadlines

Rolling, with no deadlines.

# Track record

- Cohort announcements are posted about monthly (51st to 59th in 2026).[^ev-cohort-59]

# Fit

- **Good fit if** you are a young or unconventional builder with an ambitious idea and need a small, fast grant.
- **Poor fit if** you need predictable multi-year maintenance funding for an OSS project.
