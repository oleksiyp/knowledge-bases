# Summary

Open Collective is a transparent ledger and fundraising platform. Projects join a *fiscal host* that legally holds the money, and contributors submit expenses or invoices to get paid. This makes it a common way for individual maintainers to receive funds raised by their project.[^osc-page] Open Source Collective (OSC), a US 501(c)(6), hosts more than 3,000 projects and charges a fixed 10% host fee on incoming external money, plus payment-processor fees.[^osc-page][^osc-fees] The platform is now run by OFiCo, a nonprofit consortium of the largest hosts. In March 2026 it moved organizations to usage-based pricing, while hosted collectives remain free of platform fees.[^oc-pricing-2026][^oc-wiki]

# Eligibility

- Open-source projects that meet OSC's eligibility rules and terms of fiscal sponsorship.[^osc-page]

# What it funds

- Any project expense or contributor payout that the project's admins approve, using donations, sponsorships and grants routed through the collective.

# Amounts & terms

- OSC takes 10% of incoming external funds and has no other platform fees.[^osc-fees] Donations to OSC are not tax-deductible in the US because it is a 501(c)(6).[^osc-page]

# How to apply

- Create a collective and apply to a host (OSC for open source).[^osc-page]

# Deadlines

None.

# Track record

- 3,000+ projects hosted by OSC.[^osc-page]

# Fit

- **Good fit if** your project has several contributors and needs a neutral, transparent treasury.
- **Poor fit if** you only want personal tips. [GitHub Sponsors](/programs/individuals/github-sponsors.md) has lower fees for that.
