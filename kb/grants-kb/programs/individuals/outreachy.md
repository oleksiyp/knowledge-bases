# Summary

Outreachy pays interns $7,000 for a three-month, fully remote internship on an open-source project, working with an experienced mentor.[^outreachy-applicant-guide] It is aimed at people who face under-representation, systemic bias or discrimination in tech.[^outreachy-home] There are two cohorts a year. The December 2026 cohort's initial application closed on 31 August 2026. Approved applicants are in the contribution period from 5 October to 2 November 2026, and the internship runs from 7 December 2026 to 8 March 2027.[^outreachy-applicant-guide]

# Eligibility

- The program is open to people from any background who face under-representation, systemic bias or discrimination in tech. The invitation explicitly includes women (cis and trans), trans men, genderqueer people, and US residents from under-represented racial or ethnic groups.[^outreachy-home]
- Interns must be able to commit about 30 hours a week for the three months.[^outreachy-applicant-guide]
- An initial application (essays plus eligibility checks) must be approved before applicants can contribute to projects.[^outreachy-applicant-guide]

# What it funds

- Internship work on programming, documentation, design, UX, research and data science for participating open-source communities.[^outreachy-home]
- It does not fund independent projects. You pick from the communities' listed projects.

# Amounts & terms

- The stipend is US$7,000 for the internship.[^outreachy-applicant-guide]
- Work is remote and mentored, at about 30 hours a week.[^outreachy-applicant-guide]

# How to apply

1. Submit the initial application during the short window (December 2026 cohort: 24–31 August 2026).[^outreachy-applicant-guide]
2. If approved (results on 5 October 2026), make contributions to one or more projects during the contribution period.[^outreachy-applicant-guide]
3. Submit a final application through the project by the deadline (2 November 2026, 16:00 UTC). Interns are announced on 30 November 2026.[^outreachy-applicant-guide]
