# Summary

HeroDevs sells extended support for end-of-life open source. It set up a $20 million Open Source Sustainability Fund alongside a $125M growth investment from PSG.[^herodevs-fund][^herodevs-psg] The fund pays maintainers of OSI-licensed projects to adopt clear lifecycle and EOL practices.[^herodevs-fund] The program page gives grants of $2,500–$15,000. Some press coverage of the launch cites up to $250,000, so treat larger amounts as unverified.[^herodevs-fund] The commercial context matters: HeroDevs benefits commercially when projects publish clear EOL timelines.

# Eligibility

- Maintainers of OSI-licensed projects (developer tools), with versions approaching or past EOL.[^herodevs-fund]

# What it funds

- Work on versioning and communication, documenting support status, support tiers, security processes and migration guides.[^herodevs-fund]

# Amounts & terms

- $2,500–$15,000.[^herodevs-fund]

# How to apply

- Submit the online form. HeroDevs reviews it, then you complete a best-practices form.[^herodevs-fund]
