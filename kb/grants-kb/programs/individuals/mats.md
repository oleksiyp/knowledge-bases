# Summary

MATS is a 12-week, fully funded fellowship in AI alignment, interpretability, security and governance, with mentors from Anthropic, Google DeepMind, OpenAI, Redwood Research and ARC.[^mats-w27] Winter 2027 runs from 19 January to 10 April 2027 in Berkeley and London. It pays $1,600 a week ($19,200 in total), plus housing, meals, travel and a compute budget.[^mats-w27][^mats-w27-ea] Applications closed on 6 September 2026.[^mats-w27] It is brief here. See the AI category guide for other AI-safety funding, such as the [Anthropic Fellows Program](/programs/ai/anthropic-fellows-program.md).

# Eligibility

- Applicants must be 18 or older before the program starts. US and non-US citizens are welcome, and no prior AI-safety experience is required.[^mats-w27]

# What it funds

- Mentored research projects across the Empirical, Theory, Strategy & Forecasting, Policy & Governance, Systems Security, Biosecurity and Founding & Field-Building tracks.[^mats-w27]

# Amounts & terms

- The stipend is $1,600 a week, about 40 hours a week (20 hours possible in exceptional cases).[^mats-w27] The compute budget is up to $8k a month for non-technical tracks and $16k a month for technical tracks.[^mats-w27-ea] Fellows can apply for a funded 6–12-month extension.[^mats-w27]

# How to apply

- Apply in the posted application window on matsprogram.org.
