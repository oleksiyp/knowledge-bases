# Summary

Patreon is a general creator-membership platform. It is free to start and charges "10% of the income you earn on Patreon" plus payment-processing, currency-conversion and payout fees. It handles tax collection and supports PayPal, Apple Pay, Venmo and cards.[^patreon-pricing] Third-party coverage says the flat 10% replaced the old tiered plans for new creators from August 2025.[^patreon-fees-2026] Developers usually do better on GitHub Sponsors, which is free for personal sponsors. Patreon fits people who also publish tutorials, videos or other content.
