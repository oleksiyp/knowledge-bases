# Summary

The EPF pairs aspiring protocol developers with Ethereum core-development mentors so they can make real contributions to clients and research.[^epf7] Cohort 7 runs from June to November 2026. Applications closed on 13 May 2026. It is "a smaller, more focused cohort, prioritizing depth of engagement over breadth".[^epf7] Selected participants get a monthly stipend. Permissionless participation is possible, but it does not come with a stipend.[^epf7]

# Eligibility

- Aspiring protocol developers who apply through the EPF wiki. Selection decides who gets stipends.[^epf7]

# What it funds

- Contributions to Ethereum clients and protocol R&D, including censorship resistance, privacy and security.[^epf7]

# Amounts & terms

- A monthly stipend for selected fellows. The amount is not published.[^epf7]

# How to apply

- Use the EPF wiki application. Full details are in the cohort GitHub repository.[^epf7][^epf7-repo]
