# Summary

The Recurse Center (RC) is a free, self-directed programming retreat. As the FAQ puts it, "You will never receive a bill from RC".[^rc-faq] From 2021 RC offered need-based living-expense grants to people from groups under-represented in programming: up to $7,000 for a 12-week batch, $3,500 for 6 weeks and $500 for a one-week mini retreat.[^rc-grants-2021] As of 2026 RC says the grants program is paused for budget reasons and will update its diversity page if it restarts. (This comes from a search snippet of that page. The live page did not show grant text when fetched.)[^rc-diversity]

# Eligibility

- Historically: women (cis or trans), trans, genderqueer and non-binary people, and Black, Latino/a/x, Native American and Pacific Islander programmers attending RC.[^rc-grants-2021]

# What it funds

- Living expenses while attending RC to work on your own programming projects. These are often open-source, but that is not required.

# Amounts & terms

- See the historical amounts above.[^rc-grants-2021]

# How to apply

- Not available while paused.

# Deadlines

None.

# Track record

- RC reports disbursing over $1.7M in need-based grants (search-result snippet of RC's site; not independently verified).

# Fit

- RC itself (free) is still a good fit for self-directed learners who can cover their own living costs.
