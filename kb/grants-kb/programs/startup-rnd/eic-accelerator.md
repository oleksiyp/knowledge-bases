# Summary

The EIC Accelerator is Europe's flagship non-dilutive grant (plus optional equity) for single startups and SMEs with high-risk, high-impact innovations. Grants are a lump sum "below €2.5 million" for up to 24 months; equity investments of €1–10M are available via the EIC Fund[^eic-acc]. For 2026 the EIC shortened templates (from ~50 to ~20 pages) and moved to evaluations every two months[^hezelburcht-wp26], with six full-proposal cut-offs; the next is **4 November 2026**[^eic-acc]. The 2026 budget is about €634M (≈€414M open, ≈€220M challenges)[^eic-acc][^hezelburcht-wp26].

# Eligibility

- Startups, SMEs and small mid-caps (≤499 employees) established in EU Member States or Horizon Europe associated countries; mid-caps only for investment-only[^eic-acc].
- UK entities can receive the grant component only — "equity funding is not available to any UK applicants"[^iukbc-eic].

# What it funds

- Development and scale-up of breakthrough innovations from roughly TRL 5/6 to market, in any field (open) or defined Challenges[^eic-acc]. Pure software is eligible if it is a genuine technological breakthrough; incremental SaaS generally scores poorly.

# Amounts & terms

- Grant: lump sum below €2.5M; equity: €1–10M (larger amounts via STEP Scale-up)[^eic-acc]. STEP Scale-Up (~€300M, €10–30M tickets) is a separate equity instrument in 2026[^hezelburcht-wp26].
- Business acceleration services (coaching) included.

# How to apply

1. **Short proposal** (form, 10-slide pitch deck, 3-minute video), accepted any time and batched monthly[^eic-acc].
2. **Full proposal** (~20 pages + financials) to a cut-off after a GO[^eic-acc].
3. **Jury interview** for top-ranked proposals[^eic-acc].
4. Grant agreement and investment due diligence[^eic-acc].

National "plug-in" schemes (e.g., Czech TA ČR SIGMA) can fast-track companies to step 2 — see [/programs/startup-rnd/tacr-sigma.md](/programs/startup-rnd/tacr-sigma.md).
