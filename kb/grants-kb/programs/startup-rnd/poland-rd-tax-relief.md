# Summary

Polish taxpayers can deduct 200% of R&D personnel costs and 100% of other qualified costs (150% for small taxpayers, 200% for certified R&D centres) via the CIT/BR or PIT/BR attachment[^plumm-br]. Software development qualifies when it involves creative, systematic work producing new solutions, not routine maintenance[^plumm-br]. The relief can be combined with **IP Box** (5% tax on income from qualifying IP such as software copyrights), provided the same costs are not used twice[^plumm-br][^efekta-br].

# Eligibility

- Companies and sole traders taxed in Poland conducting R&D[^plumm-br].

# What it funds

- Staff, materials, equipment, expert opinions, IP costs for R&D.

# Amounts & terms

See amount_text; carry forward 6 years[^plumm-br].

# How to apply

Keep separate R&D cost records and time allocation; claim in the annual return[^plumm-br]. Consider a tax ruling for IP Box.

# Deadlines

Annual tax return deadlines.
