# Summary

Cluster 4 is Horizon Europe's main pot for digital R&I (AI, data, software, cloud-edge, quantum, virtual worlds, space). The 2026–27 work programme carries about €1.5bn across five destinations, with calls opening from December 2025 and closing between March 2026 and September 2027[^iukbc-cl4]. Projects are large, multi-partner collaborations; startups typically join consortia as technology partners. HaDEA reported 250 proposals to the 2026 Digital call[^hadea-cl4].

# Eligibility

- At least one entity in an EU Member State plus at least two others from different Member States/associated countries; UK counts as associated[^iukbc-cl4].

# What it funds

- Research and innovation actions on defined topics; examples for 2027: HORIZON-CL4-2027-04-DATA-03/-08 (€17.5M–€19M per project), DIGITAL-EMERGING-11 (€44M, single project), HUMAN-01 (€4.8–5.6M, 8 projects)[^iukbc-cl4].

# Amounts & terms

- Up to 100% of eligible costs for research actions (70% for profit-making entities in innovation actions — standard Horizon rules; check topic).

# How to apply

Build a consortium, submit through the EU Funding & Tenders Portal; most topics single-stage.
