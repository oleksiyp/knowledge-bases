# Summary

NSF's SBIR/STTR program ("America's Seed Fund") is the most software-friendly of the US SBIR agencies: it is topic-agnostic across "nearly all technology areas", takes no equity and leaves IP with the company[^nsf-26-510][^seedfund]. Phase I awards go up to $305,000 and Phase II up to $1.25M under solicitation NSF 26-510[^nsf-26-510]. The program was frozen during the SBIR/STTR authorization lapse (1 Oct 2025 – 13 Apr 2026)[^crowell-reauth]; NSF reopened Project Pitch submissions on 2 June 2026 with a first full-proposal deadline of 27 July 2026[^grantedai-nsf]. The next full-proposal deadline is **4 November 2026**[^nsf-26-510].

# Eligibility

- US small business (≤500 employees including affiliates) compliant with the SBIR/STTR Policy Directive[^nsf-26-510][^nsf-26-511].
- PI must be at least 51% employed by the small business at award time (SBIR)[^nsf-26-511].
- STTR requires a formal partnership with a non-profit research institution[^nsf-26-510].
- Phase I and Fast-Track require an **invitation** obtained through a Project Pitch; max two pitches per company per year, three lifetime pitches per technology[^nsf-26-510].
- The 2026 reauthorization added stronger foreign-risk screening of applicants' foreign affiliations and investors[^crowell-reauth].

# What it funds

- Technically risky R&D toward commercially viable, deep-technology products in "nearly all technology areas and market sectors"[^nsf-26-510] — AI, software platforms, security, etc. qualify if there is genuine technical innovation.
- Not for: incremental product development, straightforward software engineering without technical risk, or market-research-only work (see NSF's own pitch guidance on seedfund.nsf.gov)[^seedfund].

# Amounts & terms

| Instrument | Max | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|
| Phase I | $305,000 | 6–18 months |
| Phase II | $1,250,000 | ~24 months |
| Fast-Track (I+II) | $1,555,555 | — |
| Phase IIB supplement | $50,000–$500,000 | — |
| Strategic Breakthrough (Phase II awardees) | up to $30,000,000 | — |

Source: NSF 26-510[^nsf-26-510]. NSF expects roughly 180 SBIR + 50 STTR Phase I and ~90 Phase II awards per year (est. budget $210M under 26-510)[^nsf-26-510]. Strategic Breakthrough awards require matching funds per the 2026 Act[^crowell-reauth]. No equity taken; awardees keep their IP[^nsf-26-510].

# How to apply

1. Register (SAM.gov/UEI, Research.gov) — allow weeks.
2. Submit a 3-page-equivalent **Project Pitch** on seedfund.nsf.gov; NSF replies by email (typically weeks); an invitation is valid for the next two deadlines[^nsf-26-510].
3. Submit the full proposal by a deadline; review takes several months[^nsf-26-510].

# Track record

NSF says it historically funded about 400 companies a year[^seedfund].

# Fit

- Good fit if: US-incorporated startup with a technically novel software/AI/security product and a commercial (not just government) market.
- Poor fit if: you are outside the US, majority foreign-owned, or the work is routine software development.
