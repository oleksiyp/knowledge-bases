# Summary

Enterprise Estonia (EIS) runs the country's startup and R&D grants. The **Start-up Grant** gives up to €20,000 (80% aid rate) to companies under 3 years with fewer than 10 employees; its €5.02M budget was fully committed on 11 Aug 2026 and applications closed on 14 Aug 2026, to reopen when funds become available[^eis-startup]. The **R&D employees grant** refunds an amount equal to 50% of income tax paid for R&D staff[^eis-rd-employees]. Estonian-registered companies founded by e-residents can access these grants[^eresident-funding].

# Eligibility

- Start-up Grant: ≤3 years old, <10 FTE, turnover ≤€40K, excluded sectors apply; mandatory pre-consultation with a regional development centre[^eis-startup].

# What it funds

- Early business development (Start-up Grant); R&D staffing (R&D employee grant).
