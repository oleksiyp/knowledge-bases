# Summary

EIC Pathfinder funds visionary, early-stage science-to-technology research. 2026 budget: about €262M; Pathfinder Open closed 12 May 2026 and **Pathfinder Challenges close 28 October 2026**[^hezelburcht-wp26]. Grants are "up to EUR 4 million or more if duly justified" plus booster grants up to €50K[^iukbc-eic]. Relevant for startups mainly as consortium partners (Open) or as single applicants in Challenges.

# Eligibility

- Open: consortia of at least three entities from three different EU/associated countries (UK eligible)[^iukbc-eic].
- Challenges: single applicants or 2-member consortia, UK included[^iukbc-eic].

# What it funds

- Low-TRL (proof-of-principle) research on radically new technologies; challenge topics set annually in the Work Programme.

# Amounts & terms

- Up to €4M; booster grants ≤€50K for portfolio activities[^iukbc-eic].

# How to apply

Submit via the EU Funding & Tenders Portal before the deadline.
