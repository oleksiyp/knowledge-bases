# Summary

Mozilla Builders ran a 12-week accelerator in 2024 on **local AI**, offering "up to $100,000 in non-dilutive funding" per project; applications closed 1 Aug 2024 and Demo Day was 5 Dec 2024[^mozb-programs]. Fourteen open-source AI projects were selected[^mozb-cohort], including Elixir's Nx[^elixir-nx]. As of Oct 2026 the programs page lists no newer accelerator cohort — only bespoke co-development/sponsorship "Collaborations"[^mozb-programs].

# Eligibility

- 2024: open-source local-AI projects/startups worldwide[^mozb-cohort].

# What it funds

- Open-source AI tooling; now only through collaborations[^mozb-programs].

# Amounts & terms

See amount_text.

# How to apply

No open call; contact via Discord/email for collaborations[^mozb-programs].

# Deadlines

None.

# Track record

# Fit

- Good fit if: you build open-source local-AI tooling and can pursue a collaboration.
- Poor fit if: you need a predictable open call.
