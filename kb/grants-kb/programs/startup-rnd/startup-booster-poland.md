# Summary

Startup Booster Poland – Smart UP is PARP's FENG-funded acceleration scheme: PARP selected accelerators which in turn recruit startups and grant up to **PLN 400,000** each, at up to 100% of eligible costs[^fingoweb-sbp]. Around PLN 270M was allocated across 17 accelerators with five development paths[^mamstartup-sbp]. **Poland Prize** — the soft-landing path for foreign startups wishing to operate in Poland — continues as one of these acceleration paths[^parp-poland-prize].

# Eligibility

- Micro and small enterprises meeting EU GBER (Reg. 651/2014) definitions; Poland Prize targets founders from outside Poland willing to establish in Poland[^fingoweb-sbp][^parp-poland-prize].

# What it funds

- Acceleration programme, MVP/product development, pilots with corporates, mentoring[^parp-sbp].

# Amounts & terms

- Up to PLN 400K per startup[^fingoweb-sbp].

# How to apply

Apply to a participating accelerator's open call (each sets its own windows)[^parp-sbp].

# Deadlines

Per accelerator; not centrally scheduled.

# Track record

Not compiled here.

# Fit

- Good fit if: early-stage startup (Polish, or foreign willing to relocate) wanting equity-free cash plus acceleration.
- Poor fit if: mid-size company.
