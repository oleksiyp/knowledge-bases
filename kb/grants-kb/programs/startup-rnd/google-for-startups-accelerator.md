# Summary

Google for Startups Accelerator runs cohort programs across the Americas, EMEA and APAC with "equity-free support for the duration of the program": mentoring from Google teams, technical bootcamps, early access to Google AI products, Cloud TPU access and Cloud credits subject to eligibility review[^gfs-accel]. In 2026 Google ran AI-first cohorts such as the India program for Seed–Series A startups focusing on agentic, multimodal and physical AI[^gfs-india-2026]. It is not a cash grant.

# Eligibility

- Varies by cohort; typically seed to Series A tech startups in the region[^gfs-india-2026].

# What it funds

- In-kind support and credits[^gfs-accel].

# Amounts & terms

No equity taken[^gfs-accel].

# How to apply

Apply to the regional/thematic cohort when open on startup.google.com[^gfs-accel].

# Deadlines

Per cohort.
