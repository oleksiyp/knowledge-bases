# Summary

NASA switched its SBIR/STTR program to a **Broad Agency Announcement** for program year 2026, releasing Phase I topics as appendices throughout the year instead of one annual solicitation[^nasa-sbir][^hesto-nasa]. After reauthorization, appendices 2026A SBIR, 2026B SBIR and 2026B STTR opened with Phase I proposals due 21 May 2026[^hesto-nasa]. Phase II-type windows remain open into autumn: SBIR Ignite Phase II closes 15 Oct 2026 and STTR Phase II on 28 Oct 2026[^nasa-sbir].

# Eligibility

- US small businesses; STTR requires research-institution partner[^sbir-gov].

# What it funds

- Topic-defined technologies for NASA missions; many subtopics involve flight software, autonomy, AI/ML, data systems.

# How to apply
