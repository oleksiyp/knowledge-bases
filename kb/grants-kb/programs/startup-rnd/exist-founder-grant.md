# Summary

EXIST is Germany's main pre-seed program for academic founders, run by the Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy (BMWE) and co-financed by ESF+[^gruenderplattform-exist]. The Founder Grant pays monthly stipends by qualification (€1,000 students to €3,000 PhDs), plus materials up to €30K for teams and €5K coaching, for up to 12 months[^gruenderplattform-exist]. Only universities and non-university research institutes can formally apply, on behalf of the team[^gruenderplattform-exist]. Sister programs: **EXIST Women** (6-month pre-phase) and **EXIST-Forschungstransfer** (for research-intensive spin-outs; up to €250K materials in Phase I over 18 months)[^qonto-exist]. Software startups are common EXIST recipients when the product is innovative.

# Eligibility

- Students (at least halfway through), graduates within 5 years, researchers and technical staff of German universities/research institutions; teams of up to 3[^gruenderplattform-exist].
- Requires a link to a host institution, convincing innovation potential and a founding network[^exist-gs].

# What it funds

- Founders' living costs, prototype materials, coaching before/around incorporation.

# Amounts & terms

See amount_text; no equity, no repayment.

# How to apply

Contact the university's founder service (Gründungsnetzwerk); the institution submits the application (~4–5 months)[^gruenderplattform-exist].
