# Summary

i-Nov is the France 2030 innovation contest for single-company startup/SME projects, operated by Bpifrance with ADEME. Under wave 10 rules, projects of €1M–€5M lasting 12–36 months received aid of 45% (small) or 35% (medium) of costs, 75% as grants and 25% as repayable advances, with a "Digital" theme alongside health, mobility and energy[^capdigital-inov]. The 14th wave was launched in 2024[^lmi-inov14] and laureates of the 2024/2025 contests were celebrated in 2025[^esr-ceremony]. **We could not verify a 2026 wave** — check Bpifrance's calls page.

# Eligibility

- Mono-partner projects led by a startup or SME; subcontracting ≤30% of budget[^capdigital-inov].

# What it funds

- R&D&I projects with strong growth potential in themes set per wave[^capdigital-inov].
