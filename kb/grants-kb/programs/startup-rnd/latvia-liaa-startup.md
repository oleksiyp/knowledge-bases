# Summary

LIAA's startup offer combines several EU-cofinanced instruments: **Support for Start-ups** (up to €300K; for SMEs with qualified VC investment or developing innovative products), **Science-intensive Start-ups** (up to €150K; companies <5 years commercialising research-institution results), and **innovation vouchers** (45–100% up to €25K), all listed as open until 30 April 2027[^liaa-startups]. The startup programme is implemented together with Altum and aims to encourage commercial use of innovative ideas[^liaa-jaunuznemumi].

# Eligibility

- Latvian MSMEs meeting each program's criteria (e.g., VC co-investment for some startup support)[^liaa-startups].

# What it funds

- Product development, staff, R&D services (vouchers), commercialisation.
