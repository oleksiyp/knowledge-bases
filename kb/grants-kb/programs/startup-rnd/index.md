# Startup & company R&D grants (non-dilutive)

* [28DIGITAL (formerly EIT Digital) venture programmes](28digital-eit-digital.md) - Former EIT Digital KIC, rebranded 28DIGITAL in Oct 2025, runs venture incubation/acceleration for European digital and deep-tech startups (mostly services and coaching rather than cash); 2026 Venture Incubation Program startup call closed June 2026.
* [Australia Industry Growth Program](australia-industry-growth-program.md) - Australian matched grants for innovative SMEs (turnover < A$20M) in National Reconstruction Fund priority areas — A$50K–A$250K early-stage commercialisation, A$100K–A$5M commercialisation & growth; advisory-service intake currently paused.
* [Australia R&D Tax Incentive (RDTI)](australia-rdti.md) - Australian R&D tax offset — refundable (corporate tax rate + 18.5%) for companies under A$20M turnover, non-refundable tiers above; registration within 10 months of year-end; 2026 Budget proposes major changes from 1 Jul 2028.
* [AWS Activate (startup credits)](aws-activate.md) - Amazon Web Services credits for startups — Founders tier up to $5,000 for self-funded startups, Portfolio tier up to $200,000 via an Activate Provider (VC/accelerator), invite-only AI credits beyond; pre-Series B, founded within 10 years.
* [Bpifrance Bourse French Tech](bourse-french-tech.md) - Bpifrance subsidy of up to €30,000 (up to 70% of costs) for young innovative French startups to fund early technical/prototype and feasibility work; rolling, typically via an accredited incubator.
* [Canada SR&ED Tax Incentive](canada-sred.md) - Canada's Scientific Research & Experimental Development tax credit — 35% refundable enhanced credit for CCPCs on up to $6M of qualifying spend (Budget 2025), 15% general rate; software development resolving technological uncertainty qualifies.
* [DARPA SBIR/STTR & Small Business Programs](darpa-small-business.md) - DARPA's SBIR/STTR topics (released through the DoD DSIP monthly cycle) and other small-business on-ramps for high-risk R&D, including AI/ML software topics; active again since April 2026.
* [Digital Europe Programme (DIGITAL)](digital-europe-programme.md) - EU deployment-oriented grants (not research) for AI, cybersecurity, data spaces, skills and digital uptake, incl. cascade funding to SMEs; €96M ECCC cybersecurity call (incl. AI4SME) closes 14 Jan 2027.
* [DoD SBIR/STTR (incl. AFWERX & SpaceWERX)](dod-sbir-sttr.md) - US Department of Defense (styled "Department of War" in 2026 documents) SBIR/STTR contracts for US small businesses, including software, AI and cyber topics; monthly topic releases resumed after April 2026 reauthorization; AFWERX replaced its broad Open Topic with "Focused Open Topics".
* [DOE SBIR/STTR](doe-sbir-sttr.md) - US Department of Energy SBIR/STTR grants for small businesses on topics including energy software, HPC, AI and grid cybersecurity; now run by DOE's Office of Technology Commercialization and reauthorized from 13 Apr 2026; next broad Phase I opportunity expected late 2026.
* [EIC Accelerator](eic-accelerator.md) - European Innovation Council grants below €2.5M (plus optional €1–10M equity) for startups/SMEs in EU and Horizon-associated countries with breakthrough deep-tech (incl. AI/software); short proposals rolling monthly, full-proposal cut-offs every two months — next 4 Nov 2026.
* [EIC Pathfinder (Open & Challenges)](eic-pathfinder.md) - EIC grants up to ~€4M for early-stage, high-risk research toward breakthrough technologies by consortia (Open) or single/small teams (Challenges); Pathfinder Challenges 2026 close 28 Oct 2026.
* [EIC Transition](eic-transition.md) - EIC grants up to €2.5M to mature results from previous EIC Pathfinder, ERC Proof of Concept or Horizon projects into business-ready technology; 2026 deadline was 16 Sep 2026.
* [Enterprise Estonia (EIS) Start-up Grant & R&D grants](enterprise-estonia-grants.md) - Estonian business agency grants — Start-up Grant up to €20,000 (80% rate) for companies under 3 years (2026 budget exhausted, closed 14 Aug 2026) plus R&D-employee and development grants; open to Estonian-registered firms incl. e-resident-founded ones.
* [EXIST Founder Grant (EXIST-Gründungsstipendium) & EXIST family](exist-founder-grant.md) - German federal (BMWE, ESF+) stipends for university/research-institute founder teams — €1,000–€3,000/month per founder for up to 12 months plus up to €30K materials and €5K coaching; applied for via the university; EXIST Women and EXIST-Forschungstransfer for later/other stages.
* [France 2030 — i-Nov Innovation Contest](france-2030-i-nov.md) - State innovation contest operated by Bpifrance (with ADEME) funding single-company startup/SME R&D projects of roughly €1M–€5M at 35–45% (grants + repayable advances), incl. a digital theme; 2026 wave status unverified.
* [Germany Research Allowance (Forschungszulage)](germany-forschungszulage.md) - German R&D tax allowance open to all tax-resident companies incl. startups — 25% (35% for SMEs) of eligible R&D costs, base raised to €12M/year from 2026; two-step process via BSFZ certificate then tax office; software R&D eligible.
* [ZIM — Central Innovation Programme for SMEs (Germany)](germany-zim.md) - German federal (BMWE) grants for SME R&D projects (individual, cooperative, network) incl. software, up to 45–55% of costs; applications stopped since 7 Jul 2026 due to exhausted budget — resumption at earliest early 2027 (except international bilateral calls).
* [Google for Startups Accelerator](google-for-startups-accelerator.md) - Equity-free, ~3-month Google accelerator programs by region/theme (Americas, EMEA, APAC; many AI-first cohorts) offering mentoring, technical support and Google Cloud credits rather than cash; cohort calls open at different times per region.
* [Horizon Europe Cluster 4 — Digital, Industry and Space](horizon-europe-cluster-4-digital.md) - Collaborative Horizon Europe R&I grants (€1.5bn over 2026–27) on AI, data, software, emerging digital tech and space; consortia of 3+ entities from EU/associated countries; next AI/Data topics open 17 Nov 2026 and close 18 Mar 2027.
* [Innovate UK competitions (Growth Catalyst, Frontier AI & thematic calls)](innovate-uk-competitions.md) - Innovate UK's post-Smart portfolio of themed and challenge-led grant competitions for UK businesses — e.g., Frontier AI: SME Champions (£150K–£250K projects, closes 11 Nov 2026), Growth Catalyst Investor Partnerships and Early Stage: New Innovators.
* [Innovate UK Smart Grants](innovate-uk-smart-grants.md) - Innovate UK's former open, sector-agnostic grant competition for game-changing R&D by UK SMEs (£25K–£2M); closed to new applications since early 2025 while Innovate UK realigns funding to the Industrial Strategy — no reinstatement as of Oct 2026.
* [Israel Innovation Authority — Tnufa (Ideation) & Startup Fund](israel-innovation-authority.md) - Israel Innovation Authority conditional grants for Israeli entrepreneurs and startups — Tnufa up to NIS 200K (80% of budget) for proof of concept; Startup Fund matching of up to NIS 2M (pre-seed) / NIS 6M (seed) alongside private investment, expanded for deep tech from 15 Jul 2026.
* [Japan NEDO Deep-Tech Startups Support Program (DTSU) & SBIR Promotion Program](japan-nedo-dtsu.md) - NEDO subsidies for Japanese deep-tech startups — DTSU stages up to ¥300–500M (STS), ¥0.5–1bn (PCA), ¥2.5bn (DMP) with VC co-investment ≥1/3 of costs; plus Japan's SBIR Promotion Program calls (2026 Phase 2 call May–Jun 2026).
* [Korea TIPS (Tech Incubator Program for Startups)](korea-tips.md) - Korean Ministry of SMEs and Startups program pairing private operator investment with government R&D grants — General TIPS raised to KRW 800M over 2 years in 2026, Scale-up TIPS up to KRW 3bn, new Global TIPS up to KRW 6bn.
* [Latvia LIAA startup & innovation support](latvia-liaa-startup.md) - Investment and Development Agency of Latvia programs — Support for Start-ups up to €300K, Science-intensive Start-ups up to €150K, innovation vouchers up to €25K — open until 30 Apr 2027 (budget permitting).
* [Mozilla Builders Accelerator](mozilla-builders.md) - Mozilla's open-source local-AI accelerator that gave up to $100K non-dilutive funding to 14 projects in its 2024 cohort; no new accelerator cohort announced since — only bespoke "Collaborations" listed (Oct 2026).
* [NASA SBIR/STTR](nasa-sbir-sttr.md) - NASA SBIR/STTR contracts for US small businesses (space, aeronautics, and enabling software/AI/autonomy); moved to a rolling FY2026–27 Broad Agency Announcement with appendices; Phase II/Ignite windows open through Oct 2026.
* [Netherlands MIT (MKB-innovatiestimulering Regio en Topsectoren)](netherlands-mit.md) - Dutch SME innovation subsidies — feasibility projects (now run regionally) and R&D collaboration projects incl. an AI variant; 2026 windows closed 15 Sep 2026; RVO's national instruments marked temporarily closed.
* [Netherlands WBSO (R&D tax credit)](netherlands-wbso.md) - Dutch payroll-tax R&D credit explicitly covering new software development — 36% of R&D wage costs up to €391,020 (50% for starters), 16% above; budget rising to €1.97bn in 2027 with unchanged rates; apply in advance (by 20 Dec for a 1 Jan start).
* [NIH SBIR/STTR (Seed)](nih-sbir-sttr.md) - NIH/HHS SBIR/STTR grants for US small businesses building health technology including health software, digital health and AI; new FY2026 parent NOFOs released May 2026 after the lapse, next standard receipt date 5 Jan 2027.
* [NRC Industrial Research Assistance Program (IRAP)](nrc-irap.md) - Canada's advisor-led, non-repayable contributions to incorporated Canadian SMEs (≤500 FTE) for technology R&D and commercialisation, incl. software/AI; rolling via Industrial Technology Advisors, plus targeted streams (AI Assist, Defence Industry Assist launched Jan 2026).
* [NSF SBIR/STTR (America's Seed Fund)](nsf-sbir-sttr.md) - US National Science Foundation's equity-free SBIR/STTR grants for US small businesses commercialising deep tech (software/AI included) — Phase I up to $305K, Phase II up to $1.25M; reopened June 2026 after the 2025–26 authorization lapse, next deadline 4 Nov 2026.
* [Poland R&D tax relief (ulga B+R) and IP Box](poland-rd-tax-relief.md) - Polish income-tax deduction of 200% of R&D personnel costs and 100% (150% for small taxpayers) of other R&D costs, combinable with the 5% IP Box rate on income from qualifying IP such as software copyrights.
* [Poland FENG "Ścieżka SMART" (PARP / NCBR)](poland-sciezka-smart.md) - Poland's main EU-funded (FENG 2021–27) R&D grant: modular projects with mandatory R&D module, up to 80% of eligible costs; PARP call for single SMEs runs 29 Oct – 29 Dec 2026, NCBR consortium call closes 16 Oct 2026.
* [SPRIND Challenges](sprind-challenges.md) - Germany's Federal Agency for Breakthrough Innovation runs staged, equity-free competitions (often pre-commercial procurement) with multi-million-euro funding per team; open now incl. Recoding Medicine (health-data AI, closes 16 Oct 2026) and AI-Native Chip Design (closes 30 Nov 2026).
* [PARP Startup Booster Poland – Smart UP (incl. Poland Prize)](startup-booster-poland.md) - PARP (FENG-funded) acceleration via 17 selected accelerators giving early-stage micro/small startups up to PLN 400,000 at up to 100% of costs; the Poland Prize path targets foreign startups relocating to Poland; recruitment windows set by each accelerator.
* [Startup SG Founder & Startup SG Tech (Singapore)](startup-sg.md) - Enterprise Singapore grants — Startup SG Founder (S$50K grant matched to S$10K founder capital, via Accredited Mentor Partners) and Startup SG Tech (proof-of-concept up to S$400K, proof-of-value up to S$800K since Jan 2025) for proprietary technology.
* [Czech TA ČR SIGMA (incl. EIC Accelerator plug-in)](tacr-sigma.md) - Technology Agency of the Czech Republic's SIGMA programme for applied R&D; 18th call (Sub-goal 1) supports Czech small deep-tech startups (TRL 4+) with feasibility studies and a fast-track to EIC Accelerator step 2 — closes 27 Oct 2026.
* [UK R&D Tax Relief (merged RDEC & ERIS)](uk-rd-tax-relief.md) - UK corporation-tax relief for R&D incl. software development resolving technological uncertainty — merged 20% RDEC scheme for most companies and Enhanced R&D Intensive Support (186% deduction, 14.5% payable credit) for loss-making R&D-intensive SMEs; accounting periods from 1 Apr 2024.
* [Ukrainian Startup Fund (USF)](ukrainian-startup-fund.md) - State fund under Ukraine's Ministry of Digital Transformation giving equity-free grants to Ukrainian startups; 2026 activity includes the €1M EU-backed Startup EDGE program (€20K pre-seed / €40K seed; call closed Jan 2026), a revived Student Startup Fund and thematic grant calls.
