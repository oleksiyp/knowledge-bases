# Summary

ZIM is Germany's largest technology-open R&D grant for the Mittelstand, including software R&D. On **7 July 2026** the BMWE imposed a temporary application stop for all project forms and sketches because of limited budget funds[^ihk-sh-zim]. Applications already received continue to be assessed; calls with international partners are exempt; a restart is expected no earlier than early 2027, depending on the federal budget[^mum-zim][^ihk-bw-zim]. The current guideline has applied since 1 Jan 2025[^zim-faq].

# Eligibility

- SMEs (and some larger Mittelstand firms) with a business operation in Germany; research organisations as cooperation partners.

# What it funds

- Individual R&D projects, cooperation projects (with firms/research institutes), innovation networks.

# Amounts & terms

- Funding rates by size/region and project type (e.g., small firms up to 45% individual / 55% cooperation)[^mum-zim]. Details in the ZIM guideline.

# How to apply

Currently not possible (except international calls). When reopened: online via the Förderzentrale Deutschland portal.

# Deadlines

None while paused.

# Fit

- Poor fit if: you need money now — consider the [Forschungszulage](/programs/startup-rnd/germany-forschungszulage.md).
