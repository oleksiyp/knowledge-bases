# Summary

The Ukrainian Startup Fund was founded in 2018 and since 1 Jan 2023 is managed by the Ministry of Digital Transformation[^usf-about]. Its 2026 flagship is **Startup EDGE** — a €1M program with the EU4Innovation East project (EU-funded, co-funded by France, implemented by Expertise France) granting up to €20K (pre-seed) or €40K (seed) to DeepTech, GreenTech and EdTech startups[^usf-about][^getgrant-edge]; the call closed 21 Jan 2026 and first-cohort winners were announced in May 2026[^getgrant-edge][^usf-news]. Recent USF news also lists a revived Student Startup Fund (Aug 2026), an "Innovative Prosthetics" grant program of up to UAH 8M (Sep 2026) and a Cloudflare partnership offering up to $100K in cloud credits[^usf-news].

# Eligibility
