# Summary

SPRIND Challenges are staged competitions in which teams receive funding and coaching through successive elimination rounds[^sprind-challenges]. Software/AI-relevant challenges open as of October 2026: **Recoding Medicine** (curate high-value health datasets and demonstrate AI applications; up to 13 teams in stage 1; funded as pre-commercial procurement; closes 16 Oct 2026)[^sprind-recoding] and **AI-Native Chip Design** (AI-driven architecture search/RTL generation/verification; up to €9.6M per team; joint with Dutch agency NADI; closes 30 Nov 2026)[^sprind-chip]. The €125M **Next Frontier AI** challenge closed on 1 June 2026[^tnw-nextfrontier].

# Eligibility

- Generally broad (companies, research groups, individuals); Recoding Medicine requires demonstrable access to at least one suitable dataset[^sprind-recoding]. Check each call's participation agreement.

# What it funds

- Breakthrough prototypes against a defined challenge; Recoding Medicine expects a substantial public contribution from each team (open data/models)[^sprind-recoding].
