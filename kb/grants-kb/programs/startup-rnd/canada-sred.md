# Summary

SR&ED is Canada's largest R&D support: corporations, individuals, trusts and partnerships doing eligible work can claim deductions and investment tax credits[^cra-sred]. Budget 2025 raised the enhanced 35% refundable credit's expenditure limit to **$6M** for taxation years beginning on or after 16 Dec 2024 (max refundable credit ~$2.1M), extended the enhanced rate to some Canadian public corporations, and restored capital expenditures[^leyton-sred]. A practitioner source reports these Budget 2025 measures received Royal Assent[^sreducation-assent].

# Eligibility

- Any taxpayer doing SR&ED in Canada; the 35% refundable rate is for CCPCs (and now certain public corporations) within limits[^leyton-sred].

# What it funds

- Experimental development, applied and basic research; software work qualifies when it addresses technological uncertainty (not routine development).

# Amounts & terms

- 35% refundable on first $6M; 15% general rate (non-refundable for most) above that[^leyton-sred].

# How to apply

File T661 (and provincial forms) with the tax return[^cra-sred]. CRA announced new administrative processes (e.g., pre-claim approval) launching 1 April 2026 (secondary)[^leyton-sred].
