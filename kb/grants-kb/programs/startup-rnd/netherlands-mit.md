# Summary

MIT supports Dutch SMEs with feasibility studies and collaborative R&D. RVO notes feasibility projects are "no longer carried out by RVO" (handled regionally), its R&D collaboration and R&D collaboration-AI instruments are temporarily closed, and knowledge vouchers will not reopen[^rvo-mit]. Regional 2026 openings ran April–September: feasibility 7 Apr–15 Sep 2026 (first come, first served) and R&D collaboration 9 Jun–15 Sep 2026 (tender)[^stimulus-mit][^hezelburcht-mit].

# Eligibility

- Dutch SMEs; R&D collaboration requires at least two SMEs[^hezelburcht-mit].

# What it funds

- Feasibility of innovative products/processes/services; industrial research/experimental development in collaboration[^hezelburcht-mit].
