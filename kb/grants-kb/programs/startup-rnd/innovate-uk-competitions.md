# Summary

With Smart closed, Innovate UK funds businesses mostly through themed, challenge-led competitions on the Innovation Funding Service (IFS) and through the **Growth Catalyst** package[^casrai-smart]. For software/AI startups the most relevant open call is **Frontier AI: SME Champions, Phase 1** — £3.1M pot, projects with £150K–£250K eligible costs, closes **11 Nov 2026**, with a possible Phase 2 (up to £1M, 12 months)[^ifs-frontier-ai]. Also listed: "Efficient, Structured and Controllable AI Systems" (£1.66M, SMEs, closes 18 Nov 2026) and UK–Germany Bilateral CR&D Round 5 (£2.4M, closes 1 Dec 2026)[^ifs-search].

# Eligibility

- UK-registered businesses; many calls SME-only; single vs collaborative per competition[^ifs-frontier-ai].
- Growth Catalyst Investor Partnerships requires an invitation from Innovate UK's pool of investor partners[^ukri-gc-ip2].
- Early Stage: New Innovators was for micro/small firms that had not previously received Innovate UK funding[^ifs-new-innovators].

# What it funds

- Frontier AI: feasibility of ambitious AI/ML in health & life sciences, advanced materials, national security & defence, AI safety & assurance[^ifs-frontier-ai].
- Investor Partnerships: grants matched with private investment for SMEs[^ukri-gc-ip2].

# Amounts & terms

| Competition | Size |

| Early Stage: New Innovators (2025) | £25K–£50K, 3–6 months[^ifs-new-innovators] |

# How to apply

Apply via IFS; read the competition brief and scope; projects are scored by independent assessors. Innovate UK Business Connect offers free guidance.
