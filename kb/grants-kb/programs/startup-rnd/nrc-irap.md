# Summary

NRC IRAP is "Canada's leading innovation assistance program for SMEs", operating for over 75 years with a network of 250+ Industrial Technology Advisors (ITAs)[^canada-di-assist]. It provides advice and non-repayable contributions — chiefly toward R&D salaries and contractors — to incorporated, for-profit Canadian SMEs with up to 500 FTEs[^granthub-irap]. In January 2026 the government announced $241M starting 2025–26 for a new Defence Industry Assist (DI Assist) stream for defence and dual-use tech[^canada-di-assist]. An AI Assist stream supports SME AI adoption/development[^granthub-irap].

# Eligibility

- Incorporated, for-profit, operating in Canada, ≤500 FTE, developing technology-based products/processes/services[^granthub-irap].

# What it funds

- Staff salaries on R&D, subcontractors/consultants, limited overhead; advisory services at no cost[^granthub-irap]. Software R&D is commonly funded.

# Amounts & terms

- No published maximum; sized per project; paid as a contribution agreement (claims against incurred costs)[^granthub-irap].

# How to apply

Call 1-877-994-4727 or use the NRC IRAP website to be assigned an ITA; scope the project together; then a formal proposal and contribution agreement[^canada-di-assist][^granthub-irap].
