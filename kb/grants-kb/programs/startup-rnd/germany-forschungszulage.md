# Summary

Every company with tax residence in Germany has a legal claim to the Forschungszulage if it does R&D, regardless of size, legal form or sector[^bsfz]. It is a two-step process: a BSFZ certificate confirming the project is R&D, then the tax office grants the allowance (paid out even in loss years)[^bsfz]. The "Gesetz für ein steuerliches Investitionssofortprogramm" increased the maximum base and flat rates[^bsfz]; from 2026 the base is €12M per year, the rate 25% (35% for SMEs), plus a 20% overhead flat rate on personnel costs[^steinbeis-fz]. With ZIM paused, this is the most reliable German R&D support for software startups.

# Eligibility

- Any company taxable in Germany doing basic research, industrial research or experimental development[^bsfz].

# What it funds

- Personnel costs, contract research, depreciable assets (since 2024 changes) for certified R&D projects.

# How to apply

Apply online at BSFZ for the project certificate, then file the Forschungszulage claim with the tax office (ELSTER)[^bsfz].
