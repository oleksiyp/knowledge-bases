# Summary

For accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2024 the UK replaced its SME scheme and RDEC with a **merged RDEC (20%)** and **Enhanced R&D Intensive Support (ERIS)** for loss-making SMEs whose relevant R&D spend is at least 30% of total expenditure[^govuk-merged]. ERIS gives an additional 86% deduction (186% total) and a payable credit worth up to 14.5% of the surrenderable loss[^govuk-merged]. Software development qualifies when it seeks an advance in science or technology by resolving technological uncertainty; since 1 April 2023 mathematical advances also count[^govuk-rd].

# Eligibility

- UK companies subject to Corporation Tax undertaking qualifying R&D[^govuk-rd].
- ERIS: SME, loss-making, R&D intensity ≥30%[^govuk-merged].

# What it funds

- Staff costs, subcontractors (with restrictions), software/cloud/data costs (per HMRC rules) for qualifying projects. Routine coding, UX polish or standard integrations generally do not qualify.

# Amounts & terms

- RDEC 20%; ERIS 86% enhancement + 14.5% payable credit; PAYE cap £20,000 + 300% of relevant PAYE/NIC[^govuk-merged].

# How to apply

Check whether you must pre-notify HMRC (claim notification), submit the additional information form, then claim in the CT600; SMEs can request advance assurance[^govuk-merged][^govuk-rd].

# Deadlines

Rolling, tied to each company's accounting period.

# Fit

- Good fit if: UK software company doing genuinely novel engineering (new algorithms, systems-level advances).
- Poor fit if: building standard web/mobile products with established techniques.
