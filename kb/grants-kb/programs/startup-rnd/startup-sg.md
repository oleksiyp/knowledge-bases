# Summary

Startup SG is Singapore's umbrella for government startup support. **Startup SG Founder** offers a S$50,000 grant matched against S$10,000 raised by first-time founders, delivered through Accredited Mentor Partners[^osome-sg][^startupsg-founder]. **Startup SG Tech** funds proof-of-concept (up to S$400K) and proof-of-value (up to S$800K) for proprietary technology, caps raised from S$250K/S$500K for applications from 2 Jan 2025[^deeptechsg-tech].

# Eligibility

- Founder: ≥2 Singapore citizens/PRs as first-time founders, ≥51% local shareholding, Singapore-registered[^osome-sg].
- Tech: proprietary technology, ≥30% local shareholding (secondary)[^osome-sg].

# What it funds

- Founder: venture start-up costs; Tech: technical development milestones.

# Amounts & terms

Milestone-based tranches; Tech grantees raise paid-up capital by 10%/20% of grant for POC/POV[^deeptechsg-tech].

# How to apply

Founder via an Accredited Mentor Partner; Tech via the Business Grants Portal[^osome-sg].
