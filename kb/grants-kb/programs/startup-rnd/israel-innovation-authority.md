# Summary

The Israel Innovation Authority (IIA) is the government's main tech R&D funder. **Tnufa (Ideation)** provides a conditional grant of up to NIS 200,000 over 12 months (80% of the approved budget) to entrepreneurs — even before incorporation — for prototypes, IP protection and business development; founders need not quit their jobs or give up project rights[^iia-tnufa]. The **Startup Fund** co-invests with private investors; a June 2026 update (effective 15 Jul 2026) raised pre-seed support to NIS 2M (up to 60% match) and seed to NIS 6M (50%), targeting deep-tech companies, with higher ceilings for priority regions and under-represented founders[^natlaw-startupfund].

# Eligibility

- Israeli entrepreneurs and new Israeli startups (Tnufa)[^iia-tnufa]; Startup Fund requires ≥15% private-sector commitment at application and closing within 6 months of approval[^natlaw-startupfund].

# What it funds

- Tnufa: materials, subcontractors, consultants, patent attorneys, exhibitions — not salaries/overheads[^iia-tnufa].
