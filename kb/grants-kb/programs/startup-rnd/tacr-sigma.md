# Summary

SIGMA is TA ČR's umbrella applied-research programme with sub-goals and multiple calls per year[^tacr-sigma]. The **18th call, Sub-goal 1 (commercialisation of R&D&I)** targets small enterprises and startups with breakthrough deep-tech at TRL 4 or higher; outputs are English feasibility studies, winners get coaching and may receive EU certification to enter **EIC Accelerator step 2** directly (EIC plug-in)[^tacr-sigma18][^vedavyzkum-sigma18]. Open 10 Sep – **27 Oct 2026**[^tacr-sigma18].

# Eligibility

- Czech small enterprises/startups; financial statements published 2022–2024, beneficial ownership registered; video pitch and TRL-4 evidence in English[^tacr-sigma18].

# What it funds

- Verification of applied research results for practical use and commercial preparation[^vedavyzkum-sigma18].
