# Summary

EIC Transition bridges research results to market-readiness (technology validation plus business case). Grants up to €2.5M; the 2026 deadline was 16 September 2026[^iukbc-eic]. Only open to applicants building on results of eligible prior projects (EIC Pathfinder, ERC PoC, Horizon Pillar 2, and from 2026 also research-infrastructure results)[^alientt].

# Eligibility

- Must exploit results from an eligible precursor project; 2026 relaxed timing (precursor started >18 months before cut-off)[^alientt].
- UK applicants eligible if they have a qualifying Horizon-funded precursor[^iukbc-eic].

# What it funds

- Technology maturation and validation in relevant environments, business-model development.

# Amounts & terms

- Up to €2.5M plus €50K booster[^iukbc-eic].

# How to apply

Funding & Tenders Portal, then interview.
