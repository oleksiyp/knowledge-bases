# Summary

TIPS is Korea's private-led startup R&D scheme: accredited operators (VCs/accelerators) invest first and the government adds R&D grants. For 2026, General TIPS R&D support rose from KRW 500M to **KRW 800M over two years**, Scale-up TIPS offers up to KRW 3bn, and a new Global TIPS track up to KRW 6bn for startups with overseas investment[^ktd-tips][^asiae-tips]. At least 50% of General and Scale-up projects go to non-capital-region startups[^ktd-tips].

# Eligibility

- Korean startups selected and invested in by a TIPS operator; foreign-founder eligibility not stated in sources[^ktd-tips].

# What it funds

- R&D; follow-up R&D up to KRW 1.5bn over 3 years for graduates[^ktd-tips].

# Track record

General TIPS projects planned to rise from 700 to 800, Scale-up from 152 to 300 in 2026[^ktd-tips].
