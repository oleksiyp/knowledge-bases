# Summary

NEDO's DTSU subsidises Japanese deep-tech startups across three phases — STS (up to ¥300M or ¥500M), PCA (up to ¥500M or ¥1bn) and DMP (up to ¥2.5bn) — requiring VC/CVC/operating-company investment of at least one third of eligible expenses, with a VC/CVC as largest investor[^nedo-dtsu]. Recruitment runs FY2023–FY2027 within a ¥87.7bn budget to FY2032; 111 companies were supported in the first eight rounds[^nedo-dtsu]. NEDO also runs Japan's **SBIR Promotion Program**; a 2026 Phase 2 call was announced[^nedo-sbir-p2].

# Eligibility

- Japanese deep-tech startups with qualifying investors[^nedo-dtsu].

# What it funds

- Long-horizon R&D toward commercialisation; deep-tech focus (software alone rarely qualifies unless deep-tech).
