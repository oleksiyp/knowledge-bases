# Summary

The Industry Growth Program funds commercialisation by innovative SMEs and startups in National Reconstruction Fund priority areas (incl. "enabling capabilities" such as software/AI)[^bga-igp]. Two grant streams: A$50K–A$250K and A$100K–A$5M[^bga-igp]. Applicants must first receive an Advisory Service report; the program is **currently paused to new Advisory Services applications**, though existing report holders can still apply for grants[^bga-igp].

# Eligibility

- Combined turnover < A$20M over prior 3 years; ABN; incorporated company/co-op/trustee; innovative product/process/service in NRF priority areas[^bga-igp].

# What it funds

- Commercialisation projects; not minor product improvements[^bga-igp].

# Amounts & terms

Matched funding (co-funding ratio not stated on the page)[^bga-igp].

# How to apply

Advisory Service first (paused), then grant application[^bga-igp].

None while paused.
