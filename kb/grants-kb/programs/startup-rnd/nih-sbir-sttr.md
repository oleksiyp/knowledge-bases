# Summary

NIH runs the largest civilian SBIR/STTR grant program; health IT, clinical software and AI tools qualify when tied to an NIH institute's mission. During the 2025–26 lapse NIH closed its open NOFOs and cancelled the April 2026 receipt date[^grantedai-nih]. After the 13 April 2026 reauthorization[^crowell-reauth], NIH released new parent NOFOs on 28 May 2026 (PA-27-100 SBIR, PA-27-102 STTR, PAR-27-098 Commercialization Readiness Pilot, PA-27-101 Phase IIB Strategic Breakthrough)[^mnsbir-nih]. The next standard receipt date is **5 January 2027**[^mnsbir-nih].

# Eligibility

- US small business; STTR requires a research-institution partner.
- HHS caps submissions at 9 SBIR/STTR applications per small business per fiscal year[^mnsbir-nih][^nih-not-od-26-090].
- ORCID iDs required for senior/key personnel; no late submissions for small businesses[^mnsbir-nih].

# What it funds

- R&D toward commercial health products — including software as a medical device, digital therapeutics, data/AI tools for research or care.

# Amounts & terms

- Budget guidelines: Phase I $323,090; Phase II $2,153,927; CRP up to $4,191,495; Phase IIB Strategic Breakthrough up to $30M requiring 100% matching[^mnsbir-nih]. Institutes may exceed guidelines with justification.

# How to apply

Register (SAM, eRA Commons, Grants.gov), apply to a parent or targeted NOFO; peer review by CSR study sections, then council. Talk to the institute program officer first.
