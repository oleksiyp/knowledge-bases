# Summary

DARPA publishes SBIR/STTR topics through the DoD's monthly DSIP releases and supports small businesses via its Small Business Programs Office and DARPAConnect learning resources[^darpa-topics]. Topics are narrow and ambitious; recent ones include ML/LLM systems for interactive analysis of very large datasets (FALCON, DPA26BZ03-DV016)[^grantedai-darpa]. Phase I amounts commonly fall around $100K–$225K per secondary sources[^grantedai-darpa].

# Eligibility

- US small businesses (SBIR rules); STTR requires a research-institution partner[^grantedai-darpa].

# What it funds

- Topic-defined, high-risk R&D (AI, autonomy, microsystems, cyber). Seedlings and DSO "Disruption Opportunities" are other routes[^grantedai-darpa].

# Amounts & terms

- Contracts; amounts per topic. See [DoD SBIR/STTR](/programs/startup-rnd/dod-sbir-sttr.md) for the 2026 reauthorization changes.

# How to apply

Monitor DARPA topics page and DSIP pre-releases; submit to the release close date[^darpa-topics].

# Deadlines
