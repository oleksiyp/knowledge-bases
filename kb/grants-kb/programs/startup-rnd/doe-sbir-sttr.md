# Summary

DOE's SBIR/STTR program funds energy, science and security R&D by small businesses — topics regularly include scientific software, HPC/AI tools, data management and grid cybersecurity. DOE states the programs "have been reauthorized" effective 13 April 2026 and are "now managed by the DOE Office of Technology Commercialization (OTC)"[^doe-sbir]. A secondary calendar reports the FY26 "Genesis Mission" Phase I opportunity has closed and a broader Phase I opportunity is expected later in autumn 2026[^grantedai-cal].

# Eligibility

- US small business; topic-specific; Letters of Intent typically required before full applications[^grantedai-cal].

# What it funds

- Topic-defined R&D within DOE mission areas (energy, fundamental science, nonproliferation, environmental management)[^doe-sbir].

# Amounts & terms

- Phase I up to about $200,000 for ~6 months per secondary source[^grantedai-cal]; Phase II per FOA. Grants, no equity.

# How to apply

Watch the FOA release, submit a Letter of Intent, then the full application via DOE's portal[^grantedai-cal]. Contact sbir-sttr@hq.doe.gov for forward-looking questions[^doe-sbir].

# Deadlines

Next FOA dates unverified as of 2026-10-03; historically Release 1 in late summer/fall and Release 2 in winter[^precision-cal].

# Track record

DOE describes "hundreds of annual awards"[^doe-sbir].

# Fit

- Good fit if: research-software, HPC, energy-data or grid-security startup.
- Poor fit if: consumer software.
