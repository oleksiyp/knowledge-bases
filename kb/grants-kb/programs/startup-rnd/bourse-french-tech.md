# Summary

The Bourse French Tech is Bpifrance's first-ticket subsidy for innovative startups: up to €30,000 covering up to 70% of eligible expenses, paid first at signature and the remainder on completion[^infogreffe-bft]. The aid listing was updated in September 2026 and shows it as active[^infogreffe-bft]. Practitioners report the company should typically be under one year old and backed by an accredited support structure (incubator/accelerator/French Tech network)[^compass-bft].

# Eligibility

- SMEs under the EU definition[^infogreffe-bft]; in practice young (<1 year) SAS/SARL companies with an innovative project and own funds (secondary)[^compass-bft].

# What it funds

- Prototyping, internal/external R&D, technology maturation with significant technical risk[^infogreffe-bft].
- Not: expenses incurred before submission[^infogreffe-bft].

# Amounts & terms

- Max €30K, 50–70% coverage, project up to 24 months[^infogreffe-bft].

# How to apply

Via Bpifrance (regional office / online), usually with an incubator's support letter; 1–3 months processing[^infogreffe-bft].

# Deadlines

Rolling.

Not compiled here.

# Fit

- Good fit if: newly created French tech startup building an MVP.
- Poor fit if: company older than ~1 year (look at Bpifrance innovation aid instead).
