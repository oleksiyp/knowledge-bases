# Summary

DIGITAL funds deployment and uptake of digital capacities — AI, cybersecurity, data spaces, advanced skills — rather than research. The 2025–2027 work programme was published in March 2025[^errin-wp]. The 10th set of calls (EdTech accelerator, AI for compliance, AI in medical screening, health data, skills with cascade funding) closed **1 October 2026**[^kaila-dep][^dsj-dep]. The European Cybersecurity Competence Centre's €96M call opened 1 Sep 2026 and closes **14 Jan 2027**, including AI-based cybersecurity tools (€15M) and "AI4SME" (€20M)[^iniziativa-eccc].

# Eligibility

- Legal entities in EU Member States and DIGITAL-associated countries; some security topics restrict participation (check topic).
- SMEs often benefit through **cascade funding** (financial support to third parties) run by funded consortia[^dsj-dep].

# What it funds

- Deployment, piloting and uptake actions; not fundamental R&D.

# Amounts & terms

- Typically 50% co-funding (higher for SMEs in some actions); cascade grants up to €150K per entity in skills topics, €120K in EHDS topic[^kaila-dep].

# How to apply

EU Funding & Tenders Portal (or ECCC portal for cyber topics). Watch for open calls by consortia offering cascade funding — far lower effort for startups.
