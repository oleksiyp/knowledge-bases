# Summary

EIT Digital, one of the EIT's first Knowledge and Innovation Communities, became independent as **28DIGITAL** in October 2025 after completing its 15-year EIT cycle[^eit-28digital]. Its 2026 Venture Incubation Program (VIP) is a 12-week bootcamp/mentoring programme for early teams in deep tech, strategic tech and green/social tech, delivered with partner incubators[^eit-vip]. The startup call ran 10 Apr – 9 Jun 2026[^vip-2026]. It is mainly non-cash support; check each call for cash components.

# Eligibility

- Multidisciplinary teams (≥2) and startups incorporated 2025 or later, in EU/Horizon-associated countries[^vip-2026].

# What it funds

- Venture-building services, MVP validation, incorporation and investor readiness[^vip-2026].

# Amounts & terms

- Cash per startup not stated in the sources reviewed.

# How to apply

Open calls on 28digital.eu; partner organisations apply separately to deliver programmes[^eit-vip].
