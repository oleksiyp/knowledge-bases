# Summary

Smart was the UK's go-to open competition for any-sector, disruptive R&D by SMEs, including many software/AI startups. UKRI now states: "Smart grants are now closed for new applications as funding is being aligned to Innovate UK's new strategic direction"[^ukri-smart]. The last competition listed is the November 2024 round[^ifs-smart-nov24]; the pause was announced in January 2025 and, as of mid-2026, no reinstatement in its previous form had been announced[^casrai-smart]. Innovate UK has published a final impact evaluation of the programme[^ukri-smart].

# Eligibility

- Historically: UK-registered businesses of any size, SME-led.
