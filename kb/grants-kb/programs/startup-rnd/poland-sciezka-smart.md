# Summary

Ścieżka SMART (SMART Path) under the European Funds for a Modern Economy (FENG) programme is Poland's flagship R&D grant. Projects are modular with a mandatory R&D module (industrial research/experimental development) and optional modules such as infrastructure, competences, internationalisation and IP protection[^consultingpolska-smart]. PARP handles single micro/small/medium firms; NCBR handles large firms and consortia[^consultingpolska-smart]. The next PARP SME R&D window runs **29 Oct – 29 Dec 2026**; the NCBR consortium window closes **16 Oct 2026**[^sciezka-harmonogram].

# Eligibility

- PARP track: SMEs operating in Poland, single-applicant projects[^consultingpolska-smart].
- NCBR track: consortia (up to 3 entities) / large enterprises[^sciezka-harmonogram].

# What it funds

- R&D staff, commissioned research, equipment, prototype materials, software licences, testing[^consultingpolska-smart] — software/AI R&D is eligible.

# Amounts & terms

- Up to 80% of eligible costs; PARP SME R&D: min PLN 3M R&D costs, max aid PLN 50M (secondary)[^consultingpolska-smart].

# How to apply

Electronic application in PARP's (LSI) or NCBR's system within the window; expert panel evaluation.
