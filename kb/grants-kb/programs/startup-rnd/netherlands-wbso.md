# Summary

WBSO reduces payroll tax for R&D work and is one of the few schemes that explicitly names **developing new software** as eligible[^rvo-wbso]. Rates: 36% in the first bracket (up to €391,020 S&O wage base), 50% for starters, 16% above[^rvo-wbso]. The September 2026 policy letter raises the 2027 budget to €1.968bn with unchanged rates and says the ministry will study broadening software definitions to cover AI development (AI projects up 77% in five years)[^fvm-wbso]. Applications must be made before the R&D period: for a 1 January 2027 start, by **20 December 2026**[^hollands-wbso].

# Eligibility

- Entrepreneurs (companies and self-employed) paying Dutch wage/income tax, doing systematic technical-scientific research or developing technically new products/processes/software[^rvo-wbso].

# What it funds

- R&D wage costs (plus other costs/expenditure via fixed or actual method).
- Only future work — no retroactive applications[^rvo-wbso].
