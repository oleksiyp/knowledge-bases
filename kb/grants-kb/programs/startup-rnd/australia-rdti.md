# Summary

The RDTI offsets part of the cost of eligible R&D. Companies with aggregated turnover under A$20M get a **refundable** offset equal to their corporate tax rate plus 18.5%; larger companies get non-refundable offsets of tax rate + 8.5% (R&D up to 2% of expenses) or + 16.5% (above 2%)[^bga-rdti-overview]. Activities must be registered within 10 months of the income year's end[^bga-rdti-overview]. The government announced significant changes from **1 July 2028**[^bga-rdti]: proposed +4.5 pp offset rates for core R&D, removal of supporting R&D, refundable threshold raised to A$50M but limited to a company's first 10 years, minimum spend A$50K — all unlegislated[^ey-rdti-2026].

# Eligibility

- Australian companies (incl. foreign-owned with Australian PE) conducting core R&D with ≥A$20K spend (current rule; per EY the minimum rises to A$50K under the proposal)[^ey-rdti-2026].

# What it funds

- Core experimental R&D and (until 2028 proposals) supporting R&D; software qualifies when experimental.
