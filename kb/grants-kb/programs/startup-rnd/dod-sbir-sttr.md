# Summary

DoD is the largest SBIR/STTR agency and buys R&D through **contracts** against published topics (many on software, AI, autonomy and cyber). After the authorization lapse ended on 13 April 2026[^crowell-reauth], DoD moved to monthly topic releases on DSIP; FY26 Release 6 opened 23 Sep 2026 and closes **21 Oct 2026**[^precision-cal]. AFWERX/SpaceWERX retired the broad "propose-anything" Open Topic in favour of mission-driven **Focused Open Topics**[^grantengine-afwerx][^afwerx-reauth-qa]. A new Strategic Breakthrough Phase II vehicle (up to $30M, 1:1 match) was created by the 2026 reauthorization[^crowell-reauth][^grantengine-afwerx].

# Eligibility

- US small business (≤500 employees) meeting SBA ownership/control rules; STTR needs a research-institution partner[^sbir-gov].
- Enhanced foreign-risk due diligence under the 2026 Act[^crowell-reauth].

# What it funds

- Topic-specific R&D for military/dual-use needs; FY26 AFWERX priority themes include autonomous mass, command & control/battle management, counter-incursion and agile combat; Space Force priorities include defensive cyber operations[^grantengine-afwerx].
- Does not fund unsolicited ideas outside a topic (use Focused Open Topics where available).

# Amounts & terms

- Phase I and II amounts are set per topic/component. AFWERX 26.1 Phase I: up to $75K (SBIR) or $110K (STTR), 3-month feasibility study (secondary source)[^grantengine-afwerx]. SBA's general guidance: Phase I $50K–$275K, Phase II $750K–$1.8M[^sbir-gov].
- Strategic Breakthrough Award: up to $30M sequential Phase II, 1:1 matching, ≥20% from non-SBIR DoD funds (AFWERX)[^grantengine-afwerx].
- Contracts, not grants; government takes no equity; SBIR data rights apply[^sbir-gov].

# How to apply

Register on DSIP, watch monthly pre-releases (topic authors can be contacted during pre-release), submit before the release's close date[^precision-cal][^dsip-active].

# Track record

DoD funds thousands of SBIR/STTR awards a year; agency-wide figures on SBIR.gov (≈4,000 companies/year across all agencies)[^sbir-gov].

# Fit

- Good fit if: US startup with dual-use software (autonomy, cyber, data fusion, C2) and appetite for defense customers.
- Poor fit if: you want unrestricted grant money or cannot meet ownership/foreign-risk rules.
