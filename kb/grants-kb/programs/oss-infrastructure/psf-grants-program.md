# Summary

The PSF Grants Program funds Python community activity — conferences, workshops, meetup subscriptions, Django Girls/PyLadies workshops — and explicitly **not** development work or sprints.[^psf-grants] It was paused in August 2025 after hitting its 2025 cap amid falling PSF revenue.[^psf-pause][^psf-2026-round] A limited 2026 round ($90,000 budget) accepted applications 4–25 August 2026 for events between 1 December 2026 and 30 April 2027; the PSF stresses this is not a full reopening.[^psf-2026-round][^psf-grants] Included here because people often look to the PSF for software funding — for Python code, see Alpha-Omega (PSF security engineers) and the Sovereign Tech Fund (CPython/PyPI).

# Eligibility

- Organisers of Python conferences (≥60% Python content) and workshops (≥6 h/day instruction).[^psf-grants]

# What it funds

- Events and community; NOT development work, sprints, hackathons, travel, swag, equipment.[^psf-grants]

# Amounts & terms

- Conferences ≤$2,000 (≤$15/attendee/day); workshops ≤$1,500 (≤$25/student/day).[^psf-grants]

# How to apply

- Online form during the window; review 25 Aug–11 Sep; decisions 14 Sep 2026.[^psf-grants]
