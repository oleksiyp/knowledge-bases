# Summary

The Gem Fellowship gives grants to individual Ruby developers who maintain and improve free open source Ruby tools.[^gem-fellowship] Contributed Systems (maker of Sidekiq Pro/Enterprise) committed $100,000 per year for three years; grants range $2,500–$25,000.[^gem-fellowship] The 2026 round took 40 applications requesting $439,000 and funded eight (Rouge, ruby-git, TTY::Markdown, SimpleCov & Ruby Toolbox, Herb, Ferrum & Cuprite, Bridgetown, Ruby-Asterisk).[^gem-2026] Applications for 2027 open **1 December 2026** for one month.[^gem-fellowship]

# Eligibility

- Individual Ruby developers working on open source Ruby tooling.[^gem-fellowship]

# What it funds

- Maintenance and improvements of community Ruby tools/gems.[^gem-fellowship]

# Amounts & terms

- $2,500–$25,000 per project.[^gem-fellowship]

# How to apply

- Submit a proposal during December; gem.coop members select in January.[^gem-fellowship]
- Competition: ~4.4× oversubscribed in 2026 by dollars requested.[^gem-2026]
