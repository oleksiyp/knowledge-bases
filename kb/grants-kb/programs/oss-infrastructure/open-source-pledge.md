# Summary

The Open Source Pledge (launched by Sentry in October 2024) asks companies to pay at least $2,000 per employed developer per year to open source maintainers and foundations "to prevent maintainer burnout and supply chain risk".[^osp-home] Members publish annual reports of what they paid; the pledge reports $7,421,535 paid in total and over $3M in the past year.[^osp-home] Members include Sentry and Zerodha's FLOSS/fund.[^sentry-750k-2025][^floss-blog] For maintainers it is a source of unsolicited money — being discoverable (GitHub Sponsors, thanks.dev, Open Collective, funding.json) is what matters.

# Eligibility

- Maintainers/projects are chosen by member companies.[^osp-home]

# What it funds

- Unrestricted maintainer/foundation support.[^osp-home]

# Amounts & terms

- ≥$2,000 per developer per year from each member.[^osp-home]

# How to apply

- Maintainers: no application; make your project fundable. Companies: join and report payments.[^osp-home]

# Deadlines

| Date | Event |
|---|---|

# Track record

- $7.4M+ total paid.[^osp-home]

# Fit

- Good fit if: your project is a common dependency of software companies.
- Poor fit if: you need a defined grant.
