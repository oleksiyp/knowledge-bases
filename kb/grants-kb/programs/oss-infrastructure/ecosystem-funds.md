# Summary

Ecosystem Funds let companies give to a whole ecosystem (e.g., Python, Rust, JavaScript, Django) instead of picking projects; ecosyste.ms dependency data selects critical projects and Open Source Collective pays them.[^ecosystems-ga][^funds-ecosystems] Every fund with a balance of $1,000+ distributes 100% monthly, paying via GitHub Sponsors, Open Collective, Patreon, Ko-fi etc.; at GA it covered 291 ecosystems and had paid 136 projects.[^ecosystems-ga] Sentry contributed $75k in 2025.[^sentry-750k-2025] Open Source Collective itself is a fiscal host moving ≈$25M/yr for ~2,500 projects.[^ecosystems-ga]

# Eligibility

- Projects identified as critical in an ecosystem by ecosyste.ms data.[^ecosystems-ga]

# What it funds

- Unrestricted support.[^ecosystems-ga]

# Amounts & terms

- Distribution proportional to the fund's algorithm; monthly.[^ecosystems-ga]

# How to apply

- No application; maintain `funding.yml` so payments route to you (otherwise OSC pays via Open Collective).[^ecosystems-ga]

- 375 payments to 136 projects at GA (Apr 2025).[^ecosystems-ga]

# Fit

- Good fit if: you maintain a high-centrality package.
- Poor fit if: you need project funding.
