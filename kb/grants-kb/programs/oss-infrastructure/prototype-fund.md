# Summary

The Prototype Fund is a low-threshold German federal program (funded by the Federal Ministry of Research, Technology and Space, BMFTR, and run by Open Knowledge Foundation Deutschland) for freelance developers and small teams building innovative public-interest free/open source software.[^ptf-dsee][^ptf-wiki] Current priority areas are **data security** and **software infrastructure** (libraries, protocol implementations, developer tools).[^ptf-dsee][^ptf-granted] Funding is up to €47,500 per person / €95,000 per team for six months, with an optional four-month "second stage".[^ptf-granted] Applications for cohort 03 run **1 October–30 November 2026**.[^ptf-reflecta][^ptf-dsee] The official site (prototypefund.de) blocked automated fetching; details here come from the program wiki and two German funding databases.

# Eligibility

- Individuals 18+ or teams of up to 4; residence and tax liability in Germany, self-employed/freelance or partly released from employment.[^ptf-dsee][^ptf-granted]
- Teams form a GbR; per a program announcement, team members may be in other EU countries as long as the GbR is based in Germany (unverified on the official site).[^ptf-granted]
- Not eligible: companies/institutions/associations as applicants, hardware-only projects, double funding.[^ptf-granted]

# What it funds

- New open source software prototypes and infrastructure in data security and software infrastructure; personnel and material costs.[^ptf-dsee]
- Three funding streams reported for the new edition: "Innovation", "Resilience", "Up and Coming" (unverified on official site).

# Amounts & terms

- Up to €47,500 per individual / €95,000 per team for 6 months; second stage up to €31,667 / €63,000 for 4 months.[^ptf-granted]
- 95% funding rate — applicants contribute 5%.[^ptf-wiki]
- Code must be released under an open licence.[^ptf-reflecta]

# How to apply

- Submit via bewerben.prototypefund.de answering structured questions (project, social impact, technical approach, prior art, users, milestones, team).[^ptf-dsee][^ptf-granted]
- A jury selects ~25–30 projects per round; selected teams then file a formal application approved by the ministry's project agency.[^ptf-granted]
- Evaluation: societal value, sustainability/accessibility, technical feasibility, open source release.[^ptf-reflecta]

# Fit

- Good fit if: you're a Germany-resident freelancer/small team prototyping an OSS tool for data security or developer infrastructure.
- Poor fit if: you're outside Germany, a company, or maintaining a mature project (see [Sovereign Tech Fund](/programs/oss-infrastructure/sovereign-tech-fund.md)).
