# Summary

Restack is NLnet's new cascade-funding program under the European Commission's **Open Internet Stack** initiative (Horizon Europe grant agreement 101299072), continuing the NGI Zero approach after the NGI Zero Commons Fund closed.[^nlnet-restack][^nlnet-3grants] It gives small-to-medium R&D grants of €5,000–€50,000 (scalable for proven projects) for free and open source internet technology — core infrastructure, alternatives to closed technologies, security, reproducibility, standards and the devices that run them — with €7M to distribute.[^nlnet-restack] The first call opened 3 September 2026 and closes **3 November 2026 12:00 CET**.[^nlnet-restack] It is arguably the best low-effort, high-hit-rate grant for individual OSS developers anywhere in the world.

# Eligibility

- Anyone — individuals, SMEs, academics, public sector, non-profits, communities — regardless of nationality.[^ngi-opencalls]
- All results under a recognised free/open source licence; scientific outputs open access.[^nlnet-restack][^ngi-opencalls]
- Must fit Open Internet Stack goals. AI projects excluded unless the AI is already in wide societal use (>1M active human users).[^nlnet-restack]
- NLnet's generative-AI policy applies and is being tightened (restricting LLM-generated work).[^nlnet-call-nov]

# What it funds

- Core internet infrastructure, protocols, standards implementations, security, reproducible builds, decentralisation, privacy-friendly devices and end-user software that is part of an open stack.[^nlnet-restack]
- Not: closed/proprietary work, generic AI.[^nlnet-restack]

# Amounts & terms

- €5,000–€50,000 per proposal; can be scaled up significantly with proven potential.[^nlnet-restack]
- Under the predecessor fund NGI Zero, first proposals were capped at €50k, larger proposals required prior successful NGI0 completion, with a €150k per-proposal and €500k lifetime cap; payment is per completed milestone.[^nlnet-commons-guide] Expect similar rules (verify in the Restack guide).
- NLnet also provides free in-kind support (security audits, accessibility, packaging, licensing) to funded projects (NGI0 practice).

# How to apply

- Short web form (project abstract, relevant experience, budget breakdown, comparison with existing efforts, ecosystem). Read the guide for applicants, eligibility and FAQ first.[^nlnet-restack]
- Two-stage review: eligibility and scoring (technical excellence 30%, relevance/impact 40%, value for money 30%; must score >5/7), then strategic selection with clarifying questions; independent committee validates.[^nlnet-commons-guide]
- Tip: budget as tasks with hours × rate; reviewers scrutinise rates.[^nlnet-commons-guide]

# Track record

- New in 2026; predecessor NGI Zero funds supported hundreds of projects (100+ in Commons Fund alone, e.g. KiCad, Kdenlive, Bottles, BeaconDB) — see [NGI Zero Commons Fund](/programs/oss-infrastructure/nlnet-ngi-zero-commons.md).

# Fit

- Good fit if: you're an individual or small team building/maintaining open internet tech and can define €5–50k of milestones.
- Poor fit if: you need large salary-scale funding at once, or your project is mainly AI/LLM-based.
