# Summary

The NGI Zero Commons Fund was NLnet's EU Next Generation Internet fund for "new internet commons across the whole technology spectrum, from libre silicon to middleware, from P2P infrastructure to convenient end user applications", with €21.6M through 2027.[^nlnet-commons] The thirteenth and final call closed on 1 June 2026 and no new applications are sought.[^nlnet-commons] Its role is taken over by [Restack](/programs/oss-infrastructure/nlnet-restack.md) under the EU Open Internet Stack initiative. Kept here for reference and for its 100+ funded projects.[^nlnet-commons-projects]

# Eligibility

- Was open worldwide to individuals and organisations; results under FOSS licences.[^nlnet-commons]

# What it funds

- (Historic) Internet commons technology of all layers.[^nlnet-commons]

# Amounts & terms

- €5,000–€50,000 for first proposals; larger only after a successful NGI0 project; €150k per proposal and €500k lifetime per entity caps; milestone payments.[^nlnet-commons-guide]

# How to apply

- Closed. Apply to [Restack](/programs/oss-infrastructure/nlnet-restack.md) or [CodeSupply](/programs/oss-infrastructure/nlnet-codesupply.md) instead.[^nlnet-commons]
