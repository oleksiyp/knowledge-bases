# Summary

CodeSupply is a Horizon Europe pilot (DG CONNECT) in which NLnet and consortium partners build an open, federated, digitally sovereign catalogue of trusted software metadata.[^nlnet-3grants] Its open calls fund FOSS work on supply-chain security: aggregating and using metadata on origin and licensing, security vulnerabilities and quality metrics.[^nlnet-codesupply] Grants are €5,000–€50,000 from a €400,000 pot; the first call closes **3 November 2026 12:00 CET**.[^nlnet-codesupply] Highly relevant for package-manager, SBOM, and CRA-tooling maintainers.

# Eligibility

- Individuals and organisations of any kind, worldwide; results under recognised FOSS licences; open-access publications.[^nlnet-codesupply][^ngi-opencalls]

# What it funds

- Integrations of supply-chain metadata into FOSS applications, improvements to key infrastructure, auxiliary FOSS efforts.[^nlnet-codesupply]

# Amounts & terms

- €5,000–€50,000 per proposal; milestone-based payment (NLnet practice).[^nlnet-codesupply]

# How to apply

- Same NLnet proposal form as Restack; read the guide for applicants, eligibility and FAQ.[^nlnet-codesupply]
