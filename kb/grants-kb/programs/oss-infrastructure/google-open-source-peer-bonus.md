# Summary

The Google Open Source Peer Bonus recognises external open source contributors nominated by Google employees with a small cash award described as "a small token of appreciation".[^gpb-2024] The last public announcement found is the first 2024 group (June 2024: 130 winners, 25 countries, 78 projects).[^gpb-2024] No 2025 or 2026 announcements were found on Google's Open Source Blog, so the program's current status is unverified and it is marked paused here.[^gpb-label]

# Eligibility

- External (non-Google) contributors nominated by Googlers.[^gpb-2024]

# What it funds

- Recognition of past contributions.[^gpb-2024]

# Amounts & terms

- Amount not disclosed by Google.[^gpb-2024]

# How to apply

- Nomination only.[^gpb-2024]
