# Summary

For a full breakdown of topics, funded projects, cascade funds and rules, see the dedicated knowledge base [Horizon Europe Open Internet Stack](https://okf.zengarden.space/b/horizon-ois-kb).


Under Horizon Europe Cluster 4 the European Commission funds the "Open Internet Stack" — the successor to Next Generation Internet (NGI). Most individual developers access this money indirectly via cascade funds such as [NLnet Restack](/programs/oss-infrastructure/nlnet-restack.md); the direct calls are large consortium grants.[^nlnet-3grants] The 2026 Research & Innovation Action "Open Internet Stack Sovereign Solutions" (HORIZON-CL4-2026-04-DATA-02) had a €20.5M budget, €7M–€10.25M per project at 100% funding, and closed 15 April 2026; a companion CSA "Support for Scale" was also published.[^euroaccess-ois][^euroaccess-ois-scale][^hadea-2026]

# Eligibility

- Consortia of at least three independent legal entities, including at least one in an EU Member State and others from different Member States/Associated Countries.[^euroaccess-ois]
- Companies, SMEs, research organisations, universities, NGOs, public bodies.[^euroaccess-ois]

# What it funds

- Mature open-source solutions for networking, sovereign operating systems, open-source productivity and software supply-chain tools, with interoperability, standards compliance and community support.[^euroaccess-ois]

# Amounts & terms

- €7M–€10.25M per project, 100% funding rate (RIA); standard Horizon Europe grant agreement and reporting.[^euroaccess-ois]

# How to apply

- Via the EU Funding & Tenders portal; full Horizon proposal (excellence, impact, implementation). Opened 15 Jan 2026.[^euroaccess-ois]

# Fit

- Good fit if: you're a European company/foundation able to lead or join a multi-million consortium.
- Poor fit if: you're an individual maintainer — apply to the cascade funds instead.
