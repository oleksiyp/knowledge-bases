# Summary

Spotify's FOSS Fund, launched in 2022, donates annually to independent open source projects in Spotify's stack.[^spotify-launch] The 2025 recipients (announced January 2026) were FFmpeg (€30,000), Xiph.Org Foundation (€25,000) and Mock Service Worker (€15,000); Xiph.Org has received funding every year.[^spotify-2025]

# Eligibility

- Projects Spotify depends on, chosen internally.[^spotify-launch]

# What it funds

- Unrestricted donations.[^spotify-2025]

# Amounts & terms

- €15k–€30k per recipient in 2025.[^spotify-2025]

# How to apply

- No public application.[^spotify-2025]
