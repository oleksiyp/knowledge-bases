# Summary

Sovereign Tech Standards is a 2026–27 pilot that pays maintainers of open source digital infrastructure to participate in standards bodies (IETF, W3C, ISO) so that implementers' voices shape the specs they implement.[^sta-standards] It pays a fixed €4,800–€5,200 per month for ~10 hours/week plus in-person meeting attendance, for up to ten maintainers from end of June 2026 to June 2027.[^sta-standards] The application window ran 28 April–19 May 2026 and is closed.[^sta-standards]

# Eligibility

- Active maintainer of an OSS infrastructure project connected to IETF/W3C/ISO standards; capacity for 10 h/week and travel to meetings.[^sta-standards]

# What it funds

- Time spent in working groups, reviewing drafts, implementing/feeding back interoperability experience.[^sta-standards]

# Amounts & terms

- €4,800–€5,200 per month fixed; pilot cohort June 2026–June 2027.[^sta-standards]

# How to apply

- Applications via STA's platform during announced windows.[^sta-standards]
