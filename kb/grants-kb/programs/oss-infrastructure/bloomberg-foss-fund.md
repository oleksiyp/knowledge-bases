# Summary

Launched by Bloomberg's OSPO in January 2023, the FOSS Contributor Fund runs quarterly employee elections that award up to three $10,000 grants per cycle.[^bbg-launch] It is fiscally hosted by Open Source Collective: $360k raised and ~$320k disbursed, with the latest grants in December 2025 to Let's Encrypt and pURL.[^bbg-oc]

# Eligibility

- Projects nominated by Bloomberg employees; must be able to receive payment (e.g., Open Collective).[^bbg-launch]

# What it funds

- Unrestricted donations.[^bbg-oc]

# Amounts & terms

- $10,000 each.[^bbg-oc]

# How to apply

- No application.[^bbg-launch]
