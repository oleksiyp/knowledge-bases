# Research & scientific software grants

* [Digital Research Alliance of Canada — Research Software Maintenance Fund (RSMF)](alliance-canada-rsmf.md) - Canadian fund paying teams to keep research software used by Canadian researchers reliable, secure and sustainable — Small Maintenance Grants ≤CA$150K/12 mo, Large Sustaining Grants ≤CA$500K/24 mo; 2026 LOIs closed 26 June 2026, full proposals by invitation.
* [Better Scientific Software (BSSw) Fellowship](bssw-fellowship.md) - DOE- and NSF-funded fellowship giving at least three U.S.-affiliated people up to $25,000 each to promote better scientific software practice; 2027 applications close 30 October 2026.
* [Chan Zuckerberg Initiative Essential Open Source Software for Science (EOSS) — ended](czi-eoss.md) - CZI program (2019–2024) that granted $100K–$400K over two years to maintain open-source scientific software; six cycles, last RFA closed Oct 2023; no further cycles — succeeded in 2026 by the Renaissance Philanthropy-hosted Open Source for Science Fund.
* [DFG Research Software Infrastructures (Forschungssoftwareinfrastrukturen)](dfg-research-software-infrastructures.md) - Permanent DFG (LIS) programme funding German research and infrastructure institutions to build, establish and consolidate research software infrastructures at technical, organisational and individual (skills) levels; no fixed budget cap; two submission dates a year (March and August).
* [DOE ASCR Next-Generation Scientific Software Technologies / Software Stewardship (CASS)](doe-ascr-software-stewardship.md) - DOE Office of Science ASCR funding that carries the Exascale Computing Project software legacy through a set of software stewardship organizations federated as CASS; awarded to national labs and partners, no open call as of Oct 2026.
* [Netherlands eScience Center — Open eScience Call (OEC)](escience-open-escience-call.md) - Annual Dutch call giving research teams up to 3 person-years of in-kind eScience Center RSE expertise for 3–3.5-year projects that need advanced research software; 8 projects in 2026; full proposals closed 18 Jun 2026, decisions Nov 2026.
* [Netherlands eScience Center — Call for Open and Sustainable Research Software (OSS)](escience-open-sustainable-research-software.md) - Dutch call (with Open Science NL) giving 8 teams up to €250K each — ~€225K in-kind RSE time plus ≤€25K for community activities over ≤2 years — to make existing open research software more sustainable; 2026 full proposals closed 3 Sep 2026, decisions Dec 2026.
* [Netherlands eScience Center — Research Software Sustainability Fellows (RSSF)](escience-rssf-fellowship.md) - Dutch fellowship (eScience Center + Open Science NL) naming 20 Netherlands-based research-software sustainability ambassadors, each with 40 hours of eScience Center expertise and €2,000 for expenses; 2026 applications closed 11 Jun 2026.
* [Helmholtz ScienceServe — Boosting Research Software at Helmholtz](helmholtz-scienceserve.md) - Internal Helmholtz Association call (Initiative and Networking Fund) giving Helmholtz-centre teams up to €200,000 each to improve quality, reusability and long-term availability of research software; 2nd call closed 14 Aug 2026, projects start Mar 2027.
* [Horizon Europe Research Infrastructures — INFRAEOSC (European Open Science Cloud)](horizon-europe-infraeosc.md) - EU Horizon Europe destination funding consortia that build the EOSC ecosystem (FAIR data services, research software, AI-readiness), with all software required to be open source; 2026 calls (≈€50M) closed 16 Jun 2026; 2027 calls open 9 Mar and close 15 Jun 2027.
* [Institute for Research Software (formerly SSI) Fellowship Programme](irs-ssi-fellowship.md) - UK fellowship giving ~20 researchers/RSEs per year £4,000 each plus mentoring to promote good research software practice; 2027 applications close 5 October 2026.
* [NASA ROSES Support for Open-Source Tools, Frameworks, and Libraries (OSTFL, F.7) and Open-Source Science elements](nasa-roses-ostfl.md) - NASA Science Mission Directorate element funding sustainment of high-value open-source science software (Foundational ≤5 yrs, Sustainment ≤3 yrs); last solicited in ROSES-2024, not solicited in ROSES-2025, and ROSES-2026 still unreleased as of late Aug 2026.
* [NCI Informatics Technology for Cancer Research (ITCR)](nci-itcr.md) - NCI program funding cancer-research informatics software across its lifecycle — early-stage U01 ($300K/yr), advanced-development U24 and sustainment U24 (up to $600K/yr, 5 yrs); next due date 19 October 2026.
* [NIH BRAIN Initiative Research Resource Grants for Technology Integration and Dissemination (U24)](nih-brain-resource-u24.md) - BRAIN Initiative U24 funding maintenance, minor enhancement and dissemination of validated neuroscience resources including open-source software and models; ~$10M/yr for 10–20 awards; next due date 6 October 2026.
* [NIH Research Software Engineer (RSE) Award (R50)](nih-rse-award-r50.md) - NIH salary-support award for research software engineers building and disseminating biomedical software; up to $200K/yr salary (≤$300K direct over 3 yrs); final due date 4 December 2026 under RFA-OD-24-011.
* [NIH ODSS Building Sustainable Software Tools for Open Science (R03)](nih-sustainable-software-r03.md) - Trans-NIH (Office of Data Science Strategy) award funding collaborations between scientists and software engineers to make biomedical research software robust and sustainable; predecessor offered up to $300K direct over 2 years; 2026 reissue (RFA-OD-26-034) forecast but posting unconfirmed.
* [NSF CSSI — Cyberinfrastructure for Sustained Scientific Innovation](nsf-cssi.md) - NSF's main program for building and sustaining research software and data cyberinfrastructure (Elements up to $600K, Frameworks up to $5M, Transition to Sustainability up to $1M); paused, waiting for a new solicitation after the Dec 1, 2025 deadline.
* [NSF OAC Cybersecurity Innovation for Cyberinfrastructure (CICI)](nsf-oac-cici.md) - NSF OAC program funding security tools, datasets and resilience work for scientific cyberinfrastructure, including AI-data integrity; $600K–$1.2M over 3 years; annual deadline third Wednesday of January (next 20 Jan 2027).
* [NSF OAC Integrated Data Systems & Services (IDSS)](nsf-oac-idss.md) - NSF OAC program funding national-scale and pilot data systems and services for science, with all developed software required to be open source; $500K pilots up to $10–30M systems; next deadline (Categories I and III) 27 July 2027.
* [NSF PESOSE — Pathways to Enable Secure Open-Source Ecosystems (formerly POSE)](nsf-pesose.md) - NSF TIP program funding organizations to build, expand and secure community-governed open-source ecosystems around research-born software; $300K planning to $1.5M ecosystem/security awards; open, next deadline 2 March 2027.
* [NumFOCUS Small Development Grants (SDG)](numfocus-small-development-grants.md) - NumFOCUS microgrants of up to $10,000 per proposal (≤$10K per project per year) for NumFOCUS-sponsored and affiliated scientific OSS projects; open-issue proposals selected by committee review plus lottery; last labelled round 2025-R2 (deadline 17 Oct 2025); 2026 round schedule not published.
* [Open Source for Science Fund — Open Source for the Life Sciences (OS4LS)](os4science-open-source-life-sciences.md) - Multi-donor fund (Biohub, Wellcome, Kavli; hosted by Renaissance Philanthropy) succeeding CZI EOSS; inaugural 2026 call gives up to $250K (domain tools) or $1M (foundational libraries/ecosystems) over 2 years to open-source life-science software; 2026 round closed, decisions Oct 2026.
* [OSCARS Open Calls for Open Science Projects and Services (EU, completed)](oscars-open-science-calls.md) - Horizon Europe-funded OSCARS cascading grants (≈€16M across two calls, €100K–€250K lump sums for 12–24 months) for open-science projects and services built on European research infrastructures and EOSC; both calls done (2024, 2025), no further calls.
* [R Consortium Infrastructure Steering Committee (ISC) Grant Program](r-consortium-isc-grants.md) - Twice-yearly grants (typically a few thousand to ~$10K+) for projects that strengthen R's technical and social infrastructure — packages, tooling, community; 2026 round 2 closed 1 Oct 2026, decisions 1 Nov 2026; next round expected spring 2027.
* [rOpenSci Champions Program](ropensci-champions.md) - 12-month mentoring program with a participation stipend for researchers building R research software and open-science communities; 2026–27 cohort in Spanish for Latin America (applications closed 20 Feb 2026).
* [Schmidt Sciences Virtual Institute for Scientific Software (VISS)](schmidt-viss.md) - Schmidt Sciences network of university scientific software engineering centers (Cambridge, Georgia Tech, Johns Hopkins, U. Washington) that provide professional RSE teams to research groups; $40M over five years at launch; no open application call — researchers engage centers directly.
* [SciML Small Grants (Julia scientific machine learning bounties)](sciml-small-grants.md) - Rolling bounty-style micro-grants ($100–$2,250) for defined tasks in the Julia SciML ecosystem, funded by donations through NumFOCUS; claim a project, get an exclusive window, get paid on completion.
* [Simons Foundation Scientific Software Research Faculty Award](simons-scientific-software-faculty-award.md) - Simons Foundation award creating permanent research-faculty positions at U.S. PhD-granting universities for mathematicians, astronomers and theoretical physicists who lead scientific software — salary support plus $50K/yr research allowance for 5 years; up to six per cycle; LOI each January.
* [Alfred P. Sloan Foundation — Better Software for Science (discontinued)](sloan-better-software-for-science.md) - Former Sloan Technology program line on practices, norms and institutions for discovery-enhancing research software; no longer accepting proposals, superseded by Sloan's Open Source in Science line.
* [Alfred P. Sloan Foundation — Open Source in Science (Technology program)](sloan-open-source-in-science.md) - Sloan Technology program line funding tooling, institutions, career paths, economic models and incentives for open-source research software, hardware and models (not individual project development); rolling letters of inquiry; grants commonly $100K–$400K+.
* [UKRI / Institute for Research Software — Research Software Maintenance Fund (RSMF)](uk-research-software-maintenance-fund.md) - UKRI Digital Research Infrastructure fund run by the Software Sustainability Institute (now Institute for Research Software) paying UK-led teams to maintain existing research software; Small ≤£150K/12 mo, Large ≤£500K/24 mo (round 1); round 2 awarded Aug 2026; no round 3 announced.
