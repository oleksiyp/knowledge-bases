# Summary

PESOSE (NSF 26-506) is the 2026 successor to NSF's Pathways to Enable Open-Source Ecosystems (POSE). It is run by the Directorate for Technology, Innovation and Partnerships (TIP) and expects 40–60 awards from about $40 million.[^nsf-26-506] It does **not** pay for writing a research product. It pays for the *organization* around one: governance, a managing organization, contributor onboarding, and (new in Track 3) safety, security and privacy hardening.[^nsf-pose-landing][^nsf-26-506] There are two deadlines a year (first Tuesday of September and of March). The September 1, 2026 round has closed and the next deadline is **March 2, 2027**.[^nsf-26-506]

# Eligibility

- U.S. institutions of higher education, U.S. non-profit non-academic organizations, **U.S.-based for-profit companies**, state/local governments, Tribal Nations, and other federal agencies/FFRDCs.[^nsf-26-506]
- No limit on the number of proposals per organization or per PI/co-PI.[^nsf-26-506]
- You need an existing open-source product that came out of research and has external users and contributors. Proposals must include at least 3–5 letters of collaboration from users or contributors.[^nsf-26-506]
- Track 1 and Track 2 teams must take part in "I-Corps for PESOSE" experiential training.[^nsf-26-506]

# What it funds

- **Track 1 – Scoping and planning** an open-source ecosystem (OSE): community discovery, governance and sustainability planning.[^nsf-26-506]
- **Track 2 – Establishing and expanding** a sustainable OSE around a robust open-source product: managing organization, infrastructure, contributor pipeline.[^nsf-26-506]
- **Track 3 – Improving safety, security and privacy** of an existing OSE and its products. This absorbs the scope of the standalone 2024–25 Safe-OSE solicitation, which offered up to $1.5M for 24 months.[^nsf-26-506][^nsf-safe-ose]
- Not funded: development of the open-source product itself, or ecosystems that are already well resourced.[^nsf-pose-landing]

# Amounts & terms

| Track | Max award | Duration | Est. awards |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Scoping & planning | $300,000 | up to 1 year | ~30 |
| 2 Establishing & expanding | $1,500,000 | up to 2 years | ~10 |
| 3 Safety, security, privacy | $1,500,000 | up to 2 years | ~10 |

Source: NSF 26-506.[^nsf-26-506] Proposal titles must start with "PESOSE: [Track]:". Voluntary cost sharing is not allowed.[^nsf-26-506]

# How to apply

- Submit full proposals through Research.gov by 5 p.m. local time on the deadline. There is no preliminary proposal stage.[^nsf-26-506]
- Questions go to pose@nsf.gov.[^nsf-pose-landing]
- Award terms under the 2025 NSF policy changes include certifications about DEI programming. The Carpentries turned down a POSE award it had been recommended for in 2025 rather than accept those terms. Read the award conditions before you apply.[^carpentries-pose]

# Track record

- POSE ran under NSF 23-556 and NSF 24-606 before it was renamed PESOSE in NSF 26-506.[^nsf-pose-landing] The award count for earlier rounds was not checked for this entry.

# Fit

- **Good fit if:** a lab or university spin-out has a research-born OSS tool with real outside users and needs governance, a foundation-like home, or a security program. Companies are eligible too.
- **Poor fit if:** you need money to write code or add features (use CSSI, NIH or OS4Science instead); you are outside the U.S.; or you cannot accept the current federal award conditions.
