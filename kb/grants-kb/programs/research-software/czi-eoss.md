# Summary

EOSS funded maintenance, growth, development and community work for open-source software essential to biomedical science. It was a model for maintainer-focused research software funding.[^czi-eoss] It ran **six cycles**. The last (Cycle 6) took LOIs until October 17, 2023, and awards started around June 2024. Grants were **$50K–$200K a year for two years**.[^czi-eoss] The CZI page now says the program is **not accepting applications**.[^czi-eoss] In May 2026 the EOSS team's design was carried into the **Open Source for Science Fund**, hosted by Renaissance Philanthropy and seeded with $20M from Biohub and Wellcome. That fund counts EOSS's record as $58M across 230+ projects.[^rp-os4s]

# Eligibility

- Closed. Historically open to OSS projects worldwide, via fiscal hosts or institutions.

# What it funds

- Historical: maintenance, community growth, documentation, usability and diversity work for widely used scientific OSS.[^czi-eoss-blog]

# Amounts & terms

- $100K–$400K over 2 years (Cycle 6).[^czi-eoss]
