# Summary

RSSF is the fellowship pillar of the Open Science NL-funded Research Software Sustainability Programme.[^osnl-esc] It appoints **20 fellows** who champion sustainable research software in their communities. Each receives **40 hours** of eScience Center expertise and **€2,000** toward expenses, plus invitations to networking events.[^esc-rssf-2026] The 2026 deadline was June 11, 2026, with interviews September 2–3.[^esc-rssf-2026]

# Eligibility

- Based in the Netherlands. Researchers, developers, RSEs, domain advocates, and leaders or facilitators who work with research software.[^esc-rssf-2026]

# What it funds

- Advocacy, training and community activities for research software sustainability.[^esc-rssf-2026]

# Amounts & terms

- €2,000 cash plus 40 hours in-kind.[^esc-rssf-2026]

# How to apply

- Online application, then interview.[^esc-rssf-2026]
