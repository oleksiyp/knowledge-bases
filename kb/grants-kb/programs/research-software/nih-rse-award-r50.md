# Summary

The NIH RSE Award (R50) pays the **salary of a research software engineer**, a person who builds, maintains and disseminates biomedical, behavioral or health software and trains its users, so that the RSE has a stable position rather than depending on soft money.[^sg-rfa-od-24-011] The current notice, RFA-OD-24-011, was posted July 17, 2024 and closes **December 4, 2026**, its last due date.[^sg-rfa-od-24-011] The award is up to $300,000 in direct costs over up to 3 years: at most $200,000 a year for salary only, plus up to $2,500 a year for travel.[^granted-r50]

# Eligibility

- U.S. organizations: higher education, non-profits, for-profits, governments and Tribal entities. **Non-U.S. organizations cannot apply**, though foreign components may be allowed under NIH rules.[^sg-rfa-od-24-011]
- Check the NOFO for how the RSE must be positioned relative to NIH-funded research (details not verified here).

# What it funds

- Salary support for RSEs who develop and disseminate software, tools and algorithms for NIH-funded research.[^sg-rfa-od-24-011]
- Not research project costs. Clinical trials are not allowed.[^sg-rfa-od-24-011]

# Amounts & terms

- See summary. Indirect costs are paid on top.[^granted-r50]

# How to apply

- NIH ASSIST/Grants.gov under RFA-OD-24-011 by December 4, 2026, 5 p.m. local time.[^sg-rfa-od-24-011]
