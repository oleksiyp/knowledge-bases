# Summary

The BSSw Fellowship gives individuals up to **$25,000** to carry out an activity that promotes better scientific software and produces a public artifact, such as a workshop, tutorial series or community resource.[^bssw-apply] It is jointly funded by the U.S. Department of Energy and the National Science Foundation and the 2027 call was also announced by the DOE-funded Consortium for the Advancement of Scientific Software (CASS).[^bssw-2027-open][^cass-bssw] At least three Fellows are chosen each year, plus honorable mentions. Applications for the 2027 cohort close **October 30, 2026**, and NSF has said the deadline will not be extended.[^bssw-apply]

# Eligibility

- Applicants must be affiliated with a U.S.-based institution that can receive federal funding, such as a national lab, university or U.S. company.[^bssw-2027-open]
- Any career stage. Applicants submit a CV and a supervisor letter of recommendation.[^bssw-apply]

# What it funds

- Activities that improve scientific software practice: workshops, tutorials, training material, community engagement artifacts.[^bssw-apply]
- It does not pay for developing your own research code. The output is outreach, training or community work.

# Amounts & terms

- Up to $25,000 per Fellow, performance period April 2027 – April 2028. Fellows are recognized at the US-RSE annual conference in October 2027.[^bssw-apply]

# How to apply

- Short online application with CV and supervisor letter. Q&A webinar on September 24, 2026; Fellows announced January 2027.[^bssw-apply]

# Track record

Fellows have included graduate students, early-career and senior staff in academia, national labs and small businesses (see BSSw "Meet Our Fellows").[^bssw-apply]

# Fit

- **Good fit if:** you are a U.S.-based RSE or researcher with a concrete plan to teach or spread software best practices.
- **Poor fit if:** you are outside the U.S., or you need to pay developers to write code.
