# Summary

INFRAEOSC is the Horizon Europe Research Infrastructures destination for an **operational, open and FAIR European Open Science Cloud**, including EOSC Federation services.[^rea-infraeosc] The 2026–2027 work programme says that **all software developed under this destination must be open source**, licensed CC0, EUPL or another FSF/OSI-recommended license.[^wp-infra-2026-27] The 2026 call (2 topics, about €50M) ran March 10 – **June 16, 2026**. Selections are expected in November 2026 and projects start around June 2027.[^rea-infraeosc] The **2027 calls open March 9, 2027 and close June 15, 2027**.[^wp-infra-2026-27] The 2025 call had 5 topics, €106.5M and 34 proposals.[^rea-infraeosc]

# Eligibility

- Standard Horizon Europe consortium rules: legal entities in Member States and associated countries, usually at least 3 partners from 3 countries (check each topic).[^wp-infra-2026-27]

# What it funds

- 2026 topics include uptake of FAIR data management practices and of EOSC by research communities and infrastructures.[^rea-infraeosc] Earlier topics covered skills and training for Open Science and AI-readiness and machine actionability in EOSC.[^rea-infraeosc]

# Amounts & terms

- Multi-million-euro consortium grants. Budgets per topic are in the work programme.[^wp-infra-2026-27]

# How to apply

- EU Funding & Tenders Portal; consortium proposal.[^rea-infraeosc]
