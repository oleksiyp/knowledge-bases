# Summary

The SDG program gives microgrants of **up to $10,000** to NumFOCUS **fiscally sponsored and affiliated** projects (NumPy, pandas, Julia, Bioconductor, GeoPandas, Spyder, mlpack and others) for code, docs, workshops, community work and travel.[^numfocus-sdg] Since 2025, proposals are filed as **public GitHub issues**. A committee screens them for appropriateness, then a **randomization script** ranks the appropriate ones until the round's money runs out, taking into account each project's funding earlier in the year.[^sdg-repo] There were two rounds in 2025: Round 1 due June 6 and Round 2 due **October 17, 2025**.[^numfocus-sdg] Several 2026 proposals have been filed in the repository, but as of October 2026 there is no 2026 round label or deadline, so the 2026 schedule is **unverified**.[^sdg-repo]

# Eligibility

- Only NumFOCUS Sponsored or Affiliated projects. One proposal per cycle, at most $10K per project per year.[^numfocus-sdg][^sdg-repo]

# What it funds

- Code development, documentation, usability, community and diversity initiatives, workshops, and developer travel to project events.[^numfocus-sdg]
- Reviewers favor work that benefits the scientific computing community broadly and projects not funded before.[^numfocus-sdg]

# Amounts & terms

- Up to $10K. A completion report is due within three months after the one-year award period.[^numfocus-sdg]

# How to apply

- Open an issue in the proposals repository using the template. Project leads must confirm the proposal, otherwise it is closed as spam.[^sdg-repo]

# Track record

- 2025 funded proposals include GeoPandas static plotting, Spyder docs, mlpack, HoloViz machine-readable interfaces for AI codegen, TARDIS, JuliaHealth docs, and Bioconductor service maintenance.[^sdg-repo]

# Fit

- **Good fit if:** your project is already under NumFOCUS and needs a few thousand dollars for a well-scoped task.
- **Poor fit if:** you are not a NumFOCUS project. Apply for affiliation first, or use other small funds.
