# Summary

VISS is a Schmidt Sciences initiative, originally under Schmidt Futures. It funds a network of **scientific software engineering centers** at the University of Cambridge, Georgia Tech, Johns Hopkins and the University of Washington.[^viss-page] Each center has a faculty director, an engineering lead and a team of professional research software engineers (RSEs) who build platform-grade, open, scalable code with university research groups.[^viss-page] The network launched with **$40M over five years**.[^cam-viss] Schmidt Sciences published a 2025 impact report, so the network continues, but there is **no open application call**: researchers get support by working with a host center.[^viss-page]

# Eligibility

- Researchers at, or collaborating with, the host universities. Each center selects projects (see e.g. JHU SSEC).[^jhu-ssec]

# What it funds

- In-kind professional RSE effort on scientific software, with access to HPC, large databases and ML expertise.[^viss-page]

# Amounts & terms

- Institutional funding to the centers; projects receive engineering time, not cash.[^viss-page]

# How to apply

- Contact the scientific software engineering center at the relevant university, e.g. the Johns Hopkins SSEC.[^jhu-ssec]

# Deadlines

None public.

# Track record

- Four inaugural centers. Schmidt Sciences reports progress in a 2025 impact report.[^viss-page][^jhu-viss]

# Fit

- **Good fit if:** you are at, or partnering with, Cambridge, Georgia Tech, JHU or UW and need engineers rather than cash.
- **Poor fit if:** you want a grant for your own team.
