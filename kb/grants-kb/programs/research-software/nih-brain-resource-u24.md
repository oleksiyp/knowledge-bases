# Summary

RFA-NS-27-001 is a reissued BRAIN Initiative U24 that pays to **disseminate validated neuroscience technologies** to the research community. It explicitly includes maintenance, minor enhancements and distribution of open-source computational models and software packages, along with user training.[^rfa-ns-27-001] NIH plans about $10M a year for 10–20 awards of up to 5 years, with one renewal possible.[^rfa-ns-27-001] Due dates are June 10, 2026 (passed), **October 6, 2026**, June 10, 2027 and February 18, 2028.[^rfa-ns-27-001]

# Eligibility

- U.S. organizations. **Non-U.S. entities and non-domestic components of U.S. organizations cannot apply**, and foreign subawards are prohibited.[^rfa-ns-27-001]
- The resource must already be validated, for example through publications. Novel, unproven technology development is out of scope.[^rfa-ns-27-001]

# What it funds

- Dissemination and support of reagents, instrumentation services, imaging and recording systems, **open-source software maintenance and distribution**, user training and core facilities.[^rfa-ns-27-001]

# Amounts & terms

- No specific budget cap; budgets must reflect actual needs. Up to 5 years.[^rfa-ns-27-001]

# How to apply

- NIH ASSIST/Grants.gov to RFA-NS-27-001. See the BRAIN funding page for related analysis-software RFAs.[^brain-funding]
