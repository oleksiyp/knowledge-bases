# Summary

ScienceServe is an **internal** Helmholtz Association call. It funds Helmholtz-centre teams to improve the **quality, reusability, visibility and long-term availability** of scientific software through best-practice engineering, closer developer–scientist exchange, and FAIR4RS.[^hifis-ss2] The first call (2025) funded **17 projects** of 12 months starting in 2026.[^hifis-ss1-awards] The second call offers **up to €200,000 per project** from the Initiative and Networking Fund, for about 10–15 projects of 18–21 months starting March 2027. It closed **August 14, 2026**.[^helmholtz-ss-call-pdf][^hifis-ss2]

# Eligibility

- Researchers, software engineers and teams at Helmholtz centres only.[^hifis-ss2]

# What it funds

- Staff, travel and consumables. Investments and overheads are not eligible.[^helmholtz-ss-call-pdf]

# Amounts & terms

- Up to €200K per project. Funds must be used in 2027–2028.[^helmholtz-ss-call-pdf][^hifis-ss2]

# How to apply

- Proposal with work packages, milestones, CVs, budget and letters of confirmation. Formal check, then pre-assessment and review. HIFIS Consulting supports funded projects.[^helmholtz-ss-call-pdf][^hifis-ss2]

# Track record

- First-call projects include ASPECT-NEXT (GPU-ready geodynamics), BoostFastSurfer (brain MRI), mHMv6 (hydrology), RiboVerse and ODIS2ODV.[^hifis-ss1-awards]

# Fit

- **Good fit if:** you work at a Helmholtz centre.
- **Poor fit if:** you don't. Outsiders can only join as partners where the call allows.
