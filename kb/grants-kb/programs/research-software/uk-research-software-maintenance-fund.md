# Summary

The RSMF is the UK's dedicated fund for **maintaining existing research software**. UKRI's Digital Research Infrastructure programme pays for it, and the Software Sustainability Institute runs it. The Institute was renamed the **Institute for Research Software** on September 1, 2026.[^ukri-rsmf-r2][^irs-rename] Round 1 (EOIs due May 30, 2025) funded 13 projects for nearly £3M in December 2025: four Large awards of up to £500K over two years and nine Small awards of up to £150K over one year.[^ukri-rsmf-r1][^ssi-rsmf-r1-awards] Round 2 (EOIs due February 25, 2026; 229 EOIs received) funded 19 projects of up to £150K each, about £2.73M in total, announced August 26, 2026.[^ssi-rsmf-r2-awards] The overall programme is described as a £5.8M UKRI investment. **No third round has been announced** as of October 2026.[^ssi-rsmf-r2-awards]

# Eligibility

- The lead applicant must be at a UKRI-eligible UK research organization. International collaborators are allowed.[^ukri-rsmf-r1]
- The software must already exist and currently be used to enable UK research. New software development is excluded.[^ukri-rsmf-r2]

# What it funds

- Maintenance, refactoring, porting (e.g. GPU), dependency updates, contributor onboarding, and governance for software in active research use.[^ssi-rsmf-r2-awards]

# How to apply

- Two stages: a short expression of interest, then an invited full proposal. Contact grants@software.ac.uk.[^ukri-rsmf-r1]

# Track record

- Round 2 included MDAnalysis, OpenFOAM GPU refactoring, the Isca climate framework and FLAME GPU.[^ssi-rsmf-r2-awards] Round 1 Large awards included Palaeoverse (≈£480K over 2 years).[^ropensci-palaeoverse]

# Fit

- **Good fit if:** you lead or co-lead widely used research software from a UK institution.
- **Poor fit if:** you want to build something new, or no UK organization can lead.
