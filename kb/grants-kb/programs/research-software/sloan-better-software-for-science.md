# Summary

Better Software for Science was a Sloan Technology-program line. Its aim was to develop the practices, norms and institutions that promote development and adoption of discovery-enhancing software, adapting software-engineering practice from industry to academic workflows and incentives.[^sloan-bss] Like its successor, it funded tooling, institutions, economic models and incentives rather than individual projects.[^sloan-bss] Sloan's program page now says **new proposals are no longer accepted**. Applicants should use the **Open Source in Science** line instead.[^sloan-bss][^sloan-ossci]
