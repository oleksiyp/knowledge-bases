# Summary

The **Open Source for Science Fund** launched on May 4, 2026. It is a multi-donor fund hosted by Renaissance Philanthropy (director: Dario Taraborelli) with **$20M** anchor funding from **Biohub** and **Wellcome**, plus support from the Kavli Foundation and the Research Software Alliance.[^rp-os4s] It was designed by the former CZI EOSS team and is that program's practical successor.[^rp-os4s] Its first call, **Open Source for the Life Sciences (OS4LS)**, funds mature, adopted open-source software for life-science research, with particular attention to AI readiness and interoperability.[^os4ls] The 2026 round is closed: LOIs were due June 8 and full proposals July 21. Decisions are expected in October 2026, with projects starting from December 1, 2026.[^os4ls]

# Eligibility

- Open-licensed software with a public repository and demonstrated adoption in life-science research.[^os4ls]
- The PI must be a core maintainer or a designated project representative, with buy-in from the maintainer community. The proposal must align with the project roadmap.[^os4ls]
- Grants go to organizations, not individuals. Independent projects need a fiscal sponsor such as NumFOCUS or Code for Science & Society.[^os4ls]
- Universities, non-profits, for-profits, labs and government units are eligible. International applicants are welcome subject to U.S. compliance (OFAC, export controls, FCPA).[^os4ls]

# What it funds

- **Track 1 — Domain-specific tools:** representing, analyzing and visualizing biological data within a discipline.[^os4ls]
- **Track 2 — Foundational libraries and ecosystem initiatives:** core dependencies across life-science domains, or collaborative cross-project efforts.[^os4ls]
- Technical advances and fixes for community bottlenecks, including readiness for AI-native research.[^os4s-launch]

# Amounts & terms

| Track | Max | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $250,000 ($125K/yr) | 2 years |
| 2 | $1,000,000 ($500K/yr) | 2 years |

No fixed number of awards.[^os4ls]

# How to apply

- Two stages: an LOI through the portal, then an invited full application.[^os4ls]

# Track record

- First awards are due in October 2026. The predecessor EOSS program deployed $58M across 230+ projects.[^rp-os4s]

# Fit

- **Good fit if:** you maintain a widely used life-science library (Python, R or Julia bioinformatics, imaging, single-cell) under an open license and have a fiscal host.
- **Poor fit if:** your project is new or has little adoption, or is outside the life sciences (future calls may widen scope; unverified).
