# Summary

The Open eScience Call supports research that depends on **developing and applying advanced research software** to solve an urgent methodological challenge with broad community support.[^esc-oec-2026] Funding is **in-kind**: up to **3 person-years** of eScience Center research software engineers, over 3–3.5 years.[^esc-oec-2026] OEC 2026 funds 8 projects, two in each of four research areas. Project propositions closed March 30, 2026 and full proposals June 18, 2026 (extended). Decisions are due in November 2026.[^esc-oec-2026] The call runs every year (OEC 2025 before it).[^esc-oec-2025]

# Eligibility

- The lead applicant holds a PhD and works at a Dutch research-performing organization, with a contract covering the project. At least one co-applicant must come from a different Dutch institution.[^esc-oec-2026]
- The lead must commit at least half a day per week on average.[^esc-oec-2026]

# What it funds

- Joint research projects in which eScience Center RSEs build the software. The research idea counts for 25% of the score.[^esc-oec-2026]

# Amounts & terms

- In-kind RSE time only; no cash budget stated.[^esc-oec-2026]

# How to apply

- A lightweight project proposition first, then a full proposal, submitted through ConfTool.[^esc-oec-2026]
