# Summary

The Fellowship Programme of the Software Sustainability Institute, renamed the Institute for Research Software in September 2026, selects about **20 Fellows a year**, including up to three international Fellows.[^ssi-fellows-2027][^irs-rename] Each Fellow gets a **£4,000** budget to organize or attend activities that promote good software practice in research, plus mentoring, cohort learning, monthly community calls and the Collaborations Workshop.[^ssi-fellows-2027] The 2027 round opened August 10, 2026 and closes **October 5, 2026**. Selection Day is November 12, 2026.[^ssi-fellows-2027]

# Eligibility

- Any career stage and background: PhD students, RSEs, professors, research computing directors, and people without formal software backgrounds who support research.[^ssi-fellows-2027]
- International applicants must show how their plans build international collaboration or benefit UK research culture.[^ssi-fellows-2027]

# What it funds

- Workshops, training, travel to events, community building around research software practice.[^ssi-fellows-2027]
- It does not pay salary or software development.

# Amounts & terms

- £4,000 per Fellow. Tenure January 1, 2027 – March 31, 2028.[^ssi-fellows-2027]

# How to apply

- Application form plus a **six-minute screencast video**. Shortlisted candidates attend an all-day online Selection Day with group activities.[^ssi-fellows-2027]
