# Summary

The Digital Research Alliance of Canada's RSMF pays to keep research software that Canadian researchers rely on **reliable, secure and sustainable**.[^alliance-rsmf] It has two streams: **Small Maintenance Grants** (up to CA$150K over 12 months, focused on specific modules or components) and **Large Sustaining Grants** (up to CA$500K over 24 months, platform-level work).[^guelph-rsmf] The 2026 competition has two phases. Phase 1 LOIs closed **June 26, 2026**, and Phase 2 full proposals are by invitation.[^alliance-rsmf-portal][^guelph-rsmf] The Research Software Alliance (ReSA) listed it among 2026 funding opportunities.[^resa-may-2026]

# Eligibility

- The lead applicant must be at an eligible Canadian research organization. International collaborators may participate.[^guelph-rsmf]
- The software must already exist and be in active use by external users beyond the originating team.[^guelph-rsmf]
- The team needs at least one full-time developer with code-quality expertise. Open licensing is expected unless justified, plus a defined IP and governance model.[^guelph-rsmf]
- An institutional Letter of Certification is required.[^guelph-rsmf]

# What it funds

- Maintenance, security, reliability, platform modernization and sustainability work.[^alliance-rsmf]

# Amounts & terms

- See summary. Amounts are the Alliance contribution.[^guelph-rsmf]

# How to apply

- Phase 1 LOI through the Alliance portal (software overview, activities, benefits, team, budget), then an invited Phase 2.[^guelph-rsmf]
