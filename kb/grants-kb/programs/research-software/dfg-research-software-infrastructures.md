# Summary

DFG launched *Forschungssoftwareinfrastrukturen* in 2024 as a **permanent** programme in its scientific library and information systems (LIS) area.[^dfg-ifw-24-41][^dfg-fsi] It funds developing, establishing or organizing **infrastructures for research software**, meaning networked technical and organizational services for creating, processing, accessing and maintaining research software. Projects can work at a technical level (infrastructure software and services), an organizational level, or an individual level (skills of researchers, RSEs and infrastructure staff).[^dfg-fsi] The first deadline was November 4, 2024. Since 2025 there are **two submission dates a year, in March and August**.[^tub-fsi][^kwd-fsi] Exact 2027 dates were not verified.

# Eligibility

- Researchers, and staff at scientific information infrastructures (libraries, archives, museums, research data and computing centers). Joint applications by infrastructure providers and scientific users are encouraged.[^dfg-fsi-faq]
- NFDI or EOSC participants may apply if there is no double funding.[^dfg-fsi-faq]

# What it funds

- Prototype, establishment or consolidation phases of research-software infrastructure services, such as software registries, CI/testing services, publication and archiving, and RSE training.[^dfg-fsi]
- Not: individual research software as such. The focus is infrastructure.[^dfg-fsi]

# Amounts & terms

- **No minimum or maximum** amount.[^dfg-fsi-faq]
- Required own contribution: **10%** (phase 1 prototype), **20%** (phase 2 establishment), **30%** (phase 3 consolidation).[^dfg-fsi-faq]
- Results must be released under open licenses.[^dfg-fsi-faq]
- Up to 3 years in the first cycle.[^kwd-fsi]

# How to apply

- Through the DFG elan portal by a March or August submission date, following form 12.22 (Merkblatt).[^kwd-fsi]

# Fit

- **Good fit if:** you are a German library, computing center or research group building shared research-software services.
- **Poor fit if:** you want money for a single tool's features, or you are outside Germany.
