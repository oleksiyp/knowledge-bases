# Summary

CICI funds cybersecurity work that serves scientific cyberinfrastructure: usable security in science workflows, reference security datasets, moving security innovations into production cyberinfrastructure, and integrity/provenance for AI-ready data.[^nsf-25-531] The program expects 12–20 awards totaling $8–12M, and NSF lists it as accepting proposals. The deadline is the third Wednesday of each January; the next one is **January 20, 2027**.[^nsf-25-531]

# Eligibility

- U.S. institutions of higher education and non-profit research organizations.[^nsf-25-531]
- An individual may appear on at most two CICI proposals as PI, co-PI or senior/key personnel.[^nsf-25-531]

# What it funds

- **UCSS** Usable and Collaborative Security for Science: up to $600K, 3–6 awards.[^nsf-25-531]
- **RSSD** Reference Scientific Security Datasets: up to $600K, 2–5 awards.[^nsf-25-531]
- **TCR** Transition to Cyberinfrastructure Resilience: up to $1.2M, 2–4 awards.[^nsf-25-531]
- **IPAAI** Integrity, Provenance and Authenticity for AI-Ready Data: up to $900K, 4–6 awards.[^nsf-25-531]

# Amounts & terms

All areas allow up to 3 years.[^nsf-25-531]

# How to apply

Full proposals through Research.gov by the January deadline.[^nsf-25-531]
