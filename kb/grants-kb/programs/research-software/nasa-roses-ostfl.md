# Summary

Through its Open-Source Science Initiative, NASA's Science Mission Directorate (SMD) created several ROSES program elements that paid for research software. **F.7 OSTFL** funded improvement and sustainment of existing high-value open-source tools, frameworks and libraries. F.14 High Priority Open-Source Science (HPOSS) paid about $100K for one-year projects. F.8 Supplements for Open-Source Science (SOSS) added money to existing awards. Transform to Open Science (TOPS) training grants also ran under this initiative.[^nasa-os-funding] As of October 2026 NASA lists **all of them as not currently solicited or no longer solicited**. OSTFL was last offered in ROSES-24 (proposals due June 7, 2024; awards announced October 24, 2024).[^nasa-os-funding][^roses24-f7] NASA planned to release ROSES-2026 in July 2026, but it was still "Pending" in NSPIRES at the end of August 2026, so any return of OSTFL is unknown.[^roses26-july][^granted-roses26]

# Eligibility

- Standard ROSES eligibility rules apply. Element-specific eligibility for any 2026+ reissue is unverified, so check the element text when it returns.

# What it funds

- **Foundational awards** (cooperative agreements, up to 5 years) for open-source tools, frameworks and libraries with significant impact on two or more SMD divisions.[^roses24-f7]
- **Sustainment awards** (up to 3 years) for tools with significant impact on one or more SMD divisions.[^roses24-f7]
- Typical work: extensions, documentation, infrastructure, security and maintenance.[^roses24-f7]

# Amounts & terms

- The ROSES-24 amendment did not publish award sizes or total budget.[^roses24-f7] Foundational proposals needed an Inclusion Plan in 2024. Post-2025 federal policy may change that.[^roses24-f7]

# How to apply

- Through NSPIRES once ROSES-2026 or later lists the element. Contact: HQ-SMD-CSDO-ROSES@mail.nasa.gov.[^roses24-f7]

# Track record

- ROSES-24 OSTFL selections were announced October 24, 2024. TOPS-T (ROSES-22) committed $6.5M over 3 years.[^nasa-os-funding]

# Fit

- **Good fit if (when it returns):** you maintain a library such as an astronomy, heliophysics or Earth-science package that several NASA science divisions depend on.
- **Poor fit now:** nothing is open. Monitor ROSES-2026 amendments.
