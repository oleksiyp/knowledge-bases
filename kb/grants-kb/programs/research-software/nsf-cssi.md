# Summary

CSSI, run by the Office of Advanced Cyberinfrastructure (OAC) in CISE with co-funding from many directorates, has been NSF's main route for funding research software as research infrastructure. Applicants are labs and university research software teams.[^nsf-22-632] It has three award classes: **Elements** (small groups), **Framework Implementations** (larger interdisciplinary teams), and **Transition to Sustainability** (helping existing NSF-funded cyberinfrastructure move to a sustainable model).[^nsf-cssi-page] The last deadline under NSF 22-632 was **December 1, 2025**, with a program webinar on November 18, 2025.[^nsf-22-632][^nsf-cssi-webinar-2025] As of October 2026 NSF lists the program as "awaiting a new solicitation" and **not accepting proposals**, so no December 2026 round is confirmed.[^nsf-cssi-page]

# Eligibility

- U.S. institutions of higher education, U.S. non-profit non-academic organizations, and NSF-sponsored FFRDCs.[^nsf-22-632]
- Each person may be PI or co-PI on only **one** CSSI proposal per deadline, across all classes. Extra proposals are returned without review.[^nsf-22-632]
- NSF strongly recommends contacting the program officer in the relevant division before you submit.[^nsf-cssi-page]

# What it funds

- Elements: robust software or data services for demonstrated science and engineering needs.[^nsf-cssi-page]
- Frameworks: sustainable community frameworks that solve common research problems.[^nsf-cssi-page]
- Transition to Sustainability: carrying out a sustainability plan for existing cyberinfrastructure with demonstrated NSF-supported impact.[^nsf-cssi-page]
- NSF intends support for any one Element or Framework line to be finite, at most 10 years.[^nsf-cssi-page]

# Amounts & terms

| Class | Size | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Elements | up to $600,000 (≤$200K/yr) | 3 years |
| Framework Implementations | $600,001–$5,000,000 ($200K–$1M/yr) | 3–5 years |
| Transition to Sustainability | up to $1,000,000 (≤$500K/yr) | 2 years |

Under NSF 22-632 the program anticipated about $34M a year for roughly 35 awards: up to 20 Elements, 10 Frameworks and 5 Sustainability awards.[^nsf-22-632]

# How to apply

- Full proposals through Research.gov. Watch the CSSI page for the replacement solicitation, which may change classes, sizes or eligibility.[^nsf-cssi-page]

# Track record

- CSSI has run since NSF 18-531/19-548 and replaced the earlier SI2 program line. Per-year award counts were not checked for this entry.[^nsf-cssi-page]

# Fit

- **Good fit if:** you are a U.S. academic team with a research software or data service that several science communities use and you can show NSF-relevant impact.
- **Poor fit if:** you are a company, an individual or non-U.S.; or you need money before the new solicitation is published.
