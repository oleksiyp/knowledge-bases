# Summary

rOpenSci's Champions Program trains and mentors people who develop **R research software** and grow open-science communities. It runs for 12 months with 13 two-hour workshops, one-to-one mentoring, community building and a research software project, and pays a **participation stipend** on completion.[^ropensci-champs-2026] The 2026–27 cohort is run **entirely in Spanish with a Latin America focus**. Applications closed **February 20, 2026**.[^ropensci-champs-2026] The program received 81 Champion and 14 Mentor applications from 23 countries.[^ropensci-news-feb-2026]

# Eligibility

- 2026: from or working in Latin America, fluent in Spanish, an R user with experience in software development, reproducible analysis or open science, and willing to follow the rOpenSci Code of Conduct.[^ropensci-champs-2026]

# What it funds

- The participant's time (stipend), training and mentoring for building or improving an R package.[^ropensci-champs-2026]

# Amounts & terms

- Stipend amount not disclosed in the 2026 call.[^ropensci-champs-2026]

# How to apply

- Online application, with a community call and an application clinic beforehand.[^ropensci-champs-2026]
