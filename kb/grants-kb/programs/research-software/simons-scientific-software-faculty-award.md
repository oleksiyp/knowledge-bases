# Summary

This award addresses a career problem: people who build and maintain scientific software rarely get permanent faculty jobs. The Simons Foundation pays for a **new, non-term-limited research or clinical professor (or professor of practice) position** at a U.S. PhD-granting institution, held by someone who has led development or maintenance of scientific software.[^simons-ssrfa] Funding covers 100% of salary in years 1–2 and 50% in years 3–5 (the institution pays the rest), plus a **$50,000 a year research allowance**. Up to six awards are made per cycle.[^simons-ssrfa] The 2026–27 LOI deadline was **January 21, 2026**, and awards start by September 1, 2027.[^simons-ssrfa]

# Eligibility

- PhD in mathematics, astronomy or theoretical physics (related fields if mathematically sophisticated), and a leading role developing or maintaining scientific software.[^simons-ssrfa]
- The institution creates a new PI-status position that is not term-limited, with at most 50% teaching or computational support duties. U.S. PhD-granting institutions only. No citizenship requirement.[^simons-ssrfa]

# What it funds

- Salary for the position, plus a research allowance usable for staff or postdocs, travel, equipment, visitors and workshops.[^simons-ssrfa]

# Amounts & terms

- See summary. Indirect costs up to 20% of modified total direct costs. Potentially renewable.[^simons-ssrfa]

# How to apply

- Institution-backed LOI. If invited, a full proposal is due three months before the start date (by June 1, 2027 for this cycle).[^simons-ssrfa]
