# Summary

OSCARS (Open Science Clusters' Action for Research and Society) connects ESFRI research infrastructures organized into five Science Clusters (environment, life science, astronomy and particle physics, photon and neutron science, social sciences and humanities).[^oscars-calls] It ran **two cascading-grant open calls worth about €16M in total**: March 15 – May 15, 2024 and March 14 – May 14, 2025. Each offered **€100K–€250K lump sums** for 12–24 month open-science projects and services, many of them software.[^oscars-calls] OSCARS states there are **no more upcoming calls**. Funded projects run until mid-2027.[^oscars-calls]

# Eligibility

- Closed. Past eligibility details are in the call announcements (not re-verified here).[^oscars-2nd]

# What it funds

- Historical: new or improved open-science services, tools and data/software pipelines aligned with research infrastructures and EOSC.[^oscars-2nd]

# Amounts & terms

- €100K–€250K lump sums.[^oscars-calls]
