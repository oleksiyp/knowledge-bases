# Summary

Schmidt Sciences' Science of Trustworthy AI program funds basic technical research to "understand, predict, and control risks from frontier AI systems". It has three aims: characterizing misalignment, generalizable measurement and intervention, and overseeing superhuman-capability and multi-agent systems.[^ss-tai][^ss-tai-agenda] The 2026 RFP had two tiers. **Tier 1** gave up to $1M and **Tier 2** gave $1–5M or more, both over 1–3 years. Universities, research institutes and nonprofits worldwide could apply, and budgets could include GPU/CPU compute.[^ss-tai-granted] The deadline was **17 May 2026**.[^ss-tai-granted]

# Eligibility

- Universities, research institutions and nonprofits worldwide.[^ss-tai-granted]

# What it funds

- Empirical and theoretical safety science, including the research software and benchmarks it needs.[^ss-tai-agenda]

# Amounts & terms

- See tiers. Schmidt Sciences generally caps indirect costs (10% on its joint multi-agent call).[^ss-tai-granted]
