# Summary

The Survival and Flourishing Fund distributes philanthropic money, mainly from Jaan Tallinn, through its "S-Process", in which recommenders evaluate applications together. The 2026 round expected to distribute **$20–40M**: $14–28M in the Main Round (Main, Freedom and Fairness tracks) and $6–12M in Theme Rounds (Climate, Animal Welfare, Human Self-Enhancement).[^sff-2026] The Main Round deadline was **22 April 2026**, with results announced in September and payouts by the end of 2026.[^sff-2026][^sff-lw] Completing the rolling application also files a **Speculation Grant** request automatically, and over 95% of past applicants have received one.[^sff-2026] Applications submitted after a deadline count toward the next round.[^sff-lw]

# Eligibility

- Registered charities, fiscally sponsored independent researchers, and for-profits (grants or investments; US, UK, Canada and Australia preferred). International charities are eligible except in US-designated adversary nations.[^sff-2026]

# What it funds

- Mostly existential-risk reduction, especially AI safety research, tooling, policy and field-building.[^sff-2026]

# Amounts & terms

- See amount_text.[^sff-2026]

# How to apply

- Submit the rolling application plus any theme supplements. Speculation Grants must be approved within two weeks of a round deadline.[^sff-2026]
