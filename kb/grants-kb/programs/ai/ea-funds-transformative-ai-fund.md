# Summary

In August 2026 EA Funds **closed the Long-Term Future Fund (LTFF)**, a long-standing source of small AI-safety grants, and replaced it with the **Transformative AI Fund**, run by a new full-time team led by Lowe Lundin.[^ltff-closed][^taif-forum] The new fund makes early-stage, fast, failure-tolerant grants to individuals, new organizations and new projects. Its main focus is technical AI safety and AI governance, plus field-building and forecasting.[^taif-forum] Grants are typically **$10k–$150k** and rarely exceed $300k. Applications are always open.[^taif] It launched with a seed of about $1M and hopes to raise several times that.[^taif-forum]

# Eligibility

- Individuals, new organizations, and existing organizations with new projects.[^taif]

# What it funds

- Safety research, upskilling, tooling, field-building and forecasting. A small share goes to non-GCR transformative-AI topics such as digital sentience.[^taif-forum]
