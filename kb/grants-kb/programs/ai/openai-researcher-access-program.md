# Summary

The Researcher Access Program gives individual researchers and small teams up to **$1,000 in OpenAI API credits**. The credits are valid for 12 months and can be used on any publicly available model.[^rap-granted] It targets research on responsible deployment, AI safety and alignment, and the societal impact of AI. OpenAI especially encourages early-career researchers and those with limited resources in API-supported countries.[^rap-granted] Applications are reviewed quarterly, and credits can take up to four weeks to arrive after a decision.[^rap-granted][^rap-impactopen] It is small, but it is one of the few lab programs open to independent researchers.

# Eligibility

- Researchers (academic or independent) in countries where the OpenAI API is supported.[^rap-granted]
- Priority goes to early-stage researchers and those with limited financial or institutional resources.[^rap-granted]

# What it funds

- API credits for research that uses OpenAI models: evaluations, safety, alignment, social-science studies and similar work.[^rap-granted]
- No cash, and no access to unreleased models.

# Amounts & terms

- Up to $1,000 in credits, valid for 12 months.[^rap-granted]

# How to apply

- Submit the online form describing your research question and how you plan to use OpenAI products.[^rap-form][^rap-granted]

# Fit

- **Good fit if:** you run evals or social-science studies on OpenAI models on a small budget.
- **Poor fit if:** you need more than about $1k of compute. Look at [Coefficient Giving's RFPs](/programs/ai/coefficient-giving-technical-ai-safety-rfp.md) or [NAIRR](/programs/ai/nsf-nairr-pilot.md) instead.
