# Summary

The EuroHPC Joint Undertaking runs **19 AI Factories and 13 Antennas** across Europe. They give subsidised GPU supercomputer access, free of charge for research institutions, SMEs and startups using AI for innovation.[^ehpc-x][^ehpc-coaif] There are several access modes. **Playground** is for entry-level users and SMEs, with FIFO approval within about 2 working days for 1–3 months.[^ehpc-playground] **Fast Lane** gives SMEs and startups up to **50,000 GPU-hours** for 1–3 months, approved within about 4 working days.[^ehpc-fastlane] **Large Scale** is for projects above 50,000 GPU-hours, for up to a year. A separate track covers AI for Science and collaborative EU projects.[^ehpc-calls] For EU startups this is the largest no-equity, no-VC-required source of training compute.

# Eligibility

- Entities in EU Member States or EuroHPC participating states: SMEs, startups, industry, academia and the public sector, depending on the mode.[^ehpc-calls][^ehpc-playground]

# What it funds

- GPU-hours on a single EuroHPC system, plus expert support from the AI Factory that hosts you.[^ehpc-fastlane]

# Amounts & terms

- See amount_text. Free for SMEs and startups in Playground and Fast Lane.[^ehpc-x]

# How to apply

- Send a short proposal using the template through the EuroHPC access portal. Playground and Fast Lane check eligibility and technical fit on a first-come, first-served basis.[^ehpc-portal][^ehpc-playground]

# Deadlines

| Date | Event |

- **Poor fit if:** you are outside Europe or need managed cloud services.
