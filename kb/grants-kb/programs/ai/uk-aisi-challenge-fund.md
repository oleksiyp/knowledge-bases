# Summary

The UK AI Security Institute (AISI) launched its **£5M Challenge Fund** on 5 March 2025. It gives **£50k–£200k** grants for "pressing, unresolved questions in AI safety and security" across safeguards, control, alignment and societal resilience.[^aisi-grants][^aisi-cf-ukauthority] Researchers at eligible UK and international academic institutions and non-profits can apply.[^aisi-grants] The process has two stages, an EOI and then a full application, and payments are monthly in arrears against milestones.[^aisi-grants] As of October 2026 the AISI grants page lists the Challenge Fund and the earlier Systemic Safety Grants (20 projects under way) as **closed**.[^aisi-grants]

# Eligibility

- UK and international academic institutions and non-profits, subject to due diligence.[^aisi-grants]

# What it funds

- Technical mitigations, better evaluations and stronger safeguards.[^aisi-cf-ukauthority]

# Amounts & terms

- £50k–£200k, paid monthly in arrears against milestones.[^aisi-grants]

# How to apply

- EOI decision in 2–4 weeks, full application about 2 weeks, funding decision 4 weeks after that.[^aisi-grants]
