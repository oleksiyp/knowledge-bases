# Summary

AI2050 is a Schmidt Sciences initiative supporting "exceptional people working on key opportunities and hard problems" so that AI benefits society by 2050.[^ai2050-home] On 1 October 2026 it named its fifth cohort: **28 fellows (22 early-career, 6 senior)** receiving up to **$16M** in total over three years. That brings the program to 127 fellows and up to $80M committed.[^ai2050-2026] Senior fellows are chosen by closed nomination. Third-party listings report awards of $300k–$500k each.[^ai2050-aiss]

# Eligibility

- Early-career: within about 10 years of the PhD (postdocs and pre-tenure faculty). Senior: established researchers, by nomination.[^ai2050-aiss]

# What it funds

- The fellow's research agenda on AI's hard problems, from safety to scientific applications. Extra collaboration and compute funding are possible.[^ai2050-2026]
