# Summary

Cloudflare for Startups gives credits in three tiers. **Tier 3 ($10k)** is for bootstrapped startups that have raised under $1M. **Tier 2 ($100k)** is for companies with under $5M raised and an affiliated partner. **Tier 1 ($350k)** is for companies with $5M+ raised and partner backing.[^cf-startups] Workers AI, Cloudflare's serverless inference, has its own caps of $2.5k, $10k and $50k by tier. AI Gateway is temporarily not covered.[^cf-startups] Credits last one year, and review usually takes 48 hours.[^cf-startups] It is one of the few AI-inference credit programs open to bootstrapped founders.

# Eligibility

- For-profit tech startups founded within 10 years, funded up to Series B, with a live website, social presence and business email, applying for the first time.[^cf-startups]

# What it funds

- Cloudflare developer platform usage: Workers, Workers AI, R2 and more.[^cf-startups]

# Amounts & terms

- See the tiers above. Valid one year or until used.[^cf-startups]
