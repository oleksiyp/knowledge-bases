# Summary

The NVIDIA Graduate Fellowship pays **up to $60,000** for one award year through the student's university. It goes to PhD students whose research could lead to major advances in accelerated computing.[^nv-gf] Fellows must do a summer internship at an NVIDIA research office. International students are eligible.[^nv-gf] The 2027–2028 cycle opened on 30 September 2026 and closes on **30 October 2026**.[^nv-gf]

# Eligibility

- PhD students who have finished their first year, in CS, CE, systems architecture, EE or related fields, and are doing thesis research.[^nv-gf]
- Must be full time for the nine-month award year and available for the internship (US, Canada, Germany, Sweden, Finland, Switzerland, Israel, Taiwan or Singapore).[^nv-gf]

# What it funds

- Stipend, tuition and mandatory fees.[^nv-gf]

# Amounts & terms

- Up to $60k for one year.[^nv-gf]

# How to apply

- Submit a 2-page research proposal, a CV, 2–3 recommendation letters (at least one from your advisor) and internship availability.[^nv-gf]
