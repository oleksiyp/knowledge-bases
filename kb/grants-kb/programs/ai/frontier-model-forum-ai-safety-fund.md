# Summary

The AI Safety Fund is a collaborative fund of **$10M+** set up in 2023 by Frontier Model Forum members (Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, OpenAI) and philanthropic partners. It pays for independent research on frontier-model safety.[^fmf-first] Its RFPs covered biosecurity, cybersecurity, and AI agent evaluation and synthetic content. In **December 2025, 11 grantees received more than $5M**, chosen from 100+ proposals.[^fmf-grantees] The Forum now runs the fund directly and says the remaining money will support "narrowly-scoped research projects that target urgent bottlenecks". No open call is published.[^fmf-grantees]

# Eligibility

- Independent researchers and institutions (past RFPs).[^fmf-first]

# What it funds

- Safety evaluations, red-teaming, and agent monitoring and oversight.[^fmf-grantees]
