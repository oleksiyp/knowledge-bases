# Summary

Foresight Institute's AI for Science & Safety Nodes program combines **grants, compute and in-person hub space** in San Francisco and Berlin, with a community xNode in Cape Town.[^foresight-rfp] The RFP has three areas: **Local Compute** (community-owned compute infrastructure), **Coordination and Accountability**, and **AI-first Science** in bio, neuro and nano.[^foresight-rfp] Grants are typically **$30k–$100k** and larger awards are possible. **All work products must be open-sourced**, and overhead is capped at 10%.[^foresight-rfp] The current deadline is **31 October 2026**, with decisions about three months later.[^foresight-rfp]

# Eligibility

- Individuals, teams, nonprofits and for-profits (for-profits must justify why they need a grant).[^foresight-rfp]
- Priority to applicants who will contribute in person at the SF or Berlin nodes.[^foresight-rfp]

# What it funds

- AI-first projects that use AI, not big team budgets, as the main engine of progress.[^foresight-rfp]

# Amounts & terms

- See amount_text. Mandatory open-source release of code, data and outputs.[^foresight-rfp]

# How to apply

- Use the online application on the RFP page.[^foresight-rfp]

- Foresight has run AI safety grants since 2024 (about $4.5–5.5M a year reported).[^foresight-opdesk]

# Fit

- **Good fit if:** you build open-source AI tooling for decentralized compute, verification or science, and can join a node.
- **Poor fit if:** your code must stay proprietary.
