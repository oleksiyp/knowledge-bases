# Summary

Meta's Llama Impact Grants funded organizations using the open-weights Llama models to tackle social and economic problems. The global program received over 800 applications from 90+ countries. Its second round gave **more than $1.5M to 10 recipients**, and the last global call closed on 22 November 2024.[^llama-granted][^llama-31-call] Meta later ran regional editions. One was a **$20,000** grant for Sub-Saharan African startups and researchers with Data Science Africa, which had an April 2025 deadline.[^llama-africa] No 2026 round could be found, so the program is listed as discontinued. Meta's grant page now redirects to developer.meta.com.

# Eligibility

- Organizations deploying Llama-based solutions; regional editions add geographic limits.[^llama-31-call][^llama-africa]

# What it funds

- Building and scaling Llama-based applications in areas such as education, health and agriculture.[^llama-africa]
