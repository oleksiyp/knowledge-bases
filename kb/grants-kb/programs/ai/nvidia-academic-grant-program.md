# Summary

NVIDIA's Academic Grant Program gives university faculty cloud, hardware and software grants, typically **up to 30,000 H100 GPU-hours** or desk-side hardware. Grantees also get model access, letters of support and possible GTC speaking slots.[^nv-agp] There are three tracks: Simulation & Modeling, AI Training & Model Development, and AI Inference, Agents & Systems Software.[^nv-agp] As of October 2026 NVIDIA says the program is **not accepting new applications**.[^nv-agp]

# Eligibility

- Full-time faculty at accredited institutions that award PhDs.[^nv-agp]
- Projects should use NVIDIA models from ai.nvidia.com or NVIDIA software extensively.[^nv-agp]

# What it funds

- GPU-hours or hardware for research.[^nv-agp]

# Amounts & terms

- See amount_text. Proposals must follow NVIDIA's mandatory template.[^nv-agp]

# How to apply

- Paused. NVIDIA offers a proposal template, a "Level Up Your Proposal" webinar and an example proposal for when calls reopen.[^nv-agp]
