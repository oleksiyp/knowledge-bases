# AI software, compute & AI-safety grants

* [Anthropic AI for Science (Claude API credits for researchers)](anthropic-ai-for-science.md) - Up to $20,000 in Claude API credits over 6 months for academic and nonprofit researchers on high-impact science, especially biology and life sciences. Reviewed monthly; alongside a free Claude Team plan for scientists.
* [Anthropic Claude for Open Source (and Project Glasswing maintainer access)](anthropic-claude-for-open-source.md) - Six months of free Claude Max 20x (about $1,200 value) for qualifying open-source maintainers and contributors, capped at 10,000 recipients. Since April 2026 it is also the route for maintainers into Project Glasswing / Claude Mythos Preview access. Rolling.
* [Anthropic Claude for Startups](anthropic-claude-for-startups.md) - Claude API credits plus top-tier rate limits for young startups with institutional equity funding. Amounts are not published by Anthropic; third parties report $5k direct up to $25k–$100k via VC partners. Rolling, about 2-minute application.
* [Anthropic Economic Futures Program — Research Awards](anthropic-economic-futures-program.md) - Rolling research awards of up to $50,000 (plus API credits) for empirical research on AI's economic impacts. Not software funding as such, but relevant to data and tooling projects. Launched June 2025.
* [Anthropic Fellows Program](anthropic-fellows-program.md) - Four-month, full-time, mentored AI safety / security / ML-systems / economics-policy research fellowship. Pays about $3,850/week plus about $15k/month in compute. Rolling; the next deadline is 18 October 2026 for the January 2027 cohort.
* [ARIA Safeguarded AI — Cybersecurity rolling call (Blue Teams)](aria-safeguarded-ai.md) - UK ARIA's £59M Safeguarded AI programme runs a £20M rolling call for 3–6 Blue Teams (about £2.5–3.5M each) building production-grade, security-critical software whose key properties are backed by machine-checked proofs, using AI-assisted formal methods. Next cut-off 31 Oct 2026.
* [AWS Cloud Credit for Research](aws-cloud-credit-for-research.md) - AWS promotional credits for faculty, research staff and graduate students at accredited research institutions. Students are capped at $5,000; faculty and staff awards are uncapped. Rolling with 30–60 day reviews; Free Tier accounts have been ineligible since Feb 2026.
* [AWS Generative AI Accelerator](aws-generative-ai-accelerator.md) - Equity-free, eight-week hybrid accelerator for about 40 late-seed to Series A generative-AI startups. Up to $1M in AWS credits each, ending at re:Invent. The 2026 application window was reportedly 10 June–10 July.
* [Cloudflare for Startups (incl. Workers AI credits)](cloudflare-for-startups.md) - Cloudflare credits of $10k (bootstrapped), $100k or $350k (partner-backed), with Workers AI sub-caps of $2.5k–$50k. For-profit startups up to Series B; about 48-hour review; rolling.
* [Coefficient Giving — Career Development & Transition Funding (and AI safety capacity building)](coefficient-giving-career-development-transition.md) - Rolling grants for individuals building career capital toward reducing catastrophic AI or bio risk (courses, independent study, graduate study, transition periods). About six-week decisions. Coefficient's AI-safety capacity-building program is also open on a rolling basis.
* [Coefficient Giving (formerly Open Philanthropy) — Technical AI Safety Research RFP](coefficient-giving-technical-ai-safety-rfp.md) - The largest non-governmental open call for technical AI safety. About $40M across 21 research areas, funding everything from API and compute credits to 6–24-month projects and new research orgs, starting with a 300-word EOI. The 2026 RFP closed on 21 Aug 2026.
* [Cooperative AI Foundation — Research Grants & PhD Fellowships](cooperative-ai-foundation-grants.md) - Grants for research that improves the cooperative intelligence of advanced AI, plus PhD fellowships. Backed by a $15M commitment. The general research-grant call closed Jan 2025 and the PhD fellowship Nov 2025; CAIF co-runs the $10M multi-agent safety fund (closed Aug 2026).
* [EA Funds Transformative AI Fund (successor to the Long-Term Future Fund)](ea-funds-transformative-ai-fund.md) - Fast, early-stage grants (typically $10k–$150k, rarely over $300k) to individuals and new projects in technical AI safety, AI governance and field-building. Launched August 2026 to replace the closed Long-Term Future Fund; always open.
* [EuroHPC AI Factories Access (Playground, Fast Lane, Large Scale)](eurohpc-ai-factories-access.md) - Publicly funded EU supercomputer GPU time through 19 AI Factories, free of charge for research institutions, SMEs and startups. Playground (entry level, about 2-day approval), Fast Lane (up to 50,000 GPU-hours for 1–3 months, about 4-day approval) and Large Scale (above 50k GPU-hours, up to 1 year). Permanent rolling calls.
* [Foresight Institute — AI for Science & Safety Nodes grants](foresight-ai-safety-nodes.md) - Grants (typically $30k–$100k) plus compute and hub space in San Francisco and Berlin for open-source, AI-first projects in local compute, coordination and accountability, and AI-first bio/neuro/nano science. All outputs must be open-sourced. Deadline 31 Oct 2026.
* [Frontier Model Forum AI Safety Fund (AISF)](frontier-model-forum-ai-safety-fund.md) - A $10M+ fund from Anthropic, Google, Microsoft and OpenAI plus philanthropies for independent research on frontier-AI safety (biosecurity, cybersecurity, agent evaluation, synthetic content). Over $5M went to 11 grantees in Dec 2025. Not accepting open applications; remaining funds go to narrowly scoped projects.
* [Google for Startups Cloud Program (AI tier)](google-for-startups-cloud-ai.md) - Google Cloud credits for startups, usable on Gemini and Vertex AI. Start tier up to $2k, Scale up to $200k, and up to $350k for AI-first startups with institutional funding. Rolling.
* [Google Gemini Academic Program](google-gemini-academic-program.md) - Gemini API credits and higher rate limits for faculty, staff and PhD students at academic institutions in supported countries. Reviewed monthly.
* [Google.org AI Impact Challenges & Generative AI Accelerator](google-org-ai-impact-challenges.md) - Google.org open calls of $30M each, paying $0.5M–$3M per organization plus a technical accelerator, for nonprofits, academics and social enterprises building AI for science or government services. Permissive open-source licensing applies to funded IP. The 2026 calls closed in April.
* [Google TPU Research Cloud (TRC)](google-tpu-research-cloud.md) - Free Cloud TPU quota for researchers who agree to share results openly (papers, open-source code, blog posts). Rolling invitations; other GCP costs are not covered.
* [Hugging Face Community GPU Grants & ZeroGPU](hugging-face-community-gpu-grants.md) - Free GPU hardware upgrades for Hugging Face Spaces that show community value (academic demos, open-source projects), requested from Space settings. ZeroGPU gives free shared RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPUs to Gradio Spaces. Rolling, no deadline.
* [Lambda Research Grant](lambda-research-grant.md) - Up to $5,000 in Lambda GPU cloud credits (B200/H100/A100) plus mentoring for AI/ML researchers. Expanded at CVPR 2026 to cover whole research groups. Rolling application.
* [Manifund AI Safety Regranting](manifund-ai-safety-regranting.md) - Fast, small grants ($5k–$50k typical) to early-stage AI-safety projects and individuals, decided by independent regrantors with their own budgets. 2026 target is $2M–$5M across 10–35 regrantors. Projects post publicly on Manifund; rolling.
* [Meta Llama Impact Grants](meta-llama-impact-grants.md) - Meta grants for projects using Llama for social and economic impact (global rounds 2023–24, then regional editions such as a $20k Sub-Saharan Africa grant with Data Science Africa). The global program has been closed since Nov 2024; no open round as of Oct 2026.
* [Microsoft Accelerating Foundation Models Research (AFMR) → Agentic AI Research and Innovation (AARI)](microsoft-afmr-aari.md) - AFMR gave academics Azure AI model access and credits. It has concluded, and its pages are kept as an archive. The successor AARI works with invited academic partners and Grand Challenges; it has no open application.
* [Microsoft AI for Good Lab Open Call (Washington State)](microsoft-ai-for-good-lab-open-call.md) - Two-year program with $5M in total Azure credits plus scientific collaboration with Microsoft's AI for Good Lab, for Washington State projects in sustainability, public health and human rights. Applications closed; awardees announced after 20 March.
* [Microsoft for Startups (Founders Hub) — Azure & AI credits](microsoft-for-startups.md) - Up to $150,000 in Azure credits (Azure OpenAI and Foundry models included) for startups, released in tiers. Most applicants start at $1k–$5k; the larger tiers go to investor-backed companies. Rolling.
* [Mozilla Foundation Incubator — Democracy x AI Cohort](mozilla-democracy-x-ai.md) - $50,000 grants for 10 teams with working AI tools that strengthen democratic institutions, with up to $250k more for 2 teams in a Sustain Track. At least partial open source is required. The 2026 call closed on 16 March 2026.
* [Scaling AI Safety for a Multi-Agent World (Schmidt Sciences, Google DeepMind, ARIA, Cooperative AI Foundation, Google.org)](multi-agent-ai-safety-fund.md) - A $10M joint fund for research on risks from interacting AI agents (sandboxes, agent-network science, agent infrastructure, multi-agent oversight). Tier 1 up to $300k; Tier 2 $300k–$1M over 1–2 years. Deadline was 8/9 Aug 2026; notifications due autumn 2026.
* [NSF National AI Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot — resource allocations](nsf-nairr-pilot.md) - Free AI compute, models and datasets for US researchers and educators (including startups with federal grants) through rolling Start-Up Project requests (up to about 2,000 GPU-hours for 3 months) and larger research allocations. An Operations Center was awarded on 1 Sep 2026, and existing opportunities continue.
* [NVIDIA Academic Grant Program](nvidia-academic-grant-program.md) - Compute grants for PhD-granting-institution faculty (up to 30,000 H100 GPU-hours, RTX PRO 6000 GPUs or DGX Spark systems) in generative AI, simulation, and inference/agents/systems software. Paused; not accepting applications as of Oct 2026.
* [NVIDIA Graduate Fellowship](nvidia-graduate-fellowship.md) - Up to $60,000 for PhD students (after their first year) in accelerated computing, AI, systems and related fields, with a required summer internship at NVIDIA. 2027–28 applications are due 30 October 2026.
* [OpenAI Codex for Open Source](openai-codex-for-open-source.md) - Gives core maintainers of widely used public projects six months of ChatGPT Pro with Codex, API credits from the $1M Codex Open Source Fund, and conditional Codex Security access. Launched March 2026; applications are rolling.
* [OpenAI Cybersecurity Grant Program](openai-cybersecurity-grant-program.md) - OpenAI grants, mostly API credits, for defensive-security teams using frontier models on open-source and critical-infrastructure security. Re-scoped in February 2026 around a $10M API-credit pool for teams with a vulnerability-remediation track record; applications are rolling.
* [OpenAI Daybreak for Frontline Defenders (incl. Patch the Planet)](openai-daybreak-frontline-defenders.md) - OpenAI's $1B pledge (Sep 2026) of subsidised Daybreak cyber-AI access, training and support for under-resourced defenders, open-source maintainers among them. Expected to be used over about six months; expressions of interest are open.
* [OpenAI Foundation People-First AI Fund](openai-people-first-ai-fund.md) - Unrestricted grants from the OpenAI Foundation to US community nonprofits exploring AI ($40.5M to 208 orgs in Dec 2025; $50M to 163 orgs in 2026). Not a software-development grant. No open round as of Oct 2026.
* [OpenAI Researcher Access Program](openai-researcher-access-program.md) - Up to $1,000 in OpenAI API credits, valid 12 months, for researchers studying responsible deployment, safety, alignment and societal impact of AI. Applications are reviewed quarterly (Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec).
* [Schmidt Sciences AI2050 Fellowships](schmidt-sciences-ai2050.md) - Three-year fellowships ($300k–$500k reported) for early-career and senior researchers working on hard problems in beneficial and safe AI. Selection is by nomination. The fifth cohort of 28 fellows was announced on 1 Oct 2026.
* [Schmidt Sciences — Science of Trustworthy AI RFP](schmidt-sciences-trustworthy-ai.md) - Research grants for technical AI safety science (misalignment, measurement and interventions, oversight of superhuman systems). Tier 1 up to $1M and Tier 2 $1–5M+ over 1–3 years, with compute budgets allowed. The 2026 RFP closed on 17 May 2026.
* [Survival and Flourishing Fund (SFF) — S-Process Grant Rounds](survival-and-flourishing-fund.md) - Annual S-Process rounds that recommend $20–40M (2026), largely to AI-safety and existential-risk organizations. Charities, fiscally sponsored researchers and some for-profits are eligible. The rolling application also triggers Speculation Grants. The 2026 Main Round closed 22 Apr; later applications roll into the next round.
* [The Alignment Project (UK AISI-led coalition)](uk-aisi-alignment-project.md) - International alignment-research fund led by UK AISI with partners including OpenAI, Microsoft, AWS, Anthropic and Schmidt Sciences. £50k–£1M grants plus compute and API credits; round 1 gave £27M to 60+ projects. Applications closed; next round unclear.
* [UK AI Security Institute Challenge Fund (and Systemic Safety Grants)](uk-aisi-challenge-fund.md) - £5M fund giving £50k–£200k grants to UK and international academic and non-profit researchers on AI safety and security (safeguards, control, alignment, societal resilience). Closed to new applications as of Oct 2026, as is the earlier Systemic Safety programme.
