# Summary

The AWS Generative AI Accelerator is an equity-free, eight-week hybrid program for startups working on hard generative-AI problems. Each startup gets **up to $1M in AWS credits**, mentors, and go-to-market help, and the program ends at re:Invent.[^aws-genai-blog][^aws-genai-granted] Cohorts are about 40 startups.[^aws-genai-sbt] Third-party listings put the 2026 application window at 10 June–10 July 2026. The AWS program page now redirects and could not be read.[^aws-genai-granted]

# Eligibility

- Late-seed to Series A startups building generative-AI products or infrastructure.[^aws-genai-granted]

# What it funds

- AWS credits, including Trainium and Inferentia access, plus mentoring and marketing support.[^aws-genai-blog]

# Amounts & terms

- Up to $1M in credits. AWS takes no equity.[^aws-genai-granted]

# How to apply

- Apply online during the annual window.[^aws-genai-blog]
