# Summary

Anthropic's Economic Futures Program launched on 27 June 2025. It offers **research awards of up to $50,000** for empirical work on how AI affects labour, productivity and value creation. Applications are rolling, and API credits are available through institutional partnerships.[^efp-launch] In July 2026 press reported a **$200M Economic Futures Research Fund** making much larger grants. This could not be confirmed on an Anthropic page, so treat those figures as unverified.[^efp-200m]

# Eligibility

- Researchers studying the economic impacts of AI.[^efp-launch]

# What it funds

- Empirical research, policy development forums and economic data collection.[^efp-launch]
- Software is funded only as research infrastructure, such as datasets or measurement tools.

# Amounts & terms

- Up to $50k per research award.[^efp-launch]

# How to apply

- Rolling application from the program page.[^efp-launch]
