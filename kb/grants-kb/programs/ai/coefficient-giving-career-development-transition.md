# Summary

Coefficient Giving's Career Development and Transition Funding (CDTF) supports individuals anywhere, at any career stage, who want to build skills for reducing global catastrophic risk, including AI risk.[^cdtf-streamlyne] It pays for graduate study, unpaid internships, independent study, postdocs, courses, sabbaticals and short transition periods.[^cdtf-streamlyne] Applications are short: a proposal and personal statement of 250 words each, a budget, a CV and transcripts. Review takes about six weeks.[^cdtf-streamlyne] Coefficient's **capacity-building** program, for projects that grow society's capacity to handle transformative AI, is also open on a rolling basis until further notice.[^cg-capacity-granted]

# Eligibility

- Individuals worldwide who can show the funding would materially change what they can do.[^cdtf-streamlyne]

# What it funds

- Upskilling and transition time, for example a software engineer moving into safety engineering.[^cdtf-streamlyne]

# Amounts & terms

- Sized to the budget you request.[^cdtf-streamlyne]

# How to apply

- Online form; an interview is possible.[^cdtf-streamlyne]
