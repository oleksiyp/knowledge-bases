# Summary

Anthropic's AI for Science program gives researchers at academic and nonprofit institutions **up to $20,000 in Claude API credits over six months**. It focuses on high-impact science, especially biology and the life sciences, and evaluates submissions on the first Monday of every month.[^afs-support] Anthropic updated the official rules on 25 August 2026. Award amounts are at Anthropic's discretion, and every application goes through a biosecurity assessment.[^afs-rules] Principal investigators can also get a free or discounted **Claude Team plan for scientists** for 12 months, with AI for Science credits on top for heavier workloads.[^team-scientists] A third-party report describes a 2026 "Claude Science" cycle: up to $30,000 in credits plus $2,000 of Modal compute for 50 projects, with a 15 July 2026 deadline. This could not be confirmed on an Anthropic page.[^granted-claude-science]

# Eligibility

- Researchers at academic and nonprofit research institutions working on high-impact science.[^afs-support]
- Applicants must be 18+, not resident in sanctioned countries, and not Anthropic staff or family. Institutional consents are required.[^afs-rules]
- Team plan: PIs at accredited universities or nonprofit institutes in the natural sciences, mathematics, CS, engineering and related fields.[^team-scientists]

# What it funds

- API credits for standard Claude models. They do not cover the web app or unreleased models.[^afs-support]
- Usage-policy exemptions are not granted.[^afs-support]

# Amounts & terms

- Up to $20k over six months.[^afs-support]
- Team plan: standard seats free and premium seats $15/month for 12 months.[^team-scientists]

# How to apply

- Submit the Google Form linked from the help-center article. Selection is based on scientific merit, impact, feasibility and team credentials, followed by the biosecurity review.[^afs-support][^afs-rules]
- Team-plan decisions usually take 5–7 business days.[^team-scientists]

# Fit

- **Good fit if:** you are a lab scientist or research-software developer with a concrete, inference-heavy project.
