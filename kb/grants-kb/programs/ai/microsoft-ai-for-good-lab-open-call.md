# Summary

The Microsoft AI for Good Lab Open Call is a two-year program. It provides **$5M in Azure service credits** in total, plus scientific collaboration with the lab, for AI projects in sustainability, public health and human rights.[^msaig-open-call] Projects must be based in Washington State or benefit its residents. Applications are closed, and awardees were to be announced after 20 March.[^msaig-open-call]

# Eligibility

- Nonprofits, academics, individual researchers, startups and businesses, based in or benefiting Washington State.[^msaig-open-call]
- Applicants must be able to deploy on Azure.[^msaig-open-call]

# What it funds

- Azure credits and collaboration time with Microsoft researchers.[^msaig-open-call]

# Amounts & terms

- $5M pool over two years.[^msaig-open-call]

# How to apply

- The call is closed. Watch Microsoft Research for future calls.[^msaig-open-call]
