# Summary

Manifund puts money in the hands of **regrantors**: experts with independent budgets who make fast, small grants to early-stage AI-safety projects.[^manifund-2026] In 2025, 13 regrantors distributed $2M. The 2026 target is $2M at minimum (10–15 regrantors with $100k each) and up to $5M.[^manifund-2026] Typical grants are **$5k–$50k** to registered charities and individuals, with for-profits possible after due diligence. Third-party tracking counts about $3.7M donated to around 100 projects in 2026 so far.[^manifund-granted] Past grants seeded Timaeus, Eleos AI and the WMDP benchmark.[^manifund-2026]

# Eligibility

- Individuals and charities; for-profits after due diligence.[^manifund-granted]

# What it funds

- AI-safety research, tooling, benchmarks and field-building.[^manifund-2026]

# Amounts & terms

- See amount_text. Proposals are public on Manifund.

# How to apply

- Create a public project on manifund.org. Regrantors and donors fund it directly.[^manifund-granted]

# Track record

- Timaeus ($143k, 2023), WMDP benchmark ($190k, 2024) and others.[^manifund-2026]

# Fit

- **Good fit if:** you are an independent researcher or small tool-builder who needs a quick $10–50k and is comfortable with public proposals.
- **Poor fit if:** you need confidentiality or large multi-year budgets.
