# Summary

Lambda's Research Grant gives qualifying AI/ML researchers **up to $5,000 in GPU cloud credits** and mentoring from Lambda's Chief Scientific Officer.[^lambda-hpcwire] At CVPR 2026 Lambda widened it to whole research groups and said it expects to sponsor hundreds of researchers in the coming year.[^lambda-cvpr]

# Eligibility

- AI/ML researchers and, since 2026, research groups.[^lambda-cvpr]

# What it funds

- Lambda Cloud GPU instances for training and fine-tuning.[^lambda-hpcwire]

# Amounts & terms

- Up to $5k.[^lambda-hpcwire]

# How to apply

- Apply through the application form linked from Lambda's research pages.[^lambda-hpcwire]
