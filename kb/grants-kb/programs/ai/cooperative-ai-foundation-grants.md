# Summary

The Cooperative AI Foundation (CAIF) is a charity backed by a **$15M commitment from Macroscopic Ventures**. It funds research on making advanced AI cooperate well, favouring "differential progress" that strengthens cooperation without enabling deception.[^caif] It runs open calls for **research grants** (last deadline 18 January 2025) and **PhD fellowships** (the 2026 cohort's deadline was 16 November 2025; 14 fellows).[^caif][^caif-phd-2026] In 2026 it co-ran the $10M "Scaling AI Safety for a Multi-Agent World" fund (see [that program](/programs/ai/multi-agent-ai-safety-fund.md)).[^caif] Educational or advocacy projects are not funded.

# Eligibility

- Researchers worldwide. PhD fellowship for current or incoming PhD students in cooperative AI.[^caif-phd-2026]

# What it funds

- Research on cooperative intelligence, multi-agent evaluation and testbeds.[^caif]
