# Summary

The NAIRR Pilot gives US researchers and educators no-cost access to AI computing, models, datasets and educational platforms. Resources come from federal and industry partners.[^nairr-home] It has four pathways: Deep Partnerships, Start-Up Projects, Research Resources and Educational Resources.[^nairr-home] On **1 September 2026** NSF awarded the permanent NAIRR Operations Center to SDSC and TACC, and stated that "all existing opportunities remain available".[^nairr-home] **Start-Up Project** requests are reviewed in about 3 weeks. They provide one resource for three months, for example up to 2,000 GPU-hours on Bridges-2, Anvil AI, Expanse AI or Vista.[^nairr-startup]

# Eligibility

- US-based researchers and educators at US institutions: academics, graduate students (with a faculty support letter), nonprofits, federal agencies, FFRDCs, state, local and tribal agencies, and **startups and small businesses with federal grants** (for example SBIR).[^nairr-startup]
- An institutional email address is required.[^nairr-startup]

# What it funds

- Compute allocations, model and data access, and training.[^nairr-home]
- No cash.[^nairr-granted]

# Amounts & terms

- Start-Up: one resource for three months, and you must start using it within two weeks.[^nairr-startup]
- Research allocations: typically 12 months.[^nairr-granted]

# How to apply

- Submit through the XRAS portal (submit-nairr.xras.org), listing only grants that directly support the work.[^nairr-startup]
