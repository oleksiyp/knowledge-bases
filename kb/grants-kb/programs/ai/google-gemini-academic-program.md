# Summary

The Gemini Academic Program gives qualified academic researchers (faculty, staff and PhD students in supported countries) Gemini API credits and higher rate limits for research projects.[^gemini-research] Applications are reviewed monthly. Google grants and removes access at its discretion.[^gemini-research] Priority areas are evaluations and benchmarks, scientific discovery, embodiment and HCI, emergent capabilities, and multimodal understanding.[^gemini-research] Response times vary, and the developer forum has threads from applicants asking how to follow up.[^gemini-forum]

# Eligibility

- Individuals affiliated with a valid academic institution or academic research organization in a supported country.[^gemini-research]

# What it funds

- Gemini API credits and rate-limit increases.[^gemini-research]

# Amounts & terms

- Not published. Access is discretionary and revocable.[^gemini-research]

# How to apply

- Use the online form linked from the Gemini for Research page.[^gemini-research]
