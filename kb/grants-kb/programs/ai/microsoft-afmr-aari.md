# Summary

Microsoft Research's AFMR program gave academic researchers worldwide access to frontier models through Azure AI services. Microsoft says it "has reached its conclusion" and keeps the pages for reference.[^afmr] Its successor is **Agentic AI Research and Innovation (AARI)**, which focuses on high-value applications, foundational research and the safety of agentic systems. AARI works with named academic partners and "Grand Challenges".[^aari] The AARI page publishes no amounts, eligibility rules or application process.[^aari]

# Eligibility

- AFMR: academic researchers (closed). AARI: invited academic partners.[^afmr][^aari]

# What it funds

- Model access and credits, plus collaboration on agentic AI.[^aari]

# Amounts & terms

- Not published.

# How to apply

- There is no open application. Watch Microsoft Research for AARI calls or workshops.[^afmr]
