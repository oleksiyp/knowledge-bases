# Summary

The Google for Startups Cloud Program gives credits in tiers. **Start** offers up to $2,000 for pre-funded startups under five years old. **Scale** offers up to $200,000 for pre-seed to Series A companies. **Scale AI** offers up to $350,000 for AI-first startups.[^gfs-devto] The Scale tiers need institutional funding: SAFEs count, but angel money, grants and crowdfunding do not.[^gfs-gupta] Credits can be spent on Google Cloud, including Gemini on Vertex AI.[^gfs-gupta]

# Eligibility

- Startups founded in the last five years (Start tier). Scale tiers need institutional pre-seed to Series A funding.[^gfs-devto][^gfs-gupta]

# What it funds

- Google Cloud and Vertex AI/Gemini usage, plus support and training perks.[^gfs-devto]

# Amounts & terms

- See the tiers above. Third-party guides say credits run over two years.[^gfs-gupta]
