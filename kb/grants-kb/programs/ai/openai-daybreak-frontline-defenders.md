# Summary

In September 2026 OpenAI pledged **$1 billion** in subsidised access to its "Daybreak" cybersecurity models, plus training and hands-on support, for resource-strapped defenders.[^register-daybreak] Open-source maintainers are a named priority group, alongside water and grid operators, state and local governments, community banks and nonprofits.[^register-daybreak][^droptimes-daybreak] The $1B is OpenAI's own valuation of discounted product usage, not a cash fund, and OpenAI expects it to be used over about six months.[^register-daybreak] For maintainers, the related **Patch the Planet** effort with Trail of Bits, HackerOne and Calif pairs AI vulnerability research with expert patch development.[^patch-planet]

# Eligibility

- Critical-infrastructure operators, community banks, nonprofits, open-source maintainers, SLTT governments (through an MS-ISAC pilot) and other defenders with limited security resources.[^register-daybreak]
- US organizations come first; OpenAI said partner countries would follow within weeks.[^droptimes-daybreak]
- There are two tiers: Daybreak Blue for defence and Daybreak Red for authorized offensive testing.[^register-daybreak]

# What it funds

- Subsidised model access, training and technical support.[^register-daybreak]
- Patch the Planet: Trail of Bits engineers work full time with Codex Security and GPT-5.5-Cyber across participating OSS projects. They had found hundreds of issues and merged dozens of patches at the time of reporting.[^eweek-patch]
- No cash grants to individuals have been published.

# Amounts & terms

- $1B across the whole initiative. Per-recipient amounts are not published.[^register-daybreak]
- At launch, participants did not get access to OpenAI's newest "Astra" model.[^register-daybreak]
- Registering interest "does not guarantee eligibility, funding, model access, partner services or a particular timeline".[^droptimes-daybreak]

# How to apply

- Register interest on the Daybreak application page and say whether you need access, funding or both.[^daybreak-apply][^droptimes-daybreak]

# Track record

- Patch the Planet grew from 19 to about 50 participating projects, including cURL and Python.[^eweek-patch][^patch-planet]

# Fit

- **Good fit if:** you maintain security-critical OSS, or defend a small public-interest organization, and can use frontier-model scanning right away.
- **Poor fit if:** you need multi-year cash funding.
