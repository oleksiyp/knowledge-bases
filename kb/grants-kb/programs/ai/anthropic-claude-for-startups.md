# Summary

Claude for Startups is open to any early-stage founder for community access. **Credits** go only to startups that have institutional equity funding, were founded in the last four years, and have not received Anthropic startup credits before.[^claude-startups] Accepted startups also get Anthropic's highest rate limits while on credits.[^claude-startups] Anthropic does not publish credit amounts. Third-party guides report about $5k for direct applicants, $25k with a VC or accelerator partner referral, and up to $100k for top-tier VC nominations.[^secboulevard-startups]

# Eligibility

- Institutional equity funding, founded within 4 years, and no prior Anthropic startup credits.[^claude-startups]
- Bootstrapped founders can join the program but not get credits.[^claude-startups]

# What it funds

- First-party Claude API usage through the Claude Console. Credits are not valid on Bedrock, Vertex AI or other resellers.[^claude-startups]

# Amounts & terms

- Credits for 12 months (reported). Standard pricing starts automatically afterwards.[^claude-startups][^secboulevard-startups]

# How to apply

- About two minutes: Console account, company email, website and a description. Ask your investor for a partner referral code.[^claude-startups]
