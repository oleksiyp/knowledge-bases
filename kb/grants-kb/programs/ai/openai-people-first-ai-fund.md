# Summary

The People-First AI Fund is the OpenAI Foundation's grant program for US community organizations. Its first wave in December 2025 gave **$40.5M in unrestricted grants to 208** organizations.[^pfaf-update][^pfaf-initial] The 2026 round gave **$50M to 163 nonprofits** in 35 states and DC, chosen from 1,669 applications by external expert review. The Foundation plans to pay every grant in full before the end of 2026.[^pfaf-2026] It funds AI adoption by community organizations, not software development, and is listed here for completeness.

# Eligibility

- US nonprofits. The 2026 round targeted community services, arts and culture, and local journalism and media.[^pfaf-2026]
- The official announcement does not publish detailed eligibility rules such as budget bands.[^pfaf-2026]

# What it funds

- Unrestricted operating support. Grantees decide for themselves how, or whether, to use AI tools.[^pfaf-2026]

# Amounts & terms

- 2026: $50M across 163 grantees. 2025: $40.5M across 208.[^pfaf-2026][^pfaf-update]

# How to apply

- Open-call application with external expert review. No future cycle has been announced.[^pfaf-2026]
