# Summary

AWS Cloud Credit for Research gives promotional credits to researchers at accredited institutions. Student awards are capped at **$5,000**. Faculty and staff awards have no cap, though recipients may get less than they ask for.[^aws-ccr] Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis over 30–60 days, and credits are valid for one year.[^aws-ccr] Since 16 February 2026, AWS Free Tier accounts cannot receive these credits.[^aws-ccr] The credits cover GPU instances, Bedrock and SageMaker, so they are often used for AI work.

# Eligibility

- Full-time faculty, full-time research staff, and graduate, postgraduate or PhD students at accredited research institutions.[^aws-ccr-uw]
- Accounts must not be Free Tier (from 2026-02-16).[^aws-ccr]

# What it funds

- AWS usage for the proposed research.[^aws-ccr]

# Amounts & terms

- See the caps above. Credits last one year or until used up.[^aws-ccr]

# How to apply

- Submit a project description: problem, summary, AWS services you will use, timeline and milestones. Add a plan for sharing outcomes, any future AWS use, AWS contacts and any public datasets.[^aws-ccr]
