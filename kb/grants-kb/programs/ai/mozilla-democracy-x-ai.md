# Summary

The Mozilla Foundation's Democracy x AI cohort put **$1M** into technologists building AI tools that strengthen democratic institutions, fight misinformation or protect civic space.[^moz-granted] It funds **10 projects at $50,000**, and two top teams move on to a **$250,000 Sustain Track**.[^moz-granted] Applicants need working technology, a team committed for 12 months, and at least part of the code open source or a commitment to open it during the grant.[^moz-opdesk] Initial proposals closed on 16 March 2026.[^moz-opdesk]

# Eligibility

- Teams, organizations, startups, nonprofits and individuals legally able to receive funds from a US 501(c)(3). Applications must be in English.[^moz-opdesk]
- Products must be beyond the wireframe stage.[^moz-opdesk]

# What it funds

- AI tools for democracy, plus mentorship, workshops and cohort calls.[^moz-opdesk]

# Amounts & terms

- $50k, with a possible $250k Sustain award. Partial open-sourcing is required.[^moz-opdesk][^moz-granted]

- Online form (closed for 2026).[^moz-opdesk]
