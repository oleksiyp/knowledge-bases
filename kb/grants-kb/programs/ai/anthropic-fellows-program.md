# Summary

The Anthropic Fellows Program pays technical people to do four months of full-time empirical research with Anthropic mentors. Fellows receive a weekly stipend of **$3,850 / £2,310 / C$4,300** plus benefits, and about **$15k per month** for compute and research expenses.[^fellows-opdesk][^fellows-post] The program does not require a PhD, prior ML experience or publications. It looks for strong coding, comfort with AI tools, and the ability to make progress on ambiguous problems.[^fellows-post] Applications are rolling, with the next cut-off on **18 October 2026** for the January 2027 cohort.[^fellows-post] It currently runs three tracks: AI Safety & Security, ML Systems & RL, and The Anthropic Institute (Economics & Policy). Fellows can be based in London, Ontario, remote US or San Francisco.[^fellows-greenhouse]

# Eligibility

- Work authorization or residency in the US, UK or Canada, depending on the listing.[^fellows-greenhouse]
- Able to work full time (40 h/week) for four months.[^fellows-opdesk]
- Strong technical fundamentals. Credentials are not required.[^fellows-post]

# What it funds

- Empirical research on scalable oversight, adversarial robustness, AI control, model organisms, mechanistic interpretability, AI security and model welfare.[^fellows-post]
- Fellows are expected to produce public outputs, mainly papers, and code is often released.[^fellows-post]

# Amounts & terms

- Stipend about $61.6k over four months, plus a compute budget of about $15k/month. Extension is possible.[^fellows-opdesk]
- Fellows are not employees. Outcomes: over 35% of past fellows joined Anthropic and over 65% published.[^fellows-post]

# How to apply

- Apply through the Greenhouse listings for each track. Selection includes technical assessments and interviews.[^fellows-greenhouse][^fellows-post]

# Fit

- **Good fit if:** you are a strong engineer pivoting into safety research and can relocate or work in the listed locations.
- **Poor fit if:** you need part-time or long-term funding, or you are outside the US, UK or Canada.
