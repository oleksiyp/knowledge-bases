# Summary

Schmidt Sciences, Google DeepMind, ARIA, the Cooperative AI Foundation and Google.org pooled a **$10M fund** for foundational research on risks from large ecosystems of interacting AI agents.[^mas-caif] There are four research clusters: sandboxes and testbeds, the science of agent networks, agent infrastructure (identity, reputation and commitment protocols), and multi-agent oversight and control.[^mas-portal] **Tier 1** gives up to $300k and **Tier 2** gives $300k–$1M, both over 1–2 years. Schmidt-funded awards cap indirect costs at 10%.[^mas-portal] The deadline was in August 2026: the Cooperative AI Foundation says 8 August and the application portal says 9 August AoE. Notifications are due in autumn 2026.[^mas-caif][^mas-portal]

# Eligibility

- Individual researchers, teams, institutions and multi-institution collaborations at universities, national labs, institutes and nonprofits, worldwide.[^mas-portal]

# What it funds

- Multi-agent safety research. Out of scope: single-agent safety, capability work without a safety focus, toy systems that do not engage frontier models, and straightforward application of standard mechanisms.[^mas-portal]
- Building open agent-infrastructure software and testbeds fits well.
