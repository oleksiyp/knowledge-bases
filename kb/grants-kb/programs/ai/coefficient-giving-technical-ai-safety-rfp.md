# Summary

Open Philanthropy renamed itself **Coefficient Giving** in November 2025.[^cg-wiki] Its Technical AI Safety Research RFP offered about **$40M** across 21 research areas, including adversarial ML, interpretability, reward hacking, white-box techniques and theoretical approaches to superintelligence alignment.[^cg-tais-granted] Grant types run from quick API and compute credits to 6–24-month projects, academic start-up packages and seed funding for new research organizations.[^cg-tais-granted] Applications start with a **300-word expression of interest**, which gets a response within about two weeks.[^cg-tais-granted] The 2026 deadline was **21 August 2026** and the RFP is now closed. Other rolling programs under "Navigating Transformative AI" stay open.[^cg-tais-granted][^cg-nta]

# Eligibility

- Individual researchers, academic groups, and new or existing research orgs worldwide with ML experience.[^cg-tais-granted]

# What it funds

- Technical safety research, including the software and benchmarks that come out of it.[^cg-tais-granted]
- Governance work goes through a separate RFP.[^cg-nta]

# Amounts & terms

- Sizes vary. Third-party listings report individual grants up to about $187,500, and org grants are larger.[^cg-tais-granted]

# How to apply

- Submit a 300-word EOI; promising ones are invited to a full proposal. Questions go to technicalaisafety@coefficientgiving.org.[^cg-tais-granted]

- Coefficient Giving and Open Philanthropy have been the largest philanthropic funder of AI safety for years.[^cg-wiki]

# Fit

- **Good fit if:** you are an ML researcher with a concrete empirical safety agenda.
- **Poor fit if:** you build general AI products.
