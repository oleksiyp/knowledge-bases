# Practical tips that apply everywhere

- **Lead with maintenance and security outcomes**, not new features. The largest pots in
  2026 (Sovereign Tech, Alpha-Omega, NLnet, OTF) fund hardening, audits, dependencies and
  standards.
- **Apply to rolling programs first.** They are quicker and lower-stakes. Use dated calls for
  bigger asks.
- **Show users and dependants**: download counts, dependents, and who would be hurt if the
  project disappeared.
- **Milestone-based payment is the norm** (NLnet, Sovereign Tech, OTF). Scope deliverables you
  can demonstrate.
- **Check legal-entity requirements.** Many programs need a company or nonprofit, or a fiscal
  host such as Open Source Collective.
