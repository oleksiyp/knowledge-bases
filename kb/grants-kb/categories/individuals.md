# Overview

Most grant money for software goes to organizations and projects. An individual developer has five realistic routes in 2026:

1. **Paid mentorships and internships** for newcomers. Google Summer of Code accepted 1,141 contributors out of 15,245 applicants in 2026, paying $750–$6,600 by purchasing power parity.[^gsoc-2026-contributors][^gsoc-stipends] Outreachy pays $7,000 for three months.[^outreachy-applicant-guide] LFX Mentorship runs three terms a year. From July 2026 it cut its minimum stipend to $1,000 (keeping the $6,600 maximum) so it can fund more people.[^cncf-stipend-2026]
2. **No-strings grants and fellowships for talent**, not tied to software. Emergent Ventures announced cohorts 51 to 59 in 2026.[^ev-cohort-59] The Thiel Fellowship pays $250k over two years to people aged 22 or under.[^thiel-faq] O'Shaughnessy pays $100k for one year and drew 11,813 applicants in 2026.[^osv-fellowship] FUTO microgrants of $1k–$5k go to open-source tools.[^futo-grants]
3. **Paid maintainer roles at language foundations.** These include Django Fellows ($82.26/hour in 2025)[^dsf-fellow-call-2025] and PSF Developers-in-Residence, boosted by Anthropic's $1.5M gift in January 2026.[^psf-anthropic] The Rust Foundation launched its Maintainers in Residence program in August 2026.[^rust-mir-first] Germany's Sovereign Tech Fellowship sits in the oss-infrastructure category.[^sta-fellowship]
4. **Residencies with large budgets** for open AI and science work: Astera Institute and, for AI safety, MATS.[^astera-fall26-x][^mats-w27]
5. **Donation platforms.** GitHub Sponsors (0% fee from personal sponsors),[^gh-sponsors-fees] Open Collective fiscal hosting (10% at OSC),[^osc-fees] thanks.dev dependency-based giving[^canonical-thanksdev] and Liberapay.

**What changed in 2025–26:** Several individual-focused programs have stopped or paused:
- Google Season of Docs has ended.[^gsod-ended]
- The GitHub Accelerator has had no cohort since 2024.[^gh-acc-site]
- The Recurse Center paused its living-expense grants.[^rc-diversity]
- The Rust Foundation Fellowship closed and was replaced by the Maintainers Fund.[^rf-fellowship-page][^rust-mir-first]
- Polar sunset donations and issue funding.[^polar-v2]

New money came instead from corporate gifts to language foundations (PSF, Rust) and from maintainer funds such as HeroDevs' $20M fund.[^psf-anthropic][^herodevs-fund]

# Tips

- **Mentorship programs reward early engagement.** GSoC organizations reviewed 23,371 proposals in 2026,[^gsoc-2026-contributors] so contact your organization as soon as it is announced. Outreachy requires real contributions during its month-long contribution period.[^outreachy-applicant-guide]
- **Check PPP tables.** GSoC, LFX and Summer of Bitcoin all scale stipends by country, so the same project can pay half as much in lower-cost countries.[^gsoc-stipends][^cncf-stipend-2026]
- **Talent grants want progress, not ideas.** Thiel explicitly requires "meaningful progress toward a concrete vision".[^thiel-faq] 1517 asks for a five-minute Loom video,[^medici] and Emergent Ventures gives no feedback.
- **Stack donation rails.** Use GitHub Sponsors for personal sponsors (0% fee) and invoiced billing for companies (which avoids the 3% card fee).[^gh-sponsors-fees] Check thanks.dev for money already allocated to your packages but unclaimed. thanks.dev staff often have to chase maintainers to set up payouts.[^canonical-thanksdev] The thanks.dev fee is reported inconsistently: 5% commission in Canonical's 2025 post, and 0% plus a voluntary tip in the oss-infrastructure entry. Check the current terms.
- **Foundation roles are hired, not granted.** Watch the DSF, PSF and Rust Foundation blogs. Django's last Fellow call was open for only about three weeks (June 2025).[^dsf-fellow-call-2025]
- **Climate-focused ML work** usually needs a university principal investigator (see Climate Change AI's Innovation Grants in the research and AI categories),[^ccai-2026] so individuals should partner with an academic.
