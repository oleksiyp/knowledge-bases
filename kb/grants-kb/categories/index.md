* [AI software, compute credits & AI-safety grants](ai.md) - Guide to grants, fellowships and compute/API-credit programs for AI software, open-source maintainers using AI, and AI-safety research and tooling, verified as of October 2026.

* [Startup & company R&D grants (non-dilutive)](startup-rnd.md) - Government grants, R&D tax credits and equity-free programs for startups and companies building software — US SBIR/STTR, EIC, Innovate UK, national schemes across Europe, Ukraine, Israel, Canada, Australia and Asia, plus corporate credits — status as of October 2026.
* [Web3 / crypto ecosystem grants & retroactive public-goods funding](web3.md) - Map of blockchain-foundation grants, DAO programs, Bitcoin developer funders and retroactive/quadratic public-goods funding for software, with 2026 status (many paused or restructured), payout currency and KYC notes.
