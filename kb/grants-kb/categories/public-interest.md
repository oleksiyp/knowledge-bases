# Overview

Public-interest technology funding covers internet-freedom and digital-rights tools, digital public goods (DPGs) and digital public infrastructure (DPI), civic tech, journalism tech, accessibility, and climate and health software. The money comes from five sources: government-backed funds (OTF, European ministries), private foundations (Ford, Knight, Gates, Mozilla, Wellcome), Internet-registry foundations (ISOC, APNIC, LACNIC), corporate philanthropy (Google, AWS, Okta, Twilio) and intermediaries (CIVICUS, Borealis, Access Now).

**What changed in 2025–26:**

- **US federal funding disruption.** On 15 March 2025 USAGM terminated the Open Technology Fund's grant. OTF sued. The termination was rescinded but payments were withheld, and a court ordered the FY2024 funds released in June 2025.[^wiki-otf] In October 2025 OTF went back to court over FY2025 funds, and the government appealed to the DC Circuit on 26 January 2026.[^wiki-otf][^courtlistener-otf] Congress had appropriated $43.5M for OTF in 2025, and Tor, Let's Encrypt and F-Droid were among the grantees exposed.[^heise-otf-2025] **As of October 2026 OTF is operating.** The Internet Freedom Fund takes rolling concept notes, and a Surge & Sustain call closes on 15 October 2026.[^otf-iff][^otf-sas][^otf-funds] Even so, applicants should plan for possible payment delays.
- **USAID and State Department.** USAID cancelled 53 of 54 media-development projects. The US had provided $135–248M a year (2023–24) in media assistance, roughly 35% of all government-donor funding for media.[^carnegie-media-2026] The State Department's DRL Internet Freedom Annual Program Statement last showed a January 2025 SOI deadline. This KB found no 2026 DRL internet-freedom solicitation.[^drl-aps]
- **Replacement funders have only partly filled the gap.** Carnegie found 48% of affected media groups saw *less* donor support in 2025 and only 21% saw more, mostly from European donors.[^carnegie-media-2026] The clearest European-funded channels still open are:
  - The **CIVICUS Digital Democracy Initiative** (EU, Danida, Norway), with rolling digital-resiliency grants.[^civicus-drg]
  - The **Common Good Cyber Fund** (Australia, Canada, Germany, the Netherlands and the UK, plus Craig Newmark Philanthropies and ISOC). It ran a 2026 call of at least $3.5M within a $40M+ 2026–29 Safer Internet Initiative.[^cgcf-2026-pr]
  - The **Digital Defenders Partnership** is also funded by European ministries, but most of its funds were closed or invitation-only in October 2026.[^ddp-home]
- **Programs that closed or went quiet:** Climate Change AI Innovation Grants (no future calls),[^ccai-ig] Microsoft AI for Accessibility (no live programme on microsoft.com),[^ms-a11y-innov] ISOC BOLT (closed),[^isocf-bolt] the Mozilla Technology Fund (succeeded by the Mozilla Incubator) and the Bezos Earth Fund AI Grand Challenge (Phase II awarded; no new call).[^bef-phase2]
- **AI is the dominant theme of new money.** Examples are Google.org's $30M AI for Science and AI for Government challenges, Mozilla's Democracy x AI cohort, Gates Grand Challenges on AI, AWS Imagine's frontier-AI tier and the Patrick J. McGovern Foundation (all linked below).

# Tips

- **Read OTF's eligibility rules before you write.** OTF does not fund basic connectivity, ICT4D or policy advocacy.[^otf-iff] Applicants in OFAC-sanctioned countries are ineligible for all OTF funds.[^otf-iff] OTF's guidebook keeps a maintained list of alternative funders, last updated February 2026, which is useful if OTF is not a fit.[^otf-alt]
- **Use the small, fast funds first** if your organisation is at risk: CIVICUS resiliency grants take about 6–8 weeks,[^civicus-drg] and OTF Rapid Response reviews applications as they arrive.
- **For DPI and DPG software, get registered first.** The DPGA Registry, with 255 DPGs, is the credential DPI funders look for.[^dpga-home] Co-Develop does not take unsolicited proposals; it co-creates grants with countries.[^codev-faq]
- **Many big foundations are invitation-only:** Ford,[^ford-opps] Omidyar[^omidyar-faq] and Rockefeller.[^rf-faq] Reach them through their open intermediaries (e.g. Borealis DIF x Tech, Co-Develop, Press Forward) or by becoming visible in their grantee networks.
- **Wikimedia deadlines are fixed and predictable.** Rapid Fund cycles fall on the 1st of Jul, Sep, Nov, Feb and Apr.[^wm-rapid]
- **AI framing currently dominates new calls.** If your public-interest tool uses ML, say so concretely. If it does not, don't force it. Funders such as Google.org ask for alignment with responsible-AI principles and open-source licensing.[^gorg-ai-science]
