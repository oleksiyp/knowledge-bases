# Overview

Non-dilutive money for software companies comes in three shapes: **competitive R&D grants/contracts** (SBIR/STTR, EIC, Innovate UK, national agencies), **R&D tax credits** (UK, Canada, Netherlands, Germany, Poland, Australia — often the most reliable money for a software startup with salaried engineers), and **equity-free accelerators/credits** (Google, AWS, Microsoft, UNICEF).

What changed in 2025–26:

- **US SBIR/STTR lapsed for six months.** Authority expired 30 Sep 2025 and was restored on 13 Apr 2026 by the Small Business Innovation and Economic Security Act (S. 3971), extending the programs to 30 Sep 2031, adding Strategic Breakthrough awards (up to $30M, with matching), per-company proposal caps from FY2027 and tougher foreign-risk screening[^crowell-reauth]. NSF reopened under NSF 26-510 (Phase I up to $305K)[^nsf-26-510]; AFWERX replaced its broad Open Topic with Focused Open Topics[^grantengine-afwerx].
- **Innovate UK Smart grants are closed** while funding is "aligned to Innovate UK's new strategic direction"[^ukri-smart]; UK support now flows through themed competitions such as Frontier AI: SME Champions[^ifs-frontier-ai].
- **EIC Accelerator** streamlined for 2026 with six cut-offs (next 4 Nov 2026) and grants below €2.5M[^eic-acc].
- **Germany's ZIM stopped taking applications on 7 Jul 2026** for budget reasons[^ihk-sh-zim]; SPRIND expanded its challenge model, including several AI challenges open in autumn 2026[^sprind-challenges].
- **Canada** doubled SR&ED's enhanced-rate expenditure limit to $6M[^leyton-sred]; **Australia** paused Industry Growth Program advisory intake[^bga-igp] and proposed big RDTI reforms from July 2028[^ey-rdti-2026].
- **EIT Digital** became the independent **28DIGITAL** (Oct 2025)[^eit-28digital].

# Tips

- **Claim the tax credit first.** For most software startups with salaried engineers, R&D tax regimes (WBSO, SR&ED, UK RDEC/ERIS, Forschungszulage, RDTI, ulga B+R) are lower-effort and more predictable than competitive grants. WBSO must be applied for *before* the work period[^rvo-wbso].
- **Software must show technological uncertainty.** All tax schemes and NSF exclude routine development; frame the technical risk, not features[^nsf-26-510].
- **US: register early** (SAM/UEI, agency portals) and get the NSF Project Pitch invitation before planning to a deadline[^nsf-26-510]. Watch the new FY2027 per-company proposal caps[^crowell-reauth].
- **EIC: the short proposal is open year-round**; a GO lets you pick a later cut-off and comes with coaching days[^eic-acc]. UK companies can get the grant but not EIC equity.
- **UK: no more open-scope grants** — map your product to a themed competition and its sector[^ifs-frontier-ai].
- **Budgets run out**: Estonia's Start-up Grant[^eis-startup] and Germany's ZIM[^ihk-sh-zim] both closed mid-2026 when funds were exhausted — apply early in a budget year.
