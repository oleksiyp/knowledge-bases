# Verdict: mixed

* [AI assistance with measured accepted outcomes](/ideas/ai-operations/copilots-with-measurement.md) - AI assistance can change engineering work, but adoption and perceived speed are not sufficient productivity evidence.

# Verdict: too-early

* [Incident agents with bounded authority and reproducible evaluations](/ideas/ai-operations/bounded-incident-agents.md) - AI diagnosis is promising, but the reviewed evidence does not establish reliable unattended remediation across production environments.

# Verdict: winning

* [Inference operations are still capacity, reliability and cost engineering](/ideas/ai-operations/inference-is-operations.md) - AI serving creates new workload characteristics but still depends on explicit scheduling, service objectives and unit economics.
