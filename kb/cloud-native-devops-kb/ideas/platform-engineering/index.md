# Verdict: mixed

* [Delivery and developer-experience metrics without ranking theater](/ideas/platform-engineering/measurement-without-gaming.md) - Measurement is useful for local improvement and becomes misleading when activity is treated as individual value.
* [Developer portals are interfaces, not the whole platform](/ideas/platform-engineering/portals-and-golden-paths.md) - Catalogs and templates help discovery, but a portal cannot substitute for functioning delivery and ownership systems.

# Verdict: winning

* [Internal platforms as products with measurable users](/ideas/platform-engineering/platform-as-product.md) - Platform engineering works best as a service to developers with feedback, ownership and a narrow initial scope.
