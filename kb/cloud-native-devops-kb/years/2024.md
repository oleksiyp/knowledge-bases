# 2024: Selective simplification and project continuity

## Interpretation

OpenTofu reached GA; Flux gained support after Weaveworks closed; Cilium-adjacent and runtime-security work continued; Istio ambient, Karpenter 1.0 and Prometheus 3.0 made concrete capability changes. The recurring theme is refinement of infrastructure already in use.

XZ challenged assumptions about trusted maintenance, while Gitpod’s architecture change challenged universal Kubernetes fit. Adobe’s published case shows both substantial GitOps use and the control-plane work necessary to sustain it. Success and failure often coexist within the same implementation journey.
