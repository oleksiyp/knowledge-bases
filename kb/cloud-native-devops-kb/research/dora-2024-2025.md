# DORA 2024–2026: platforms, AI and evidence boundaries

## Finding

The 2024 research discusses platform productivity benefits alongside delivery tradeoffs. The 2025 report frames AI as amplifying existing organizational conditions.[^dora24][^dora25]

## Method and limitations

These are survey-based research programs with modeled relationships. A correlation between a practice and an outcome does not show that adopting the named tool will produce the same outcome. Definitions, organizational maturity and simultaneous interventions matter.

## Interpretation for decisions

Use the findings to select hypotheses for local improvement: feedback quality, clear priorities and delivery stability deserve measurement. Avoid turning cross-team comparisons into individual rankings or quoting coefficients as guaranteed returns.

## 2026 framework update

DORA’s public April 2026 description of its AI ROI report presents a practical framework and calculator for estimating potential returns.[^roi] That is a different evidence type from a randomized productivity estimate. This note uses the public description, not a review of the gated report’s full contents. A scenario calculation is useful only to the extent that its inputs match the organization’s observed outcomes.
