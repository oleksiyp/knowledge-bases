# METR productivity evidence and its 2026 revision

## Finding

The early-2025 experiment found a slowdown for its experienced open-source developer sample. In February 2026, METR described a later study whose selection effects and concurrent-agent timing made the current effect unreliable to estimate.[^metr25][^metr26]

## Method and limitations

Random assignment strengthens the original study’s causal interpretation within its tasks and tools. It does not grant external validity to every developer or later agent. The follow-up’s uncertainty also does not establish either zero benefit or a precise improvement.

## Interpretation for decisions

The useful research lesson is to include review, rework and accepted quality, and to update evaluations as tools change. User preference matters for adoption but is not by itself a counterfactual productivity measurement.
