# AWS December 2021: internal dependencies can impair recovery tools

## Finding

AWS’s account of the December 7, 2021 US-EAST-1 event describes internal network congestion affecting service capabilities, with impaired operations including new resource creation.[^aws21]

## Method and limitations

The provider report is the primary account of its own systems. It does not mean every running customer workload stopped. Distinguish impairment of control operations from the health of already-running data paths.

## Interpretation for decisions

The operational inference is to examine whether a recovery plan requires precisely the APIs or tools likely to be unavailable. Pre-provisioned headroom, independent access and tested degraded operation may be more useful than an untested plan to create capacity during the incident.
