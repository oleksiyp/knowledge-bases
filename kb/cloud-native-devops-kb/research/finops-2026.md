# FinOps 2026: a large community sample, not an industry census

## Finding

The report lists 1,192 respondents representing over $83 billion in annual cloud spend and describes AI cost management as a leading concern.[^finops26]

## Method and limitations

The sample is drawn from the FinOps community and is weighted toward larger organizations. Individual charts have smaller respondent counts. The page also presents different comparison percentages in its executive and detailed sections for leadership influence; this KB does not rely on those inconsistent multipliers.

## Interpretation for decisions

Use the report to understand practitioner priorities and design local cost-governance questions. Do not treat the aggregate as the fraction of all companies managing AI costs or as evidence that a particular FinOps intervention caused savings.
