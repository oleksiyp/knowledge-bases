# 37signals: a successful cloud exit with a bounded interpretation

## Finding

37signals reported maintaining availability during its 2023 cloud exit and later completing its Kamal migration in 2025.[^37][^kamal]

## Method and limitations

The account is written by the company’s leadership. It does not supply an independently audited matched comparison for organizations with different demand, staffing or recovery needs. This KB therefore does not adopt an unverified universal savings percentage.

## Interpretation for decisions

Compare total responsibility as well as compute price. Predictable demand and a team prepared to own infrastructure can favor owned capacity. The same move can be unattractive for an organization that values rapid elasticity or depends heavily on managed services.
