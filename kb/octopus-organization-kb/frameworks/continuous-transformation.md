# Source formulation

Jana Werner and Phil Le-Brun's *The Octopus Organization: A Guide to Thriving in a World of Continuous Transformation* is published by Harvard Business Review Press. Their official overview advocates ongoing, small changes and rapid learning, with teams acting independently while staying coordinated.[^book]

# Central ideas

- Decision-making is distributed across the organization.
- Adaptation is part of everyday work.
- Small experiments provide feedback for subsequent changes.

The website presents these as a practical alternative to large transformation programs.[^book] Its claims about faster results are author claims; this bundle does not establish a general causal effect.
