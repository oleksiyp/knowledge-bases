# Source principle

The official clarity guide emphasizes plain language, practical purpose, explicit strategic choices, and customer-related goals. It treats metrics as aids to judgment and learning, warning that rigid targets can encourage gaming. It also favors changes to work and behavior over repeatedly redrawing organization charts.[^clarity]

# Practical interpretation

Use a short written brief before delegating a decision:

- Customer problem: who is affected, and how?
- Desired outcome: what observable improvement matters?
- Trade-off: which competing objective takes lower priority?
- Boundary: what must the experiment preserve?
- Review: which evidence would change the decision?

# Illustrative measures

For the [onboarding pilot](/playbooks/team-pilot.md), track successful completion alongside support contacts and rollback events. A faster flow that creates more unresolved customer problems should prompt investigation. These are proposed measures for the example, not a prescribed scorecard from the authors.
