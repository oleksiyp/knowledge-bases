# Source principle

*The AI-First Enterprise* describes teams with explicit missions, designated decision-makers, clear interfaces, and customer-focused measures. It connects autonomy with psychological safety, iteration, and defined risk boundaries. Teams should respond to feedback and stop work that does not meet its targets.[^ai-enterprise]

# Practical interpretation

Maintain a small shared decision log with the problem, owner, evidence, chosen action, affected teams, and review date. Link it to the relevant experiment and customer outcome. Give another team enough context to reuse or challenge a result.

If AI is used to summarize these records, retain links to the underlying evidence and a responsible person for consequential decisions. This is an implementation suggestion, not a claim that adding an AI tool creates an octopus organization.

# Illustrative dependency

An onboarding team publishes a changed event definition before an analytics team updates its reports. The interface names the owner and compatibility expectations. Local authority then has an explicit coordination mechanism.
