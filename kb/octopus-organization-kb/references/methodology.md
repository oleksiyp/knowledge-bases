# Scope

This bundle answers what the octopus organizational model means and how a reader might examine its practical implications. It covers two published formulations; it is not a history of every use of the metaphor, a biological reference, or a systematic review of organizational performance.

# Method

Primary author materials were consulted on 2026-10-03. The [source map](/references/source-map.md) records what was available. Full books were not reviewed. Author prescriptions are described as prescriptions; no quantitative performance improvement is asserted.

The custom `evidence` field distinguishes source summaries, mixed summary and interpretation, original synthesis, source catalogs, and methodology. Illustrative examples and the [pilot](/playbooks/team-pilot.md) are original applications, not reported company case studies.

# OKF conventions

The bundle uses OKF v0.2 concept frontmatter, directory indexes, a dated log, bundle-relative links, and footnotes keyed to source IDs. Generation metadata identifies the writer. No human or independent verification is asserted; `verified` is omitted. A review date marks when sources should be revisited.[^okf]

# Maintenance

When adding evidence, identify the formulation, use the primary source where available, link the new concept into an index, and record the change in the log. Recheck publication attribution and link availability at the next review. Preserve distinctions between observed results, author claims, and interpretation.
