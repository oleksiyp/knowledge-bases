# Purpose

This checklist is a synthesis for evaluating the [two formulations](/overview.md), not a validated assessment instrument or an authors' official maturity model.

| Observable symptom | Diagnostic question | Possible adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| Every decision still needs a manager | Does the team have written authority? | Clarify the decision boundary and owner. |
| Teams pursue incompatible outcomes | Are shared trade-offs explicit? | Revisit the common goal and interfaces. |
| Experiments continue regardless of results | Was a stop condition agreed? | Review evidence before adding scope. |
| AI outputs are treated as final decisions | Who checks evidence and owns the consequence? | Name an accountable reviewer. |
| Local improvements create downstream rework | Are affected teams included in the feedback? | Track the end-to-end outcome. |
| Staff avoid reporting failed experiments | What happened the last time someone reported bad news? | Examine leadership behavior and incentives. |

# Evidence questions

A company anecdote can illustrate a practice without proving that the entire company follows the model. Ask what changed, when, for whom, against which baseline, and whether alternative explanations were considered.

Faster decisions alone are insufficient evidence of better outcomes. Pair speed with quality and customer impact, then investigate trade-offs.
