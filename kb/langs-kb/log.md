# Bundle Update Log

## 2026-10-03
* **Initialization**: Created the OKF v0.2 bundle on programming language and runtime ideas, Oct 2018 – Oct 2026, organized into eras E1–E4.
* **Creation**: Six research agents were launched, one per slice: systems languages and memory safety; managed languages and VMs; dynamic languages; JavaScript, web and WebAssembly; functional and research languages; and compilers, tooling, accelerators and AI.
* **Update**: First publish of partial results. The managed, JS/Wasm and functional slices are complete; the systems, dynamic and tooling/AI slices are still in progress. A draft [executive summary](/executive-summary.md) and the [methodology](/references/methodology.md) were added. Synthesis concepts and verification are pending.
* **Update**: Research stopped early by request. All six slices are written except 9 ideas (language editions, subinterpreters, JIT for dynamic languages, copy-and-patch JIT, gradual typing for dynamic languages, Python superset languages, Python packaging, multiple dispatch, time-to-first-plot), 4 languages (Q# and quantum, SQL evolution, Nix, COBOL/Fortran) and 6 runtimes (LLVM, MLIR, Cranelift, GCC, eBPF, GPU runtimes). Links to these concepts are currently broken. Synthesis concepts and verification were not done.
