# What happened
RFC 9669 published the BPF instruction-set specification on the IETF Standards Track in October 2024.[^announcement]

# Why it matters
**Interpretation:** an instruction-set standard provides a common reference. It should not be confused with a stable specification for every kernel helper, attachment point or userspace runtime.
