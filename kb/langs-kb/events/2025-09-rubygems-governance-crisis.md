# What happened
Ruby Central described changes to stewardship and production access for RubyGems and Bundler. In October the Ruby project announced a transition of their repositories to Ruby core stewardship, while Ruby Central would continue operating the service.[^transition][^announcement]

# Why it matters
**Interpretation:** control of source repositories and operation of a package registry are separate responsibilities. The later division of those roles is more precise than treating the incident as a service shutdown.
