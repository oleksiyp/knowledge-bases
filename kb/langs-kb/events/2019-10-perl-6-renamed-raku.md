# What happened
The language previously called Perl 6 was renamed Raku in October 2019. The project FAQ records the change and distinguishes Raku from Perl.[^announcement]

# Why it matters
**Interpretation:** a separate name clarifies that users face a different language rather than an ordinary Perl version upgrade. Renaming alone establishes no adoption trend.
