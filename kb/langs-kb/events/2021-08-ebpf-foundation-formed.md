# What happened
Facebook, Google, Isovalent, Microsoft and Netflix launched the eBPF Foundation under the Linux Foundation. The announcement established a shared institutional home for ecosystem work.[^announcement]

# Why it matters
**Interpretation:** cross-company sponsorship supports infrastructure continuity. It does not by itself standardize every kernel hook, helper or deployment environment.
