# What happened
METR reported a randomized study of 16 experienced open-source developers working on 246 tasks in familiar repositories. With the early-2025 AI tools tested, participants took 19% longer on average when allowed to use AI.[^announcement]

# Why it matters
**Interpretation:** the result challenges blanket productivity claims in this setting. It does not measure all developers, unfamiliar repositories, every task type or later generations of tools.
