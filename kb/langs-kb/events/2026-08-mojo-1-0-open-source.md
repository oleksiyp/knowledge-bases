# What happened
Modular opened the Mojo compiler and tooling under Apache 2.0 with LLVM exceptions on August 18. This followed the August 11 announcement of Mojo 1.0.[^release][^announcement]

# Why it matters
**Interpretation:** compiler availability materially changes who can inspect and build the implementation. It does not establish complete Python compatibility or broad production adoption.
