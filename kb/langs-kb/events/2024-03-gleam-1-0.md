# What happened
Louis Pilfold released Gleam v1.0.0 on 2024-03-04. It was the first stable release, covering the language, compiler, build tool, package manager, formatter, language server and the WASM/JS APIs. Gleam follows semantic versioning, so backwards compatibility became a priority.[^gleam-v1][^infoworld-gleam-v1] The announcement was open about funding. Fly.io provided about half, and the creator earned less than half a median London lead-developer salary.[^gleam-v1]

# Why it matters
- It gave the BEAM a **sound static type system** years before Elixir's gradual types were complete, and put a polished all-in-one toolchain on Hex and OTP.
- After v1, adoption signals rose quickly. In its first Stack Overflow appearance (2025) Gleam was the 2nd most admired language at 70%, behind only Rust.[^so-2025-tech]
- It also showed the gap between sentiment and production use. Only about 6% of 841 respondents to Gleam's own 2024 survey used it in production.[^gleam-survey-2024]
