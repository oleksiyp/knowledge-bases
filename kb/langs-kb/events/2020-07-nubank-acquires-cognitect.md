# What happened
On 2020-07-23 Nubank announced it had acquired Cognitect, the consultancy founded by Clojure's creator Rich Hickey and Stuart Halloway that also built the Datomic database. Clojure stayed an independent open-source project led by Hickey, with Nubank as its main corporate sponsor. Datomic development continued under Halloway and Hickey.[^nubank-cognitect]

# Why it matters
It turned a language that relied on a small consultancy into one funded by its largest production user. The follow-through was real. In April 2023 Nubank made all Datomic editions free, with binaries under Apache-2.0.[^datomic-free] When Hickey retired from commercial work in August 2023, Nubank kept employing core developers Alex Miller and Fogus to work on Clojure under his guidance.[^next-rich] By 2025, fintech was Clojure's leading industry, and 26% of survey respondents worked in organisations of over 1,000 people, many of them presumably at Nubank.[^soc-2025] The trade-off is concentration: Clojure's fortunes are now tied to one bank's technology strategy.
