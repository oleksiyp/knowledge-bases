Ruby 4.0 included experimental ZJIT. Its release notes said the new compiler could outperform the interpreter but was not yet faster than YJIT.[^announcement]

# Why it matters
**Interpretation:** a successor compiler can be worth developing before it beats the incumbent. Experimental integration is not evidence of a completed replacement.
